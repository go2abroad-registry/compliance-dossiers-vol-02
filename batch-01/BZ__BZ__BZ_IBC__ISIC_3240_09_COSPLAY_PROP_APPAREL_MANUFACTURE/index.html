Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service, Ministry of Finance (portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

A non-resident International Business Company (IBC) is automatically exempt from national income tax under the International Business Companies Act. The IBC must apply to the Belize Tax Service for a General Sales Tax (GST) registration number only if it makes taxable supplies of goods or services in Belize exceeding the statutory threshold. The application is submitted on Form GST-01 together with the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office address, and proof of the local registered agent. Registration is granted generally within seven working days. No separate TIN is issued solely for income tax purposes because IBCs are outside the income tax net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of goods and to services supplied to non-residents for use wholly outside Belize, provided the supplier retains evidence of export, receipt of payment in foreign currency, and the non-resident status of the customer. For theatrical costume and synthetic wig manufacturing, the sale of finished goods shipped directly to foreign buyers qualifies for 0% GST.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Form C-5)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Bank Credit Confirmation

No real-time fiscal clearance mandate exists. Electronic invoicing is permissible but not compulsory. There is no API-based clearance, digital stamping, or government pre-clearance requirement; paper and PDF invoices are accepted for GST compliance.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to post-audit verification by the Belize Tax Service. Wrongful or fraudulent claims attract a penalty of 50% of the tax evaded plus monthly interest at 2.25%, and may additionally expose the IBC to a clawback of the full zero-rated benefit, cancellation of GST registration, and criminal liability with imprisonment up to five years under the General Sales Tax Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize operates a fixed-exchange-rate regime with the Belize dollar pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Current-account transactions are generally convertible for non-residents; resident capital-account transfers require exchange-control approval. A qualifying International Business Company is treated as a non-resident entity and is excluded from the Exchange Control Act restrictions, provided it does not carry on business with Belize residents. Equity injections, shareholder loans, sales collections, and profit remittances from the IBC may therefore be made in USD without CBB conversion approval. Profit-remittance hurdles are predominantly documentary: the bank will require proof of beneficial ownership, source-of-funds evidence, a board resolution declaring the dividend, and a solvency review. No Belize withholding tax is imposed on IBC distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters, export proceeds must be repatriated through an authorised dealer and 100% of the foreign-currency receipts must be surrendered into Belize dollars within the CBB-stipulated timeline, normally not exceeding six months from the date of shipment, unless a specific exemption has been granted. This surrender obligation does not apply to a qualifying non-resident IBC under the International Business Companies Act. Accordingly, export receipts from cosplay costumes, licensed theatrical robes, and synthetic wigs may be retained in USD in the IBC's offshore or Belize-based external account without mandatory conversion, as long as no Belize-resident business is transacted.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) acting as the exchange-control authority under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 263, and the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 89.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-Medium. Non-bank electronic money institutions incorporated in the UK, EU, or US generally do not maintain a Belize nexus and are not passport-licensed to provide domestic Belize clearing. Many EMIs decline newly formed Belize IBCs because of perceived offshore-country risk, lack of economic substance, and the absence of direct correspondent banking relationships. EMIs may be used only as complementary e-commerce collection rails for Stripe and Shopify settlement balances, not as the primary treasury or trade-settlement rail.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Qualifying International Business Company ('IBC') is exempted from Belize income tax, capital gains tax, Business Tax and all withholding taxes under section 6 of the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — dividends and other distributions declared by an IBC to non-resident shareholders are paid free of Belize withholding tax and free of any Belize income tax under IBCA section 6. There is no statutory obligation on the IBC to appoint a withholding agent or file dividend WHT returns.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Belize statutory Local File or Master File threshold in Belize dollars has been gazetted for IBCs. The only applicable international benchmark is the OECD/G20 BEPS Country-by-Country Reporting threshold (consolidated group revenue of USD 750 million) for groups covered by the BEPS Inclusive Framework; a medium-sized cosplay manufacturing/retail group will not reach this threshold but must nonetheless retain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation for intra-group transactions under the 5-year record-keeping requirement in the IBC (Amendment) Act, 2018.

Economic substance scope: Applicability: Belize's Economic Substance Regulations — SI No. 59 of 2019, as amended — apply to relevant entities carrying on relevant activities in or from Belize, namely banking, insurance, fund management, financing/leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centre, shipping, and intellectual property holding. Tailoring and retailing of cosplay costumes, theatrical robes and synthetic wigs is not a listed relevant activity; therefore the IBC is outside the ESR reporting cycle provided it does not (i) hold exploitable IP as a pure IP-holding vehicle, or (ii) operate as a distribution and service centre for affiliated enterprises in Belize. The IBC must nevertheless demonstrate minimum substance adequate to its business: a Belize registered office, a licensed registered agent, accurate books and records physically available in Belize, an identifiable principal function performed by directors, and beneficial ownership transparency. Adverse substance reviews by the Registrar or IFSC can trigger disqualification of the exempt status.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax exemption abuse and unlawful domestic trade

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the IBC tax exemption certificate; retroactive assessment to Belize corporate income tax at 25% of taxable income plus Business Tax on gross receipts; interest and default penalties of up to 50% of the tax assessed; disqualification from enforcing contracts entered into in breach of IBCA section 8. Each director or officer knowingly party to the breach is liable to a fine of up to BZ$10,000.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the IBC's assessed Belize taxes and penalties where the exemption certificate was obtained or used by fraud; the courts may pierce the corporate veil, issue a departure prohibition order preventing the director from travelling, and commit the director for contempt or tax fraud up to a maximum of 5 years imprisonment.
Shell company and mandatory economic substance deficiency

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may refuse annual renewal, strike the company off, or refer the file to the IFSC; ESR-related penalties include civil penalty notices of up to BZ$50,000 and cancellation of the IBC license; the company may be flagged as an economic substance risk in the jurisdiction's tax information exchange responses.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly certify the annual declaration with false substance facts are exposed to fines, disqualification orders of up to 5 years, and personal civil liability to the company and creditors for management failures; travel bans may be imposed pending restoration of records.
AML, CFT and beneficial ownership non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act, Cap. 104, as amended: fines up to BZ$250,000 for legal persons, forfeiture of the merchant settlement balances and assets derived from the trade, cancellation of the IBC's exempt status, and inclusion in adverse FATF/CFATF mutual evaluation follow-up reports.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are subject to custodial penalties of up to 14 years for predicate money-laundering offences, personal civil restitution orders, and international arrest or extradition risk where Stripe/Shopify proceeds are layered through foreign bank accounts.
Fraudulent trading and trading after strike-off

Statutory sanction: Use of a struck-off company is an offence under the IBCA; the directors become personally liable for new contractual debts and for the minimum annual registry fees accruing each month; restoration requires discharge of arrears, late penalties, and an application to the Registrar or Court. The company's bank and payment-acquirer relationships are automatically terminated upon public notice of strike-off.

Director personal liability: The court may order the directors to restore all settlement funds collected during the struck-off period, impose a director disqualification order, and commit the directors for contempt or fraud where the IBC was presented as a solvent licensed entity to payment processors.
False declarations, forged certificates and cross-border tax evasion

Statutory sanction: The offence is punishable on summary conviction with a fine of up to BZ$20,000 and imprisonment of up to 3 years, or on indictment with an unlimited fine and imprisonment of up to 7 years; the IBC forfeits all exemptions and is subject to immediate strike-off.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for guilty directors: personal fines, confiscation of settlement proceeds, disqualification, and extradition in the case of foreign tax evasion where the false Belize certificate is used to defraud the tax authority of the target market.
