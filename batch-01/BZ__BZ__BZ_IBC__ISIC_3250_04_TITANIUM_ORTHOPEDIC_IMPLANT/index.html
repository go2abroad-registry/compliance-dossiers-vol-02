A virtual office or registered-agent-only office is permissible for an International Business Company where the company does not maintain physical operations in Belize, provided the registered office is at the address of a licensed registered agent in Belize and is open during normal business hours. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; taxpayer self-service and filing through the official Belize Tax Portal administered by the Tax Service Department

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned IBC is by default exempt from Belize income tax and has no statutory obligation to obtain a Belize taxpayer identification number (TIN) unless it carries on a taxable activity in Belize, becomes an employer, or voluntarily registers for goods and services tax. To obtain a TIN, the entity must file the prescribed Tax Service Department registration application through the Belize Tax Portal, attaching the IBC certificate of incorporation, good standing certificate, and evidence of a local agent or place of business if applicable. If the IBC does not have a physical Belize presence, the Tax Service Department may issue a temporary TIN for customs and GST purposes and require, for GST registration, the designation of a resident representative. Processing is generally completed within seven to ten business days after submission of complete documentation. A person with no domestic taxable supply is not ordinarily required to register, but a TIN is mandatory when making taxable supplies above the GST threshold or when importing/exporting through the ASYCUDA World customs platform.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election mechanism exists under Belize domestic law. An International Business Company is automatically tax-exempt for up to 50 years pursuant to the International Business Companies Act, subject to satisfaction of the economic substance filing obligations where applicable. An IBC may elect to surrender its tax-exempt status and become an ordinary taxable Belize company by written notice to the registrar, after which the Income and Business Tax Act applies in full. No statutory hybrid-payable entity classification election is available in Belize for an IBC.

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply of goods qualifies for zero-rated GST treatment when the supplier and recipient respectively are properly registered and the goods are physically exported from Belize by or on behalf of the supplier within the period contemplated by the contract, with title passing to the recipient overseas. For a titanium orthopedic implant manufacturer, the export sale must be supported by a valid cross-border contract, customs declaration proving physical removal, and payment received through banking channels. Zero-rating also applies to directly related services supplied to non-residents where the place of performance and enjoyment is outside Belize, including freight, logistics, and export-related engineering services. The taxpayer must retain sufficient documentary evidence at the time of filing; otherwise the supply is treated as taxable at 12.5% GST.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged in ASYCUDA World
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (where claimed under trade agreements)
  • Export Permit or health/technical certificate for high-risk medical devices if applicable
  • GST export schedule and zero-rating worksheet retained for tax audit

Belize does not currently operate a mandatory real-time electronic invoicing, digital stamping, or government fiscal clearance system for standard GST invoices. Registered suppliers must issue invoices containing the prescribed statutory particulars, retain them for audit, and file GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Portal. Customs transactions are processed through the ASYCUDA World electronic single window. No API-based pre-clearance or fiscal e-invoice stamping is required for export transactions at this time, although the Tax Service Department may require specific electronic reporting formats for taxpayers selected for high-risk audit.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to post-clearance audit by the Tax Service Department and Customs. Where the documentary bundle is incomplete or the physical export cannot be established, the previously zero-rated amount is clawed back as output GST, together with statutory interest at the rate prescribed under the Tax Administration Act and an additional penalty of up to 200% of the tax assessed. Fraudulent or reckless claims may give rise to criminal prosecution, fines of up to BZD 25,000 per offence and imprisonment for up to two years. In addition, failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day exchange-control deadline can result in penalties under Belize exchange-control legislation, loss of GST registration privileges, and enhanced audit scrutiny for subsequent export quarters.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged to the United States dollar at a fixed exchange rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Belize restricts capital-account convertibility for resident entities under the Exchange Control Act, but International Business Companies (IBCs) incorporated under the International Business Companies Act are deemed non-resident for exchange-control purposes and therefore enjoy unrestricted convertibility on both capital and current accounts. Profit remittances to foreign shareholders in the form of dividends, royalties, or management fees are freely transferable without central bank approval, provided the IBC remains in good standing and the beneficial owner, intermediary, and underlying payment are supported by adequate AML/KYC documentation. Commercial banks in Belize will nevertheless conduct independent source-of-funds and source-of-wealth checks before releasing inbound dividends or redemption proceeds to offshore beneficiaries.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Belize Exchange Control Regulations, a resident exporter is generally required to surrender foreign-currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer within 30 days of receipt and to convert 100% of eligible proceeds into Belize dollars unless an exemption is granted. However, a Belize IBC is statutorily exempt from all exchange-control restrictions, including any mandatory surrender timeline or conversion ratio, by virtue of the International Business Companies Act. Thus, for this entity there is no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio. In practice, export proceeds received in USD may be retained in USD-denominated accounts or converted at the pegged rate through the commercial bank's FX desk at the company's discretion, subject to the bank's internal review and the submission of a bill of lading/invoice where requested.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to medium feasibility. Belize does not license independent cross-border electronic money institutions (EMIs) within its domestic banking framework. A Belize IBC may open accounts with overseas licensed digital EMIs (e.g., a UK FCA-authorised EMI or an EU-authorised payment institution) subject to the EMI's own KYC and risk appetite, but such offshore EMI relationships are operationally fragile because many corresponding banks reject Belize IBC structures absent evidence of physical substance. The Central Bank of Belize has introduced a fintech innovation sandbox but has not yet promulgated a full EMI licensing regime. Accordingly, offshore EMIs may be used as a supplementary clearing rail but not as the primary treasury channel for trade settlement.

Shareholder capital injections are routed as SWIFT MT103 wires from the shareholder's offshore bank to the Belize commercial bank, referencing the IBC registration number and a board resolution that authorizes the capital contribution. Acceptable payment purpose codes include 'Equity capital investment' (FDI) and 'LOAN' for shareholder loans; the Belize bank will allocate the inbound remittance to the capital escrow account or the main operational account as instructed. The bank must verify the ultimate origin of funds and will require a copy of the certified board resolution, the shareholder register, and a capital-injection statement from the corporate secretary. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (standard corporate rate under the Belize Income and Business Tax Act, Cap. 55; the Belize International Business Company ('IBC') is taxed at this rate only on income accrued in or derived from Belize, while foreign-source international business income remains outside the Belize tax net in accordance with the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

File the annual return and ESR declaration within 90 days after the IBC's fiscal year-end; the statutory annual licence fee is payable on or before each anniversary of incorporation.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 — Company Name and IBC Registration Number Official certificate name of the IBC, registration number, and date of incorporation as shown in the registry charter. Enter the name exactly as registered; append the jurisdiction and entity identifier (BZ/BZ_IBC/ELF code) for cross-referencing in bank KYC files. Late annual-licence fee: surcharge of 10% per month up to 50% of the fee; strike-off and dissolution of the IBC follows one year of arrears. Late ESR declaration: automatic fixed penalty of BZD 10,000 escalating to BZD 50,000 per year on continued default, with publication of the default and automatic exchange of information to foreign tax authorities.
Box 2 — Registered Office and Registered Agent in Belize Street address of the registered office in Belize and identity of the licensed registered agent. A non-resident IBC must maintain a registered office and licensed agent in Belize; post-office-box-only addresses are admissible only with prior Registry approval; provide full agent licence particulars. Late annual-licence fee: surcharge of 10% per month up to 50% of the fee; strike-off and dissolution of the IBC follows one year of arrears. Late ESR declaration: automatic fixed penalty of BZD 10,000 escalating to BZD 50,000 per year on continued default, with publication of the default and automatic exchange of information to foreign tax authorities.
Box 3 — Directors and Beneficial Owners Schedule Full legal names, passport numbers, service addresses, nationality and shareholding percentage of each director and of every shareholder holding 10% or more of the issued capital. Non-resident individuals must submit notarised copies of passports and proof of residential address; the bearer-share prohibition requires full beneficial-ownership disclosure to the registered agent. Late annual-licence fee: surcharge of 10% per month up to 50% of the fee; strike-off and dissolution of the IBC follows one year of arrears. Late ESR declaration: automatic fixed penalty of BZD 10,000 escalating to BZD 50,000 per year on continued default, with publication of the default and automatic exchange of information to foreign tax authorities.
Box 4 — Economic Substance Activity Declaration Declaration of the relevant activity performed (IP holding, distribution and service centre, financing and leasing, etc.) and confirmation of core income-generating activities in Belize. For an orthopedic-implants manufacturer holding proprietary EB-3D process or porous-titanium patent IP, declare the IP-holding category, local staff numbers, premises and operating expenditure; a pure manufacturing function is outside the enumerated ESR relevant-activity list but still requires declaration of the entity's applicable category. Late annual-licence fee: surcharge of 10% per month up to 50% of the fee; strike-off and dissolution of the IBC follows one year of arrears. Late ESR declaration: automatic fixed penalty of BZD 10,000 escalating to BZD 50,000 per year on continued default, with publication of the default and automatic exchange of information to foreign tax authorities.
