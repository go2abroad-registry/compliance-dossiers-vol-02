Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance and Economic Development, Government of Belize (portal: https://tax.belize.gov.bz); customs execution by the Belize Customs Department under ASYCUDA World; company registry: International Business Companies Registry, Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry.

E-government filing portal

An IBC incorporated under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) is statutorily exempt from all Belize income, capital gains, stamp, inheritance and business taxes for 50 years from incorporation and is not required to procure an income-tax TIN; its statutory identifier is the IBC registered number from the Belize Companies Registry. If the IBC nevertheless engages in any taxable domestic supply (generally prohibited by the IBC Act), it must register for GST on the Belize Tax Portal and obtain a GST registration number; an export-only IBC remains exempt and files the annual economic substance confirmation rather than income tax returns. For exports, the IBC must hold a valid import/export licence from the Belize Trade Licensing Board and be activated as an exporter in ASYCUDA World before lodging a Customs Export Declaration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The IBC may apply to the Minister of Finance for election into ordinary-resident (domestic) tax status, which revokes the IBC exemption and subjects the company to income tax and GST on Belize-source activities; conversely, a domestic Belize company may convert to IBC status and obtain the 50-year exemption certificate. Belize does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box regime, but the IBC's constitutive documents may designate its tax classification for foreign treaty, bank and license filings, and the directors may resolve to apply hybrid-mismatch alignment rules under Belize economic substance legislation. A formal Tax Exemption Certificate may be issued by the Minister to evidence zero-tax status to banks, customs and counterparties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory registration where taxable supplies exceed BZ$75,000 (approximately US$37,500) in any 12-month or calendar-year period; registration is also obtainable voluntarily by export-only traders to claim zero-rating, and exporters must file returns even when all supplies are zero-rated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods, including professional-grade intraocular lenses, are zero-rated under Schedule III of the General Sales Tax Act where the goods are exported by the supplier to a non-resident consignee outside Belize within the statutory export window (generally within 28 days of supply or under an agreed export contract term), the supplier retains full export documentation, and the goods are not returned, retained or diverted to the Belize domestic market. Ancillary freight and logistics services supplied to a non-resident in direct connection with the exported lenses may also be zero-rated, while the underlying manufacturing and exported-services income remains fully exempt for the IBC under the IBC Act subject to economic substance filing.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) lodged electronically in the ASYCUDA World system; controlled medical-device export permit where applicable
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Airway Bill (air) evidencing dispatch of the intraocular lenses to the overseas consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank FX inflow credit advice issued by a licensed Belize bank confirming receipt of export sale proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice (FOB terms) and detailed packing list
  • Certificate of Origin issued by the Belize Chamber of Commerce or authorized certifier where preferential tariff treatment is claimed
  • ISO 13485/CE-required device certificates and sterility certificates for the destination-market importer and Belize customs audit

No real-time fiscal clearance, SAF-T or digital stamping regime is currently in force. GST-registered traders must issue serially numbered, prescribed-content tax invoices (seller identity, GST number, description, taxable value, GST at 12.5% or 0%) and file prescribed GST returns through the Belize Tax Portal under mandatory e-filing. All export declarations must be lodged electronically through the ASYCUDA World single-window customs system, which is the only API-based clearance used for export substantiation.

Export zero-rating is audited by the Belize Tax Service Department and Belize Customs Department using ASYCUDA records, bank FX inflow reports and bill-of-lading matching. Where zero-rating is claimed without genuine export (false declarations, goods diverted into Belize, or export proceeds not repatriated to Belize within the prescribed exchange-control period), the Comptroller of Revenue assesses the unpaid GST with a penalty of up to 100% of the tax and monthly interest, and criminal prosecution for tax fraud carries fines of up to BZ$50,000 and imprisonment of up to three years, with joint and several personal liability of directors. Customs penalties include forfeiture of the lenses and fines up to BZ$25,000 or imprisonment up to five years for fraudulent declarations; an IBC additionally faces registry sanctions (striking-off, revocation of the exemption certificate, and BZ$100 per day reinstatement penalties) and rejection of its economic substance filing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) operates under a fixed exchange-rate regime pegged to the U.S. dollar (USD) at 2.00 BZD per 1.00 USD. Current-account transactions are generally freely convertible through authorized dealers, while capital-account transfers, including foreign direct investment inflows and outward profit remittances by an International Business Company (IBC), are subject to declaration, authorized dealer intermediation, and exchange-control approval. IBCs are statutorily non-resident for exchange-control purposes; remittances of profits and capital from Belize-source operations may be restricted, but an IBC conducting offshore business should route funds without domestic conversion concerns. Profit remittance hurdles arise when the IBC inadvertently generates Belize-source income or when the beneficiary banking jurisdiction requires evidence of underlying commercial substance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act and Central Bank of Belize Notices, Belize resident exporters must repatriate export proceeds within 180 days of shipment and are required to surrender foreign-currency receipts to an authorized dealer, converting them into Belize dollars at the official pegged rate. IBCs that are non-resident are not normally obliged to surrender export receipts to the Central Bank; however, if the IBC is treated as resident for exchange-control purposes due to management and control in Belize or conducts a Belize trade, the statutory surrender timeline and full conversion ratio apply. For the IBC treasury blueprint, contracts and shipping documents should be structured to preserve non-resident status and avoid surrender obligations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (exercising foreign exchange control authority under the Belize Exchange Control Act, Chapter 35 of the Laws of Belize)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability. Belize IBCs are not eligible for mainstream offshore EMI account opening because the IBC is an unregulated offshore vehicle, and the intraocular lens manufacturing scope is classified as a high-risk special-license activity under AML/CFT frameworks. Reputable EMIs regulated by the FCA or FinCEN generally decline IBCs without a regulated parent or nexus. Legal constraints include lack of passporting into Belize, inability to obtain local EMI licensing without a full banking license, and heightened due diligence on medical-device trade. An EMI may be used only as a liquidity sub-account after the regime-shielded primary banking relationship is established.

Master / Local File threshold: BZD 10,000,000 in cross-border related-party transactions; for IBCs benefiting from a full tax exemption, the Belize transfer-pricing rules in the Income and Business Tax Act are practically inapplicable, but the Belize International Financial Services Commission retains supervisory authority if the IBC holds a special license or carries on regulated activity; mandatory Local File and Master File production is compelled when the total related-party transaction value exceeds BZD 10,000,000 and the entity is not fully tax-exempt under the IBC Act.

An exempt IBC is generally not within the net of the Economic Substance rules if it is tax resident outside Belize; however, if the intraocular acrylic lens business is managed and controlled in Belize and the manufacturing or IP-related income is attributable to Belize activity, the IBC must satisfy full economic substance by maintaining adequate premises, full-time employees, board meetings in Belize, and core income-generating activities in Belize. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Offshore Tax Evasion and Sham Exemption

Statutory sanction: Unpaid tax assessed at the full corporate rate (25% business tax and individual income tax rates where applicable) plus interest at 2.5% per month or part of a month, and a penal tax of up to 100% of the tax evaded; criminal prosecution before the Belize High Court for tax fraud attracts a fine of up to BZD 50,000 and imprisonment for up to five years.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the tax, penalties, and interest if the corporation is used as a vehicle to evade taxes. Belize courts will pierce the corporate veil when the company is a facade or alter ego; directors face civil restitution, a travel ban imposed by the Financial Intelligence Unit, disqualification as a director, and imprisonment for up to five years.

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BZD 10,000 for each year of non-compliance with the Economic Substance Act; continued non-compliance after a 30-day notice triggers a further fine of BZD 100,000 and strike-off from the Belize Registry; any revenue obtained from the relevant activity is subject to a ministerial order freezing the IBC's bank accounts. The statutory sanction also allows a Belize court to require surrender of the corporate registration and the winding-up of the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers can be personally examined by the Enforcement Committee and held jointly liable for up to the amount of the IBC's annual financial benefit; a director of a struck-off company is barred from acting as a director of any Belize company for a period of three years, and a personal travel prohibition may be imposed pending the liquidation examination.

Statutory sanction: Under the Belize Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, the IBC and its authorized agents face fines of up to BZD 500,000 for a first offense and BZD 1,000,000 for a second offense; the court can order forfeiture of the entire transaction value, revocation of the IBC's license, and closure of the Belize bank account.
