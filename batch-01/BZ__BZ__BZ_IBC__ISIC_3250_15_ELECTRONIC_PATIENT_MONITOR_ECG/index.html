Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (Ministry of Finance) – official portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a fixed establishment or other taxable presence in Belize must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Belize Tax Service using the prescribed registration form, supported by certified copies of the entity's formation documents, registered office certificate, evidence of local address, and appointment of a local tax agent. For an International Business Company (IBC), the entity is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax, business tax, stamp duty and capital gains tax; the IBC may obtain a Certificate of Exemption from its registered agent or the Belize Companies Registry under the International Business Companies Act. A foreign entity that makes taxable supplies in Belize and meets the GST threshold must additionally register for GST and obtain a VAT/GST ID number from the Belize Tax Service, filing a separate GST registration application.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign entity establishing a fixed establishment or other taxable presence in Belize must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Belize Tax Service using the prescribed registration form, supported by certified copies of the entity's formation documents, registered office certificate, evidence of local address, and appointment of a local tax agent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An IBC may elect under the International Business Companies Act to be treated as a Belize domestic company, thereby losing its tax-exempt status and becoming subject to ordinary Belize income tax at the applicable corporate rates; this election is irrevocable and requires filing of an election notice with the Companies Registry. Belize does not operate a US-style check-the-box regime, and no hybrid mismatch elections are currently available. Certain cross‑border arrangements may be challenged under Belize's participation in the OECD Base Erosion and Profit Shifting framework, requiring self-assessment and filing of additional returns where applicable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD $150,000 (Belize dollars) in annual taxable supplies; registration is mandatory for every person (including a foreign entity with a fixed establishment in Belize) who carries on a taxable activity and has taxable supplies exceeding this threshold. Foreign entities may voluntarily register if they make taxable supplies and wish to claim input credits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exportation of tangible goods from Belize, including multi-parameter electronic patient monitors, is zero-rated under section 44 of the General Sales Tax Act when the exporter is registered for GST and the goods are physically removed from Belize within a reasonable period. Also zero-rated are services physically performed offshore, and services directly related to goods exported from Belize, provided the services are supplied to an international recipient, paid in foreign currency, and not used in Belize. The supplier must obtain and retain documentary evidence of export.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence of foreign currency payment
  • Certificate of Origin (where applicable)
  • GST Export Schedule and zero-rating supporting worksheet

Belize does not currently mandate a real-time electronic invoicing or fiscal-clearance digital stamping system. Registered taxpayers must issue serially numbered tax invoices in an approved electronic or paper format, retain a valid GST registration number on invoices, and file monthly GST returns and payments via the Belize Tax Service online e-file portal. Although electronic filing is required for registered taxpayers, there is no continuous API-based clearance or pre-approval of invoices by the tax authority.

The Belize Tax Service may audit any zero-rated export transaction and require production of the complete export documentation bundle within a stated period. If the export is false, unsupported, or the goods are found to have remained in or returned to Belize, the taxpayer must repay the zero-rated GST together with interest at 15% per annum and penalties of up to 100% of the tax under the General Sales Tax Act. Where export proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days, the Central Bank of Belize foreign exchange regulations may be engaged, leading to administrative fines, loss of export status and judicial enforcement; deliberate fraud may also result in criminal prosecution.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged to the US dollar (USD) at a fixed rate of BZD 2.00 per USD 1.00 under the Currency Act; the current account (trade goods, services, dividends, interest) is freely convertible for authorized dealer transactions, while capital account movements remain subject to CBB exchange control approval under the Exchange Control Act. Belize International Business Companies (IBCs) are statutorily designated as non-resident entities for exchange control purposes: their operations must be transacted in a foreign currency, and they are exempt from convertibility restrictions, CBB prior approval, and surrender obligations on foreign-held funds; profit remittance by an IBC to foreign shareholders is permitted without CBB licensing provided the dividend payment is made from an external (offshore) foreign-currency account, whereas conversion into BZD or onshore settlement with a Belize resident would trigger exchange control scrutiny.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters under the Exchange Control Act, foreign currency export proceeds must be repatriated to Belize within six months from the date of export and, upon receipt, must be offered for sale to an authorized dealer in exchange for BZD within one month absent an approved retention facility or designated foreign-currency account. Non-resident IBCs, including the target entity under the International Business Companies Act, are expressly exempt from repatriation and mandatory surrender ratios and may retain 100% of export proceeds in free foreign-currency accounts held with a licensed Belize bank or licensed foreign bank.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), operating under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262 and the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 58 of the Laws of Belize; the CBB serves as the sole monetary, foreign exchange, and financial stability authority, with delegated exchange control powers administered through authorized dealers (licensed domestic commercial banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low Feasibility: Belize has no statutory digital banking, electronic money institution (EMI), or payment-service-issuer licensing regime distinct from the Banking Act; non-bank fintech providers cannot lawfully hold client funds or issue payment instruments in Belize without a Banking and Financial Institutions Act license or a Money Services Business license under the FSC supervision framework. Offshore EMIs (e.g., Revolut Business, Wise Business, Airwallex) do not hold Belize licenses, would be deemed unauthorized deposit takers if they onboard a Belize IBC with onshore settlement, and local authorized dealers are prohibited from routing corporate payments through unlicensed third-party payment aggregators; cross-border EMI rails may only be used by the IBC as a treasury visibility tool outside Belize, subject to strict prohibition against receiving trade receivables or paying trade payables from Belize-located operations.
