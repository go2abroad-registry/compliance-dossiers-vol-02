Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service, Ministry of Finance, operating the MyTax online portal (mytax.belize.gov.bz); customs matters are handled by the Belize Customs and Excise Department under the Belize Customs and Excise Act.

E-government filing portal

A foreign juridical person or an International Business Company is not automatically obliged to obtain a Belize Tax Identification Number unless it carries in Belize a taxable activity or a permanent establishment, employs local staff, or applies for VAT registration. The IBC's statutory identity is evidenced by its Companies Registry registration number issued under the International Business Companies Act. To obtain a TIN/VAT number, the entity or its local tax representative submits Form TN-01 to the Belize Tax Service via the MyTax portal, accompanied by a certificate of incorporation, proof of registered office, and a notarized authorization of the applicant. VAT registration, when required, is made by completing the VAT-01 form through the same portal and is effective from the date indicated in the registration certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone – Belize International Business Companies are statutorily exempt from income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and all present or future Belize taxes under section 4 of the International Business Companies Act. The entity is treated as a non-resident offshore limited company unless an election to be taxed as a Belize resident is made; foreign-source income, including brokerage commissions from raw cotton bale trading, remains outside the Belize tax net.

Available election forms

Belize has no U.S.-style check-the-box classification. An IBC may elect under section 92 of the International Business Companies Act to convert into a domestic-trader company, which would terminate the automatic tax exemption. In addition, the Business and Economic Substance Act permits an IBC to make an annual substantive economic presence notification but does not create a hybrid entity election; Belize has not enacted OECD hybrid mismatch optionality. Single-member IBCs are recognized as corpus of members and are entitled to a sole member resolution without losing limited liability.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 100,000 annual taxable turnover (well below the threshold IBCs are ineligible to register unless making domestic taxable supplies). An IBC engaged wholly in offshore export brokerage has no domestic taxable supplies and therefore no VAT registration requirement.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Value Added Tax Act, exports of goods and services are zero-rated only if the supplier is a VAT-registered person and the goods are physically exported from Belize within a prescribed deadline, or the service is performed directly for a non-resident who derives no benefit in Belize. An IBC engaged solely in international brokerage of ginned raw cotton bales is not, in the default offshore status, entitled to claim zero-rating because it is not VAT registered and makes no taxable supply. If the IBC were to voluntarily register, a genuine export sale of raw cotton bales with full customs evidence could be invoiced at 0% VAT, allowing recovery of any Belize input VAT attributable to the export.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document style) officially processed and stamped by Belize Customs and Excise Department
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical transit of the cotton bales from Belize to the named foreign buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a licensed bank in Belize demonstrating the receipt of export proceeds in hard currency
  • Sales contract or brokerage agreement identifying the foreign buyer/end customer and commission terms
  • Supplier invoice, weight certificates, and cotton bale inspection certificate (when available) to support the declared value and standard ISIC 4620 qualities

Belize does not operate real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory electronic invoicing APIs. There is no B2B e-invoice digital stamping requirement. Export invoices, proforma invoices, and brokerage commission invoices are issued in paper or PDF form and must be kept for at least 10 years for audit purposes. Local domestic taxable supplies above the registration threshold require approved cash register receipts, but an exempt IBC is outside this regime.

A false or fraudulent zero-rating claim may trigger a re-assessment of VAT at 12.5% with retrospective effect, plus default interest at the statutory rate, and a civil penalty of 150% of the tax undercharged. Criminal prosecution for tax fraud can lead to fines up to BZD 50,000 and imprisonment for up to five years, with seizure of goods and forfeiture of the export documents. Where an IBC is found to have claimed export VAT refunds while failing to prove physical export or timely repatriation of proceeds within 180 days, the Belize Tax Service may claw back all recovered input VAT, impose a 20% surcharge, and refer the matter for economic substance compliance review, the failure of which carries administrative penalties up to BZD 100,000 and possible striking-off under the Business and Economic Substance Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) operates under a fixed peg of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. The peg ensures full convertibility for current-account transactions (trade in goods, services, interest, dividends, and foreign remittances) when routed through an authorised dealer bank. Capital-account inflows and outflows—including direct investment, equity injections, loans, and portfolio movements—are generally subject to exchange control approval if they touch the resident banking system, although Belize IBCs that are beneficially owned by non-residents and that conduct business wholly outside Belize are treated as non-resident entities. For such an IBC engaged in international brokerage of raw cotton bales, no exchange control approval is required for the receipt of offshore sale proceeds and the remittance of profit to shareholders, provided that (i) the IBC holds no funds in a resident Belize dollar account, (ii) all transactions are denominated in USD or other foreign currency, and (iii) the IBC files an annual non-resident declaration with its banking partner. Profit remittance to a non-resident shareholder is unrestricted under the International Business Companies Act if the company has not traded with Belize residents and has no assets in Belize beyond bank deposits. A de facto hurdle remains: the CBB's automated surveillance system flags any transfer above BZD 10,000 from a foreign account to a Belize bank, and the bank must confirm the IBC's non-resident status on the account-opening tax form before treating the funds as freely convertible.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control (Export Proceeds) Regulations, a resident exporter must repatriate export proceeds to Belize within 90 days from shipment and surrender all convertible foreign currency to an authorised dealer unless an exemption or retention limit is expressly granted. The standard residency retention limit is 20% of the proceeds for operating balances; the remaining 80% is converted to BZD at the official peg. For a Belize IBC, the obligation is suspended in two material ways: (1) where the IBC acts exclusively as an offshore broker and takes title to cotton bales outside Belize, no physical export from Belize occurs and the proceeds are not export proceeds; and (2) where the IBC does directly export cotton from Belize, the CBB routinely grants a 100% retention allowance to International Business Companies that file a quarterly External Transactions Form (EC-3(t)) and maintain the export document package (commercial invoice, bill of lading, customs declaration F-48/EC-2). The exemption is not automatic: if the IBC fails to submit the annual declaration and the bank has not been notified, the bank may unilaterally convert 80% of any USD credit to BZD at the peg, resulting in permanent economic loss. The compliance department therefore must place a standing instruction with the bank's treasury desk designating the IBC account as a 'Non-Resident External Transactions Account' and renew that designation annually.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) acting under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Cap. 261) and the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 57, Revised Edition 2006), with ancillary supervision by the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) for non-bank financial services and by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) for AML/CFT compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Belize is classified by major EU/UK electronic money institutions (EMIs) as a high-risk third-country jurisdiction for money-laundering purposes. Nearly all mainstream cross-border EMIs—including Wise, Revolut Business, Currenxie, and Airwallex—will not onboard a Belize-incorporated IBC without an existing commercial bank relationship, a physical economic nexus in Belize, and a substantiated underlying trade chain. Legal constraints arise under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act (Cap. 260; 2003 Revision) and the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act (MLTPA), which require any person providing money-transfer or e-money services in Belize to be licensed by the Central Bank or the IFSC. An unlicensed EMI credit to a Belize bank account triggers a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) if the source cannot be verified. The only practical fintech channel is a fully regulated RAFT (Remittance and Foreign Transfer) account operated by an authorised dealer bank, which can provide a USD clearing account in the United States, but this is functionally a correspondent bank product rather than an EMI product. The IBC should plan to maintain a primary commercial bank account in Belize and may use an EMI only as a secondary collection tool for sub-threshold buyer deposits, provided the EMI is a licensed money-service business in the US or UK.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The initial equity injection must be structured as a direct inward remittance from the sole shareholder's personal or corporate account in a jurisdiction acceptable to the Belize bank. The bank will require the SWIFT MT103 to include a recognised payment purpose code (recommended SWIFT code 'EQUI' or the IMF BPM6 direct investment code 500) and the reference 'Capital Subscription – BZ__BZ__BZ_IBC__ISIC_4620_01'. Prior to sending, the shareholder must execute and provide (i) a board resolution authorising the capital increase, (ii) a shareholder subscription agreement, (iii) a declaration of non-resident status, and (iv) a letter of consent to the bank's KYC request. The bank's treasury desk will verify the incoming wire against the export of goods or capital subscription documentation; no CBB approval is needed if the IBC is non-resident, but the bank may impose a 48-hour holding period for wires exceeding USD 50,000. Upon clearing, the funds are recorded as paid-up share capital and are eligible for redeployment as trade advancement or brokerage commission. If the IBC instead receives shareholder loans, they must be evidenced by an intercompany loan agreement with an arm's-length interest rate; otherwise the bank will classify them as unproven equity and may freeze the surplus above USD 200,000.For cross-border raw cotton bale export brokerage, the treasury must route settlements through one of the following clearing rails in order of preference: (1) a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) opened by the issuing bank in the buyer's jurisdiction and advised to the IBC by the Belize bank; payment under the LC is made by the issuing bank's SWIFT MT103 to the IBC's designated USD account, and the IBC then executes an outward SWIFT MT103 to the upstream ginner or supplier; (2) a documentary collection under URC 522 using D/P or D/A terms, where the IBC's bank sends the bill of lading, invoice, and other stipulated documents through the collecting bank; funds are released upon acceptance or payment; and (3) an open-account net settlement with a direct SWIFT MT103 from the buyer's account after the cotton has been delivered, supported by an electronic matching mechanism in the bank's trade system. Each outward transmission must be matched against a corresponding inward trade file; the bank will refuse a payment if the stated commodity code does not align with ISIC 4620 (wholesale of textile raw materials). The correspondent clearing rails are typically via JP Morgan Chase New York or Citibank New York for USD. The IBC should request a 'tier-2' standing settlement instruction to avoid delays when the buyer's originating bank is in a non-SWIFT country with limited routing, and should always instruct 'SHARE' or 'OUR' on charges to fix the net amount.Onshore USD/BZD foreign exchange forward contracts offered by Belize commercial banks or their overseas correspondents, with tenors up to 12 months and a mandatory underlying transaction (invoiced cotton sale or purchase).Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing BZD executed through an authorised dealer under the International Banking Act, as long as settled in USD and documented with an underlying textile commodity exposure.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CIRS) to hedge USD/EUR and USD/GBP mismatches between the IBC's receipt and disbursement currencies, subject to bank approval and only for hedging—not for speculation.Plain vanilla foreign exchange options purchased by the IBC (buy call or put) to collared USD/BZD or USD/GBP exposures, with clearly stated option premiums.FX collars and zero-premium structures combining a purchased put and sold call on the USD/BZD pair solely to protect the expected economic margin on confirmed cotton brokerage contracts.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW. Belize is classified by major EU/UK electronic money institutions (EMIs) as a high-risk third-country jurisdiction for money-laundering purposes. Nearly all mainstream cross-border EMIs—including Wise, Revolut Business, Currenxie, and Airwallex—will not onboard a Belize-incorporated IBC without an existing commercial bank relationship, a physical economic nexus in Belize, and a substantiated underlying trade chain. Legal constraints arise under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act (Cap. 260; 2003 Revision) and the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act (MLTPA), which require any person providing money-transfer or e-money services in Belize to be licensed by the Central Bank or the IFSC. An unlicensed EMI credit to a Belize bank account triggers a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) if the source cannot be verified. The only practical fintech channel is a fully regulated RAFT (Remittance and Foreign Transfer) account operated by an authorised dealer bank, which can provide a USD clearing account in the United States, but this is functionally a correspondent bank product rather than an EMI product. The IBC should plan to maintain a primary commercial bank account in Belize and may use an EMI only as a secondary collection tool for sub-threshold buyer deposits, provided the EMI is a licensed money-service business in the US or UK.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable; Belize has no transfer pricing documentation obligation for IBCs. For economic substance reporting, an annual filing is required when gross income exceeds BZD 1,000,000, and audited accounts are required above BZD 5,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (International Business Companies) Act, 2019 applies categorically to IBCs. Distribution and service centre operations (including raw cotton bale brokerage) are 'relevant activities.' The company must prove that, within Belize, (i) the management and control of the core CIGA takes place, (ii) adequate physical offices and local employees are maintained, and (iii) operational expenditure proportionate to the business is incurred. A simplified economic substance report is due annually with the IFSC; failure to substantiate results in the imposition of penalties and exchange of information with offshore tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Transaction

Statutory sanction: Under the IBC Act and Economic Substance Act, penalties of BZD 100,000 plus imprisonment up to 3 years upon conviction; the IBC may be struck off.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally and severally liable for restitution of underpaid foreign tax liabilities and can face travel bans and potential extradition if tax evasion is alleged.
Pure Shell Company (No Economic Substance)

Statutory sanction: IFSC enforcement action: fine from BZD 10,000 to BZD 50,000, cancellation of registration, and mandatory strike-off with a bar on re-registration for up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors become personally liable for all corporate debts upon continuation of business after strike-off; civil seizure of personal assets may follow.
AML/CFT Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, financial penalties up to BZD 500,000 and forfeiture of the trade proceeds.

Director personal liability: Company directors may be sentenced to imprisonment up to 10 years and face disqualification from acting as a director of any Belize company.
False DTA Representation

Statutory sanction: Belize Ministry of Finance may revoke the certificate of residence, impose a BZD 25,000 administrative penalty, and notify the foreign tax authority.

Director personal liability: Directors may be subject to civil restitution claims in the foreign tax authority for the entire tax evaded, together with interest, and potential criminal extradition proceedings.
