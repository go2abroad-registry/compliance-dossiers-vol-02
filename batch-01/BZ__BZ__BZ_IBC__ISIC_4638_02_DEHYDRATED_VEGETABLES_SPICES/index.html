Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize (official online tax portal: https://tax.gov.bz; electronic filing and GST services portal: https://etax.belize.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Belize International Business Company (IBC) is incorporated through a licensed registered agent with the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) Registry. The Registry issues a Certificate of Incorporation and a unique company registration number. Because an IBC is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax and General Sales Tax (GST), no Belize Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) or GST/VAT registration number is required for a pure offshore business trading in dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chilli pepper. If the IBC voluntarily acquires a domestic Belize taxable presence, files an election to convert to a domestic company, or becomes liable to GST, the registered agent submits a Tax Registration Application together with the Certificate of Incorporation, memorandum and articles, directorship/beneficial ownership details, and proof of registered office. Belize Tax Service then issues a TIN and, if applicable, a GST registration number. For a purely offshore IBC exporter, the Registry company number and Certificate of Incumbency serve as the sole tax-related identifiers.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the Belize International Business Companies Act, an IBC automatically enjoys a full exemption from income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, and GST for a period of 50 years from the date of incorporation. Belize does not operate a check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election. The only relevant statutory election is the ability of an IBC to pass a special resolution and apply to the Registry to continue as an ordinary Belize domestic company under the Companies Act, which would terminate the tax-exempt IBC status and bring it into the Belize corporate income tax and GST registration net. No alternative partnership or hybrid passthrough treatment is available under Belize domestic tax law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 100,000 of taxable supplies in any 12-month period; voluntary registration is available to persons making only zero-rated supplies, including exporters

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A GST-registered Belize supplier may zero-rate the supply of goods if the goods are physically exported from Belize within the time prescribed by the General Sales Tax Act and the export is supported by Customs and transport documentation. Services supplied to a non-resident recipient and consumed entirely outside Belize are similarly zero-rated. A pure offshore Belize IBC trading dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chilli pepper outside Belize falls outside the Belize GST charge altogether; however, if the IBC holds an elective GST registration, export zero-rating applies provided the goods leave Belize and documentary evidence is preserved. The exporter must issue a valid tax invoice, retain all customs declarations, and obtain foreign exchange inward remittance evidence of payment for the export sale.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Belize does not currently mandate a real-time continuous transaction control (CTC) fiscal clearance regime, nor does it require digital stamping or API-based pre-clearance of every tax invoice. Registered GST businesses must issue sequentially numbered GST invoices and file periodic GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Service e-services portal. For export goods, physical clearance is processed through the Belize Customs and Excise Department using the ASYCUDA World electronic customs management system, and the export declaration must match the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and inward remittance certificate.

Belize Tax Service may audit export transactions and, within the statutory assessment period, retrospectively disallow zero-rating if the goods were not physically exported, documentation is forged, or payment is not repatriated within 180 days. On disallowance, the exporter becomes liable for GST at 12.5% on the supply together with interest at the statutory rate and late-payment surcharges. Knowingly making a false export declaration or materially incomplete documentation constitutes an offence punishable by fine and, in the case of individuals, imprisonment; directors and officers of the IBC may also be pursued. Belize Customs and Tax Service reconcile ASYCUDA export declarations against bills of lading, inward remittances, and foreign exchange repatriation records, and fraudulent claims may trigger criminal prosecution, monetary penalties, personal liability, and a clawback of any input VAT refund claimed in respect of the export.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged at BZ$2.00 = US$1.00 under a managed fixed-rate arrangement administered by the Central Bank of Belize. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted for bona fide trade settlements through authorized dealers; capital-account inflows and outflows are presumptively permissible for an International Business Company because Section 66 of the International Business Companies Act (Chapter 270) expressly exempts an IBC from the Exchange Control Act (Chapter 104). Accordingly, profit remittances and dividend distributions from an active dehydrated-vegetables trading IBC face no statutory approval hurdle and no mandatory conversion; funds may be maintained and cleared in USD. Residual practical hurdles are confined to documentary traceability (underlying trade contract, invoice, shipment evidence) and the correspondent-bank risk appetite of the receiving institution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For locally incorporated resident exporters, the Exchange Control Act and CBB Exchange Control Notices require repatriation of export proceeds within 180 days from shipment and full surrender (conversion to Belize dollars) at the authorized-dealer official rate unless a retention account has been expressly approved by the Central Bank. This statutory surrender obligation does not bind a Belize International Business Company: the IBC Act exempts a captive IBC from exchange-control restrictions, allowing the entity to hold 100% of USD export receipts onshore in a foreign-currency account without any conversion timeline or ratio. To preserve the exemption and avoid accidental residency characterization, the IBC should not conduct local Belize retail business, own Belize real estate, or borrow domestically without the CBB's prior consent.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Chapter 261, Subsidiary Laws of Belize), headquartered in Belize City; the Foreign Exchange Administration function is discharged by the CBB Exchange Control Department operating through authorized dealers.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for the Belize IBC; the IBC is wholly exempt from Belize corporate income tax, business tax, and all direct taxes. Resident Belizean companies outside the IBC regime are taxable at 25%, but the BZ_IBC does not enter that net.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Belize does not impose dividend withholding tax on an IBC's distributions to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual license fee and registry annual return due by 31 August of each year or within the prescribed anniversary window (typically within three months after the incorporation anniversary) as specified by the Registrar.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months after the end of the IBC's financial period; a company formed during a period must comply for the initial shorter period ending on the last day of the financial period.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Belize CIT transfer-pricing threshold applies to IBCs because they are tax-exempt. Under the ESR Act, any IBC carrying on a relevant activity with connected-party transactions must maintain contemporaneous TP/substance records; the operational threshold is effectively BZD 0 (no turnover threshold).

Economic substance scope: Applicable. The Economic Substance (International Business Companies) Act 2019 requires an IBC engaged in a relevant activity to demonstrate economic substance in Belize proportionate to its activity. For a dehydrated spices bulk exporter acting as a distribution and service centre, the IBC must show: physical office in Belize; full-time employees; adequate operating expenditure; board meetings and management decisions in Belize; and records evidencing CIGA. Tax exemption does not supersede ESR obligations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Offshore Shell Misuse

Statutory sanction: Belize: penalties under the ESR Act (up to BZD 50,000 plus daily penalties) and strike-off under the IBC Act. Foreign home jurisdiction: recharacterization, penalties up to 200% of the tax evaded, criminal fines, and imprisonment for tax fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute sham transactions may be held personally liable for restitution, disgorgement of profits, and penalties; where fraud is proven, courts may issue travel bans, freeze personal assets, and extradite the directors.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Belize ESR Act, a failure to satisfy the economic substance test may result in penalty notices, BZD 10,000 - 50,000 fines, daily default penalties, exchange of information with treaty partners, and eventual strike-off under the IBC Act.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines and disqualification from acting as trustees or directors; the court may order shareholders' veil-piercing, joint liability for regulatory penalties, and a ban on reincorporating in Belize.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under Belize's Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act and Proceeds of Crime Act: fines up to BZD 1,000,000 and imprisonment for up to 14 years; confiscation of any property linked to the offence.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners who consciously permit money laundering face personal criminal liability, imprisonment, exclusion orders, asset freezes, and travel bans; extradition may be available for foreign predicate offences.
False Representations to the Registrar

Statutory sanction: In Belize: imprisonment for up to 5 years and fines; in any foreign jurisdiction using the document: fraudulent document offences, mandatory minimum prison terms, and treaty-benefit denial.

Director personal liability: Executing a false declaration makes the signatory personally liable; the Registrar may revoke the IBC's certificate of incorporation and directors may be ordered to pay restitution to defrauded tax authorities.
