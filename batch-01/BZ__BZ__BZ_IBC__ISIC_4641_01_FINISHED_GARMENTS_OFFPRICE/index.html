Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize) — official portal: https://www.mof.gov.bz and the Belize Tax Service online filing system; corporate registration is separately administered by the Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR) under the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry Act.

E-government filing portal

A foreign applicant incorporates an International Business Company (IBC) by filing Articles of Incorporation with the Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR) via a licensed registered agent, accompanied by a Certificate of Good Standing and due-diligence pack (certified passport, proof of address, bank reference). Because an IBC is statutorily tax-exempt under the International Business Companies Act, no income tax TIN or Belize tax registration is mandatory for offshore operations. However, an IBC may voluntarily obtain a Belize Tax Service TIN/GST registration number for domestic banking, invoicing, employment withholding, or customs transactions; the voluntary application requires the Certificate of Incorporation, corporate resolutions, registered agent letter, and beneficial-ownership declarations. The TIN is issued at no charge with an average processing timeline of 3–10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An IBC may irrevocably elect under ss. 187–190 of the International Business Companies Act to terminate its tax-exempt status and be taxed as a resident company under the Belize Income Tax Act (effectively an 'opt-in' to domestic taxation on worldwide income). There is no US-style check-the-box classification; hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized by Belize domestic law. IBCs may also apply the International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2019 for qualifying pure equity holding and headquarters activities, but such economic substance filings are compliance declarations, not tax classification elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 150,000 (US$75,000 approx.) of annual taxable supplies within Belize; IBCs deriving offshore income and making no local taxable supplies are outside the charge to Belize GST and are not required to register, although voluntary registration is permitted for input-tax recovery.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of finished garments and all movable goods are zero-rated under the Belize General Sales Tax Act where (a) a supply is made to a non-resident, (b) the goods are physically exported from Belize within 90 days of the time of supply, and (c) the exporter retains documentary proof of removal and receipt of consideration in foreign currency. Cross-border offshore services rendered to non-residents for use wholly outside Belize are also zero-rated, provided the supplier is a registered person and the services are not performed in connection with land or goods situated in Belize. An IBC that is outside the GST net entirely has no zero-rating claim to file, but a voluntarily registered IBC engaged in garment liquidation exports may claim zero-rating and corresponding input credits.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Belize Customs and Excise Department Export Entry / Form C-58)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the Belize Central Bank or a licensed commercial bank evidencing receipt of foreign currency export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list identifying the garments, quantities, and buyer
  • Evidence of delivery to a foreign destination within the 90-day export window

Belize operates no mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, critical-track licensing, or API-based e-invoicing stamping regime. Registered GST vendors must issue sequential tax invoices showing the vendor's TIN, customer TIN, GST-exclusive price, GST amount, and total consideration, and must file monthly GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Service online portal. Records must be retained in Belize or accessible electronically for at least 6 years. There is no digital stamping obligation or continuous transaction control clearance requirement.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to post-clearance audit by the Belize Tax Service and Customs and Excise Department. False or unsupported claims trigger recovery of the uncharged GST, a default assessment, interest at the statutory rate, and civil penalties of 100% of the tax. Fraudulent export zero-rating (including forged bills of lading, over-valued invoices, back-to-back circular transactions, or fictitious remittances) constitutes an offence under the GST Act and the Customs Regulation Act, with fines up to BZD 50,000 and imprisonment for up to 3 years. Additionally, an IBC found to lack economic substance or to have made false export/proceeds declarations may be struck off by the CCAR, and the International Tax Authority may issue an economic-substance penalty and retroactively reclassify the IBC as a resident company, exposing the entire historical offshore income to Belize income tax. Export proceeds must be repatriated within 180 days of shipment or the export zero-rating is retroactively reversed with interest.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize maintains a dual-tier monetary regime: the current account is freely convertible at the CBB-anchored peg, while the capital account benefits from statutory liberalization for International Business Companies (IBCs). An IBC qualifying under the International Business Companies Act, Chapter 270, must transact exclusively in a currency other than BZD and is prohibited from conducting business with Belizean residents without a special licence; consequently, all USD-denominated dividends, shareholder loans, capital redemptions, and liquidation proceeds are freely remittable aboard without prior CBB exchange-control approval, provided the IBC has not transacted in BZD and has met its Economic Substance (Business Activities) Act 2019 filing obligations. Practically, profit remittance hurdles arise only where the IBC accumulates BZD from any domestic transaction, which would trigger CBB convertibility scrutiny under the Exchange Control Regulations 1974; the off-price wholesale apparel liquidation model, executed purely as an offshore intermediary between non-Belizean suppliers and non-Belizean buyers, avoids all residual convertibility frictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies to Belize IBCs. The Exchange Control Regulations require license-fibers, insurance applications, and securities licences for residents, but IBCs are classified as non-resident entities for exchange-control purposes; they may retain 100% of export and trade proceeds indefinitely in any convertible foreign currency (USD, EUR, GBP) at a licensed bank without any timeline to convert into BZD. A de facto compliance condition is that no physical goods may be imported into Belize customs territory under the IBC's name; if goods transship through Belize ports, the Customs and Excise Duty Act would trigger export-licensing steps and surrender rules. The standard blueprint therefore uses pure invoice-and-titre intermediation with goods routed directly from foreign warehouses to foreign liquidation buyers, ensuring zero exposure to Belize export-surrender formalities.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262 of the Laws of Belize (Revised Edition 2020); the CBB operates the Belize dollar (BZD) statutory pegged exchange rate system at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, and issues all exchange-control guidance governing licensed banks, currency dealers, and non-resident corporations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM with material legal constraints. Belize has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime; offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, Mercury) are not authorized banking institutions under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, Chapter 270A. An IBC may use a US-licensed fintech or UK FCA-authorised EMI solely as a collection and disbursement rail, but such accounts cannot hold the statutory capital escrow and cannot issue Letters of Credit or provide treasury-grade FX execution. The CBB requires the principal operating and capital accounts to reside at a domestic or offshore-licensed bank domiciled in Belize (or a recognised financial centre with a formal correspondent relationship); deploying an EMI as the sole treasury hub would create a KYC integrity gap and jeopardise the Belize registered agent's substantive filing continuity. Legal constraints include the absence of any CBB oversight over the EMI, the prohibition under the Data Protection Act on transferring beneficial-owner documents to unregulated processors, and the practical risk that US EMIs impose sectoral restrictions on liquidation/overstock apparel settlement, which historically triggers enhanced automated underwriting reviews. Recommended structure: use the EMI only for inbound receivables up to US$25,000 per transaction with a threshold of US$250,000 per month, while all treasury, supplier payments above that threshold, and FX instruments route through Belize Bank Limited or Caye International Bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injection must be routed via SWIFT MT103/MT202, using the beneficiary bank's SWIFT/BIC and the IBC's exact legal name, referencing 'CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION' in the payment narrative; the designated purpose code for the wire should be issued as 'GCR' (General Capital Remittance) by the remitting bank, or 'LEN/LOAN' if structured as a shareholder advance. Prior to settlement, the IBC's board resolution must be passed and filed with the registered agent, expressly stating the capital amount and permitted use, and the corporate bank must submit an internal FIU notification only where the injected amount exceeds US$100,000 in a single banking day (a statutory filing, not a blocking condition). ACH Direct is disallowed for inbound capital injections because the US ACH network may fail to memorialize the SDN/OFAC narrative checks that Belize banks demand; the only compliant transfer rail for capital is authenticated SWIFT (MT103/MT202) or a wire through CIPS (for RMB) or CHIPS, but all must terminate at the Belize bank's segregated escrow sub-account before being converted into the IBC's operational USD account.For supplier settlements under US$250,000 per shipment, use SWIFT MT103 wire with full invoice and packing-list metadata; for value above US$250,000 or when purchasing an entire liquidation estate from a major US or European retailer, use an irrevocable confirmed documentary Letter of Credit (UCP 600) with the beneficiary able to negotiate against a pro forma invoice and the IBC's customshouse receipt; ACH Direct is permitted only for US-domestic vendor payments below US$10,000 and strictly for operational expenses, never for trade inventory. All outbound trades must be explicitly labelled 'TRADE PAYMENT', with the commercial invoice number included in field 70 (Remittance Information) of the MT103, and the beneficiary's bank account must be in the same name as the vendor contract; deviation requires a letter of indemnity and a second-level AML approval. Settlement of the LC will be via the Belize bank's correspondent account at the issuing bank's New York or London correspondent, creating a clean clearing chain without routing through BZD.USD/BZD spot transactions at the CBB reference rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00; the spot market is de facto pegged, and no forward premium exists because the CBB guarantees convertibility of current-account transactions only; therefore spot swaps are the sole onshore hedge.Offshore USD/EUR deliverable forwards or swaps executed through a US/EU correspondent bank of Belize Bank Limited or Caye International Bank, with tenors up to 12 months, used to hedge euro-denominated sourcing commitments from Portuguese, Spanish, or Italian overstock distributors.Natural hedge via USD-denominated netting and payment-matching between US-dollar receivables and euro-denominated payables, executed intercompany or through the treasury sub-ledger, without any derivative ISDA documentation.Back-to-back documentary Letters of Credit (deferred-payment LC at 60–120 days upon invoice) for trade-flow deferral, effectively hedging currency and liquidity risk without a separate derivative contract.Participated trade finance with a confirming bank providing multi-currency advances against the IBC's receivables, acting as a synthetic FX-neutral credit hedge; permissible under Belize banking regulation provided the confirming bank holds a valid correspondent licence.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM with material legal constraints. Belize has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime; offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, Mercury) are not authorized banking institutions under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, Chapter 270A. An IBC may use a US-licensed fintech or UK FCA-authorised EMI solely as a collection and disbursement rail, but such accounts cannot hold the statutory capital escrow and cannot issue Letters of Credit or provide treasury-grade FX execution. The CBB requires the principal operating and capital accounts to reside at a domestic or offshore-licensed bank domiciled in Belize (or a recognised financial centre with a formal correspondent relationship); deploying an EMI as the sole treasury hub would create a KYC integrity gap and jeopardise the Belize registered agent's substantive filing continuity. Legal constraints include the absence of any CBB oversight over the EMI, the prohibition under the Data Protection Act on transferring beneficial-owner documents to unregulated processors, and the practical risk that US EMIs impose sectoral restrictions on liquidation/overstock apparel settlement, which historically triggers enhanced automated underwriting reviews. Recommended structure: use the EMI only for inbound receivables up to US$25,000 per transaction with a threshold of US$250,000 per month, while all treasury, supplier payments above that threshold, and FX instruments route through Belize Bank Limited or Caye International Bank.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for qualifying IBCs under the International Business Companies Act; the standard Belize corporate income tax rate is 25% for taxable resident companies.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Belize imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions made by an IBC to its shareholders, and no dividend tax applies at the shareholder level in Belize.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold is codified in Belize. As an administrative benchmark, controlled transactions exceeding BZD 400,000 (approximately USD 200,000) per annum should be supported by an intercompany services agreement, a functional analysis, and a benchmarking note to demonstrate arm's length pricing.

Economic substance scope: The Belize Economic Substance Act (2019) and IBC (Economic Substance) Rules apply to any IBC engaged in a relevant activity, including a distribution and service centre, financing or holding-company operation. An off-price apparel wholesale operation is a distribution activity and must maintain substantive presence in Belize: office or physical premises; local management and directors adequate to core activity; proportionate qualified employees; and local operating expenditure for the income generation. Pure equity-holding IBCs face reduced substance requirements but remain obliged to file the annual ESR return. Non-compliance triggers penalties, information exchange, and recharacterisation of the entity as a domestic taxable company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under the Belize Income Tax Act, penalties can reach 100% of the tax undercharged; fines up to BZD 50,000; imprisonment up to 5 years; interest accrues at 10% per annum. The IBC may also be struck off and its financial assets frozen by court order.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling officers who sign false declarations or direct the fraudulent scheme are jointly and severally liable for the tax and penalties; Belize courts will pierce the corporate veil to recover funds; travel bans and civil restitution orders may be imposed.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The Belize International Business Companies and Economic Substance provisions authorise penalties from BZD 2,500 to BZD 5,000, daily penalties up to BZD 1,000, Gazette publication of the offence, strike-off of the IBC, and mandatory exchange of information with foreign tax authorities, often leading to reclassification and back-tax assessments abroad.

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered agent who knowingly enable shell arrangements face disqualification, a BZD 20,000 fine, and up to 2 years' imprisonment; directing officers may be barred from serving as directors for 10 years and be subject to personal asset seizure for civil recovery.
AML / Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Under the Belize Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Funding Act, fines may reach BZD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years; proceeds of crime are subject to confiscation, goods are forfeited, and the Financial Intelligence Unit may freeze accounts and revoke compliance approvals.

Director personal liability: Directors who know or suspect money laundering are personally criminally liable; travel bans can be invoked pending investigation; extradition may be sought under bilateral agreements; civil restitution, including disgorgement of personal benefits, is enforceable against company officers.
