Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; online tax services portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

A foreign enterprise cannot directly obtain a Belize tax identity. The applicant must first incorporate a Belize International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270, through a licensed registered agent, and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation from the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (BCCAR). The IBC must then retain a registered office in Belize, file registered agent details, constitutional documents, director and beneficial ownership records under the AML/CFT framework, and apply to the Belize Tax Service for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). The application must include the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, registered office certificate, board resolution authorizing the tax filing agent, certified director/shareholder identification, and proof of registered agent engagement. Because the IBC is a tax-exempt entity under Belize law, the BTS will typically assign a TIN and classify the entity as exempt/non-filing; no income tax, General Sales Tax, or business tax registration is required where the IBC does not carry on taxable local activity. An IBC conducting a relevant activity such as wholesale distribution must also assess Belize Economic Substance Act obligations and file the appropriate economic substance return or claim foreign tax residence exemption where valid.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

The tax exemption is automatic by statute under the International Business Companies Act; no check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists for a Belize IBC. An IBC may elect to continue or convert into an ordinary Belize company under the Companies Act, thereby losing the IBC statutory tax exemption and becoming subject to Belize income tax, General Sales Tax, and business tax on Belize-source activity. It may also make an election under the Economic Substance Act to rely on tax residence in an approved foreign jurisdiction, supported by a foreign tax residence certificate, which can discharge local substance requirements. Belize has no domestic hybrid mismatch election regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Under the General Sales Tax Act, a person carrying on a taxable activity in Belize is required to register for GST when annual taxable turnover exceeds BZD 75,000. A Belize IBC whose wholesale distribution operations occur offshore and whose supplies are outside the charge to Belize GST or are zero-rated exports is not required to register; registration would be required only if the IBC makes local taxable supplies in Belize above the threshold and no specific exemption applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Belize IBC may claim the 0% GST rate on the supply of clinical diagnostic ELISA assays and reagents where the goods are exported from Belize to a destination outside Belize, title and risk pass to the non-resident buyer outside Belize, and the goods do not enter Belizean domestic consumption. Diagnostic reagents are ordinary export goods and are not subject to a local exemption; eligibility for zero-rating requires physical export, commercial invoice value, customs export proof, foreign buyer identity, and payment evidence. Off-island or transit arrangements where goods move directly from a non-Belize manufacturer to a non-Belize customer without entering Belize may fall outside the GST net entirely rather than being zero-rated supplies, and the IBC must document the flow to prevent characterisation as a Belize taxable supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (validated by Belize Customs)
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/TT payment credit evidence
  • Certificate of Origin, where applicable
  • Copy of the GST/TIN registration certificate or IBC tax exemption confirmation
  • Manufacturer's certificate of analysis and product description substantiating diagnostic reagent classification

Belize does not currently mandate a real-time e-invoicing system, continuous transaction controls, digital tax stamping, or fiscal clearance for wholesale distribution. Tax invoices must nevertheless comply with General Sales Tax invoice content rules, be issued within prescribed time limits, and be retained for audit purposes. The Belize Tax Service may access records electronically on request, and exporters should maintain an export evidence file with customs declarations, transport documents, invoices, payment proofs, and the inward remittance certificate.

The Belize Tax Service may audit any zero-rated export claim and require proof of physical export and full receipt of export proceeds. Failure to produce the mandatory documentation bundle within the applicable period, circular payment arrangements, false export declarations, or abusive use of IBC tax exemption results in retrospective clawback of 12.5% GST, interest from the date of supply, monetary penalties up to 200% of the tax due, and criminal fines or imprisonment under the General Sales Tax Act and Belize revenue administration legislation. Where the IBC has been used for fraudulent zero-rating or tax evasion, the competent authority may also recommend deregistration, striking off by the Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry, and personal liability for directors and beneficial owners.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize maintains a hard-pegged BZD/USD fixed exchange rate of BZD 2 = USD 1, with an exchange-control regime administered by the CBB. Inward remittances for current-account trade in goods and services are freely convertible through authorized dealers after provision of the invoice, contract, customs declaration, and shipping documents. Capital-account investments, portfolio transfers, shareholder loans, and capital-repatriation transactions require CBB approval, unless the receiving entity is a non-resident IBC holding an exchange-control exemption or a designated foreign-currency account. Profit remittance from this tax-exempt Belize IBC is generally assessable as a dividend or loan repayment out of offshore trade proceeds; the bank will require a signed board resolution, audited or management financial statements, the non-resident status certificate, an economic-substance declaration, and an undertaking that no income was earned in Belize. No Belize tax or withholding is levied on the remittance itself, but bank-level documentary clearance is the effective gate; failure to prove offshore source may reclassify the payment as a resident account transaction and expose it to CBB approval and the domestic 100% surrender requirement on BZD uses.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a Belize exchange-control resident exporter, the statutory rule requires full proceeds of exported goods to be repatriated through an authorized dealer within six months, or 180 days, of shipment; any foreign currency received must be offered for conversion to BZD, and the mandatory conversion ratio is 100% where the exporter requires BZD funds or where the CBB has not granted a foreign-currency retention facility. If the foreign exchange is held under an approved USD retention account, the money may be held indefinitely, but any BZD disbursement from that account will be settled at the official peg rate of BZD 2 = USD 1. The subject IBC, as a non-resident International Business Company, is not ordinarily subject to this surrender regime; nonetheless, the company’s banking profile should include the CBB exemption or authorized-dealer designation, and each inbound export receipt should be supported by the final invoice, bill of lading or airway bill, customs entry, and a treasury cover note stating that the proceeds are non-resident offshore trade proceeds not subject to domestic surrender.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB); Exchange Control Department; Financial Intelligence Unit of Belize (FIU-Belize), operating under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Exchange Control Act, and Banking and Financial Institutions Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium for auxiliary treasury functions and Low for primary trade-clearing rail. Belize does not license standalone electronic-money institutions, and a foreign EMI, for example Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, or Currencycloud, is not an authorized dealer for CBB exchange-control purposes; consequently, it cannot issue commercial documentary credits, operate a Belize branch, or provide an official BZD settlement account. An offshore EMI may be used to receive small collections or pay executive expenses, but only if it has an independent banking partner that accepts Belize IBC beneficial-ownership filings and if the account is reconciled monthly to the principal Belize commercial bank; all invoices, customs declarations, and IVD compliance records must remain available. The primary liquidity pool must remain at a licensed bank to avoid regulatory characterization as unauthorized deposit-taking under the Banking and Financial Institutions Act.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All shareholder capital and working-capital injections should be routed by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the shareholder’s licensed bank to the IBC’s USD account at a CBB-licensed authorized dealer in Belize. The SWIFT field 70 remittance information must state: NON-RESIDENT BELIZE IBC - ISIC 4649 - IVD REAGENT DISTRIBUTION - EQUITY SHARE CAPITAL or SHAREHOLDER SUBORDINATED LOAN; the bank will also require a board resolution, shareholder resolution, proof of identity and authority of the remitter, and a source-of-funds statement. Where the shareholder is itself a Hong Kong, Singapore, US, or EU corporation, the intermediary and receiving banks will expect the MT103 to carry the ultimate originator and no individual cash or third-party payments; if the capital injection exceeds USD 250,000, the CBB authorized-dealer approval and FIU report are triggered in most banks as an enhanced due-diligence file. The identification of purpose cannot be merely investment; it must specify equity, loan, or intercompany funding so that the clearing rail is consistent with Belize’s exchange-control distinction between resident and non-resident flows.Trade settlements for IVD reagent procurement should use: (i) SWIFT MT103 for standard supplier telegraphic transfers; (ii) irrevocable commercial documentary letters of credit issued through the Belize bank via SWIFT MT700 and confirmed by a top-tier correspondent in the US, UK, or EU, with all transport documents routed under UCP600; (iii) MT707 for amendments where shipment dates change; and (iv) documentary collections via MT400 or MT910 where the supplier accepts usance terms. USD disbursements will be cleared through the Belize bank’s US correspondent account; EUR disbursements through TARGET2 or SEPA via the relevant European correspondent; and GBP through CHAPS or Faster Payments via the London clearer. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for the Belize International Business Company (IBC) under Section 6(2) of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270); the general Belize corporate income tax headline rate for unexempt resident companies is 25%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Belize imposes no dividend, distribution, or withholding tax on dividends paid by an IBC to its non-resident shareholders; dividend and capital distributions are expressly within the IBC tax exemption.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None - Belize has no codified Local File or Master File requirement and no statutory BZD turnover threshold. Transfer-pricing adjustments against IBCs are not enforced locally because IBC profits are exempt. Where intercompany transactions exceed BZD 2,000,000 per annum, a voluntary Master File and Local File should be prepared to defend the arm's length pricing and substance of the reagent distribution chain in foreign examinations.

Economic substance scope: Belize has not enacted BVI/Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the IBC Act and the beneficial ownership regime impose a registered office, licensed resident agent, and retention of accounting records. Under OECD/global CFC and anti-abuse doctrines, an IBC lacking real direction, staff, warehousing, or procurement functions in respect of the ISIC 4649 / NAICS 424690 distribution business will be re-characterised as a shell in the shareholder's jurisdiction, triggering CFC/GILTI/PFIC inclusions and denial of treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Indictable offence with fines (including multiples of evaded tax, up to BZD 50,000 or the foreign tax equivalent), imprisonment for up to three years, and administrative strike-off of the IBC.

Director personal liability: Courts will pierce the corporate veil: directors, managers, and signatories are jointly and severally liable for restitution of unpaid taxes and fees; travel bans, asset freezes, and custodial sentences apply in serious fraud.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: No local Belize penalty arises on a zero-taxed IBC; the fatal consequences arise where foreign tax authorities re-attribute the IBC income to the de facto controller, imposing full tax, interest, and penalties of the CFC/GILTI/PFIC regimes of the market jurisdiction.

Director personal liability: Directors who certified false non-residence or signed sham board minutes are personally liable for civil restitution in foreign recovery actions; promoters may be sued for negligent structuring and misrepresentation.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act and the beneficial ownership regime: fines up to BZD 100,000, imprisonment for up to five years, revocation of the resident agent's licence, and strike-off of the IBC.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent face criminal liability for deficient AML controls; personal fines, disqualification from managing Belize companies, and asset-freezing orders are expressly available.
Prohibited Belize-Situs Trading

Statutory sanction: Loss of IBC exempt status, immediate assessment under the Income Tax Act and the Business Tax Act, pecuniary penalties, and compulsory dissolution; the exemption certificate is retrospectively voided.

Director personal liability: The Registry and tax authorities may disregard the IBC; directors and shareholders who authorised prohibited local trade are personally liable for Belize taxes, unpaid annual fees, and penalties, with criminal fines and imprisonment for wilful breach.
Transfer-Price Manipulation and Artificial Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Belize has no active transfer-pricing penalty statute; however, the foreign distribution jurisdiction may re-characterise the payments, disallow deductions, and impose withholding, diverted-profits tax, or penalties exceeding 40% of the disallowed amount.

Director personal liability: Directors approving sham pricing or backdated contracts may be joined in civil recovery proceedings; serious cases trigger extradition, personal assessment, and custodial sentences under the economic-crime legislation of the market jurisdiction.
