Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), an executive department of the Ministry of Finance and State Asset Management, consolidating the Income Tax Department, General Sales Tax Unit, and Customs and Excise Department; official portal: www.taxservice.gov.bz; e-payment gateway: pay.gov.bz; company filings via Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (BCCAR).

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident owner: (i) engage a licensed Belize registered agent and file Articles of Incorporation under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270; (ii) upon issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation (typically 24-48 hours), the registered agent submits Form TN-01 (Business Tax Registration) plus the certificate, memorandum/articles, registered office consent, registered agent consent, register of directors and shareholders, and a statutory declaration of non-resident business activity to the BTS; (iii) BTS issues a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) within 3-7 business days; (iv) no minimum capital, no local director, and no substance requirement at registration; (v) the IBC then files an annual Exempt Status Declaration and Economic Substance Return (Form ES-1) to maintain its zero-tax classification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize Dollar (BZD) is a de jure controlled currency pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 since 1976, with no offshore deliverable market. Current-account settlements for goods and services are freely handled by authorized dealers against standard supporting documents (import permits, commercial invoices, bills of lading); capital-account transfers by residents require Central Bank authorization. The BZ_IBC, as an International Business Company, is outside the exchange-control perimeter: it is statutorily treated as non-resident and exempt from the Exchange Control Act, may maintain USD-denominated accounts, and may freely remit dividends, capital and profits to non-resident shareholders. Profit-remittance friction for the surplus commercial aircraft parts business is therefore nil at the FX policy level; the only practical constraints are the commercial bank's internal compliance requirements (economic-substance notification, trade license, beneficial-ownership filings) and the absence of onshore FX hedging products.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must file the prescribed Export Declaration with the authorized dealer prior to shipment and must repatriate and surrender export proceeds to an authorized dealer within 180 days of export at the official fixed rate (BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00), or, with Central Bank consent, credit a designated USD Export Proceeds Account with conversion applied at withdrawal. The Central Bank may extend the 180-day deadline upon reasoned application. Because the BZ_IBC is a non-resident entity engaged in offshore surplus-aviation-parts trading, the statutory surrender rule does not apply to its export receipts; nevertheless, the operating bank will tag every inward remittance with a BOP goods-credit code and will require the underlying sales contract and shipping documents (including FAA 8130-3 or EASA Form 1 certificates) to evidence the trade and discharge any record-keeping obligation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Cap. 262), which administers the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 45) and the Exchange Control Regulations through licensed authorized dealers (commercial banks). The Ministry of Finance retains residual policy oversight of the fixed BZD/USD exchange regime.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium feasibility. No Belize-licensed EMI exists, and leading global EMIs (Airwallex, Wise Business, Currencycloud, Multitude) generally do not accept Belize IBCs due to (i) Belize's placement on the EU Council Annex II grey list during 2023-2024 pending reform commitments, (ii) correspondent-bank de-risking of Belize-based shell structures, and (iii) EMI policy exclusions of tax-exempt IBCs lacking operating substance. A Belize IBC may obtain an EMI account only through an intermediate EEA/UK licensed EMI client relationship that itself accepts Belize entities, which is rare and triggers Enhanced Due Diligence. Recommended structure: use a Belize commercial bank as the primary treasury and settlement account, and engage an EEA EMI strictly as a downstream multi-currency payout facilitator after the core account is fully boarded.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity or shareholder-loan injections must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 with a transparent narrative (e.g., 'Share capital contribution - BZ_IBC [registration number]' or 'Subordinated shareholder loan - BZ_IBC') routed through the Belize bank's US correspondent (typically Bank of America, Citibank, or JPMorgan Chase) to the IBC's USD account; conversion to BZD occurs at the fixed 2.00 peg only if local BZD funds are required. The remitting bank must apply the appropriate BoP purpose code ('Direct investment - equity' or 'Other investment - loans'); the beneficiary bank will require the Certificate of Incumbency, board resolution authorizing the capital raise, an executed loan agreement where applicable, and standard source-of-funds evidence. No Central Bank exchange approval is required for a non-resident IBC, but single remittances above USD 1,000,000 automatically attract Enhanced Due Diligence and potential FIU wire-pass-through review.Three clearing rails support the surplus civil aircraft parts business: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct wires for routine supplier payments to OEMs, certified surplus vendors and MROs, settled in USD (or EUR/GBP through the correspondent's multi-currency clearing) with the invoice and ATA chapter reference in the narrative; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit issued by MT700 under UCP 600 and confirmed by a top-tier global bank for large consignments, with presentation of EASA Form 1 or FAA 8130-3 airworthiness certificates and shipping documents; (3) the Escrow_Aviation rail, under which the buyer deposits funds into a US-based escrow agent's USD account and release is triggered only after independent inspection and airworthiness validation, thereby reducing documentary and counterparty risk. The Belize clearing bank debits the USD account through its nostro network; wires must be lodged before the bank's midday local USD clearing deadline to meet CHIPS/Fedwire windows, and disbursements above USD 500,000 require same-day notification under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, Cap. 316.Onshore BZD/USD derivatives: none permissible - the statutory peg at BZD 2.00 per USD eliminates market demand, and the Central Bank of Belize does not license a domestic derivatives or forward market; any BZD-based forward or swap would be outside the legal framework.Offshore deliverable forwards in USD/EUR and USD/GBP are permissible for trade-payable cover only when executed with an OECD-licensed international bank from a treasury vehicle outside Belize; the Belize bank records resulting settlements on the operational account without exchange-control involvement.FX swaps in major convertible currencies (USD/EUR/GBP/CHF) are permissible as liquidity-matching tools for colocated trade payables and receivables; no BZD leg may be included.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (USD/EUR or USD/GBP) are permissible where the IBC has medium-term liability exposure (e.g., a shareholder loan or trade-finance facility in a foreign currency), provided the counterparty is an OECD-regulated dealer and the confirmation is governed by an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement; hedge gains are tax-neutral for the exempt IBC.NDF contracts referencing BZD are expressly not permissible and would be unenforceable under the Exchange Control Act because the BZD rate is administratively fixed by statute; any offshore BZD NDF desk would operate outside Belize's legal recognition.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Belize International Business Company (IBC), per s.6 of the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270 (Revised Ed. 2011); ordinary Belize-resident companies bear the standard 25% corporate income tax rate under the Income and Business Tax Act, Cap. 55.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Belize imposes no withholding tax on dividends, deemed distributions, or liquidation proceeds paid by an IBC, whether to resident or non-resident shareholders (IBC Act, Cap. 270, s.6).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual licence fee is payable on or before 1 July of each year; a grace period applies for first-year companies, and late payment attracts a 10% per-month penalty (or part of a month) on the unpaid fee.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual Economic Substance Return is due within six months after the IBC's financial year-end (or such date as prescribed by the Economic Substance Regulations); the notification of relevant activity is generally required at the start of the activity and on each anniversary.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Belize has not published a formal transfer-pricing documentation threshold. The IBTA (general anti-avoidance and arm's-length adjustment powers) empowers the Tax Commissioner to re-characterize non-arm's-length cross-border transactions; as an internationally defensive standard applicable to the aviation spares trade, contemporaneous Local and Master Files in OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines format should be maintained where annual cross-border related-party turnover equals or exceeds BZD 1,000,000 (approximately USD 500,000).

Economic substance scope: Fully applicable. The Belize Economic Substance (Criteria and Exemptions) Act, 2019 requires an IBC carrying on a 'distribution and service centre' relevant activity (which includes distribution of certified commercial aircraft rotables and avionics surplus) to maintain: (i) a physical office in Belize; (ii) adequate full-time employees proportionate to the activity; (iii) adequate annual operating expenditure; and (iv) performance of CIGA in Belize. The entity must file the annual Economic Substance Return and cannot rely solely on its tax-exempt IBC status to avoid reporting.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Fraudulent evasion attracts liability for three times the tax evaded plus statutory penalties, criminal fines, and imprisonment up to 2 years under the Income and Business Tax Act; false declarations to the Registry also trigger deregistration and forfeiture of the IBC's Belize assets.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign or approve false filings become personally liable for civil restitution of all evaded taxes; travel bans and international arrest warrants may issue; directors face disqualification from managing any Belize-registered company.
Shell Company / Insufficient Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act, 2019, failure to meet the substance test triggers financial penalties up to BZD 100,000 (plus daily default fines), mandatory corrective filings, exchange of information with OECD treaty partners, and ultimately striking-off and asset escheat to the Crown.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally fined and imprisoned up to 2 years for knowingly issuing a false economic substance declaration; personal asset freezes can be ordered to secure the penalty; directors of struck-off companies bear restoration costs and are barred from re-forming Belize entities.
AML/CFT and Aviation Product Integrity Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act, 2015 (as amended), money laundering is punishable by fines up to BZD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years for individuals, with corporate fines and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime legislation; aviation safety regulators may additionally suspend the IBC's surplus-parts trading license.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution, extradition, travel bans, and disgorgement; personal guarantees and escrow misconduct expose directors to direct civil suits from aviation insurers; any material misrepresentation to the Civil Aviation authority lifts the corporate veil for unlimited personal restitution.
Fraudulent Trading / Piercing the Corporate Veil

Statutory sanction: The Court under the IBC Act (Cap. 270) and general company law may declare the business fraudulent: directors and shadow directors become personally liable for all corporate debts of the IBC without limitation, alongside restitution orders, contempt sanctions, and criminal conviction penalties including imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Personal assets are immediately attachable without the usual corporate liability shield; the court may impose travel bans, sequestration of personal estates, and disqualification from directorships; fraud-based judgments against the IBC are enforceable in Belize as a matter of public policy.
