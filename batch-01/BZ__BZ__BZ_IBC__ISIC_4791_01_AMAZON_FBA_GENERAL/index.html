Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service, Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize (online portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

An International Business Company (IBC) formed under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 275) is not required to register for a Belize tax identification number because it is statutorily exempt from all Belize taxes. The formation procedure is through the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry, leading to the issuance of a Certificate of Incorporation and a company registration number, which serves as the practical identifier for banking and merchant-account KYC. If a foreign financial institution or platform requires a formal TIN for tax documentation, the registered agent submits a TIN application to Belize Tax Service together with certified copies of the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles, beneficial-ownership declarations, and a letter confirming the IBC will not carry on business in Belize. Belize Tax Service will assign a TIN only where the entity is brought into a taxable status, which is an exceptional departure from the IBC exemption; in ordinary cross-border FBA operations, the IBC maintains no Belize tax registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available under Belize law. Statutory exemption under the IBC Act is automatic and non-elective while the entity remains an IBC. An IBC cannot elect to be taxed under the Belize Income Tax Act without losing its IBC status. Hybrid mismatch and CFC transparency elections are not recognized in Belize because the IBC is outside the Belize tax net; any classification or flow-through characterization will occur under the shareholder's foreign tax law, not Belize law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 in taxable turnover within a 12-month period for suppliers carrying on business in Belize; not applicable to an IBC because an IBC is restricted from conducting domestic Belize trade and because cross-border Amazon FBA sales have no Belize supply nexus. A pure-play IBC marketplace seller will not trigger Belize GST registration regardless of global turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under Belize GST is available to a GST-registered Belizean supplier for exported goods that physically depart from Belize and are sold to non-residents. Offshore services supplied to non-residents are generally outside the scope of Belize GST. A Belize IBC engaged in Amazon FBA does not claim Belize export zero-rating because the IBC is not a taxable supplier and has no Belize GST charge to neutralize; the sales occur in the foreign marketplace jurisdictions and are subject to the indirect tax rules of those jurisdictions.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Belize Customs and Excise Department) evidencing goods physically exported from Belize
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill proving departure from Belize and delivery abroad
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign-currency bank receipt evidencing settlement of the export sale

Belize does not currently mandate real-time electronic invoicing, digital stamping, or fiscal API clearance for GST. GST-registered suppliers must retain serially numbered tax invoices in accordance with General Sales Tax Regulations. An IBC is outside any e-invoicing or fiscal clearance obligation. Should the IBC ever become taxable, standard paper or PDF tax invoices would be acceptable; no technological filing mandate is currently specified by Belize Tax Service.

An IBC is outside the Belize tax system, so no local export zero-rating clawback applies to its Amazon FBA revenue. However, if an IBC were found by Belize Tax Service to have engaged in prohibited domestic activity or to have misrepresented its exempt status, the IBC could lose its exemption and become liable to income tax and GST on Belize-source supplies. For a Belize GST-registered exporter, a fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claim triggers assessment of the full 12.5% GST, interest, civil penalties up to 100% of the tax, monetary penalties for false declarations, and criminal prosecution under the General Sales Tax Act, with fines and imprisonment for directors or agents involved. Belize Tax Service may also retrospectively disallow the zero-rating treatment if the required export documentation is not produced within the prescribed audit period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is legally pegged to the United States dollar at the statutory rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, giving total USD/BZD convertibility at the fixed peg. Belize maintains an exchange-control regime under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 38, which restricts residents' capital account flows; however, an International Business Company (IBC) incorporated under the International Business Companies Act, Chapter 270, is expressly exempt from exchange control legislation and is treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes. Consequently, the IBC enjoys full current-account and capital-account convertibility in USD, EUR, GBP, and other freely convertible currencies. There are no statutory currency abundance or liquidity hurdles: the IBC may hold, transfer, and remit profits in foreign currency without Central Bank of Belize approval or surrender obligations, provided the IBC does not carry on commercial dealings with Belize-resident persons, own an interest in Belize real property (except as permitted under the IBC Act), or engage in licensed banking, insurance, or trust business.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Belize-resident exporters under the Exchange Control Act and Central Bank of Belize notices, foreign currency export receipts must generally be repatriated to an authorized dealer in Belize within the prescribed period (customarily within six months from the date of export invoice) and surrendered to the Central Bank at the official peg rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, with limited retention allowances. These surrender rules DO NOT apply to a Belize IBC: the IBC is statutorily exempt from exchange control, so Amazon FBA sales proceeds, marketplace settlements, and trade receipts in USD may be retained offshore or in the IBC's USD-denominated Belize bank account indefinitely with zero mandatory conversion ratio. The mandatory conversion ratio for the IBC is 0.00%; for Belize-resident exporting entities the mandate is full conversion into BZD at the 2:1 official rate unless a specific foreign-currency retention license is granted.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 34 of the Laws of Belize, Revised Edition 2020), which exercises monetary authority, reserves management, currency pegging, and regulation of authorized dealers under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 38

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW for the Belize IBC. Offshore digital EMI platforms and cross-border fintech corridors are usable only with rigorous enhanced due diligence and cannot be assumed as primary banking rails. Airwallex will generally accept a Belize IBC under its Asia-Pacific or UK FCA-regulated e-money arrangements provided the IBC presents a certified Certificate of Incorporation, Register of Directors, Certificate of Good Standing, economic substance documentation, a local registered-agent attestation, 12 months of source-of-funds evidence, and a corresponding bank reference; onboarding is risk-flagged due to the offshore-IBC profile. Stripe does not natively support Belize-domiciled merchant entities and expressly excludes Belize from its supported country list; the BZ IBC must therefore establish a separately licensed intermediary merchant entity (e.g., a US Delaware LLC or Hong Kong SAR company) to contract with Stripe, or use Payoneer-style virtual receiving accounts where the Belize seller is onboarded under the fintech's master license. Amazon Pay settlements may flow to the IBC only where Amazon Seller Central permits Belize as a seller country; in practice the seller bank account is commonly a US-based virtual account opened by the IBC under its legal name with a supporting letter from the licensed registered agent. Specialist EMIs serving offshore IBCs exist, but legal viability depends on compliance with the Bank and Financial Institutions Act, the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing Act, and the FATF-based AML/CFT framework administered by the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The permitted and most compliant routing for initial capital injection into the Belize IBC is a straightforward SWIFT international wire transfer in USD from the shareholder's personal or corporate account to the IBC's USD-denominated operational account held at Atlantic Bank Limited or Belize Bank Limited. The wire must include in field 50K the full legal name and address of the remitter, in field 59 the exact IBC name (matching Certificate of Incorporation) and full account number, and in field 70 the following remittance references: 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION — INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS COMPANY ACT, CHAPTER 270' plus the reference to the shareholder resolution. No Central Bank of Belize exchange control permit or prior approval is required because the IBC is exchange-control exempt, but the bank's compliance department will require the underlying share subscription resolution, the register of allotments, and a source-of-funds declaration. The payment purpose should be coded consistently with the BPM6/OECD framework as direct investment equity: for banks operating under the US wire format, use 'Equity Injection — Investment Income/Retained Earnings' or the bank-specific purpose code equivalent (typically code 7000 for direct investment, or the bank-specific 'Capital Contribution' narrative when the bank does not require a numeric code). For subsequent Amazon FBA working-capital funding, the company should continue to hallmark injections as equity contributions rather than shareholder loans to avoid thin-capitalization objections under the economic-substance rules; amounts above USD 100,000 may trigger enhanced due diligence, so the shareholder should present the same beneficial-ownership and source-of-funds evidence referenced in the bank's KYC file.Outbound settlement from the Belize IBC to support Amazon FBA operations should use tiered clearing rails carefully sequenced to minimize friction: (a) SWIFT MT103 single direct remittance is the primary rail for supplier invoices, freight, 3PL logistics, customs duties, software subscriptions, and professional fees — each MT103 must carry the supplier's full beneficiary details, ISO currency code, and the invoice/PO number in the remittance information field, with a factual narration such as 'TRADE PAYMENT AGAINST INVOICE [number] / AMAZON FBA INVENTORY'; (b) Commercial Letter of Credit (LC at sight or usance) is preferred for high-value supplier purchases above approximately USD 50,000; it is issued by Belize Bank or Atlantic Bank through correspondent banks in the US (Wells Fargo, Bank of America, or Citibank corridors) or Asia, with Incoterms 2020 rules (FOB, CIF, or FCA) and a compliant presentation pack including commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, shipping marks, and insurance certificate, thereby converting supplier risk into documented trade finance; (c) Amazon marketplace disbursements (Amazon Pay and regular Seller Central settlements) are received by the IBC through its USD account via ACH, Payoneer virtual account, or SWIFT credit from Amazon's payment service provider, then swept internally to the supplier-payment account; (d) Airwallex can be layered as a host-to-host virtual-account network where the IBC maintains a verified account and issues sub-accounts for suppliers in local currency zones; however, the IBC must never use a Belize BZD account for trade proceeds, because the fixed peg imposes unnecessary conversion friction — all Amazon proceeds should remain in USD until a receivable or payable in GBP or EUR requires an operational conversion through the bank or a non-deliverable hedge.American-style and European-style plain vanilla FX forwards (USD/EUR, USD/GBP, and USD/CAD) traded over the counter with a Belize-authorized dealer or a Tier-1 foreign counterparty, subject to ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or a bank-specific FX line approved by the IBC board; these are permissible to lock conversion rates for Amazon EU/UK/CA marketplace settlements receivable on a cash-flow-matched schedule.FX swaps (USD/EUR, USD/GBP) combining a spot-leg and forward-leg are permissible for temporary treasury liquidity bridging where IBC working capital is tied to FBA inventory cycles.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permissible in the offshore market for GBP and EUR, and are the recommended hedging instrument when the IBC does not physically settle the foreign currency but only needs to neutralize P&L translation from Amazon EU/UK settlements; the IBC should obtain a formal peg-confirmation letter from its bank but should not enter any BZD-related derivative.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW for the Belize IBC. Offshore digital EMI platforms and cross-border fintech corridors are usable only with rigorous enhanced due diligence and cannot be assumed as primary banking rails. Airwallex will generally accept a Belize IBC under its Asia-Pacific or UK FCA-regulated e-money arrangements provided the IBC presents a certified Certificate of Incorporation, Register of Directors, Certificate of Good Standing, economic substance documentation, a local registered-agent attestation, 12 months of source-of-funds evidence, and a corresponding bank reference; onboarding is risk-flagged due to the offshore-IBC profile. Stripe does not natively support Belize-domiciled merchant entities and expressly excludes Belize from its supported country list; the BZ IBC must therefore establish a separately licensed intermediary merchant entity (e.g., a US Delaware LLC or Hong Kong SAR company) to contract with Stripe, or use Payoneer-style virtual receiving accounts where the Belize seller is onboarded under the fintech's master license. Amazon Pay settlements may flow to the IBC only where Amazon Seller Central permits Belize as a seller country; in practice the seller bank account is commonly a US-based virtual account opened by the IBC under its legal name with a supporting letter from the licensed registered agent. Specialist EMIs serving offshore IBCs exist, but legal viability depends on compliance with the Bank and Financial Institutions Act, the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing Act, and the FATF-based AML/CFT framework administered by the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for qualifying International Business Companies under Section 145 of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270); the otherwise applicable Belize corporate income tax headline rate is 25% but is expressly disapplied to IBC tax-exempt profits.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for Belize IBCs. Section 145(1) and (2) of the International Business Companies Act expressly exempts all dividends, distributions, and capital payments made by an IBC to non-resident shareholders from any Belize withholding tax or similar tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 90 days after each incorporation anniversary for the annual return; the annual government fee is payable on or before 1 January each year. A phased commencement schedule applied to pre-2017 IBCs, but all IBCs now file annually regardless of incorporation date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Where a filing obligation arises, returns are due within 3 months of the Arizona fiscal year-end; for most entities the fiscal year is 1 January to 31 December. For IBCs with no Belize-source income, no statutory deadline is engaged; if the Income Tax Department issues a notice, the response is due within 30 days of the notice.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Belize statutory Local File or Master File requirement exists under the Income Tax Act or IBC Act; the domestic threshold is effectively BZD nil because Belize has no formal transfer pricing documentation regime. As a practical compliance standard, a Belize IBC engaged in Amazon FBA should prepare OECD-aligned documentation when intercompany inventory purchase, logistics, warehousing, and management fees exceed US$1,000,000 annually (approximately BZD 2,000,000), and group-level Master File documentation when consolidated revenue exceeds US$50,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Belize enacted the Economic Substance Act 2019 and supporting regulations aligning with OECD and EU standards. A Belize IBC with no tax residency in another jurisdiction is deemed Belize tax resident and must satisfy economic substance if it carries on a relevant activity. An Amazon FBA distribution business is classified as a distribution and service centre and must demonstrate in Belize: strategic direction and management from Belize, proportionate local employees, adequate premises, and operating expenditure appropriate to the profit. An annual economic substance declaration must be filed with the Registrar. Failure to comply attracts financial penalties up to BZ$100,000 for a first offence, higher penalties for continued non-compliance, and liability to strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Use of Tax-Exempt IBC in the Source Market

Statutory sanction: US civil fraud penalty of 75% of the understated tax under IRC 6663; accuracy-related penalty of 20% to 40% under IRC 6662; criminal prosecution under IRC 7201 for wilful evasion carrying a fine up to US$100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years. In Belize, any false declaration in support of the IBC exemption is an offence under Section 147 of the IBC Act, punishable by a fine up to BZ$25,000 and, on continued non-compliance, strike-off.

Director personal liability: The limited liability shield is pierced when settlement funds are commingled with personal accounts or when directors personally sign false W-8BEN-E and exemption declarations. US courts may impose personal liability under transferee provisions of IRC 6901, and the IRS may issue a civil assessment jointly against directors who control the disregarded entity. Belizean authorities may seek travel bans and asset freezing orders against directors in connection with fraudulent claims.
Economic Substance / Shell Company Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2019 and the IBC (Amendment) Act 2019, failure to file the annual economic substance declaration attracts a fine up to BZ$100,000; material non-compliance attracts a penalty of BZ$250,000 or 5% of annual turnover, whichever is higher, plus daily default penalties and potential strike-off by the Registrar.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations that the company is a pure holding entity or passive foreign investment vehicle while it operates an FBA distribution business become personally liable for the preparation of defective ESG documentation. The Belize courts may issue travel bans and freezing orders against directors and shareholders to enforce the economic substance regime.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Under Belize's Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, money laundering is punishable by a fine up to BZ$1,000,000 and imprisonment up to 12 years for individuals; the company itself may be fined up to BZ$350,000. Failure to file beneficial ownership information with the Registrar is an offence under the Companies (Amendment) Act 2020, attracting a fine up to BZ$50,000 per year and disqualification of nominee directors.

Director personal liability: Directors lose the protection of limited liability when they knowingly allow the IBC to be used as a conduit for structured settlement distributions. The Financial Intelligence Unit may issue civil restitution orders up to the full amount laundered, impose travel bans pending criminal trial, and require personal guarantees from directors for future banking relations.
Persistent Registry Delinquency and Unlicensed Agency Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Registrar may strike off the IBC under Section 190 of the IBC Act after gazetted notice. Once struck off, the company cannot enforce contracts, sell assets, repatriate profits, or maintain bank accounts; after seven years of dissolution, all assets vest in the Crown as bona vacantia. Restoration requires a court order and payment of all arrears, surcharges, and legal costs.

Director personal liability: A director who continues to operate the Amazon business after strike-off is personally liable for all debts and obligations as if the company had continued in existence. The Amazon marketplace account is suspended, payment rails are terminated, and the director is exposed to creditor claims and foreign enforcement proceedings as a controlling individual.
