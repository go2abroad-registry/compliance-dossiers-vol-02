E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists in Belize. An IBC may elect to waive its automatic 50-year tax exemption and file under the Income Tax Act (Cap. 55) and the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 297), or convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act (Cap. 250). Domestic anti-hybrid rules reflecting BEPS Action 2 disallow dual-deduction and deductible/deductible mismatches, and the Economic Substance Act, 2019 imposes reporting and economic-presence obligations on IBCs carrying on relevant activities, including distribution and service-center operations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 of annual taxable supplies (turnover) — compulsory registration under Section 12 of the GST Act; foreign e-commerce suppliers with Belize consumer sales above this threshold must register through a fiscal representative; voluntary registration permitted below the threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% GST applies to tangible goods physically exported from Belize within the GST-exporter timeframe, evidenced by customs-recognized export paperwork. Services: zero-rating applies to services supplied to non-resident persons where the benefit is enjoyed wholly outside Belize, and to offshore digital/social-commerce commissions and platform facilitation charges originating from the IBC's foreign customer base. If the TikTok Shop inventory is never imported into Belize (dropship model), no Belize GST supply arises; if goods are warehoused and shipped from Belize, the exporter must hold conclusive export evidence to support the 0% rate and any related input-credit refund claim.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Export Entry) certified and stamped by Belize Customs
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing physical departure of goods from Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice from the IBC's Belize or offshore bank showing receipt of export proceeds in a foreign currency
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed Packing List
  • Sales contract or purchase order with the non-resident buyer; TikTok Shop settlement records may serve as supplementary proof
  • GST-compliant export tax invoice stating the zero-rated nature of the supply

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API stamping, or digital invoicing stamp regime is in force in Belize. GST invoices must include the prescribed particulars of Section 43 of the GST Act (seller name/address/GST registration number, invoice date, description, price, tax amount or zero-rating statement); monthly or quarterly GST returns and export documentation are submitted through the BTSD online filing portal, with standard physical and electronic records retained for at least 6 years for audit.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims are prosecuted under the GST Act (Cap. 297) and the Tax Administration and Enforcement Act: on summary conviction, fines up to BZD 25,000 or double the tax evaded (whichever is greater) and imprisonment up to 2 years; civil assessments permit the Commissioner to claw back all zero-rated refunds, disallow input credits, and demand interest on the balance at the statutory default rate; the ordinary 6-year limitation period may be extended indefinitely for fraud, wilful neglect, or fabricated export documents, and enforcement may include distress, garnishee orders, and denial of the IBC's certificate of good standing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged at BZ$2.00 per USD 1.00. Current-account convertibility for trade in goods and services is effective and freely operable through CBB-licensed authorized dealers; capital-account transactions require CBB exchange-control approval, applied in practice through dealer-level screening rather than direct CBB licensing for standard equity injections and intercompany funding. Profit remittances by resident Belize companies require an authorized-dealer application, documented board resolution, and underlying audited or management financial statements, with dividends subject to withholding tax where applicable. A Belize International Business Company (Part XVIII of the Companies Act 2011) is statutorily exempt from the Exchange Control Act: it may freely hold, borrow, lend, and disburse foreign currency, may pay dividends and royalties abroad without CBB capital-account licensing, and is operationally insulated from the BZD peg. The material residual chokepoint is correspondent-bank de-risking due diligence, not statutory FX convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the general Exchange Control Act regime for resident exporters, export proceeds must be repatriated through an authorized dealer within a prescribed administrative window of 180 days from shipment, and absent an approved foreign-currency retention facility the effective mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of repatriated export receipts into BZD. For the Belize IBC, the Part XVIII Companies Act 2011 exchange-control exemption applies: no statutory timeline and a 0% mandatory conversion ratio, so TikTok Shop USD payout receipts may remain in the IBC's USD clearing account indefinitely without CBB filing or conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), the national monetary authority established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, administering the Exchange Control Act (Cap 40) and the Banking and Financial Institutions Act for all foreign-exchange and authorized-dealer licensing in Belize.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to a tax-exempt Belize IBC, since it has no Belize taxable income. Belize's IBTA transfer pricing provisions bind only commercial companies within the Belize tax net. If the TikTok Shop group includes a taxable affiliate abroad, the relevant foreign TP threshold (commonly group revenue above EUR 7.5M or USD 10M) triggers Local File and Master File preparation; the Belize IBC should nevertheless prepare contemporaneous OECD-compliant documentation evidencing arm's length service fees, marketing fees, and IP royalties to rebut any CFC or permanent establishment challenge in the owner's jurisdiction.

Economic substance scope: The International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations 2022 impose an economic presence test only for IBCs conducting 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, fund management, finance and leasing, headquarters, shipping, intellectual property holding, and pure equity holding). Social commerce retail via short-video platforms (ISIC 4791, NAICS 454110) is not a relevant activity, so the Belize Economic Substance regime does not mandate local management or staff for the IBC. The IBC must nonetheless maintain a registered office and registered agent in Belize, retain accounting records sufficient to explain transactions at the registered office, and avoid any misrepresentation that it conducts a Belize-based retail operation. The greater substance risk lies in foreign CFC/PE rules and in the UBO's home jurisdiction treating the IBC as a sham wrapper.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorised Domestic Business Activity (IBC Act, s. 6)

Statutory sanction: Immediate forfeiture of the IBC tax-exempt status; back-assessment to Belize corporation tax at 15% on income derived in Belize; monetary penalties of up to BZD 2,000 per offence plus the possibility of mandatory strike-off and loss of legal personality under s. 56; the offending IBC may also be barred from holding a Certificate of Good Standing.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly approved Belize-resident transactions are jointly and severally liable for the restored tax liability and any fines; the Belize Supreme Court may lift the corporate veil, order restitution of diverted assets, impose director disqualification for up to five years, and, in aggravated cases, authorise criminal prosecution.
Cross-Border Tax Evasion / CFC Abuse

Statutory sanction: Civil fraud penalties up to 75% of the understated tax plus interest in the United States (26 U.S.C. § 7201); similar reprisal regimes under UK Finance Act penalties reaching up to 200% of the tax; automatic CFC attribution of the IBC's undistributed profits to the controlling person; potential criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to five years and mutual-legal-assistance-based asset seizures.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false tax-residence certificates, backdated board minutes, or fictitious service agreements share personal criminal exposure as aiders and abettors; magistrates may issue travel bans pending extradition requests, and civil courts may pierce the IBC veil to render directors personally liable for restitution of the evaded taxes plus punitive damages.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Register Failure

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BZD 10,000 per contravention under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act with additional daily penalties for continuing non-disclosure; the IFSC may strike off the IBC, freeze its bank accounts through an anti-money laundering order, and refer the matter to the Financial Intelligence Unit for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Nominee and de facto directors face personal criminal liability including imprisonment up to ten years, unrestricted personal account freezes, confiscation of proceeds, and placement on international wanted lists enabling extradition between Commonwealth jurisdictions.
Shell Company Abuse / Economic Substance Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Under the IBC (Economic Substance) Regulations 2022, knowingly providing false information attracts fines up to BZD 50,000 and imprisonment up to three years; the IBC may be struck off, its registered agent's licence revoked, and foreign tax authorities may disbelieve any Belize tax-exemption certificate issued in respect of the shell.

Director personal liability: Belize common law permits veil-piercing where the IBC is a mere façade or sham; directors are then personally liable for the company's debts and must disgorge all profits attributed to the sham structure, in addition to criminal sanctions, travel restrictions during investigation, and extradition exposure.
