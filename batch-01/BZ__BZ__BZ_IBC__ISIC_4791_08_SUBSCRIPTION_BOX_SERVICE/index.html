Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (BTSD) under the Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment; official digital portal: Belize Tax Service Online Portal (www.taxservice.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities, including IBCs, with no Belize-source income are not automatically required to hold an income tax TIN. Upon commencing any taxable activity (including export trade), the IBC must apply for GST registration by filing Form GST-1 on the Belize Tax Service Portal, uploading the Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Incorporation, and corporate register extract. The BTSD issues a unique GST Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and GST registration certificate within 7-15 business days. For customs operations, the entity must also obtain a Customs Registration Number from the Belize Customs and Excise Department.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

IBCs are statutorily exempt from all Belize income taxes, capital gains taxes, and stamp duties, with no domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election available. The IBC may file an annual declaration to elect 'tax resident' status by central management and control in Belize, primarily for treaty access; otherwise, the default exemption remains absolute. No other entity classification elections exist under the International Business Companies Act.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 (Belize Dollars) in annual taxable supplies; however, IBCs exporting all goods/services are zero-rated and may voluntarily register to claim input credits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of physical goods (including subscription boxes) and offshore services are zero-rated under Section 30 of the Goods and Services Tax Act when supplied from Belize to a place outside Belize. Claimants must satisfy all of the following: maintain commercial export evidence, show the goods physically left Belize, receive payment through the banking system from outside Belize, and present a GST tax invoice with the statement 'Zero-rated Export Supply'. IBCs that provide services to non-residents as core offshore business also qualify.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No real-time fiscal clearance or e-invoicing API mandate currently exists. GST-registered entities must issue serial-numbered tax invoices in the prescribed format, and for export transactions, retain supporting customs documentation (export declaration, bill of lading) and foreign-currency remittance evidence. BTSD is piloting phased electronic invoicing integration, but no digital stamping or real-time clearance is yet legally enforceable.

Fraudulent export zero-rating claims (false declarations, fictitious bills of lading, or fabricated remittance certificates) are treated as tax evasion. Penalties under the GST Act include: immediate recovery (clawback) of every refund obtained, interest at 10% per annum, and administrative penalties up to 300% of the tax evaded. Criminal prosecution may lead to imprisonment for up to five years and disqualification from GST registration for a period of up to three years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belizean dollar (BZD) is pegged to the United States dollar at 2.00 BZD per 1.00 USD, and conversion in Belize is normally available at that fixed rate through licensed banks. The current-account is substantially liberalized, but the capital-account remains subject to exchange-control approval for transactions involving Belize-resident persons and BZD-denominated assets. A Belize International Business Company (IBC) is treated as a non-resident for exchange-control purposes when it transacts in foreign currency with non-residents; therefore, capital injections and profit repatriation through the IBC's foreign-currency accounts are not subject to the usual capital-account licensing. In practice, any conversion of IBC funds into BZD, or any local Belize-source payment, will require an exchange-control declaration and may trigger Central Bank review. Dividends and proceeds may be remitted freely from an IBC's foreign-currency account to non-resident shareholders, provided the IBC has no Belize-source income, has satisfied its annual IBC license fees, and the bank has verified source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident commercial exporters in Belize are subject to Central Bank exchange-control directives requiring export proceeds to be repatriated to an authorized dealer without undue delay; however, Belize does not operate a uniform statutory surrender percentage for all merchandise exports, and directed obligations are implemented through the banking system. A Belize IBC that acts as the subscription-box seller is a non-resident entity, and the Exchange Control Act does not require it to surrender or convert export proceeds received in USD into BZD. The IBC must nevertheless route inbound receipts through a licensed Belize bank or an approved foreign banking channel so that the Central Bank can observe the transaction; the bank may require documentary proof of the underlying cross-border sale and will not convert the proceeds absent instruction.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (Exchange Control Department)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Medium-High where the offering is a licensed electronic money institution with a real correspondent banking relationship and the IBC is used solely for subscription receivables collection; feasibility is Low where the EMI lacks a downstream bank sponsor or attempts to act as a full Belize deposit-taking institution. Belize law does not expressly prohibit a Belize IBC from opening a settlement wallet with an offshore EMI, but such an EMI cannot access Belize's local clearing, cannot issue BZD-denominated accounts, and is not regulated by the Central Bank of Belize. The robust structure is to use a regulated EMI as a payment collection gateway and to clear net settlements into a licensed Belize commercial bank account in favor of the IBC.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Founder capital and shareholder loans must be routed through a compliant SWIFT MT103 wire transfer to the IBC's USD account at the licensed Belize bank. The wire should state the beneficiary as the full legal IBC name and use the appropriate purpose code, typically 'CF' (Shares and other equity capital) or 'ITS' (Investment income and transactions) for equity injections, with a separate operational transaction code for loan advances. The bank will require a capital introduction file comprising the share register, latest annual return, source-of-funds explanation, and confirmation that the IBC is a non-resident entity. Funds received without the correct purpose code may be rejected or subjected to a suspicious-transaction report; therefore, the remitter should include the IBC corporate registration number and a reference matching the board resolution approving the capital contribution. Payment may be cleared through SWIFT or the US correspondent network, but the first-dollar destination must be the IBC's Belize-licensed account rather than a personal account.Outbound payments for subscription-box inventory, fulfillment, shipping, and supplier settlements should be cleared through SWIFT MT103 messages drawn from the IBC's operating account, using purpose codes such as '1300' (General trade in goods) or '1280' (Trade in services) as applicable. For high-value bulk inventory purchases, a commercial letter of credit opened through Belize Bank Limited or another tier-1 bank provides documentary protection and ensures that title documents are exchanged before payment releases. Vendors may also receive payments through the IBC's EMI aggregation account if the EMI has an MT103 clearing corridor; however, the preferred treasury rail is direct SWIFT from the Belize-licensed bank to the supplier's bank because correspondent banks perceive it as more transparent. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Belize International Business Companies (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act; standard Belize corporate income tax rate for resident domestic companies is 25% but IBCs are wholly exempt from Belize income, business, capital gains, and withholding taxes.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends or distributions paid by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders. Belize IBC Act expressly exempts all such distributions from Belize income, withholding, and business taxes.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BZD 0 (no statutory transfer-pricing Local File or Master File threshold is codified for Belize IBCs). Belize has not enacted OECD-style transfer-pricing filing thresholds for IBCs, but all cross-border related-party transactions should be documented on an arm's length basis and readily available for audit; in practice, any intercompany transaction should have contemporaneous documentation prepared, particularly where the IBC is part of a multinational subscription-commerce group.

Economic substance scope: The Belize IBC is subject to economic substance requirements consistent with Belize's international commitments. IBCs that carry on a relevant activity within Belize—including distribution and service-center activity, intellectual property holding, financing and leasing, insurance, shipping, and holding-company functions—must maintain substantial economic presence in Belize: local board meetings, strategic management and control in Belize, adequate employees with suitable qualifications, physical premises, and proportionate operating expenditure. For a subscription-box service, the distribution and fulfillment center functions must satisfy the relevant activity test where the IBC is the contracting entity; otherwise, the IBC must demonstrate that the relevant activity occurs in another jurisdiction with proper substance. Annual filing of an economic substance notice is mandatory, and failure to file or meet substance requirements can result in financial penalties and potential strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Upon audit, the Belize tax authority may assess the IBC under the Income and Business Tax Act with back taxes, interest, and penalties of up to 100% of the unpaid tax; criminal prosecution may result in fines and imprisonment. The foreign jurisdiction may also impose anti-avoidance penalties and recharacterization of the entire subscription-box revenue stream.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling non-resident shareholders may be held personally liable where the corporate veil is pierced for fraud, dishonest tax evasion, or sham transactions. Courts may order civil restitution of diverted profits, freeze assets, impose travel bans pending investigation, and sentence directors to imprisonment for criminal tax evasion.
Shell Company / Shameful Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registry may impose administrative fines, suspend or strike off the IBC, and revoke its certificate of registration. For non-compliance with the economic substance regime, the IBC may lose its tax-exempt certification and become subject to Belize income tax from the date of non-compliance, with interest and penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable for the IBC's unpaid taxes where the IBC is deemed a facade or sham. The court may order disgorgement, disqualification from acting as directors, and, where substance failures are deliberate, criminal fines and imprisonment. Non-resident directors may also face travel restrictions and extradition exposure in related foreign tax investigations.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Violations

Statutory sanction: Violations of Belize AML/CFT and beneficial ownership rules attract monetary fines, suspension of the registered agent's license, and criminal prosecution for the IBC and its officers. The IBC may be struck off and the beneficial owner flagged in international financial intelligence exchanges.

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners, and registered agents may be personally liable for civil penalties and restitution. Serious or repeated violations expose individuals to custodial sentences, asset-freezing orders, and restrictions on international movement, including the issue of anti-money-laundering watches at ports and banking institutions.
False Records or Misleading Annual Return

Statutory sanction: The offense of furnishing materially false information attracts fines under the Belize IBC Act and the Companies Act, disqualification of the officers, and possible deregistration of the IBC. Where the false records affect foreign tax liability, concurrent criminal fraud charges may be brought in any jurisdiction having exposure to the distribution chain.

Director personal liability: Directors and de facto officers who sign or authorize false filings are personally exposed to civil liability for damages, disgorgement, and contribution to the tax authority's losses. A criminal conviction may result in imprisonment, financial penalties, and a court-imposed travel ban; courts will disregard the corporate veil to reach the personal assets of the controlling shareholder where fraud is established.
