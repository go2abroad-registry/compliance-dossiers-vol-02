Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance — official portal: https://taxservice.gov.bz (BTS/TAMS e-services platform); customs formalities are handled by the Belize Customs Department within the same Ministry under the Customs and Excise Duty Act (Cap. 48); IBC incorporation and supervision is handled by the Registrar of International Business Companies and the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Engage a licensed Registered Agent in Belize. Step 2: File Articles of Incorporation (Form A) with the Registrar of International Business Companies, together with the Registered Agent's consent, and pay the statutory incorporation/registration fees (fee scale based on authorized capital). Step 3: Obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, which bears the IBC's statutory registration number. Step 4: The Registered Agent files a taxpayer registration application with the Belize Tax Service via the TAMS portal to procure the IBC's Tax Identification Number (TIN), required for annual IBC Information Returns and Economic Substance filings under the Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55). Step 5: File the annual IBC Information Return (confirming offshore, non-Belize-resident activity) and, where applicable, an Economic Substance return. No separate business license is required for cross-border DTC cosmetic retail; however, if the IBC makes any taxable supply to a Belize resident or imports goods for domestic consumption, it must also register for GST under the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 84).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — A Belize IBC is statutorily exempt under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), s.79 for 50 years from incorporation from Belize income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, exchange control, and GST on offshore activities. The exemption is conditioned on the IBC not carrying on business with Belize residents and not owning Belize land unless expressly permitted. Contingent reclassification as Entity_Level_Taxation (30% corporate income tax on taxable income plus business turnover tax at the applicable sector rate) applies retroactively upon breach of the IBC conditions, failure of economic substance requirements, or continuation as a domestic company under the Companies Act.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Belize. Express statutory elections available: (1) continuation/redomiciliation of a foreign corporation into the IBC regime under the International Business Companies Act, which automatically confers the 50-year tax exemption; (2) election by an IBC to continue as a domestic company under the Companies Act, thereby revoking the tax-exempt status and opting into standard Belize entity-level taxation; (3) election to file the Economic Substance return under the International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Rules, 2019 for any 'relevant activity'; and (4) voluntary GST registration election under s.5 of the General Sales Tax Act for a person whose taxable supplies do not yet meet the compulsory threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZ$100,000 (approx. US$50,000) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; compulsory registration for any person, resident or non-resident, making taxable supplies in Belize above this threshold; voluntary registration is available below the threshold at the taxpayer's option

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated under s.8(1) of the General Sales Tax Act where they are physically exported from Belize to a place outside Belize in the course of the supply; services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident person who uses them entirely outside Belize. The supplier must hold contemporaneous documentary evidence of export and title/risk transfer outside Belize; for the online cosmetics DTC model, qualifying supplies include goods shipped to foreign customers from Belize or goods fulfilled from a foreign warehouse to non-resident customers, provided the invoice, shipping documents, and payment records demonstrate the cross-border destination of the sale.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) processed and endorsed through ASYCUDA World by the Belize Customs Department, evidencing the physical export of the cosmetics consignment
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or courier consignment note evidencing the departure of the goods from Belize to the foreign destination in the name of the IBC exporter
  • Commercial invoice together with the Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/bank transfer advice proving settlement of the export sale in foreign currency

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, API integration, or digital stamping regime for retail/physical goods in Belize as of 2025. GST invoices must satisfy the content requirements of the General Sales Tax Act (sequential numbering, seller's GST/TIN, description of supply, tax-exclusive prices, and GST amount). Electronic filing and payment are mandatory through the BTS TAMS portal; customs declarations are processed electronically via ASYCUDA World. BTS has announced future e-invoicing pilot programs, but no operative legislation or technical mandate has yet been enacted.

The Belize Tax Service audits zero-rated export claims by cross-matching customs declarations, shipping records, bank remittance advices, and the IBC's annual GST returns. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims attract: (i) under the General Sales Tax Act, a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, a fine of up to BZ$10,000, and/or imprisonment up to 3 years; (ii) under the Income and Business Tax Act, penalties of 100% of tax understated plus monthly interest; (iii) retrospective clawback of all input VAT credits previously claimed on export supplies; (iv) under the IBC Act, breach of the offshore conditions (including non-repatriation of export proceeds within the prescribed benchmark) may cause the IBC to forfeit its tax-exempt status and be subjected to Belize corporate income tax at 30% and business tax on gross receipts from the date of incorporation; and (v) under the Economic Substance Rules, failure to satisfy substance requirements attracts penalties of BZ$10,000 plus BZ$1,000 per month and, for continued default, strike-off from the register with immediate loss of the tax exemption.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 and is freely convertible for most current-account transactions; resident domestic entities require Central Bank approval for certain capital-account flows. Belize IBCs are statutorily exempt from exchange control under Section 22 of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), so they may hold multi-currency accounts, retain USD receipts offshore, and remit dividends or return capital to non-resident shareholders without CBB approval and without Belizean withholding tax. The principal hurdle is not statutory convertibility but bank-level enhanced due diligence because offshore IBCs are commonly classified as higher-risk corporate structures by correspondent banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident domestic exporters must repatriate export proceeds into the Belizean banking system within the period prescribed by the Exchange Control (General) Regulations (administratively applied as 90 days from shipment) and must offer foreign currency to an authorized dealer for conversion into BZD at the official peg. This mandatory surrender regime does not apply to Belize IBCs: Section 22 of the IBC Act disapplies the Exchange Control Act, so e-commerce collections from the cosmetics DTC business may be held in USD in Belize international banking accounts or abroad without conversion quotas or surrender timelines.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), Belmopan, established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Cap. 262). The CBB administers the national currency and the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 40) through authorized dealer banks. Registration and AML/CFT supervision of International Business Companies are conducted by the Financial Services Commission (FSC), which succeeded the former International Financial Services Commission (IFSC).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections should be routed as SWIFT MT103 wires in USD from the shareholder to the IBC's Belize international bank account, with the remittance information referencing 'Capital Subscription - International Business Company' and the relevant share subscription or shareholder loan agreement. Belize does not use IBAN; the beneficiary is identified by bank account number and the bank's SWIFT BIC. USD funds clear from the originating jurisdiction through the Belize bank's US correspondent (Fedwire/CHIPS) and are credited directly in USD without mandatory conversion to BZD. The bank will require a source-of-funds form, a board resolution, and, for registered capital, updated share register entries and beneficial ownership filings with the registered agent.Outbound trade disbursements for cosmetics suppliers and logistics partners should use: (i) SWIFT MT103 for routine invoice settlements with full remittance information (invoice number, purchase order, incoterms); (ii) Commercial Letter of Credit via MT700/MT707 for large first-shipment purchase orders; and (iii) payment-platform channels such as PayPal, Payoneer, or Shopify Balance for small supplier and contractor payments. Belize international banks process USD outbound wires on the same business day with no tax clearance or exchange control approval required for IBCs. Currency conversion into EUR or GBP is executed at the bank's commercial rate with the BZD/USD peg anchoring the USD leg; all trade documents (commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, bills of lading) should be retained for compliance and audit.Onshore USD/BZD outright forward contracts with Central Bank-licensed banks (tenor typically up to 12 months)Cross-currency swaps for USD/EUR or USD/GBP exposure executed through Belize international banks or their foreign affiliatesOffshore nondeliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/BZD available through non-Belize bank trading desks, subject to documented commercial hedge rationale and limited liquidityMulti-currency cash pooling and sweep accounts for natural hedging of USD/EUR/GBP collections

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low-to-Moderate as a primary treasury host; Moderate as a collection-only gateway. Belize has no dedicated local EMI licensing framework, so offshore digital EMIs and neobanks (e.g., Mercury, Wise, Airwallex, Currenxie, Revolut Business, Payoneer) operate under their home-country (US/EU) regulations and generally classify a Belize IBC as a higher-risk foreign entity. Onboarding is frequently conditioned on 6-12 months of live e-commerce trading history, economic substance evidence, beneficial ownership disclosure, and physical or video identification of directors. The IBC Act does not prohibit holding funds abroad, but the absence of a Belizean regulatory passport for EMIs makes them unsuitable for capital escrow or large-ticket trade disbursements; Shopify Payments and PayPal should remain the primary customer collection rails.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Belize IBC; the entity is statutorily exempt from corporate income tax under the International Business Companies Act (IBC Act). A resident trading company is generally subject to Belize income tax at the corporate rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on dividends paid by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders; IBC distributions are exempt from Belize withholding tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to a tax-exempt Belize IBC. Prudent compliance threshold for an active cross-border e-commerce group: prepare OECD-compliant Local File and Master File when annual controlled transactions exceed BZD 3,000,000 (approximately USD 1,500,000), or when requested by the Belize competent authority during economic substance or anti-avoidance review.

Economic substance scope: The Belize Economic Substance (Amendment) Act applies to Belize IBCs carrying on relevant activities, including 'distribution and service centre' activity. Online retail of cosmetics and personal care products falls within this category. The IBC must demonstrate real management and control in Belize, physical premises, local employees, and adequate operating expenditure; failure to do so triggers information exchange, sanctions and loss of good standing.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / unauthorized Belize domestic trading

Statutory sanction: Loss of IBC exempt status; assessment to Belize income tax on undeclared profits at the ordinary corporate rate; interest and late-payment penalties; administrative strike-off; potential criminal prosecution for filing false returns.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize prohibited local trading or false declarations may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; courts can pierce the corporate veil; criminal conviction can lead to imprisonment and travel restrictions pending tax proceedings.
Sham arrangement / artificial profit shifting

Statutory sanction: General anti-avoidance reassessment; transfer pricing adjustment and attribution of taxable profit to the economically substantive entity; exchange of information with foreign tax authorities; potential IBC strike-off for failure to meet economic substance standards.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for fraudulent trading where they knowingly misrepresented the IBC's functions; personal restitution orders and director disqualification may follow.
Economic substance failure / shell company

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Belize Economic Substance regime; compulsory filing of economic substance information with the Registry; automatic information exchange with foreign tax authorities; continued failure can result in financial penalties, directors being summoned, and the IBC being struck off.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers responsible for ESR non-compliance may be subject to personal fines and disqualification; the corporate veil is not available to protect willful non-compliance with economic substance notifications.
AML/CFT failure and unverified beneficial ownership

Statutory sanction: Sanctions under Belize's Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act: criminal fines, asset forfeiture, freezing of payment accounts, suspension of the registered agent's services, and IBC strike-off if AML compliance cannot be completed.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution with substantial fines and imprisonment for knowing money laundering; no corporate shield applies to personal criminal conduct; travel bans are common in active money-laundering investigations.
False records and fraudulent annual declarations

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties for perjury and false declaration under Belize law; revocation of IBC status; restitution orders; financial penalties in addition to correction of all registry records.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause false filings to be made are personally subject to fines and imprisonment; the court may order disqualification as a director and require personal restitution of tax and penalties.
