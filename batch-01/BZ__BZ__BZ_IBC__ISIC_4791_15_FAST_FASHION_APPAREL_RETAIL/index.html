Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment); official e-services portal: https://www.taxservice.gov.bz. International Business Companies are additionally registered with the Belize International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Belize IBC is formed by a licensed registered agent filing Articles of Incorporation with the IFSC; the IFSC issues the Certificate of Incorporation and a company registration number, which serves as the primary tax-identifier for offshore operations. No local TIN is required for a purely offshore IBC; annual IFSC license fees replace substantive tax filings. If a TIN is needed for banking or KYC, the entity must submit Form TIN Application with the Tax Service Department, attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, registered-agent confirmation, and proof of premises. If the IBC engages in taxable supplies inside Belize or imports goods into Belize, it must separately apply for Income Tax registration and General Sales Tax (GST) registration (STIN) once its Belize source turnover reaches the statutory threshold; the offshore exemption is automatically forfeited when onshore business is conducted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Belize. The IBC's tax-exempt status is statutory and automatic upon incorporation. An IBC may elect to redomicile to another jurisdiction or convert to an onshore Belize company, thereby becoming subject to the territorial income tax regime (25% corporate rate on Belize-source income) and the gross-receipts Business Tax. Under the 2019 Economic Substance amendments, IBCs conducting geographically mobile activities must elect to satisfy the Belize economic substance test with the IFSC and file an annual economic substance return. No hybrid mismatch election is available under domestic law; cross-border mismatch outcomes are managed under general anti-abuse principles and OECD minimum standards.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period (mandatory GST registration). Persons making wholly zero-rated export supplies may apply for voluntary registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of goods physically removed from Belize and delivered to a non-resident customer, and to specified offshore services supplied to non-residents for use wholly outside Belize. A fast-fashion e-commerce operation qualifies only for goods exported from Belize itself; goods shipped directly from third-country warehouses to end consumers are outside the Belize GST net and cannot be zero-rated or refunded. The claimant must hold a valid GST registration and issue a 0% rated tax invoice.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form CE-1) duly certified by the Belize Customs Department
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing dispatch to a non-resident consignee outside Belize
  • Commercial invoice issued in the name of the IBC showing 0% GST and the overseas purchaser
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of foreign currency from abroad
  • GST return with the Schedule of Zero-Rated Export Sales reconciling invoices, customs records, and payment receipts

No real-time fiscal clearance, API stamping, or digital invoice clearance mandate exists in Belize. Registered persons must issue GST-compliant tax invoices containing prescribed particulars (supplier name and TIN, recipient details, value, GST amount, and issue date). GST returns are filed electronically through the Tax Service Department e-services portal; there is no SAF-T or continuous transaction control requirement.

The GST Commissioner may disallow export zero-rating where documentary evidence is incomplete or inconsistent. Fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger retrospective reassessment at 12.5% GST on the full invoice value, denial of input credits, interest, and civil penalties of double the tax underpaid; criminal prosecution under the General Sales Tax Act may result in fines up to BZD 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years. For an IBC, proven onshore activity or Belize customer nexus simultaneously voids the offshore tax exemption, exposing the entity to back assessments of income tax, Business Tax, GST, stamp duty, interest, and penalties from inception of the onshore business.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is fixed to the US dollar at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, with full current-account convertibility for trade and profit-remittance flows. Capital-account transactions require exchange-control approval unless statutorily exempt. A Belize International Business Company (IBC) under Cap. 270 of the Laws of Belize is expressly exempt from the Exchange Control Regulations, provided it does not carry on business with Belize residents, does not acquire land in Belize, and does not conduct licensed banking, insurance, or trust activity. Accordingly, the IBC may receive and retain sales proceeds entirely in USD, fund itself freely from non-resident shareholders, and remit dividends or capital distributions to non-resident owners without Central Bank approvals. Practical profit-remittance hurdles are therefore minimal; the only constraint is that any incidental conversion into BZD for local expenses would require a general authorisation through the IBC's authorised dealer bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must repatriate export proceeds to Belize within six months of shipment and surrender them to an authorised dealer for conversion into BZD at the official pegged rate. This statutory surrender regime does NOT bind a Belize IBC: the IBC is exempt from exchange control and may retain export receipts in USD or any other usable currency indefinitely in its accounts. For the fast-fashion e-commerce model, the IBC should maintain a documentary file demonstrating that all customers, payment aggregators (Shopify Payments, Klarna), suppliers, and logistics counterparties are non-residents of Belize, thereby preserving the exemption and avoiding any requirement to convert or compulsorily repatriate proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Cap. 262 of the Laws of Belize), which exercises exchange-control authority pursuant to the Exchange Control Regulations and supervises all authorised dealers and licensed commercial banks operating in Belize.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Belize IBCs are accepted by only a narrow set of offshore digital EMIs and payment facilitators (e.g., Payoneer and certain B2B treasury platforms); the principal global EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, DolarApp, and BNPL-linked banking-as-a-service providers) generally decline IBC structures due to Belize's offshore-centre status, the IBC's nil-tax profile, and non-resident economic substance concerns. EMIs are not licensed banks under Belize's Banks and Financial Institutions Act; hence they should be used strictly as collection-side payment facilitators for Shopify Payments and Klarna, with all treasury balances swept daily to a Central-Bank-supervised commercial bank account. Any fintech-EMI subscription should be disclosed in writing to the commercial bank to avoid undisclosed-account covenant breaches.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for International Business Companies on qualifying foreign-source business income; the general Belize corporate income tax headline rate is 25% for resident taxable companies.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for IBC distributions. Belize imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by an IBC to non-resident shareholders by virtue of the Section 6 IBC Act exemption.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual license fee due on or before 31 August of each calendar year; the formal IBC Annual Return is due on the incorporation anniversary date within the IFSC-directed filing window (typically 60 days).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold is codified in Belize dollars; however, economic substance regulations apply irrespective of turnover, and IBCs engaged in fast-fashion distribution must prepare and retain contemporaneous records evidencing arm's length consideration for inventory purchases, intercompany licensing, management fees, and logistics services allocated to the Belize entity.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. An IBC deriving income from relevant activities (including distribution and service center functions in the fast-fashion supply chain, or IP holding where brand intangibles are centralized) must satisfy the Economic Substance test in Belize: core income-generating activities physically performed in Belize; adequate physical premises; full-time employees proportionate to turnover; adequate annual local operating expenditure; and annual ESR notification with supporting documentation. A mere booking of invoices through Belize without local substance will fail the test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Economic Substance Failure (Shell Company / OECD FHTP)

Statutory sanction: Failure of the economic substance test; IFSC monetary penalty of up to BZD 100,000 and imprisonment of officers for up to 5 years for knowingly false declarations; automatic exchange of information to the beneficial owner's country of residence under the OECD transparency framework, prompting foreign tax re-assessments; administrative strike-off of the IBC.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate beneficial owners may be jointly and severally assessed for unpaid penalties; disqualification from acting as directors in Belize; exposure to mutual legal assistance, extradition, and travel bans where the conduct constitutes tax fraud or financial crime in the residence jurisdiction.
Tax Evasion / Abuse of the IBC Exemption (Substance-Over-Form)

Statutory sanction: The Belize exemption is void to the extent income is actually derived from Belize-source commerce or local dealings; the Income and Business Tax Act then applies at 25% plus business tax on gross receipts. Foreign tax authorities may disregard the IBC's separate legal personality, reallocate profits under PE/CFC rules, assess back taxes with penalties up to 100% of the tax at risk plus interest, and impose EU VAT on distance sales.

Director personal liability: Controllers resident where commercial operations occur face personal criminal tax charges, trust-fund recovery penalties, and enforcement of foreign revenue judgments; officers who certify false management-and-control declarations are personally indictable; travel bans may be imposed by foreign revenue courts during extradition or summons-enforcement proceedings.
Anti-Money Laundering / AML Deficiency in BNPL Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Contravention of the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act and IFSC AML/CFT rules: monetary penalties up to BZD 200,000, suspension of the registered agent's authorization, freezing orders, confiscation of the IBC's funds, and criminal money-laundering charges carrying up to 14 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors and the IBC's authorized representative are individually liable for AML program failures; personal fines, forfeiture of personal assets, imprisonment, asset restraint orders, and travel restrictions may be imposed pending investigation.
Unauthorized Domestic Business Activity in Belize

Statutory sanction: The tax exemption is void for such income; the full corporate income tax of 25% and applicable business tax on gross receipts apply, with interest and late-payment surcharges; the IFSC may revoke the IBC certificate and order strike-off.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false compliance declaration in the annual return commit an offense punishable by imprisonment; directors may be personally assessed for the unremitted tax and penalties where the company is unable to pay.
Trading While Struck-Off / Trading After Dissolution

Statutory sanction: Trade by or on behalf of a struck-off IBC is a criminal offense; the company's assets upon dissolution vest in the Government of Belize pending restoration, exposing foreign banks to asset-hold orders; directors and officers who trade after strike-off may be criminally fined and imprisoned.

Director personal liability: Directors become personally and jointly liable for all debts contracted during the struck-off period, including warehouse leases, payment-processor chargebacks, and supplier claims; restitution to creditors is enforceable against personal assets, with up to 5 years' imprisonment for continued trading.
