Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (under the Ministry of Finance) — official portal: https://taxservices.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

The International Business Company (IBC) is statutorily tax-exempt under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270). For any limited local taxable activity, the entity must apply for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, register for General Sales Tax (GST) by filing Form GST-01 with the Belize Tax Service. The application requires: certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office details, director/shareholder particulars, and proof of local business address if any Belize-source supply is contemplated. In the standard export-only IoT retail model, the IBC should obtain a TIN for administrative correspondence but normally remains outside the Belize GST net unless local supplies exceed the statutory threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available. The IBC's tax exemption is automatic, non-elective, and conditional solely on compliance with the IBC Act, including the prohibition on engaging in Belize-resident trade except as expressly permitted. No hybrid mismatch election applies to an IBC; the entity is a non-resident offshore company treated as tax-transparent in Belize only for administrative nil-return purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

GST registration is mandatory for any person or entity whose annual taxable supplies exceed BZD 75,000 (Belize dollars). IBCs engaged exclusively in exports or non-resident activities generally exceed this threshold only on the basis of export turnover, which is zero-rated and not counted as taxable supply for registration purposes unless local supplies are also made. Where a foreign-owned IBC has no Belize establishment and makes no local supplies, no GST registration is required.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% GST applies to the export of goods from Belize and to internationally supplied services (including cross-border IoT hardware retail) provided that the supplier retains documentary proof of physical export or offshore service delivery. The IBC must ensure that the goods are exported within a reasonable time of supply and that the recipient is outside Belize. For distance sales of smart-home IoT hardware via Stripe or Shopify Payments, zero-rating is available where the supplier ships from Belize using commercial courier or freight forwarder and holds export evidence. Services that are consumed entirely outside Belize (e.g., cloud platform fees, warranty support) also qualify as zero-rated supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Belize Customs and Excise Department — Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (issued by carrier and evidencing export from Belize)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds from the overseas buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory digital invoice stamping regime currently applies in Belize. The Belize Tax Service requires periodic GST returns (typically quarterly) submitted electronically through its e-tax filing portal. Standard commercial e-invoices (e.g., PDF/electronic records generated by Stripe or Shopify Payments) are acceptable for substantiation, provided they include supplier details, recipient details, a description of goods, the amount, and the applicable GST treatment. There is no API-based government clearance or validation requirement in force.

Under the Belize GST Act and the Tax Administration and Procedure Act, a taxpayer who claims zero-rating without the required documentation or with forged or misleading evidence is liable to a retrospective GST assessment at the standard 12.5% rate, plus interest at the statutory default rate (currently 2.5% per month or part thereof), an immediate penalty of up to 100% of the tax due, and, upon conviction, a fine not exceeding BZD 50,000 and/or imprisonment for up to three years. The Belize Tax Service may also disallow input tax credits in proportion to the fraudulent export claim and may freeze or claw back any refund issued. In blockchain and IoT retail cases, the tax authority retains broad discretion to disregard electronic platform records where original shipping and payment evidence is missing or internally inconsistent. No limitation period applies to cases involving fraud or willful misstatement, permitting indefinite retrospective recovery.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

BZD is fully convertible for current-account purposes; Belize applies exchange-control licensing to residents, but an International Business Company (IBC) is statutorily a non-resident under section 5 of the International Business Companies Act (Chapter 270) and is exempt from exchange-control restrictions and all exchange-licensing requirements. The IBC must maintain and transact in foreign currency (typically USD); any BZD-denominated local expenditure (e.g., registered agent fees, accounting fees) is settled from USD funds via a resident paying agent at the official peg automatically, without CBB approval. Profit remittance: dividends, interest, royalties, and management fees paid by the IBC are free of Belize tax and withholding, and there are no exchange-control gates for foreign-currency outbound remittances. Residual hurdle: if the IBC ever becomes resident (e.g., physically managed and controlled in Belize) or converts BZD cash, CBB exchange-control approval would be required for capital outflows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender obligation applies to an IBC. The IBC Act exemption removes the applicability of the Exchange Control Act, including any forced conversion or repatriation timeline; export receipts (e.g., IoT hardware sales proceeds collected via payment processors) may be retained in USD in offshore or domestic foreign-currency accounts indefinitely. For resident commercial traders (not IBCs), the Exchange Control (General) Regulations impose a 180-day repatriation window and conversion of export proceeds at the official peg; this regime is inapplicable to a qualifying IBC transacting solely in foreign currency.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Chapter 262, Laws of Belize); jointly superintends banks under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act (Chapter 263) and enforces the Exchange Control Act (Chapter 259). The Belize dollar (BZD) operates under a statutory fixed peg of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, maintained by CBB foreign-reserve management.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low feasibility for a Belize IBC. Stripe and Shopify Payments do not support Belize-domiciled entities, so the primary treasury collection rails must be routed through a payment processor of record or a supported affiliate entity. Belize has no dedicated EMI licensing framework; non-bank fintechs operating payment accounts require a license under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act and domestic substance, which is commercially impractical for a pure IBC. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Mercury, Airwallex) generally decline Belize IBCs under channel risk policies or require a resident managing entity and economic substance in their own jurisdiction. Recommended architecture: (i) use a payment aggregator/master account (e.g., Stripe or Shopify Payments held by a supported group entity) with daily settlement to the IBC USD account; or (ii) use a licensed money remitter in a friendly corridor (e.g., a UK or EU EMI backed by an e-money ancillary arrangement) that accepts IBCs with nil-tax certificates and beneficial ownership filings.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Beneficial ownership opacity: nominee shareholders, bearer-share legacy, or missing PSC particulars; banks will file a Suspicious Transaction Report or decline.

Root cause: Beneficial ownership opacity: nominee shareholders, bearer-share legacy, or missing PSC particulars; banks will file a Suspicious Transaction Report or decline.

Defense evidence: Current Certificate of Good Standing; certified register of members and register of directors; full beneficial ownership declaration filed with the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit (CIFIU) under the FIU Act; share register excerpt showing issue to a named natural person; a written source-of-funds and source-of-wealth report accompanied by audited personal financial statements and UBO bank statements for the last 12 months; resident-agent client due-diligence certificate.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an exempt Belize IBC; the general Belize corporate income tax headline rate under the Income and Business Tax Act is 25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for a Belize IBC; the IBC Act expressly exempts dividends, distributions, interest, royalties, and rental payments made by an IBC from all Belize withholding taxes

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BZD 0 - no statutory transfer pricing documentation threshold applies to a tax-exempt IBC, and Belize has not enacted Local File or Master File rules for IBCs; if the IBC transacts with connected non-resident distribution affiliates, the economic substance review will assess whether the distribution margin and remuneration are commercially arm's length

Economic substance scope: Yes. The International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Act, 2019 and its Regulations require an IBC carrying on a relevant activity to demonstrate economic substance in Belize. ISIC 4791 retail distribution of smart home IoT hardware is captured as a distribution and service centre activity. The test requires that core income-generating activities, meaning procurement, storage, inventory management, order-taking, contract negotiation, invoicing, payment collection, and customer service, be performed in Belize by adequate employees, with adequate physical premises and proportionate annual expenditure. IP-related activities receive enhanced scrutiny; a purely passive holding or pass-through structure will fail. Non-compliant IBCs face financial penalties, daily default penalties, strike-off, and possible forfeiture of the tax exemption

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Abusive Offshore Arrangement

Statutory sanction: United States: 75% civil fraud penalty under IRC Section 6663, FBAR penalties up to 100% of undisclosed account balances, 30% US branch profits tax and withholding on US-source payments, and criminal referral to the IRS Criminal Investigation Division. Belize: prosecution under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, 2013, fines, asset forfeiture, false statement penalties under the IBC Act, and strike-off by BCCAR. In both jurisdictions the principal is liable for full restitution of avoided taxes plus interest

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of distributions received; court-ordered freezing orders and travel bans; imprisonment up to 5 years for predicate money-laundering offences; disqualification from serving as a Belize director; extradition risk under bilateral arrangements
Shell Company and Economic Substance Regime Failure

Statutory sanction: Belize economic substance penalties comprising financial penalties and daily default penalties, compulsory production of books and records, strike-off, and revocation of the IBC tax exemption; after revocation the company becomes chargeable to 25% Belize corporate income tax, arrears, and interest; economic substance data is exchanged with foreign tax authorities under CRS and exchange of information instruments

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false economic substance declarations are personally liable to fines and imprisonment up to 3 years; the corporate veil is pierced for sham structures; civil recovery of avoided taxes; director disqualification orders
AML and Beneficial Ownership Non-Disclosure

Statutory sanction: Penalties and imprisonment under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, 2013, including fines up to BZ$250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years for structured concealment; the licensed registered agent is obliged to file a suspicious transaction report with the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit and may terminate the client relationship; the IBC's books and payment records may be seized

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for aiding money laundering; personal confiscation orders over personal assets; travel bans; extradition; imprisonment up to 5 years; nominee concealment defeats limited liability
Loss of Good Standing and Corporate Veil Piercing

Statutory sanction: BCCAR strikes the company off 90 days after default; the company loses legal capacity to contract, sue, or own property; restoration requires a court order plus payment of all arrears, surcharges, and penalties; trading while struck off is a statutory offence

Director personal liability: Directors who trade during strike-off are personally liable for all debts and contracts incurred; courts pierce the veil where the IBC is a mere sham; civil restitution orders against shareholders; director disqualification
Foreign Permanent Establishment and US Nexus Exposure

Statutory sanction: US federal income tax on effectively connected income at graduated rates, 30% branch profits tax under IRC Section 884 on repatriated earnings, state sales and use tax assessments with penalties and interest, and 100% trust fund recovery penalties under IRC Section 6672; the Belize IBC exemption provides no protection because it applies only to Belize taxes; FATCA and CRS reporting exposes the beneficial owner

Director personal liability: Responsible persons are liable for 100% trust fund penalties; US beneficial owners are reached through CFC and look-through rules; travel to the United States risks border detention under tax-fugitive warrants; criminal tax evasion prosecution with imprisonment up to 5 years
