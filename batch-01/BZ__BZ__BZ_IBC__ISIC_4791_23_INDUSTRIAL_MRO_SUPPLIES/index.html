Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Services (BTS), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; official taxpayer portal: https://belizetaxservices.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

A non-resident International Business Company (IBC) that remains within the International Business Companies Act and conducts all business outside Belize is exempt from Belize income tax and General Sales Tax and is not required to obtain a domestic Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) or GST registration number. If the IBC ceases to qualify for exemption, or if a foreign entity otherwise becomes liable to Belize General Sales Tax by making taxable supplies in Belize, the entity must register with Belize Tax Services by submitting the prescribed GST registration form, certified certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office confirmation, and director/shareholder identification documents. Belize Tax Services will then issue a Belize TIN and, where applicable, a GST registration certificate.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A non-resident International Business Company (IBC) that remains within the International Business Companies Act and conducts all business outside Belize is exempt from Belize income tax and General Sales Tax and is not required to obtain a domestic Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) or GST registration number. If the IBC ceases to qualify for exemption, or if a foreign entity otherwise becomes liable to Belize General Sales Tax by making taxable supplies in Belize, the entity must register with Belize Tax Services by submitting the prescribed GST registration form, certified certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office confirmation, and director/shareholder identification documents. Belize Tax Services will then issue a Belize TIN and, where applicable, a GST registration certificate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

An IBC may elect to surrender its tax-exempt status by filing a formal election with the Registrar of International Business Companies to be treated as an ordinary Belize company, after which Belize domestic tax and GST registration obligations will apply. Belize does not currently provide U.S.-style check-the-box elections or OECD-aligned hybrid mismatch elections; elections are limited to statutory IBC status changes, re-registration as a local company, and election to pay annual license fees with continued tax exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 150,000 of annual taxable supplies for a taxable person carrying on a taxable activity in Belize; reduced or specific thresholds may apply under the General Sales Tax Regulations or due to the nature of the supplies

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Belize and supplies of services to recipients outside Belize qualify for zero-rating at 0% GST if the supplier is registered for GST, the goods are removed from Belize within the statutory export period, the export is evidenced by a valid customs export declaration, and the supplier retains documentation proving delivery to the non-resident recipient. For an IBC operating exclusively outside Belize, sales of industrial MRO supplies are not subject to Belize GST at all, and therefore no zero-rating claim is necessary; if the IBC is re-registered as a local taxable company, exports must satisfy the documentary test to be treated as zero-rated supplies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CERP/export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank-generated foreign exchange credit advice evidencing receipt of payment
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Evidence of destination of goods in the foreign buyer's jurisdiction

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, government API-based invoice clearance, or digital stamping regime is currently in force for General Sales Tax. GST invoices may be issued in paper or electronic format, provided they contain the prescribed invoice particulars under the General Sales Tax Regulations. A future transitional e-invoicing framework may apply to prescribed sectors, but no such mandate is in force for an offshore IBC distributing industrial MRO supplies through an electronic catalog.

False or fraudulent zero-rating claims for Belize GST expose the registered taxpayer to an assessment of the uncollected GST at 12.5%, plus interest at the statutory rate under the General Sales Tax Act, a pecuniary penalty of up to 150% of the tax evaded or BZD 10,000 (whichever is greater), and possible criminal sanctions, including imprisonment. Belize Tax Services may reopen prior periods on discovery of incomplete export documentation, repatriated foreign currency not received, or round-tripping of goods, and will claw back the corresponding input tax credits or refunds with retrospective effect.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with the Belize dollar (BZD) pegged de facto to the United States dollar (USD) at BZD 2.00 per USD 1.00 since 1976; the peg is backed by the Central Bank of Belize's international reserves. Under the International Business Companies Act (IBC Act), an International Business Company is expressly relieved of exchange control regulations applicable to resident companies: Part XIII of the IBC Act disapplies the Exchange Control Ordinance for IBCs, meaning IBCs are free to conduct transactions in any currency, maintain foreign currency accounts, and repatriate capital, dividends, and profits without Central Bank approval, provided the IBC is not carrying on business with residents of Belize or holding Belizean real property. Current account transactions are effectively unrestricted for IBCs, while capital account items for IBCs are also unrestricted under the statutory exemption. Resident domestic Belizean entities remain subject to exchange control oversight, including restrictions on outward capital transfers, but an IBC's offshore status removes profit remittance hurdles for the non-resident shareholder. The remaining practical hurdles are banking counterparty compliance: many international correspondent banks impose enhanced due diligence on Belizean IBCs, which may delay or block USD clearing despite the legal convertibility. Profit remittance from an IBC is not subject to Belizean withholding tax or exchange control approval because the IBC is tax-exempt under the IBC Act and outside the Exchange Control regime; the only liquidity constraint is the availability of cleared USD funds at the receiving correspondent bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The IBC Act provides that the Exchange Control Ordinance does not apply to the operations or transactions of an International Business Company, including the receipt of export proceeds. Therefore, there is no statutory timeline for surrender of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio applicable to the IBC's foreign currency accounts. The IBC may hold 100% of export proceeds in USD or any foreign currency without any obligation to convert to Belize dollars. This applies to the B2B MRO e-commerce trading company, as its purchase and sale invoices are settled in USD. The only soft requirement arises from standard banking practice: the account-opening bank may require the customer to periodically justify large USD inflows with underlying commercial documents, but this is a customer due diligence measure, not a statutory surrender requirement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) and the Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-High for non-Belizean digital EMI platforms but with material legal and de-risking constraints. Legal constraints: (1) Under the IBC Act, an IBC may maintain bank accounts only with institutions licensed by the Central Bank of Belize or with a 'recognized offshore bank'; however, the IBC Act was amended to provide flexibility in relation to keeping accounts and records outside Belize. As a practical matter, the registered agent and directors must consult the current IBC Act section 107 (records and accounts) to ensure compliance if an offshore EMI is used. (2) The target payment rails include credit card and SWIFT wire; most high-volume B2B MRO buyers prefer standard correspondent bank routes, not EMI virtual accounts. (3) Many reputable EMIs (e.g., Mercury, Airwallex, Currenxie) are unwilling to open for Belize IBCs due to negative FATF perception, beneficial ownership transparency requirements, and correspondent banking de-risking. (4) An unlicensed EMI operating without a Belize banking licence cannot be the sole banking channel; it should be used only as an ancillary receivable collection tool. Recommended approach: maintain a primary tier-1 Belize commercial bank account for SWIFT settlement, and use a licensed foreign EMI only for small-value card collections if the EMI's onboarding policy permits Belize IBCs. Any EMI holding funds must be a regulated MSB/EMI in its home jurisdiction with a valid license; otherwise the treasury structure fails AML-bankability assessments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The inbound capital injection should be routed from the ultimate beneficial owner's personal bank account (not a corporate account in an unrelated jurisdiction) via SWIFT to the Belize bank account of the IBC. Use MT103 with clearly defined purpose code in the narrative field: for share capital injection, use a standard remittance code describing 'Capital contribution' (e.g., under SWIFT categories, use 'QUASI-CAPITAL' or 'CAPITAL' narrative; Belizean banks accept generic descriptions, but the bank's AML form must be completed with 'SUSD' or 'Investment' purpose). Step-by-step protocol: (1) execute a board resolution approving the issuance of no-par value shares or, if the IBC has only one share, record a shareholder's contribution; (2) the remitter's bank must carry out due diligence on the beneficiary; provide the IBC's certificate of incorporation, certificate of good standing, account details, and beneficial ownership declaration if requested; (3) the receiving bank will ask the IBC to sign a source-of-funds declaration; submit the board resolution, shareholder identity documents, and remittance instruction; (4) if amount exceeds a reporting threshold, the bank must report to the Financial Intelligence Unit under Belize's AML/CFT Act; there is no exchange control approval required for IBCs. For capital amounts over USD 250,000, banks may request additional confirmation of the shareholder's net worth via bank statements or a notarized net worth statement. To minimize rejection, ensure the remitter name matches the beneficial owner recorded in the IBC registry and the corporate registry; any mismatch triggers enhanced due diligence.Outbound trade disbursements for the industrial MRO supplies business are executed as follows: (1) Supplier payments in USD or CNY via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'Goods' (e.g., '7001' or 'Trade Settlement' narrative); confirm the supplier's full bank details with an independent source before initiating the wire. (2) For medium-to-large settlements where suppliers require documentary credit, use a Commercial Letter of Credit confirmed by the supplier's bank and advised by the buying bank. Because the Belize IBC may have limited credit availability, the LC route is most feasible on a cash-backed basis or when the IBC has sufficient liquidity in its account to cover the LC. (3) Cross-border disbursements above USD 10,000 should be accompanied by the underlying purchase order and invoice; banks may require a copy of the bill of lading or airway bill for high-value shipments. (4) For card-based MRO disbursements to U.S./European suppliers, use the corporate credit card issued by the Belize bank (must be set up separately). (5) Use a clearing structure that avoids routing funds to jurisdictions that trigger OFAC sanctions or FATF advisories; Belize banks' USD clearing is typically through U.S. correspondent banks. Ensure suppliers are not on any sanctions list. (6) Each outbound MT103 should reference the commercial invoice number to facilitate reconciliation. There are no statutory restrictions on capital outflows for IBCs, so no central bank approval is needed, but the bank may require the IBC to maintain consistent records of any 'matching' trade transaction for AML purposes.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on BZD/USD executed with international banks are not practically available and not necessary because the BZD/USD peg is de facto fixed and the IBC does not face Belize dollar concentration risks; Central Bank of Belize does not maintain a regulated onshore derivatives market for IBCs.Plain vanilla FX forwards on USD/CNY, USD/EUR, and USD/GBP permissible via the bank's international treasury desk for hedging supplier costs; if a Belize constituent bank cannot offer the product, the IBC must use an offshore treasury account with an investment-grade bank (subject to IBC Act records rules).Cross-currency swaps (USD principal to CNY or EUR) are permissible as hedging instruments for a trading company with MOUs/contracts demonstrating the underlying trade exposure; however, the IBC's financial statements should include them as off-balance-sheet derivatives with board approval and robust accounting records.FX options (European-style) are permissible for hedging MRO procurement exposed to currency fluctuations; strategy must be a plain vanilla collar or covered call and consistent with the IBC's risk management policy.Belize-registered banks are not major FX derivatives dealers; therefore, the permissible practical route is to execute NDF-like economic hedges with the same treasury counterparty that supports the Belize bank's USD clearing, or to denominate all contracts in USD to minimize FX exposure.Any hedging instrument must be documented in the IBC's board minutes and supported by an underlying commercial transaction or anticipated cash flow; purely speculative derivatives may jeopardize the IBC's tax-exempt status only if they become a separate business, but they remain outside Belizean tax if executed offshore.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Medium-High for non-Belizean digital EMI platforms but with material legal and de-risking constraints. Legal constraints: (1) Under the IBC Act, an IBC may maintain bank accounts only with institutions licensed by the Central Bank of Belize or with a 'recognized offshore bank'; however, the IBC Act was amended to provide flexibility in relation to keeping accounts and records outside Belize. As a practical matter, the registered agent and directors must consult the current IBC Act section 107 (records and accounts) to ensure compliance if an offshore EMI is used. (2) The target payment rails include credit card and SWIFT wire; most high-volume B2B MRO buyers prefer standard correspondent bank routes, not EMI virtual accounts. (3) Many reputable EMIs (e.g., Mercury, Airwallex, Currenxie) are unwilling to open for Belize IBCs due to negative FATF perception, beneficial ownership transparency requirements, and correspondent banking de-risking. (4) An unlicensed EMI operating without a Belize banking licence cannot be the sole banking channel; it should be used only as an ancillary receivable collection tool. Recommended approach: maintain a primary tier-1 Belize commercial bank account for SWIFT settlement, and use a licensed foreign EMI only for small-value card collections if the EMI's onboarding policy permits Belize IBCs. Any EMI holding funds must be a regulated MSB/EMI in its home jurisdiction with a valid license; otherwise the treasury structure fails AML-bankability assessments.
