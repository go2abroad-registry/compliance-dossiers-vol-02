Competent corporate registry

Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR), Ministry of Finance, Belize City, Belize

Governing statute

International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), Laws of Belize, Revised Edition 2020; incorporation under sections 4, 6, 7, 11 and 12; registered office and agent requirements under section 81.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form 1 – Application for Incorporation of an International Business Company, together with the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 15 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital requirement; typical authorized capital of USD 50,000 (or higher by election) may be issued as shares of no par value. No escrow or deposit account is required before incorporation; share subscription becomes valid upon allotment by the first board.

Funding & unlock path: National Fire Service fire load inspection and certification for warehouse or laydown premises; Belize Buildings Authority structural load clearance for crane outrigger foundations and heavy-lift travel corridors; Department of Labour (Factories Act) occupational clearance for crane operators, riggers and electrical installations, including 24-hour incident reporting protocols.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital requirement; typical authorized capital of USD 50,000 (or higher by election) may be issued as shares of no par value. No escrow or deposit account is required before incorporation; share subscription becomes valid upon allotment by the first board.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance — statutory portal: https://www.bts.gov.bz; customs/export compliance is administered by the Belize Customs and Excise Department via the ASYCUDA World Single Window.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-incorporated entity or its Belize-licensed registered agent must file the BTS registration application with the Belize Tax Service, supplying the certificate of incorporation or foreign charter, certified English translation, registered agent certificate, local business address, and principal officer particulars. Under the Tax Administration Act, 2019, the BTS issues a statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, a separate General Sales Tax (GST) registration number evidenced by a GST Registration Certificate. A Belize International Business Company (IBC) is registered under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), is exempt from the income-tax base, but must obtain a TIN for economic-substance filings and withholding purposes, and must register for GST if it makes taxable supplies exceeding the statutory threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-incorporated entity or its Belize-licensed registered agent must file the BTS registration application with the Belize Tax Service, supplying the certificate of incorporation or foreign charter, certified English translation, registered agent certificate, local business address, and principal officer particulars. Under the Tax Administration Act, 2019, the BTS issues a statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, a separate General Sales Tax (GST) registration number evidenced by a GST Registration Certificate. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No domestic check-the-box election exists in Belize. An IBC may, however, formally elect to be governed under the Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55) through notice to the Belize Tax Service, thereby becoming subject to entity-level income and business tax. Under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act, 2019, and the International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Amendment Act, 2019, a tax-exempt IBC carrying on a relevant activity — which may include freight arrangement and distribution or headquarter services within an integrated project-cargo logistics group — must either disclose tax residence in a foreign jurisdiction or demonstrate adequate economic substance in Belize. Belize has no hybrid-mismatch election framework; BEPS Action 2 anti-hybrid principles apply only to the limited extent adopted in domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 (Belize dollars) of annual taxable supplies; GST registration is mandatory for any person supplying goods or services in Belize whose aggregate taxable turnover in any twelve-month period exceeds BZD 75,000. A foreign logistics principal with no Belize establishment is not registrable unless it makes taxable supplies in Belize; however, importation of goods triggers GST at the point of entry regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods genuinely exported from Belize under the General Sales Tax Act (removal from Belize pursuant to a contract of sale or supply, evidenced by proof of physical departure), and to services physically rendered to a non-resident for use entirely outside Belize. International freight forwarding, ocean heavy-lift transportation, and out-of-gauge (OOG) project-cargo arrangement services supplied to non-resident clients for carriage wholly outside Belize qualify as zero-rated supplies. The taxpayer must retain contemporaneous evidence that the cargo was laden aboard an ocean vessel and cleared Belize Customs; where logistics milestones are performed overseas, charter-party contracts, port survey reports, and overseas customs-entry documentation must demonstrate that the supply has no use in Belize. Domestic ancillary services such as Belize stevedoring or storage supplied for the account of a Belize resident remain standard-rated at 12.5%.

  • Validated Customs Export Declaration processed and risk-cleared in ASYCUDA World
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list showing OOG dimensions and weight
  • Bill of Lading — through, NVOCC, or charter-party bill of lading — evidencing lading and destination port outside Belize
  • Cargo manifest and heavy-lift route survey or stowage plan for project cargo
  • Certificate of shipment or customs loading certificate evidencing departure from Belize waters
  • Transport and insurance documentation, including charter party contract and P&I or ICC cargo certificates
  • GST return schedule listing zero-rated supplies by invoice number
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank foreign-currency credit advice evidencing payment by the overseas principal

Belize does not currently operate a mandatory real-time continuous-transaction-control e-invoicing regime or tax-authority digital-stamping clearance mandate. GST tax invoices must show the supplier's GST number, date, description of supply, consideration, and GST charged, and must be issued within 30 days of the supply. Returns are filed electronically through the BTS e-portal under the Tax Administration Act, 2019. All export and import declarations must be processed through ASYCUDA World, the Belize Customs Single Window, which applies automated risk assessment and duty/GST calculation; the zero-rated export declaration must be validated by Customs prior to filing any refund claim.

Under the General Sales Tax Act, a false zero-rated return or recovery of GST without actual export constitutes an offense punishable by fines of up to BZD 10,000 and imprisonment, in addition to assessment of unpaid GST with interest at the statutory rate and late-payment surcharges of 10% plus 2% per month. Knowingly falsifying a customs declaration engages the Customs and Excise Duty Act penalties, including forfeiture, penalties of up to three times the value of the goods, and criminal sanctions. Retrospective clawbacks of input tax credits and zero-rating apply where the Belize Tax Service re-determines the place of supply or the fact of export within the extended limitation period. The economic-substance regime adds mandatory audit reporting for IBCs, with fines of up to USD 25,000 plus daily penalties and potential removal of the IBC's tax-exempt status. The 180-day repatriation requirement is enforced through the Exchange Control Regulations; failure to repatriate export proceeds may result in foreign-exchange penalties, denial of zero-rated input credits, and reversal of the tax exemption.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 under a currency-board-style arrangement administered by the CBB, with statutory commitment via the Currency Act. For the non-resident IBC treasury entity, current-account transactions (trade settlement, service fees) are freely convertible through authorized dealers within the BZD domestic system; capital-account transactions (equity injections, intercompany loans) require CBB Exchange Control approval only where BZD conversion or onshore utilization occurs. The IBC's external USD account sits outside the domestic conversion net, allowing unrestricted accumulation and repatriation of USD; no dividend tax or surrender mandate applies to profit remittance for a tax-exempt IBC. Practical hurdles are bank-level, not exchange-control-level: beneficiary UBO (ultimate beneficial owner) disclosure, source-of-funds evidence, and correspondent-bank clearance of USD wires routed via New York clearing correspondents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act and CBB Notices, resident export-payment recipients must offer proceeds for sale to an authorized dealer within six months (goods and related logistics services) unless retention approval is granted. This IBC qualifies for the non-resident external-account channel: proceeds physically credited to a designated external foreign-currency account at a Belize commercial bank are exempt from mandatory conversion to BZD, provided the underlying project-cargo contracts are executed offshore and invoiced in USD. If the IBC invoices a Belize-domiciled P&I club, port authority, or Belizean registered entity, the accrued proceeds are deemed domestic export receipts and become subject to the six-month surrender/retention-approval regime; mitigation is to route all Belize-cost disbursements through the parent's Paying Agent in the contractor's home jurisdiction.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), acting as the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act, Cap. 40 and the Central Bank of Belize Act, Cap. 262 of the Laws of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low — Belize has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime; the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, Cap. 142 prohibits unlicensed deposit-taking and would treat a foreign EMI's virtual account as illegal deposit-taking where advertised to Belize residents. For the non-resident IBC, a foreign EMI (for example, Wise Business or Global Reach) may be used as a billing/payout adjunct for subcontractor payments in EUR/GBP, but it cannot function as the primary corporate account or hold the Project Capex Escrow. The CBB's domestic payment system (SWIFT BIC CBBLBZBE, RTGS via CBB) is banking-license-only; therefore, treasury legal opinion must confirm that no EMI account is named as a beneficiary on the parent's equity-injection wire, to avoid an exchange-control reporting mismatch.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Wire protocol for the initial and staged equity capital injection: parent/shareholder instructs SWIFT MT103 from the home bank to the Belize bank's New York correspondent (Fedwire/CHIPS) for credit to the IBC's USD external account. MT103 field 50K must show the parent's legal name; field 59 must match exactly the IBC's beneficial-account name; field 70 (Remittance Information) must contain the transaction reference, the Belize IBC registration number (e.g., IBC/2025/00123), and a purpose code — 'EQTS' (equity) or 'GITC' (intercompany trade/general) for capital injections; 'ITCL' (intercompany loan) with the loan-agreement reference for shareholder debt. Supporting verification: original executed board resolution approving the shares/loan, a certificate of good standing from CCAR, the signed loan or subscription agreement, and an Exchange Control authorization letter from the CBB if the injection will be converted to BZD for local port/pilotage payments. All incoming wires must show full intermediary fee protocol 'SHA' (shared) and the instruction 'BENEFICIARY BANK: SWIFT/BIC CODE X BELIZE, ACCOUNT NO: X, FOR FURTHER CREDIT TO BZ IBC' to avoid correspondent misrouting; the IBC's own account cannot receive via an EMI shell.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for milestone disbursements to subcontractors, port/terminal authorities, and OOG equipment suppliers. The issuing bank is a tier-1 Belize commercial bank, settling USD through the New York clearing correspondent (typically Bank of America, JPMorgan, or Citibank), presenting by 2:30 PM ET for same-day execution and T+1 value. For high-value or capital-intensive purchases, use an irrevocable, confirmed at-sight Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700, subject to UCP600) with the Belize bank as issuing bank and a confirming bank in the supplier's jurisdiction; the LC must require presentation of: original bill of lading indorsed to the project's ultimate consignee, an OOG/heavy-lift pre-carriage receipt, the Commercial Invoice referencing the stage-gate milestone, and a certificate of logistics completion signed by the IBC's superintendent. Where the counterparty is a freight forwarder, a standby LC (SWIFT MT760) may be issued as a performance guarantee, with an automatic reduction clause tied to each milestone acceptance. All outbound dollars must leave solely from the external USD account; no BZD conversion is permitted absent a formal CBB application.Outright forward contracts in USD/EUR, USD/GBP, and USD/MXN executed offshore under ISDA 2002 terms with a top-tier international bank to fix tender-currency exposure on heavy-haul trailer/equipment procurement; settled gross in the external USD account, with no BZD leg and no CBB approval required.Non-deliverable forwards (NDF) on USD/CNY and USD/MXN referencing the official BZD peg indirectly (US dollar cleared), used for freight and axle-weight equipment purchases from Chinese and Mexican suppliers; permitted under the offshore treasury policy, provided the NDF documentation identifies the IBC as a non-resident external-account holder and the settlement bank is not part of the Belize domestic system.Cross-currency swap (USD vs EUR, USD vs GBP) matched to the project's milestone payment windows for capex procurement (port cranes, line-haul frames and modular trailers); executed offshore, the swap is a plain-vanilla floating-for-floating or fixed-for-floating, and is documented as a hedge of the project-cargo contract's net payment flows.Zero-premium collar (participating forward) over USD/EUR with 6–12 month tenor aligned to the outbound trade disbursement schedule; the premium is capitalized into the facility's all-in cost, and the surface document is governed by English law with a non-Belize governing substantive law clause to ensure settlement remains enforceable outside the Exchange Control net.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% on worldwide income for a Belize International Business Company (IBC) under the IBC Act (Cap. 270); the domestic corporate income tax headline rate under the Income Tax Act is 25%, but IBCs are expressly excluded from the charge to income tax and business tax; the only recurring state levy is the annual IFSC license fee payable to the International Financial Services Commission.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders; the IBC Act wholly exempts such distributions from Belize income tax and withholding tax. For domestic Belize companies, the Income Tax Act imposes a 15% withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents, which serves as the comparator rate if the IBC exemption is lost.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return and annual license fee are due on or before the last day of the calendar month following the anniversary month of incorporation (an IBC incorporated on 15 January 2026 must file and pay by 28 February 2026); the Form ESR-1 must be filed within 90 days of the end of the financial period, and in any event before the annual fee deadline of the following year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Belize does not yet codify OECD-style Local File and Master File documentation for IBCs; the practice benchmark applied by the IFSC and the Belize Tax Service is BZ$ 1,000,000 of annual intra-group transactions, above which the taxpayer must maintain a contemporaneous arm's-length file covering a master file, local file, and transfer pricing benchmark study. Documentation must be produced within 30 days of a BTSD or IFSC audit notice.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act 2020 and the IBC (Amendment) Act 2020 impose an OECD-aligned Economic Substance Doctrine on every IBC. Where the IBC undertakes a relevant activity, it must demonstrate in Belize: (1) management and direction through board meetings and strategic decisions; (2) core income-generating activities performed in Belize; (3) adequate physical premises; (4) full-time employees proportionate to activity; and (5) adequate operating expenditure. An ISIC 5229 project cargo entity is typically evaluated as a distribution and service centre, financing and leasing entity, or intellectual property holder, unless it owns and operates vessels, which would classify it as shipping; a purely offshore arranger with no Belize presence must file an out-of-scope ESR declaration and cannot attribute Belize-source logistics income to the exempt shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (false IBC exemption claim)

Statutory sanction: Reassessment under the Income Tax Act and Business Tax Act at the 25% corporate rate on undeclared Belize-source profit; late-payment interest at the statutory rate; a fraud penalty of up to treble the tax evaded; and criminal prosecution for willful evasion with fines of BZ$ 5,000 to BZ$ 50,000.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for fraudulent declarations: directors and controlling shareholders are jointly and severally liable for the reassessed taxes, may be prevented from leaving Belize by a departure prohibition certificate issued by the Tax Commissioner, and face imprisonment up to 2 years for knowingly certifying a false return.
Shell Company / Economic Substance failure

Statutory sanction: Economic substance penalty of up to BZ$ 10,000 per infraction plus a continuing daily penalty until compliance; the IFSC may strike the company off the register; the Belize Tax Service may deem the company tax-resident in Belize and assess worldwide income at 25%; loss of the 0% exemption is automatic.

Director personal liability: Directors who file a false ESR declaration are personally liable to the IFSC and to the company; where the shell arrangement diverts freight revenues to defeat creditors, courts will lift the corporate veil, order restitution of siphoned milestone proceeds, and issue travel bans against the directors and the ultimate beneficial owner.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act: on conviction on indictment, a fine of up to BZ$ 500,000 and/or imprisonment up to 15 years; asset freezing orders by the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit; forfeiture of cargo equipment, bank accounts, and freight proceeds connected to the offence.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for money laundering predicates within their knowledge; failure to report suspicious transactions carries a personal fine of up to BZ$ 100,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; after criminal conviction, civil restitution to the defrauded shipper or financing bank is enforced against personal assets of the directors.
Fraudulent Trading / False Accounting

Statutory sanction: Fraudulent trading liability under the IBC Act and Belize insolvency law: directors may be declared personally liable for the company's debts where business was carried on with intent to defraud creditors; false accounting is punishable by a fine of up to BZ$ 50,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years; ancillary sanctions include confiscation of contract proceeds and disqualification from managing any Belize IBC for up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced automatically on proof of fraud: each director and shadow director must make full civil restitution of diverted funds with penalty interest; the courts may impose a departure prohibition order until restitution is satisfied; convicted officers face imprisonment up to 3 years and forfeiture of vessels and heavy-lift equipment registered in the IBC's name.
