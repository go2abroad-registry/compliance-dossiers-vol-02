Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A mail-forwarding-only virtual office is not acceptable as the statutory registered office. Section 22 of the IBC Act, Cap. 270 requires a physical street address in Belize maintained at the premises of a licensed registered agent, where the statutory records and UBO register are kept. A virtual commercial address is permissible only as an auxiliary communication address and never as a substitute for the statutory office. Because this ISIC 5229 reverse-logistics entity will hold no local business license and will not conduct domestic business in Belize, the registered agent's physical office fully satisfies the commercial-substance requirement.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; e-Services Portal at https://taxservices.gov.bz. Customs matters are handled separately by the Belize Customs and Excise Department via the ASYCUDA World electronic platform.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Belize IBC first obtains its certificate of incorporation and registered office from the International Business Companies Registry (Registrar of IBCs, Ministry of Finance). To obtain a tax identification number (TIN), the IBC must file Form TIN-001 with the Belize Tax Service, providing the IBC certificate, articles of incorporation, registered office details, and proposed activities. If the IBC intends to make local taxable supplies or wishes to claim input VAT/GST credits, it must separately complete GST registration (Form GST-01) and meet the turnover threshold or voluntarily register. For non-resident IBCs conducting only offshore business, the tax registration is typically informational and the entity remains tax-exempt under the IBC Act.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. An IBC is statutorily exempt from all Belize taxes under the International Business Companies Act, and there is no hybrid mismatch election available. The only relevant election is a voluntary GST registration under the General Sales Tax Act if the IBC chooses to engage in local taxable activities, which would forfeit its exempt status for those supplies. No other entity-level classification election exists.

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to the supply of goods that are physically exported from Belize to a non-resident purchaser within the prescribed time frame, provided the exporter retains proof of export, the goods leave Belize under a valid customs export declaration, and consideration is paid in foreign currency. Certain internationally supplied services consumed entirely outside Belize may also be zero-rated or treated as outside the scope of Belize GST. The IBC must have no local supply nexus in Belize and must satisfy the customs officer that the goods were not diverted for local use.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA Goods Declaration) fully processed and approved
  • Commercial Invoice or Tax Invoice issued to the non-resident buyer
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical movement out of Belize
  • Packing list and container/manifest documentation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign-currency payment evidence from the buyer
  • Proof of repatriation or foreign-currency bank statement matching the export transaction

Belize does not impose a real-time e-invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate for GST purposes. Taxpayers must file periodic GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Service e-Services portal. Export declarations are lodged electronically through the ASYCUDA World customs management system, with digital submission of supporting documents and customs clearance evidence. No digital stamping or continuous transaction controls are currently required.

The Belize Tax Service may conduct export verification audits and claw back zero-rated benefits if export documentation is missing, false, or incomplete. Penalties include additional GST of up to 200% of the tax evaded, civil penalties under the Tax Administration and Appeals Act, interest accruing from the due date, and disqualification from zero-rating status. The Customs and Excise Act imposes fines and potential imprisonment for false declarations. An IBC that engages in unlicensed local business or carries on prohibited activities may also lose its tax-exempt status and be struck off under the IBC Act, with retroactive taxation of deemed local supplies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize operates a fixed-rate regime with the Belize dollar pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 and full convertibility for non-residents in current and capital transactions. A Belize IBC is exempt from the Exchange Control Act under the International Business Companies Act, Chapter 270, so capital injections, commercial trade payments, refunds, and profit repatriation may be effected in USD or any foreign currency without CBB surrender, conversion, or exit approval. Dividends and capital distributions to non-resident shareholders are not subject to withholding tax provided the IBC maintains its exempt status, pays its annual licence fee, and avoids transacting with Belize residents or deriving Belize-source income. Residual hurdles: any accidental domestic nexus (BZD receipts, Belize-resident vendors, local warehousing for Belize customers) re-engages the Exchange Control Regulations and income-tax exposure; newly formed capital-heavy IBCs may face CBB-authorised dealer scrutiny of source of funds in excess of ordinary trade volume.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations (Cap. 264, subsidiary legislation), resident exporters of goods and services must offer the full export proceeds to an authorised dealer within the statutory period, in practice six months from the date of export, and the mandatory default conversion ratio is 100% of the proceeds unless the CBB grants a retention or exemption. This regime does not apply to a Belize IBC: the IBC Act's exchange-control exemption allows the company to retain agency commissions, reverse-logistics refunds, quality-grading fees, and other foreign-currency receipts indefinitely in a USD or multi-currency account without conversion, surrender, or CBB authorisation. If funds are mistakenly credited to the IBC in BZD, they should be rejected or rebated to the remitter, because conversion and retention of BZD could be treated by the bank as evidence of domestic business activity and compromise the exempt status.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 263 of the Laws of Belize (Revised Edition 2020); the Minister of Finance and the CBB jointly administer the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 264, and the Exchange Control Regulations. Domestic commercial banks are CBB-authorised dealers, while offshore and international banks and IBC registered agents are supervised by the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC). The domestic retail clearing system is operated by the Belize Clearing House ACH (BACH).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW for digital EMIs (Airwallex, Wise Business, Payoneer) because Belize IBCs are classified by most principal banks and EMIs as offshore zero-tax structures subject to automatic enhanced due diligence. Belize law does not prohibit an IBC from using a foreign EMI, and no CBB approval is needed for non-resident digital clearing; however, the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing Act obliges the IBC's registered agent to maintain and disclose beneficial ownership to the Financial Intelligence Unit on request, which mirrors the UBO disclosure any EMI will demand. Airwallex onboarding realistically requires 6-12 months of clean transaction history at a tier-1 Belize bank, contracts evidencing a genuine reverse-logistics business with identified marketplaces or 3PL counterparties, annual turnover within the EMI's mid-tier band, economic-substance documentation or a nil-return filing under the Economic Substance Act 2019, and a complete prohibition of BZD or Belize-resident flows through the EMI. Best practice: the Belize bank account acts as the anchor treasury account, with Airwallex as an auxiliary disbursement rail for marketplace refunds and grading fees.

0% for dividends declared by an exempt IBC, s.6(1) of the IBC Act waiving all taxes on distributions. As a comparator, the domestic statutory withholding rate on dividends paid by a Belize resident company to a non-resident shareholder is 15% under the IBTA, subject to treaty relief.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No express transfer-pricing documentation threshold is codified in Belize law and no BZ$ Local File or Master File trigger attaches to the exempt IBC as a tax filing obligation. However, the IFSC may require demonstration of arm's length pricing for intra-group reverse-logistics transactions during economic substance review, and the Comptroller of Income Tax may apply IBTA anti-avoidance and reallocation powers to adjust non-arm's length related-party transactions involving any Belize taxable party without a de minimis threshold.

Economic substance scope: The Belize IBC is subject to the Economic Substance Regulations 2019 issued under the IBC Act and the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act. Cross-border return-merchandise logistics and returned-goods grading constitute a distribution and service centre relevant activity. The IBC must demonstrate annually: (i) direction and management in Belize, including board meetings and strategic decisions; (ii) core income-generating activities performed in Belize, covering contract negotiation, inventory management, quality grading and refund authorisation; (iii) adequate and proportionate local workforce, premises and expenditure; and (iv) no outsourcing of all core functions to foreign affiliates. Alternatively, the IBC may declare tax residency in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction with full tax payment there, evidenced in the annual ESR return. Failure to file the ESR return is itself a compliance breach.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Prohibited Onshore Trading

Statutory sanction: Voidance of the prohibited transactions; assessment of the IBC on the full Belize-source profit at the 25% corporate rate with default interest and penalties; criminal fine and imprisonment under the IBTA for willful evasion; and strike-off by the IFSC for breach of the IBC Act.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who caused or permitted the prohibited domestic business are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and civil restitution, are exposed to disqualification from managing Belize companies, and face imprisonment for tax evasion and false declarations.
Economic Substance Failure (Shell Company)

Statutory sanction: Under the ESR Regulations 2019 and the IBC Act, penalties up to BZ$20,000 per compliance failure plus daily default fines for continued non-compliance; the IFSC may apply to court for strike-off; company data is mandatorily exchanged with the OECD Forum on Harmful Tax Practices.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false economic substance declarations are personally liable to fines and imprisonment; the licensed Belize registered agent is subject to sanction including licence revocation; controlling individuals face travel restrictions and asset freezes upon court order.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines and imprisonment under the Beneficial Ownership Act and the Anti-Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act; civil forfeiture and freezing orders issued by the Financial Intelligence Unit; administrative penalties and potential strike-off for beneficial ownership filing failures.

Director personal liability: The MLRO, directors and de facto beneficial owners face personal criminal liability, director disqualification, travel bans and asset-seizure orders; any contractual indemnity from the company is unenforceable against AML penalties.
Fraudulent Trading and Misrepresentation to the Registrar

Statutory sanction: Misrepresentation to the Belize Registrar or IFSC is a criminal offence under the IBC Act punishable by fines and imprisonment; fraudulent refund conduct constitutes criminal fraud with restitution orders, strike-off and potential forfeiture of the IBC's assets held in Belize.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: directors and shadow directors who directed the fraudulent scheme are personally liable for civil restitution to defrauded counterparties, are subject to travel bans, disqualification and custodial sentences, and assets transferred out of the IBC may be clawed back as preferences or transactions at undervalue.
