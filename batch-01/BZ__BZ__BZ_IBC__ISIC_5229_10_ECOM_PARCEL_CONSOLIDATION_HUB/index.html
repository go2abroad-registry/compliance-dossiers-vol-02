Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance and Economic Development (official portal: www.taxservice.gov.bz); Belize Customs and Excise Department for export/import clearance under the Customs and Excise Duty Act, Cap. 48, operating the ASYCUDA World electronic customs platform.

E-government filing portal

A Belize International Business Company (IBC) incorporated under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270 (IBC Act) is statutorily exempt from all Belize taxes, including income tax, capital gains tax, stamp duties, and General Sales Tax (GST). Consequently, a pure offshore IBC parcel consolidation hub does not apply for a domestic TIN or VAT/GST registration number; its statutory identifier is the Certificate of Incorporation number issued by the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR). If the IBC breaches its offshore status by making taxable supplies in Belize, it must apply to the Tax Service Department for a TIN and file GST registration within 14 days of crossing the BZD 50,000 registration threshold. Foreign-entity branches and local trading entities follow the ordinary TIN procurement route via Form TSD-IN01, supported by incorporation documents, proof of registered office, and a resident agent appointment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize does not operate a check-the-box pass-through election and has no hybrid mismatch election. The only available statutory mechanism is re-domiciliation under section 148 of the IBC Act, whereby an IBC may elect to migrate to or from another jurisdiction, or be struck off and re-registered as an ordinary resident Belize company, thereby forfeiting exempt status; no other entity-level tax elections exist under Cap. 270.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 50,000 annual taxable turnover; non-resident persons with no fixed establishment in Belize are outside the GST net unless they make taxable supplies exceeding the threshold in Belize, which is prohibited for an IBC under the IBC Act.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the GST Act, Cap. 64, exports of goods are zero-rated where the goods are physically exported from Belize in bond or under customs control within the statutory period, and the exporter retains proof of removal; services are zero-rated only if performed wholly outside Belize or on foreign goods. A cross-border parcel consolidation hub supplying international sorting, direct-injection linehaul, and freight forwarding services to non-resident e-commerce shippers renders services offshore and therefore out of scope of Belize GST entirely; a pure IBC does not claim zero-rating because its supplies carry no Belize VAT/GST liability.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry / outward manifest)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (through or interline bill covering the consolidated parcel hub legs)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / foreign-currency credit evidence (bank wiring record of export proceeds)
  • Commercial Invoice and packing list with HS classification for sorting machinery and linehaul equipment
  • Consolidated cargo manifest detailing non-resident consignor and consignee identities

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory e-invoicing API, or digital stamping regime is currently implemented in Belize. Electronic invoicing is permitted commercially on a voluntary basis, but taxpayers must retain legible records (paper or PDF) for six years for audit purposes. Customs export clearance, however, is mandatory through the ASYCUDA World system, requiring electronic filing of export entries and outward manifests before departure of the aircraft or vessel carrying consolidated parcels.

A pure Belize IBC is exempt from exchange-control repatriation obligations (Exchange Control Act, Cap. 34 exemption under IBC Act, section 6), so the 180-day documentation deadline operates as a customs/banking evidence window rather than a strict federal repatriation mandate. Fraudulent zero-rating claims or misuse of export documentation under the GST Act attract civil penalties up to double the tax evaded, monthly interest surcharges, suspension of the export registration, and criminal fines with imprisonment terms. Where an IBC is found to have transacted with Belize residents or generated local-source income in breach of the IBC Act, it forfeits exempt status, becomes retroactively liable to income tax and GST for the full audit period, and faces clawback of all refunds plus fines not exceeding BZD 10,000 under the IBC Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The BZD is pegged to the USD at 2.00 BZD = 1.00 USD, a hard peg in force since May 1976. The subject IBC is statutorily deemed non-resident and is wholly exempt from the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 55) under Section 6(1) of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270). Current-account convertibility is therefore effectively unrestricted, and capital-account operations (equity injection, dividend declaration, shareholder loans, cross-border settlement) require no Central Bank exchange-control license, no prior approval, and no surrender obligation. Profit remittance is not subject to Belize withholding tax on interest, dividends, or royalties paid to non-residents, and the IBC itself is exempt from the Income and Business Tax Act. The only practical convertibility hurdles are correspondent-bank de-risking, bank-imposed documentary proof of underlying parcel-consolidation trade, and FATCA/CRS transparency filings. The Entity may hold multi-currency balances indefinitely without any conversion obligation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable to the Entity. For domestic Belizean resident exporters, the Exchange Control Act and authorized-dealer practice require repatriation of export proceeds and surrender to an authorized dealer within a prescribed period at the official pegged rate. However, Section 6(1) of the IBC Act wholly exempts IBCs from the Exchange Control Act; consequently there is no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for receivables arising from parcel sorting, consolidation, and direct-injection linehaul services. All USD-invoiced fee proceeds may be retained in USD-denominated accounts, and BZD conversion should occur only to fund actual local disbursements such as port charges, customs duties, and fuel.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established and governed by the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262, Laws of Belize, Revised Edition 2020; sole monetary authority for the Belize Dollar (BZD) and prudential supervisor of all authorized dealers, commercial banks, and international banks).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW with conditional acceptance. Airwallex, as the named preferred rail, can accept a Belize IBC only after heightened manual review and requires underlying logistics master agreements evidencing genuine parcel-hub activity; Payoneer and Wise apply similar or stricter jurisdiction filters. Belize has no domestic e-money institution or PSP licensing regime, so foreign EMIs operate extra-territorially without Central Bank of Belize authorization; this is not statutorily prohibited for an IBC but triggers enhanced due diligence at any Belize commercial bank serving as the master treasury account. The recommended treasury architecture is: a Belize Bank (or Atlantic Bank) USD master account as the principal liquidity node; Airwallex as a controlled sub-settlement rail for high-frequency carrier payouts; and the local clearing system for BZD disbursements. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: direct SWIFT MT103 in USD from the contributor's home jurisdiction to the IBC's Belize Bank (or Atlantic Bank) USD account with remittance narrative 'CAPITAL INJECTION - SHARE SUBSCRIPTION [IBC NAME]'. Because the IBC is exempt from the Exchange Control Act, no Central Bank license, exchange-control form, or surrender obligation attaches to the inbound wire. The receiving bank maps the remittance to its prescribed purpose code for a capital or investment transfer and requires narrative consistency with the board resolution, subscription agreement, register of members, and share certificate. Verification package: executed capital-call resolution, source-of-funds bank letter, contributor's passport, and a treasury source-and-use statement. For the parcel hub, a prudent injection is 6-8 weeks of operating float, segment-normal USD 150,000-300,000, with no forced conversion to BZD; convert only where BZD-denominated customs, port, and fuel disbursements substantiate the need.Primary rail: SWIFT MT103 direct credits for high-frequency operational payments below USD 25,000 to courier aggregators, last-mile carriers, customs brokers, and fuel suppliers, all invoiced and settled in USD to preserve the 2-to-1 peg margin. Secondary rail: commercial letters of credit, sight or usance, irrevocable and confirmed, issued by Belize Bank for annual high-value linehaul and capacity contracts, confirmed by a US correspondent such as JPMorgan Chase or Citibank, payable against transport documents including house air waybills, delivery confirmation logs, consolidated sorting manifests, and customs clearance entries. Tertiary rail: Airwallex USD wallet, controlled under the Belize treasury policy, for foreign small-carrier payouts and direct-injection linehaul providers, with local-clearing rail used only for BZD port, customs, and fuel payments settled through the Belize Bank central ledger. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an IBC (permanent statutory exemption); the general Belize resident company rate under the Income and Business Tax Act is 25%

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No domestic Belize TP documentation threshold applies to an IBC because it is outside the ambit of the Income and Business Tax Act. Compliance will be driven by the jurisdictions of the e-commerce group: master file / local file obligations commonly attach where group revenue exceeds BZD-equivalent thresholds (e.g., US parent: USD 25M; UK parent: GBP 20M group revenue). For the Belize hub, contemporaneous intercompany agreements for linehaul/consolidation fees should be economically justified against the 5 approved OECD methods.

Economic substance scope: Belize's Economic Substance Act (as amended in 2022) applies where an entity is tax resident in Belize and carries on a 'relevant activity'. Pure cross-border parcel consolidation, freight forwarding, and sorting are NOT listed relevant activities; however, a hub performing 'distribution and service centre' functions for the group, or holding IP, will be caught. Such entities must be directed and managed in Belize, maintain premises, staff, and expenditure proportionate to the activity, and file an annual economic substance return within 12 months of the financial year-end. Even if not caught, every IBC must now file a nil substance notification to the Belize IFSC; failure attracts penalties of BZD 25,000-200,000 and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Prohibited domestic activity / loss of tax-exempt status

Statutory sanction: IBC Act s.3: the company is deemed to have breached its charter; the Registrar may refuse renewal, strike off the company, and impose fines of up to BZD 10,000 per contravention; the tax exemption is void ab initio, exposing profits to 25% income and business tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the transaction are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid Belize taxes and penalties; a court may order restitution of profits and disqualification from managing any company in Belize.
Annual renewal default / administrative strike-off

Statutory sanction: Struck off the Belize Register; all corporate powers cease; assets vest in the Registrar as custodian; restoration requires a court order plus all outstanding fees, a late penalty of USD 100-500, and a restoration filing fee.

Director personal liability: Post-strike-off contracts render directors personally liable; continued trading while struck off constitutes fraudulent conduct with potential criminal fines and imprisonment of up to 5 years.
AML / shell-company and record-keeping offences

Statutory sanction: Anti-Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism Act: corporate fines up to BZD 500,000 and forfeiture of the benefit; the Companies Registry can also file a charge on the IBC and suspend its license for a period.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners can be prosecuted; maximum penalties include 5 years' imprisonment per offence, travel bans, and confiscation of personal assets.
Home-jurisdiction CFC/PFIC tax evasion

Statutory sanction: US: 75% civil fraud penalty on the underpayment plus FBAR penalty up to USD 100,000 or 50% of the account; UK: up to 200% of tax under Follower Notices; the Belize entity may also be treated as a purely artificial arrangement and disregarded/orphaned.

Director personal liability: Shareholders/directors face criminal tax evasion, imprisonment, and travel restrictions; intermediaries who assisted in the sham face extraterritorial criminal exposure under U.S. IRS / UK HMRC voidness doctrines.
Sanctions, export control, and unlicensed payment intermediation

Statutory sanction: OFAC strict-liability civil penalties up to USD 20 million per transaction; Belize exchange-control/AML sanctions include asset freezes and criminal seizure; false declarations to customs trigger criminal forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors who consciously disregard red flags are individually liable for OFAC violations; on conviction in Belize, a term of imprisonment of up to 5 years may be imposed together with a personal fine of up to BZD 250,000.
