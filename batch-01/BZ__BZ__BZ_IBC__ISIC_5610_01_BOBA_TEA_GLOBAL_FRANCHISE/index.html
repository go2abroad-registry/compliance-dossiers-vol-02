Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize); self-service portal: https://taxbelize.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities forming a Belize International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) are not required to obtain a local VAT/TIN for offshore activities; IBCs by definition are tax-exempt outside Belize and prohibited from conducting business with residents. Should the entity elect in-country taxable activity, it must file Form GST-01 with the Tax Service Department, provide certificate of incorporation, registered office consent, and evidence of physical premises or local agent, after which the Department assigns a GST registration number. Non-resident franchise operators supplying intangible services into Belize may also be required to register for GST if their annual taxable supplies to Belize exceed the statutory threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists. An IBC automatically qualifies for exemption from all Belize income and business taxes under section 7(1) of the IBC Act. The entity may irrevocably waive IBC status (electing to become a Belizean domestic ordinary company) by filing Form IBC-5, thereby becoming subject to domestic CIT and GST regimes. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available under Belizean law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 (annual taxable turnover) — mandatory registration threshold; supplies of exported goods and certain offshore services are zero-rated or exempt.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GST zero-rating applies to (i) the export of goods out of Belize; (ii) supply of services physically performed entirely outside Belize; (iii) international transport services; and (iv) supplies of intellectual property or know-how to non-resident licensees where the benefit of the supply is outside Belize. For a boba tea global franchise IBC licensing intellectual property and selling syrup concentrate to overseas franchisees, royalty payments and syrup exports will qualify for 0% GST provided the goods are physically exported and the services are rendered outside Belize and not consumed in Belize.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form C-2) stamped by Belize Customs and Excise Department
  • Signed Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Belize
  • Commercial Invoice showing purchaser as non-resident and zero-rated basis
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire confirmatory proof of receipt of export proceeds
  • License Agreement (for royalty zero-rating) with provision showing delivery of IP to foreign franchisee

No real-time fiscal clearance or government mandated e-invoice API exists. GST-registered businesses may issue manual or electronic invoices, but must issue a tax invoice containing prescribed particulars (seller name, GST number, invoice date, description, TIN of buyer, tax exclusive value, tax amount, and total). No digital stamping requirement is currently enforced.

Under the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 287), a taxpayer who fraudulently claims zero-rating on non-exported goods or fictitious supplies is liable to a penalty of 200% of the tax evaded, plus interest, and criminal fines up to BZD 10,000 or imprisonment of up to 2 years. The Tax Service Department may retrospectively claw back input tax credits claimed in connection with zero-rated outputs if the exporter fails to provide the required export documentation within the 180-day repatriation window, and cancellation of GST registration may follow.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is legally pegged to the U.S. dollar at BZD 2 : USD 1. Belize maintains selective exchange control: current-account payments for trade, royalties, and services are generally permitted and cleared by authorized dealers, subject to documentary, anti-money-laundering, and sanctions screening. Capital-account transactions involving residents require Central Bank approval. A validly registered International Business Company is treated as non-resident for exchange-control purposes if its activities and income are outside Belize; therefore capital injection and profit or royalty remittance should not be denied on convertibility grounds. In practice, Belizean banks will require an underlying contract, board resolution, source-of-funds evidence, UBO declaration, and OFAC/correspondent-bank clearance before any large outward or inward transfer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Belize Exchange Control Regulations, a resident exporter must receive export proceeds through an authorized dealer in the currency of invoice and surrender/offer the proceeds for conversion to Belize dollars within 90 days of exportation, unless a specific retention exemption is granted. The mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of foreign currency receipted where no exemption applies. A Belize IBC that does not carry on business in Belize, does not receive export proceeds in the domestic banking system, and holds receivables in an approved offshore foreign-currency account is generally outside this surrender requirement. For the target boba tea franchise model, export proceeds arising from syrup supply contracts are treated as ordinary trading proceeds and should be routed through fully documented commercial invoices, transport documentation, and collection accounts to avoid any mischaracterization.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) — Exchange Control Department, acting under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 56 of the Laws of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Belize does not operate a dedicated e-money institution licensing regime. Offshore digital EMIs operating under UK, EU, or Hong Kong licenses are not supervised by the Central Bank of Belize and generally reject Belize IBC clients due to correspondent-bank de-risking and the jurisdiction's offshore-characteristics profile. Mainstream EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, and Airwallex are not viable primary clearing rails for this IBC. They may be used only as auxiliary low-value collection tools for franchise royalty aggregation if the EMI accepts Belize corporate clients after enhanced due diligence, but a Belize licensed bank account remains the principal liquidity and clearing channel.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory BZD threshold applies to tax-exempt IBCs. For companies within the charge to Belize income tax, related-party transactions above BZD 1,000,000 (approximately USD 500,000) in annual aggregate value trigger mandatory Local File / Master File documentation. An IBC engaged in franchise licensing or syrup distribution must nonetheless maintain contemporaneous arm's-length data to support its Economic Substance Regulations return.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Regulations (IFSC, Statutory Instrument 2019) apply to IBCs carrying on a Relevant Activity. The global boba tea franchise model comprises two Relevant Activities: (1) Intellectual Property holding and exploitation through franchise licensing of brand, recipes and trade secrets; and (2) Distribution and Service Centre through the syrup and concentrate supply chain. The IBC must be managed and controlled in Belize, maintain a physical office, engage adequate qualified staff, perform core income-generating activities in Belize and file an annual Economic Substance return with the IFSC; failure results in penalties, information exchange with foreign tax authorities, and potential striking-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / abuse of IBC exemption

Statutory sanction: Assessment of tax at the 25% domestic corporation rate on undeclared profits, statutory interest on arrears, penalties of up to 100–300% of the tax (Income Tax Act, Cap. 55), and criminal fines of up to BZD 10,000; the IBC exemption is void for the offending period.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly caused, permitted or were party to the evasion are jointly and severally liable for corporate tax, penalties and interest; courts may impose travel bans, disqualification from holding a Belize directorship, and imprisonment for up to 5 years.
Shell company / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Penalty of BZD 10,000 per offence plus BZD 1,000 per month for continued default under the Economic Substance Regulations; the IFSC may exchange information with treaty partners, strike off the IBC, and refer a deemed-profit assessment of up to 1.2 times gross revenues.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for ESR penalties, restitution orders and enforcement costs; where the sham vehicle misrepresents a foreign franchisee's supply chain, the corporate veil is pierced, exposing directors to civil claims in the franchise territory, asset freezes and disqualification orders.
AML/CFT and beneficial ownership failure

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalty of up to BZD 50,000 plus BZD 5,000 per day under the Beneficial Ownership Act, 2019 and the Banks and Financial Institutions Act; freeze orders and inspection by the IFSC; in money-laundering cases, confiscation of all royalty and syrup supply proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors are designated reporting persons and exposed to criminal prosecution for money laundering under the Proceeds of Crime Act with imprisonment of up to 12 years; travel bans, passport surrender and personal confiscation orders are routinely sought against non-resident directors.
Fraudulent trading / corporate veil abuse in franchise roll-out

Statutory sanction: Civil restitution under the International Business Companies Act for fraudulent and wrongful trading; the Registrar or a appointed receiver may recover company property; foreign judgments may be recognised and enforced against Belize assets; criminal fraud and conspiracy charges carry fines of up to BZD 250,000.

Director personal liability: The court may order directors to contribute personally to company assets for every debt incurred in fraudulent trading; directors face bankruptcy orders, travel restrictions, extradition under bilateral arrangements, and imprisonment for fraud of up to 10 years.
