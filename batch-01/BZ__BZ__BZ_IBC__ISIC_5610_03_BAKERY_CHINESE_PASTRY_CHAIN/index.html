E-government filing portal

Step 2: Submit to the Belize Tax Service Department the IBC incorporation documents, registered office address, corporate KYC pack, and the prescribed Tax Identification Number (TIN) application form; the TIN is issued in the name of the IBC for compliance, economic substance, and bank filing purposes. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize has no check-the-box or fiscal-transparency election equivalent to the U.S. or EU rules. Under the International Business Companies Act, an IBC may elect to surrender its IBC tax-exempt status and re-register as a domestic company under the Companies Act, thereby subjecting itself to Belize Income and Business Tax, General Sales Tax, and full tax-residency rules. No hybrid mismatch elections exist because Belize has not enacted BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration for a person whose annual value of taxable supplies in Belize exceeds BZD 100,000 (approximately USD 50,000); non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Belize are registrable from the first supply unless the supply is zero-rated; voluntary registration is available to qualifying persons

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A GST-registered supplier may zero-rate exports of goods where (i) the goods are physically removed from Belize by or on behalf of the supplier within a reasonable time, (ii) the supplier holds conclusive written and customs evidence of export, and (iii) the supply is made to a customer outside Belize. Services supplied to a non-resident for use wholly outside Belize are also zero-rated. Supplies made by a Belize IBC entirely from offshore operations are outside the scope of Belize GST because the IBC enjoys a statutory tax exemption and lacks territorial nexus with Belize; such activities do not generate Belize GST refund claims.

  • Customs Export Declaration submitted through ASYCUDA World (Customs Export Entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill, properly endorsed and dated by the carrier, showing shipment from Belize to the foreign destination
  • Original GST Tax Invoice issued in the prescribed form with the zero-rating code
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and purchase order evidencing the export transaction
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice showing receipt of export proceeds in Belize or in the IBC's recognized offshore account
  • Certificate of Incorporation / IBC certificate and registered agent letter where the exporter is an International Business Company

Belize currently has no real-time e-invoicing, continuous transaction control (CTC), or digital stamping mandate for general GST invoices. GST-registered suppliers must issue serialized GST tax invoices with the prescribed particulars (supplier name and GST registration number, customer details, unique invoice number, date, description, tax-exclusive value, GST rate, and GST amount). GST returns are filed electronically through the Tax Service Department portal. Customs declarations are lodged electronically via the ASYCUDA World single-window processing system, and physical/excisable stamps remain applicable only to specified controlled goods such as alcoholic beverages, tobacco, and fuel.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to risk-based audit by the Belize Tax Service Department and the Customs and Excise Department. The exporter must produce the full documentary bundle within 30 calendar days of a written demand; failure to do so causes the supply to be recharacterized as taxable at 12.5% GST, with statutory interest from the original due date and late-payment penalties. Deliberately false or fraudulent zero-rating documentation is a criminal offence under the General Sales Tax Act and the Customs and Excise Duty Act, punishable by fines up to BZD 10,000, imprisonment up to three years, forfeiture of goods or of the tax evaded, and retrospective clawbacks that may extend beyond the ordinary assessment limitation period. An IBC claimant is also audited for economic substance and registered-office compliance by the International Financial Services Commission; false declarations or lack of substance can lead to deregistration, asset-restraint orders, and restoration-based penalty assessments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged to the US dollar at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 and Belize has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations for current account payments; therefore dividends, royalties, interest, and current trade transactions are generally freely convertible through authorized dealers. Capital-account transactions remain subject to Exchange Control Act approval requirements, and profit remittances by a non-resident-owned International Business Company (IBC) require the presentation of audited financial statements, tax proof, and a completed application through an authorized dealer. The IBC's tax-exempt status does not waive exchange control scrutiny, and remittances must be supported by an underlying commercial reason, e.g. a signed master franchise agreement or central kitchen supply contract.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters are statutorily required to repatriate the full export invoice value to Belize within 180 days from the date of shipment. Proceeds must be surrendered to an authorized dealer and converted to Belize dollars at the prevailing pegged rate unless a prior exemption is approved by the Central Bank of Belize for retention in a foreign currency account. The practical surrender ratio is 100% of shipped goods value after deduction of explicitly allowable freight and insurance charges evidenced by a customs export declaration, bill of lading, and commercial invoice. For the Chinese pastry chain's central kitchen exports of frozen pastries and specialty ingredients, the full amount of USD export proceeds must be declared on the prescribed Exchange Control form via the collecting commercial bank.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (Exchange Control Department)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Belize has no separate electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime; only banks and credit unions licensed under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act may hold client funds or access the local clearing system. Offshore digital EMIs licensed by the IFSC or by overseas regulators cannot be used as a primary local clearing rail because they lack Belize banking licenses and cannot transfer Belize dollars into the Central Bank's clearing or settle export proceeds through an authorized dealer. Such EMIs may serve only as a secondary gateway for small-value franchise royalty payments, and any use must be supported by a fully documented flow of funds to a licensed onshore bank account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory transfer-pricing documentation threshold is codified in Belize for IBCs because they are zero-tax entities; nonetheless, multinational franchise groups with cross-border related-party flows exceeding BZ$5,000,000 (approximately US$2,500,000) annually should prepare OECD-compliant Local File and a Country-by-Country report to satisfy the tax authority of the operating jurisdiction and to defend against beneficial-ownership attribution

Economic substance scope: The Belize Economic Substance Act (2018/2019) applies to entities conducting 'relevant activities', including distribution and service centre, headquarters, financing and leasing, IP holding, and holding company operations. The bakery master franchise and central kitchen model will be characterized as a distribution and service centre or headquarters only if strategic management, contracting, and key decision-making occur in Belize; this structurally conflicts with the IBC's legal mandate to remain non-resident. A zero-substance paper IBC attracting royalty income is rebuttably presumed a shell unless franchise agreements, board resolutions, and cash-flow governance demonstrably originate from a genuine foreign operational headquarters. Belizean ESR enforcement imposes a two-stage penalty regime: first notice up to US$10,000 and second notice up to US$25,000, with automatic exchange of information to OECD jurisdictions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Prohibited Local Commerce

Statutory sanction: Immediate forfeiture of IBC status with retroactive taxation at the ordinary Belizean corporate income tax rate of 25% on all Belizean-source profits, plus interest of 9% per annum and penalties of up to 100% of the tax due under Income Tax Act Cap. 55; affected franchise contracts declared unenforceable

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; fines up to BZ$10,000, imprisonment up to 2 years for false statutory declaration; courts may issue travel bans, asset freezing orders, and warrant enforcement against directors' foreign assets via mutual legal assistance
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Assessment Notice with penalty up to US$10,000 for first failure; second failure up to US$25,000; mandatory deregistration and automatic data exchange to foreign tax authorities, exposing the group's global tax profile

Director personal liability: Directors of the IBC who are served with ESR notices and fail to respond become personally liable for penalties; directors of the operating subsidiary face CFC income inclusion and personal tax liability in the franchise host country; admissibility of the IBC's separate legal personality is vitiated for corporate tax avoidance
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fine up to BZ$25,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years for continuing AML breach; registered agent's forced resignation leads to automatic strike-off and freezing of all corporate bank accounts; franchise royalty wires blocked under correspondent-bank sanctions filters

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners blacklisted across Caribbean-banking correspondent networks; directors and ultimate shareholders exposed to asset seizure, travel restrictions, and extradition in serious fraud or money laundering cases; extended 60-month custodial sentencing for deliberate concealment of UBO data
Late Filing and Involuntary Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the Belize Register with automatic vesting of all IBC assets in the Crown; restoration costs BZ$1,000 plus payment of all arrears; the IBC loses standing to sue franchisees and can no longer enforce the master franchise or central kitchen supply agreements

Director personal liability: Directors become personally exposed to franchisee breach-of-contract claims and supply-interruption damages because the corporate shield is pierced when the entity is struck off; creditors may invoke personal guarantees; travel bans and director disqualification orders enforceable across CARICOM member states
