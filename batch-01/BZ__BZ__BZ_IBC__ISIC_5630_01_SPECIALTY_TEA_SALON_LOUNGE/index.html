Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance of Belize – Belize Tax Service Department; official portal: www.taxservice.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

A Belize International Business Company (IBC) is tax-exempt and is not required to obtain a Belize income tax or GST registration number at formation. The statutory identifier for the IBC is the Certificate of Incorporation and company registration number issued by the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry. If a foreign-owned IBC nonetheless requires a domestic Belize TIN for customs, banking, withholding, or administrative purposes, it must submit an application for a Tax Identification Number to the Belize Tax Service Department, accompanied by certified constitutional documents, proof of the registered office and licensed registered agent in Belize, and identification documentation of directors and shareholders. No local economic presence is required solely for registration. For GST purposes, registration becomes necessary for an IBC only if it begins making taxable supplies connected with Belize that exceed the statutory threshold, which would be outside the permitted offshore IBC activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election. An IBC may elect under the International Business Companies Act to convert to an ordinary Belize company under the Companies Act, thereby surrendering its tax-exempt IBC status and becoming subject to Belize income tax, General Sales Tax, and annual business tax on its world-wide or Belize-source income as applicable. No hybrid mismatch or entity classification elections are currently available to IBCs under Belize domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZ$75,000 annual taxable turnover in Belize; persons making or likely to make taxable supplies above this threshold must register for GST

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports from Belize qualify for 0% General Sales Tax where the supplier retains documentary evidence that the goods were physically exported from Belize within the statutory period and that title and risk passed to a non-resident buyer. Services may be zero-rated where supplied directly in connection with exported goods or supplied to a non-resident and enjoyed wholly outside Belize, provided the services are not connected with Belize land or goods in Belize at the time of supply. For an IBC, zero-rating claims are available only to the extent the IBC lawfully engages in export trade or international services without violating IBC restrictions on domestic business with Belize residents.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

No national real-time electronic invoicing or fiscal clearance mandate is currently in force in Belize. Belize does not require API-based tax authority clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. GST-registered businesses are required to issue serially numbered tax invoices or sales receipts that contain the supplier's name and GST registration number, the date, the nature and value of the supply, and the GST charged. Records must be retained in Belize for audit purposes.

Belize Tax Service Department may assess additional GST, interest at the statutory rate, and civil penalties if export zero-rating is claimed without adequate supporting documentation or if the consideration is not repatriated within the 180-day foreign currency deadline. Fraudulent or deliberate zero-rating claims attract penalties of up to double the tax evaded, fines, and imprisonment under the General Sales Tax Act. The limitation period for assessment is generally six years but extends to twelve years in cases of fraud, wilful default, or neglect. An IBC that engages in prohibited onshore domestic trade loses its tax-exempt status; all Belize income tax, General Sales Tax, business tax, interest, and penalties are assessed retrospectively from the date of first prohibited activity.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged at 2.00 BZD = 1.00 USD through the CBB administered parity. Current-account transactions are generally freely convertible for residents through authorized dealers; capital-account transfers and resident borrowing from non-residents require CBB approval. A BZ_IBC is exempt from the Exchange Control Act under section 4 of the International Business Companies Act (Chapter 270) and may therefore hold, convert, and remit foreign currencies without CBB surrender, release, or approval mechanics, provided it has not been licensed to carry on business with Belize residents. Dividends and profit remittances are exempt from Belize income tax, withholding tax, and stamp duty, and may be wired directly to shareholders without a tax clearance certificate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must obtain payment for goods within six months of exportation and surrender all foreign-currency proceeds to an authorized dealer within thirty days of receipt under the Exchange Control (Export of Goods) Regulations, unless the CBB grants an extension. These surrender and mandatory-conversion rules do not apply to a BZ_IBC because section 4 of the IBC Act disapplies the Exchange Control Act; export receipts from high-end loose-leaf tea and cultural ceramics are therefore not subject to statutory timelines or conversion quotas. To preserve the exemption, the specialty tea salon must avoid unlicensed retail trade with Belize residents and should maintain bills of lading, customs declarations, and supplier contracts evidencing sales to non-resident buyers.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), Belmopan, established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Chapter 262); exchange-control administration is delegated to licensed commercial banks acting as 'authorized dealers' under the Exchange Control Act (Chapter 45); anti-money-laundering supervision is exercised by the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act (Chapter 104).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for the Belize operating entity. Belize has no locally regulated EMI licensing category, and offshore fintech EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, Mercury-type platforms) generally decline Belize IBC clients because of CFATF mutual-evaluation scrutiny and correspondent-bank de-risking. Offshore EMIs may be used at the parent treasury level in a recognized financial center for low-value supplier payments to tea-leaf vendors (Taiwan, India, China), but the Belize entity's primary clearing and merchant settlement must run through a licensed Belizean authorized dealer to satisfy local AML/CFT rules and card-scheme operating regulations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity must be wired bank-to-bank via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own account to the IBC's escrow or operational account at a Belizean authorized dealer; the wire narrative should read 'Share capital contribution - International Business Company', and the corporate authorization (board resolution authorizing the account and the capital call) must be provided to the receiving bank. No CBB prior approval applies because the IBC is exempt from the Exchange Control Act, but the bank will require the share subscription agreement, register of members, and a registered agent's capital confirmation as verification. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No mandatory threshold in Belize dollars; Belize has no statutory transfer-pricing documentation regime for IBCs. If the IBC is incorrectly assessed as a resident domestic taxpayer, the general arm's-length principle applies under the Income and Business Tax Act, but no Local File or Master File threshold has been enacted.

Economic substance scope: Belize Economic Substance Act 2019, as amended, applies to IBCs carrying on relevant activities: banking, insurance, shipping, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, intellectual-property business, distribution and service centres, and holding companies. A relevant-activity IBC must be managed and directed in Belize, demonstrate adequate physical premises, employees, and local expenditures proportionate to the income, and file an Economic Substance Return with the Belize Financial Services Commission within 12 months after the close of the fiscal year. Holding companies carry a lighter substance requirement. Non-compliance does not immediately revoke the IBC tax exemption but triggers administrative sanctions, registry strike-off, and automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Unauthorised Local Business Activity / Tax Exemption Forfeiture

Statutory sanction: Under the International Business Companies Act, an IBC trading with Belize residents without a local business licence is liable to fines of up to BZ$10,000 plus daily penalties during the unauthorised trading period. The Revenue Commissioner may revoke the exempt status and impose income tax at 25% plus business tax and penalties on all Belize-source profits.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly directed onshore tea-salon sales can be personally assessed for the company's taxes, arrears, and penalties if the corporate form is found to be a facade. Courts may pierce the corporate veil, issue civil restitution orders for diverted salon revenues, impose travel bans pending payment of liabilities, and commit directors to imprisonment for continued trading after a stop order.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Under the Belize Economic Substance Act, the Financial Services Commission may impose an initial civil penalty of up to BZ$100,000, daily penalties of BZ$10,000 for continued non-compliance, and an order striking the IBC from the register. False declarations additionally expose the company to criminal fines for fraud on the regulator.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from serving on any Belize company, made jointly liable for administrative penalty costs, and subjected to asset-freezing orders and departure bans while the Commission investigates the shell structure. Repeated violations amount to unlawful sham management, enabling seizure of personal assets traceable to the tea-salon royalty flow.
False Annual Return and Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Knowingly making a false statement in a Belize IBC annual return is a criminal offence under the International Business Companies Act; the statutory sanction includes fines up to BZ$50,000 and imprisonment up to three years. Civil recovery may equal two to three times the avoided tax, plus interest and late-payment penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrate false filings lose corporate immunity. The court may order personal restitution of the tax loss, impose travel restrictions pending settlement, and sentence directors to imprisonment where false declarations are used to conceal taxable tea-salon profits or to facilitate foreign tax evasion.
AML Violations / Money Laundering Through Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Belize's Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act imposes corporate fines up to BZ$500,000, forfeiture of the IBC's bank accounts, and revocation of any financial or money-service licence. Persons convicted of laundering through the salon-IBC conduit face imprisonment of up to 14 years and criminal fines up to BZ$100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors with actual knowledge or wilful blindness are prosecuted as principals even where the IBC vehicle remains nominally tax-exempt. Courts may issue civil recovery orders over personal assets, travel bans pending final judgment, and extradition co-operation orders for non-resident directors deemed to have orchestrated AML breaches.
