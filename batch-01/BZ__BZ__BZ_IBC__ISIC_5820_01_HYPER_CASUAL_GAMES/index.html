Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (within the Ministry of Finance; online portal: https://taxservice.gov.bz; IFSC filing portal for IBCs: https://www.ifsc.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

An International Business Company is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax and does not receive a national TIN or VAT registration ID as part of its incorporation. If the IBC nonetheless carries on non-exempt domestic supplies, it must apply for a General Sales Tax (GST) identification number via Form GST-01 filed with the Belize Tax Service, designating a domestic physical address and a local agent; approval is discretionary and subject to evidence of taxable turnover. In all other cases, the IFSC Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate of Good Standing serve as valid identity documents for international tax treaty and banking purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election is available under Belize law. An IBC may voluntarily surrender its IBC status and elect to convert into a domestic company under the International Business Companies Act, upon which it becomes fully subject to the Income Tax Act and may then apply for GST registration. This conversion is irrevocable and requires a special resolution of the board and approval of the International Financial Services Commission.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of goods (physical removal from Belize) and to services supplied to a non-resident customer where the supply is performed wholly outside Belize, or is ancillary to an exported good, and the Belize supplier receives payment in foreign currency. For a hyper-casual mobile gaming publisher, advertising-monetization services provided to offshore ad networks and app stores are zero-rated when the recipient is non-resident and the intellectual property is exploited entirely outside Belize.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic submission via CEDP portal)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the Belize Central Bank
  • Executed contract or transactional agreement evidencing offshore service recipient
  • App-vending platform payout statement and AppLovin/AdMob monthly remittance reports (for digital services)
  • Certificate of non-residence of the recipient of exported services

No e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance system is currently mandated in Belize. Paper-based invoices remain acceptable, although the Belize Tax Service requires all GST-registered persons to retain invoices, receipts, and export certificates for five years. No API integration, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls are in operation.

The Belize Tax Service, Customs Department, and Central Bank jointly audit zero-rated claims. Fraudulent zero-rating or false export documentation attracts a penalty of 100% of the tax avoided, statutory interest, retrospective clawback of any GST refund paid, criminal fines not exceeding BZD 25,000, and imprisonment for up to two years. In addition, the taxpayer's GST registration may be cancelled and all subsequent export invoices deemed taxable, with the IBC's wholesale tax exemption agreement subject to revocation by the IFSC if there is evidence of money laundering through fictitious export transactions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) has been pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 since 1976 and is fully convertible for current-account payments through authorised dealers; capital-account outflows by Belize residents require CBB exchange-control approval or a specific exemption. A Belize International Business Company (IBC) is statutorily deemed a non-resident and is expressly exempt from the Exchange Control Act, thereby enjoying unrestricted convertibility for digital advertising revenue, capital contributions, and dividend distributions, with no profit-remittance tax and no withholding tax at source imposed on non-resident shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters are generally required to offer foreign currency proceeds to an authorised dealer within three months of export and must convert such proceeds into BZD unless exempted by the CBB; however, a non-resident IBC engaged in invisible exports (ad-monetized hyper-casual game publishing) is exempt from surrender and mandatory-conversion obligations, so AdMob and AppLovin USD payouts may be retained in USD, maintained in offshore accounts, and deployed for trade settlements without CBB intervention.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, administers the foreign exchange system and authorised-dealer framework under the Exchange Control Act; commercial banking is licensed and supervised by the CBB under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act; AML/CFT oversight is exercised by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) of Belize, while the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) supervises the corporate registry and the International Business Companies regime.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the remitting bank to the Belize bank's USD Nostro account at its US clearing correspondent (typically Citibank N.A. or JPMorgan Chase); SWIFT field 70 must state 'INVESTMENT - SHARE SUBSCRIPTION - BZ_IBC' followed by the share certificate reference, and field 50 must identify the shareholder. The Belize bank credits the IBC's USD operational account only after verification of source of funds and receipt of the board resolution authorising share issuance, the updated share register, and the remitter's bank statement; a 48-hour pre-notification to the relationship manager is required. AdMob and AppLovin USD payouts arrive through the same correspondent chain in MT103 format with remittance narrative 'AD-REVENUE INVOICE [number]' and are reconciled against the ad-network payout ledger; Belize does not operate an IBAN system, so the beneficiary account number and the Belize bank's SWIFT/BIC code constitute the routing dataset.Outbound trade settlements for user-acquisition spend, developer royalties, and operating expenses are executed via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers to Meta, Google, AppLovin, and ironSource clearing accounts in the United States or Europe; MT103 COV is reserved for high-value transfers where the correspondent bank credits a downstream beneficiary account. Commercial letters of credit (UCP600) are used only for substantial IP-licensing or co-development escrow milestones and are not applied to ad-network settlements. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for Belize International Business Companies (IBCs) under Section 3 of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270). A non-IBC Belize resident company is taxable at 30% under the Income and Business Tax Act; the IBC statutory exemption rate is the controlling parameter for this entity.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - no dividend withholding tax applies to distributions made by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders. Belize does not operate a withholding mechanism on dividends at either the IBC level or the shareholder level; the distribution is legally characterized as a return of tax-exempt retained earnings.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

ESR Return: within 6 months after the financial year-end; a calendar-year company (FYE 31 December) must file by 30 June of the following year. Annual IBC license fee: due on the anniversary date of incorporation, with a 30-day grace period before the USD 200 fixed late surcharge applies. A pure equity holding company with no relevant activity files only the license fee declaration and confirms ESR status as 'Holding'.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Belize has not enacted a comprehensive OECD-standard transfer-pricing statute with explicit Local File and Master File thresholds. The prudent documentation threshold applicable to an IBC is BZD 1,000,000 (approximately USD 500,000) of annual controlled transactions; above this amount, mandatory contemporaneous documentation (Local File, Master File, and a transfer-pricing benchmarking study) is recommended to defend pricing before the Belize Tax Service and the ESR nexus review. Below the threshold, a board-minute pricing justification and transactional records suffice.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act, 2019 (ESR) applies to an IBC carrying on a 'relevant activity' in Belize. A hyper-casual games publisher is classified under 'Intellectual Property' (proprietary game code, ad technology, user database) and 'Distribution and Service Centre' (management of AdMob and AppLovin payouts). The IBC must demonstrate all of the following: (a) direction and management conducted in Belize, with actual board meetings minuted at the registered office; (b) adequate physical premises, full-time Belize employees, and local operating expenditure proportionate to the gaming revenue; (c) core income-generating activities actually performed in Belize. For IP companies, the OECD modified nexus approach is enforced: profits qualifying for the IBC exemption are confined to the income attributable to qualifying Belize-resident employees who created or substantially developed the IP; remote foreign development teams destroy the exemption claim and force reclassification of the IBC as a taxable resident company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False ESR Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal fines up to BZD 100,000 and daily default penalties of BZD 5,000; the Belize Tax Service may apply best-of-judgment assessment at a deemed 30% corporate rate on the undeclared gaming profits; automatic exchange of information with the United States, United Kingdom, and CRS/FATCA partners follows.

Director personal liability: Directors and the beneficial owner are personally liable to imprisonment of up to 3 years on indictment; conviction triggers a travel ban, seizure of gaming proceeds, and confiscation of directors' personal assets held in Belize; the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit may impose exit controls and account attachment orders.
Shell Company / Inadequate Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Automatic strike-off by the Registrar with a one-year ban on continuation of commercial activities; penalties up to BZD 100,000; the registry escalates the non-compliant entity to the OECD/EU exchange framework, resulting in blacklisting consequences and loss of the Belize tax-exempt certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally assessed for unpaid ESR penalties after the corporate veil is pierced; tax authorities execute directly against directors' personal wealth and foreign gaming receivables; formal disqualification from acting as an IBC director for five years.
Money Laundering / Beneficial Ownership Registry Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the Money Laundering (Prohibition) Act: criminal fines up to BZD 1,000,000 or twice the proceeds of crime, whichever is higher; forfeiture of all gaming payment balances; immediate freezing of corporate accounts by the Financial Intelligence Unit, and formal consent-determination proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors and the ultimate gaming operator are liable to imprisonment of up to 10 years; asset freezing; confiscation of proceeds; cancellation of Belize citizenship or permanent-residence rights; and a lifetime exclusion from the Belize and Caribbean banking system.
IBC Prohibited Local Activity / Loss of Tax Exemption

Statutory sanction: The tax-exemption certificate is void for the affected fiscal year; all historical gaming profits are reassessed at the 30% corporate income tax rate, plus a 25% negligence penalty and 12% per annum interest from the original due date of the unpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Once the IBC is retrospectively stripped of limited liability, directors become jointly and severally liable for the entire reassessed tax, penalties, and interest; Customs sanctions include seizure of corporate property and a targeted travel ban against each director until the tax debt is settled in full.
