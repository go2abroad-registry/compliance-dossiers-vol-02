Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Services Department, Ministry of Finance, Belize City; official portal: https://www.taxservices.gov.bz; electronic filing portal: https://etax.taxservices.gov.bz. Ancillary regulatory authority for IBC licensing: Belize International Financial Services Commission (IFSC).

E-government filing portal

An International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax, capital gains tax, and stamp duty and is therefore not automatically required to register as a taxpayer. Where a Belize Tax Identification Number (TIN) or tax-exemption certificate is required for bank account opening, foreign tax credit claims, or treaty-related certification, the licensed registered agent must submit to the Tax Services Department: (1) the Certificate of Incorporation, (2) Memorandum and Articles of Association, (3) registered office certificate, (4) IFSC Certificate of Good Standing, and (5) a completed TIN application identifying the beneficial owner. The Tax Services Department issues the TIN without imposing income-tax filing obligations, provided the IBC conducts no business in Belize, derives no Belize-source income, and holds no Belize real property. Annual maintenance (license fee and annual return) is effected solely through the IFSC, not the tax authority.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (IBC Act Cap. 270 s. 23 exemption; non-resident status; no Belize-source economic activity; territorial scope excluded for local taxation).

Available election forms

Belize has no US-style check-the-box election and no CFC or hybrid-mismatch election framework for IBCs. Available statutory elections: (1) application to the Minister of Finance for a written tax-exemption certificate under IBC Act s. 23(1); (2) election to maintain foreign-currency accounts and transact exclusively in foreign currency under Exchange Control (IBC) exemptions; (3) sectoral opt-in elections to be licensed as an exempt international bank, insurer, or mutual fund; (4) an IBC cannot validly elect onshore ordinary-resident tax status - carrying on business with Belize residents, acquiring Belize real property, or deriving Belize-source income automatically forfeits exempt status and exposes the entity to normal Belize corporation tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZ$75,000 (Belize dollars) of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period, below which compulsory registration is not triggered; voluntary registration is available. A tax-exempt IBC engaged solely in offshore MMORPG digital publishing with no supplies consumed in Belize falls outside the scope of GST and is not required to register; exports of goods and services to non-residents are zero-rated at 0%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% GST where they are physically exported from Belize and documentary evidence confirms departure from the customs territory. Services, including MMORPG digital publishing, in-app purchases, and subscriptions delivered over the internet through Apple App Store and Google Play, qualify as zero-rated exported services where: the recipient is a non-resident or a person carrying on business outside Belize; the service is used and enjoyed entirely outside Belize; consideration is in foreign currency; and contemporaneous evidence shows foreign place of consumption (IP geolocation, subscriber country data, platform payout records, and contracts with non-resident platform operators). An exempt IBC is not liable to output GST on such supplies and may reclaim input GST only if separately GST-registered for a Belize taxable activity.

  • Belize Customs and Excise Department electronic Customs Export Declaration (CUS entry) signed by the exporter or licensed customs broker
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or postal/courier export receipt evidencing physical despatch from Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/platform settlement statement (Apple App Store payout, Google Play payout) evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or government API clearance mandate exists in Belize. GST invoices must be issued within seven days of supply and contain the supplier/customer names, TIN and GST registration number, date, description of supply, consideration, GST-exclusive amount, GST at 12.5%, and total amount. GST returns are filed electronically on the e-Tax portal on a bi-monthly cycle, with credit/refund available for excess input GST on zero-rated export supplies subject to documentary substantiation.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims, false export declarations, or abusive refund submissions are subject to: reassessment and clawback of the full GST that would otherwise have applied, civil penalties up to double the tax evaded, interest accrual, fines up to BZ$20,000, and imprisonment for up to three years under the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 79). Customs-related breaches attract forfeiture of goods, seizure of conveyances, and additional customs penalties under the Customs and Excise Duty Act. Where an exempt IBC is misused as a sham conduit for fraudulent zero-rating or GST refunds, the IFSC may strike off the company, revoke its tax-exemption certificate, disqualify the licensed registered agent, and refer the matter for criminal prosecution; retrospective clawback applies from the date the false claim was made.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize operates a fixed-exchange-rate regime: the Belize Dollar (BZD) is pegged to the U.S. Dollar (USD) at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 (maintained since 1976) with full current-account convertibility and no documented balance-of-payments restrictions. Capital-account transactions are in principle also free, but the CBB retains supervisory reserve powers over cross-border credit operations and significant foreign-currency movements. International Business Companies (IBCs) incorporated under the International Business Companies Act (Chapter 270) are statutorily exempt from the Exchange Control Act and may freely maintain foreign-currency accounts domestically or abroad. Profit remittance by an IBC to non-resident shareholders is exempt from Belizean withholding tax and requires no exchange-control license; the operative hurdles are correspondent-bank due diligence on the ultimate beneficial-owner chain and the FATF-relevant AML/CFT filing regime under the Money Laundering and Prevention of Terrorism Act.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Belize does not impose any statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters and licensed IBCs may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts in external or local foreign-currency accounts. Although the Exchange Control Act empowers the CBB to direct partial repatriation in exceptional balance-of-payments circumstances, no such directive is in force. For ISIC 5820 digital-goods publishing, proceeds remitted by Apple App Store and Google Play are classified as cross-border service receipts; they are not accompanied by a customs export declaration and require only standard commercial-bank credit posting with supporting settlement reports retained for AML/CFT documentation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Chapter 262 of the Laws of Belize), exercising monetary authority, foreign-exchange oversight, and banking supervision; the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) of Belize operates separately under the Financial Intelligence Unit Act for AML/CFT supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-Low. Belize does not maintain a dedicated e-money-institution (EMI) or fintech payment-licensing regime; the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) does not issue digital-bank charters permitting fully remote onboarding. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Mercury, Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise Business) commonly decline Belize IBCs on jurisdiction-risk and economic-substance grounds, or impose prolonged enhanced due diligence that renders the account impractical for treasury operations. Legal constraint: the Money Laundering and Prevention of Terrorism Act requires all financial-service providers to apply customer-diligence measures at least equivalent to the FATF standard; an entirely remote digital-EMI onboarding lacking biometric, notarized, or face-to-face verification is not FATF-compliant and may be treated by Belizean banks as a secondary, non-core rail. The MMORPG's primary liquidity should therefore rely on licensed Belize banks and platform payout mechanisms, with offshore EMIs used only for incidental contractor disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: shareholder equity injections are routed by SWIFT MT103 to the IBC's Belize settlement account using ISO 20022 transaction-purpose code 'EQUI' (equity) or BPM6 direct-investment classification 'E.51 – Equity capital'; shareholder loans use purpose code 'LEND', and intra-group treasury transfers use 'INTC'. Each inbound wire must reference the IBC's registration number and be supported by a board resolution authorizing the capital movement, the UBO's source-of-funds statement, and a corporate-chain diagram tracing funding from ultimate natural persons to the Belize IBC. Correspondent banks will additionally require SWIFT GPI tracking, transaction-value screening, PEP screening, and a match against the pre-approved beneficiary list in the bank's KYC file. Capital escrow accounts may be used to stage initial equity before operational disbursements, with segregation between paid-in capital and revenue receipts to simplify later audit and tax-exemption documentation.Primary clearing rail: SWIFT MT103 for single payments and MT202 for bank-to-bank covering transfers; SWIFT GPI is mandatory to ensure end-to-end traceability for compliance audits. Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP 600) are permissible for sourcing large infrastructure or licensing contracts through Belize trade desks, although the typical MMORPG publishing model relies on MT103 payments to contractors, tax authorities, and distribution partners. Apple and Google payouts are settled through their respective payment-facilitator rails, which credit the Belize account via ACH or card-network clearing in the applicable currency. Outbound payments above USD 10,000 require pre-uploaded underlying invoices or contracts in the bank's compliance portal; permissible ISO 20022 purpose codes include 'ROYL' (royalties and licensing fees), 'SVCS' (business-to-business services), and 'INTC' (intra-group intercompany payment). E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Medium-Low. Belize does not maintain a dedicated e-money-institution (EMI) or fintech payment-licensing regime; the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) does not issue digital-bank charters permitting fully remote onboarding. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Mercury, Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise Business) commonly decline Belize IBCs on jurisdiction-risk and economic-substance grounds, or impose prolonged enhanced due diligence that renders the account impractical for treasury operations. Legal constraint: the Money Laundering and Prevention of Terrorism Act requires all financial-service providers to apply customer-diligence measures at least equivalent to the FATF standard; an entirely remote digital-EMI onboarding lacking biometric, notarized, or face-to-face verification is not FATF-compliant and may be treated by Belizean banks as a secondary, non-core rail. The MMORPG's primary liquidity should therefore rely on licensed Belize banks and platform payout mechanisms, with offshore EMIs used only for incidental contractor disbursements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory BZD threshold mandating Local File or Master File exists under Belize domestic law; the Belize Tax Service can still challenge non-arm's-length pricing under general anti-avoidance and existing audit powers. Prudent practice: prepare contemporaneous OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation and an economic substance file for all material cross-border related-party transactions involving MMORPG IP, App Store platform commissions, intercompany royalties and group service charges, including low-value-adding intra-group services.

Economic substance scope: Belize Economic Substance Regulations apply to IBCs carrying on any relevant activity. A hardcore MMORPG publisher owning or exploiting game IP, collecting Apple/Google payout revenue, and paying outsourced development fees must meet the IP-business or distribution-and-services-center substance tests. The IBC must demonstrate board meetings directed and managed in Belize, physical premises, full-time employees and adequate operating expenditure in Belize. Non-compliance triggers monetary penalties, strike-off, and exchange-of-information reporting; the 0% tax rate remains defensible only with genuine substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / false accounting declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Belize Income Tax Act and general criminal law, tax evasion carries interest and penalties of up to 100% of the deficiency, a criminal fine determined by the court, and imprisonment upon conviction; the tax authority may also reopen closed years and assess the 25% Belize CIT rate when the exemption is abused.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or procure false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the fraudulent tax loss; the corporate veil may be pierced, civil restitution may be ordered against diverted profits, travel bans may be imposed in extradition or enforcement proceedings, and imprisonment is available for deliberate evasion.
Shell company / economic substance abuse

Statutory sanction: Penalty and default amounts prescribed under the International Business Companies Act and Economic Substance Regulations, recurring daily penalties for non-compliance, automatic strike-off/restoration procedures, and mandatory exchange of information with foreign tax authorities; the 0% tax exemption may be treated as invalid retroactively if the entity is deemed to lack economic substance.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face personal liability for unpaid corporate tax recalculated at the Belize 25% rate, civil recovery, and criminal sanctions for making false economic-substance declarations; limited liability is unavailable once the company is used as an instrument of fraud or struck off.
Anti-money laundering / proceeds of serious crime

Statutory sanction: Contravention of the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act attracts substantial fines and imprisonment for individuals; the IBC's assets and platform payout balances are subject to freezing, forfeiture, and confiscation orders.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers may be criminally liable where they knowingly facilitate laundering; personal assets may be seized under confiscation frameworks, travel bans are commonly granted in cross-border financial crime investigations, and extradition may be pursued in serious cases.
Abusive profit repatriation / constructive dividend

Statutory sanction: The Belize Tax Service may recharacterize excessive payments as distributions, disallow expense deductions, apply gross-up withholding, and assess penalties and interest; in the recipient jurisdiction, anti-avoidance rules may impose CFC charges, dividend recapture, or additional withholding taxes.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve abusive transfer pricing can be held jointly liable for recovery of underpaid corporate taxes, shareholder-level tax liabilities may be enforced personally under shadow-director or fraudulent-conveyance doctrines, and travel restrictions may be imposed in civil recovery or criminal enforcement proceedings.
Unlicensed financial services / unauthorized token issuance

Statutory sanction: Regulators may apply anti-money-laundering licensing penalties, consumer-protection fines, court orders freezing digital asset inventories, and criminal penalties for conducting regulated financial business without a license.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders behind the token or payment system may be personally subject to restitution orders, settlement payments, and imprisonment; the IBC's corporate veil is disregarded where the token scheme defrauds players or is used for money laundering.
