Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (BTSD), Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment; e-portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz. IBC incorporation is administered by the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) Companies Registry under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270); customs declarations are filed through Belize Customs using ASYCUDA World.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - A licensed Belize registered agent files incorporation documents with the IFSC Companies Registry; the Certificate of Incorporation issues within 24-48 hours under IBC Act s.5. Step 2 - The registered agent submits company particulars to the BTSD, which assigns a Tax Identification Number (TIN) to the IBC; the TIN is used for bank account opening, economic substance annual returns, and administrative compliance even though the IBC enjoys a statutory tax exemption. Step 3 - No GST registration is required where the IBC makes no taxable supplies in Belize; pure offshore film-licensing royalties fall outside Belize GST. Step 4 - Non-resident ownership is permitted; a registered office and registered agent in Belize are mandatory and maintain the compliance link for tax communication.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists for IBCs. Available statutory mechanisms: (i) continuation of the IBC into or out of Belize (IBC Act s.166); (ii) conversion of the IBC into a domestic company under the Companies Act, 2013 (IBC Act s.188); (iii) ministerial order extending tax exemption to a class of persons (IBC Act s.160(3)). The automatic 50-year exemption from all Belize taxes including income tax, capital gains tax, gift tax, estate tax, inheritance tax and stamp duty (IBC Act s.160) remains in force subject to compliance with the Economic Substance Act, 2020 and to the statutory prohibition on carrying on business with Belize residents.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 100,000 of taxable supplies in any 12-month period (GST Act s.10); an IBC making only offshore film-licensing supplies is not registrable and cannot reclaim input GST on local purchases because it makes no taxable supplies in Belize.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of services to a non-resident recipient are zero-rated under the GST Act (Cap. 275) where the services are performed and the benefit is enjoyed outside Belize. Cross-border film copyright licensing royalties qualify, provided: (i) the licensee is a non-resident; (ii) the licensing agreement is negotiated and executed outside Belize; (iii) consideration is received in foreign currency and traceable to the non-resident licensee. Because the IBC is a Tax_Exempt_Zone person under IBC Act s.160 and is exchange-control exempt under IBC Act s.163, no statutory 180-day FX repatriation deadline applies; the 180-day benchmark is a bank/KYC and economic-substance cash-flow convention for documenting export proceeds.

  • Customs Export Declaration in ASYCUDA World (Cus-Exp form) - required only where goods are bundled; pure licensing services rely on the executed foreign licensing agreement
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill - required for physical goods exports; not applicable to pure royalty flows
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a licensed Belize bank evidencing receipt of foreign currency from the non-resident licensee

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction controls, or digital stamping regime exists. The BTSD e-filing portal is mandatory for GST-1 returns; ASYCUDA World is the customs clearance platform for export declarations. Commercial invoices and executed licensing contracts remain the operative export evidence; government pre-approval or fiscal stamping of invoices is not required.

GST Act (Cap. 275) offences: fines up to BZD 25,000, imprisonment up to 3 years, and recovery of tax evaded plus penalties up to 200% for fraudulent export zero-rating claims; assessment windows are generally 6 years, extended to 10 years for fraud. Economic Substance Act, 2020: failure to file the annual economic substance return attracts BZD 20,000 plus BZD 500 per day of continuing default; failure to satisfy the economic substance test for intellectual property business (film licensing) attracts a penalty up to BZD 100,000, with directors and officers personally liable and possible strike-off of the IBC. Loss of the s.160 exemption, for example by carrying on business with Belize residents, retroactively exposes the IBC to Belize income tax at 25% on Belize-source income, stamp duty, and applicable customs duties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize Dollar (BZD) is pegged to the US Dollar at BZD 2:1 under a managed peg in force since 1976. Resident current-account convertibility operates through authorized dealers; resident capital-account remittances require CBB approval. IBCs are statutorily exempt from the Exchange Control Act for foreign-currency operations (IBC Act Chapter 270, s.50): an IBC may freely hold, deal in, and remit USD and other foreign currencies without CBB conversion or surrender approval. The film-licensing entity must operate exclusively through non-resident USD accounts; any transaction in BZD with a Belize resident is prohibited and would expose the account to exchange-control forfeiture. Profit remittance is fully unimpeded for IBCs: Belize imposes no corporate income tax, capital gains tax, or dividend withholding tax, so licensing royalties may be distributed offshore without fiscal or FX clearance, subject only to bank-level AML completion.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For resident exporters, the Exchange Control Regulations (Cap. 35) require all foreign-currency export proceeds to be offered for sale to an authorized dealer within three (3) months of accrual (six months for proceeds of immovable property), with a de facto 100% mandatory conversion into BZD. For the IBC, the surrender regime is disapplied: IBC Act s.50 exempts all foreign-currency holdings and dealings, so the statutory timeline and mandatory conversion ratio on film-licensing receipts are nil. The IBC must nevertheless evidence, through licensing agreements, invoices, and bank credit advices, that all receipts were booked to a foreign-currency non-resident account in USD and never converted into or settled in BZD.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Chapter 262, Laws of Belize); exchange-control administration rests with the CBB under the Exchange Control Act (Chapter 35) and the Exchange Control Regulations. AML/CFT supervision is shared with the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) of Belize. The International Financial Services Commission (IFSC, Chapter 272) regulates IBCs and their licensed registered agents.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-Low. Belize has no domestic EMI licensing regime; the local automated clearing house (BACH, operated by the CBB) is closed to non-bank EMIs. Offshore digital EMIs (UK FCA, EU-passported, US money transmitters) may accept a Belize IBC only with a certified registry extract, UPBO identification, and the film-licensing contract dossier; many mainstream platforms geo-block or decline Belize IBCs because of the historical FATF grey-listing (Belize exited the FATF grey list in 2023) and shell-perception risk. The treasury should be anchored in a Belize bank USD account, with any EMI used only for collection of streaming-platform micro-royalties through an operating subsidiary in a recognized financial center, never as the sole clearing rail. For full-service USD clearing, the client should also evaluate dedicated International Banking Act (Cap. 275) licensees (Caye International Bank, Belize Bank International, Atlantic International Bank), which are purpose-built for IBC accounts.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for International Business Companies (IBCs); ordinary Belize territorial companies otherwise pay 25% on chargeable income (Income and Business Tax Act, Cap. 55).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Belize levies no withholding tax on dividends distributed by an IBC to non-resident shareholders; distributions are exempt from tax, stamp duty, and exchange control restrictions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No Belize transfer-pricing statute or local-file/master-file threshold applies to exempt IBCs. However, every IBC tax resident in Belize carrying on a relevant activity (IP business, distribution and service center) must file an annual Economic Substance return evidencing profitability, operating expenditure, premises, and full-time employees in Belize proportionate to film-licensing functions. An IBC claiming foreign tax residence must file a declaration identifying the foreign competent authority; the Belize Economic Substance regime does not impose a local currency turnover threshold.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. For a Belize tax-resident IBC engaged in IP licensing, the ESR demands: (i) direction and management in Belize; (ii) adequate physical premises, full-time staff, and operating expenditure; and (iii) active conduct of core income-generating activities in Belize. Non-compliance rebuts the tax-exempt status in practice, triggers penalty proceedings, and exposes the company to automatic exchange of information with foreign tax authorities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to US$10,000 per violation (increased for continuing default) under the Companies (International Business Companies) (Economic Substance) provisions; mandatory disclosure of information to foreign tax authorities; strike-off and deregistration; de facto loss of the 50-year tax exemption.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly permit the default face personal fines; corporate veil piercing in proceedings where guarantees or fraud are found; travel bans under asset recovery and mutual legal assistance measures; disqualification from serving as directors.
Tax Evasion / Treaty Shopping in Film Licensing

Statutory sanction: Denial of any treaty or credit benefit: US 30% withholding assessment, interest, and penalties against the withholding agent; recharacterization of the IBC as a conduit; anti-avoidance adjustments in the source jurisdiction; since Belize has no DTA with the US, gross royalty leakage of 30% is unavoidable and may render the structure economically fatal.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of under-withheld taxes in the foreign jurisdiction; criminal exposure for tax fraud in the source country; extradition risk under mutual legal assistance treaties; personal liability for false beneficial ownership declarations.
Anti-Money Laundering / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties and imprisonment under the Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (Prevention) Act; regulatory strike-off that freezes all payment rails including SWIFT wires and letters of credit routed through the IBC; asset freezing orders over the film catalogue.

Director personal liability: Directors prosecuted as principals: upon conviction, fines up to BZ$500,000 (US$250,000) and imprisonment up to 14 years; civil confiscation of licensing proceeds; travel bans and reporting restrictions pending trial.
Copyright / Title Piracy in Distribution

Statutory sanction: Copyright infringement injunctions, seizure, and destruction of master copies; contractual clawback and indemnity claims; criminal fines and imprisonment for willful commercial-scale infringement under the Belize Copyright (Amendment) Act and analogous foreign statutes.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly dealing in infringing masters are personally liable for damages; forfeiture of all licensing fees derived from the infringement; personal bankruptcy proceedings following civil judgments; extradition exposure in the territories where pirated content is exploited.
