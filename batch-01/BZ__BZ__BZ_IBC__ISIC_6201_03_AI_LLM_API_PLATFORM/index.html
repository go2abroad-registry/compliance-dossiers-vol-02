Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; primary portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz (web login for corporate income tax and GST returns); the Belize Companies Registry, within the Office of the Attorney General, issues the IBC company registration number and certificate of incorporation.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned IBC first obtains its statutory company number from the Belize Companies Registry via an approved local registered agent. For tax identity, the agent files Form IRS 01 (Application for Tax Identification Number / TIN) with the Belize Tax Service, together with: (i) certificate of incorporation, (ii) articles/memorandum of association, (iii) registered office address in Belize, (iv) notarized passport copy and proof of residential address of the ultimate beneficial owner, and (v) power of attorney appointing the local agent. BTS issues a 9-digit TIN. Because the IBC is tax-exempt, BTS will normally issue a TIN in a non-filer status. If the company intends to make any commercial purchases subject to GST, file a Form GST 02 application to obtain a GST registration number; for pure exporting AI/LLM API service providers, GST registration is available but not mandatory unless domestic taxable supplies exceed the threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — Belize International Business Companies are non-resident entities exempt from income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, and GST on all offshore business income under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), s. 3(1). AI/LLM API platform revenue from foreign customers is not Belize-source income; the entity does not become tax-resident in Belize so long as central management and control is exercised outside Belize and no domestic commercial licence is obtained.

Available election forms

Belize operates a territorial, unitary tax regime without a US-style check-the-box entity classification election. The only meaningful elections are: (1) conversion of an IBC to a domestic Belize company under s. 142 of the IBC Act, effectively rendering it taxable at the standard 12.5% enterprise tax and 12.5% GST on domestic supplies; (2) a special election under s. 11 of the IBC Act licensing the company to transact business domestically with a Belize resident, which exposes only that specific domestic activity to Belize corporate tax and GST; and (3) for IBCs formed before 2002, an election to remain under the original exemptions. Belize has not implemented OECD hybrid mismatch rules; no hybrid entity election or CFC look-through election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 (approximately USD 37,500) annual value of taxable supplies delivered in Belize. Exported AI/LLM API services and all B2B services to non-residents are zero-rated and therefore do not count toward the registration threshold. A Belize IBC exporting 100% of its services may nevertheless register voluntarily for GST to recover input tax on local administrative expenses, but such registration is not compulsory for a pure offshore service exporter

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for AI/LLM API inference and token-provider services under s. 32(1)(a) of the GST Act where: (i) the customer is a non-resident of Belize; (ii) the contractual place of supply is wholly outside Belize; (iii) the API service is delivered via offshore cloud infrastructure and the output is not used, consumed, or stored for the benefit of a person in Belize; and (iv) the billing address, customer TIN/VAT identification, and merchant settlement evidence demonstrate the cross-border nature of the transaction. The IBC must preserve the customer contract (or electronic terms of service), the API usage records, token settlement statements, and a certificate of offshore use signed by the customer. Where the service is underpinned by data centres in Belize or is rendered to a Belizean resident even partially, zero-rating is denied in respect of that portion.

  • Customs Export Declaration — for goods, a C&D-6 electronic declaration filed in ASYCUDA World; for digital AI/LLM API services, a 'Service Export Declaration' annex submitted via the BTS electronic portal or a bank-certified export proceeds statement if full GST-registered
  • Commercial Invoice / Final Export Invoice — must show the IBC name, TIN, customer overseas address and foreign VAT/TIN, the zero-rated annotation 'GST 0% Export of Services' or 'Export of Goods', and encrypted payment/API usage details
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for goods) — or, for digital services, the delivery certificate: the API access log, the bandwidth/usage report, and the customer's written confirmation of receipt outside Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — a Central Bank of Belize export repatriation form or bank transfer receipt (SWIFT MT103 in USD), or a merchant settlement statement from Stripe/USDC exchange showing net USD proceeds credited to the IBC's account, evidencing foreign exchange inflow

No real-time fiscal e-invoicing or continuous transaction control (CTC) regime is currently implemented in Belize. The Belize Tax Service mandates electronic filing of GST returns and electronic payment through the BTS online portal; paper invoices may be issued but must be sequentially numbered and contain the GST registration number when the business is GST-registered. No digital stamping, cryptographic QR code, or government clearance API applies to export invoices. For exports of physical goods, the Customs and Excise Department uses the ASYCUDA World electronic single-window system for the Customs Export Declaration; for pure digital AI/LLM services, parties file only the BTS electronic Annex ECS (Export of Services) summary, without customs clearance.

The Belize Tax Service retains audit power over any zero-rated export claim. For fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating, s. 54 of the GST Act imposes a penalty equal to 200% of the tax understated, plus interest at 1.5% per month, and criminal liability under s. 55 (fine up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to three years). If the zero-rating claim is dishonestly used to bring Belize-source income into the IBC exemption, the Income and Business Tax Act deems the revenue to be taxable domestic income, resulting in a 12.5% enterprise tax clawback for six years from the assessment date, and revocation of IBC tax-exempt status for subsequent years. Additionally, under anti-money laundering rules, failure to repatriate or properly document proceeds within 180 days may trigger reporting to the Central Bank of Belize and the Financial Intelligence Unit, with civil penalties of up to BZD 50,000 and director disqualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged to the US dollar at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 under the Currency and Banks Act. Current-account convertibility for resident Belizean entities is available for approved purposes subject to exchange-control licensing, while capital-account transactions require CBB permission. An International Business Company (IBC) is expressly excluded from the Exchange Control Act, meaning it is treated as a non-resident entity for FX purposes and may receive, hold, and remit any freely convertible currency without CBB approval. Profit repatriation by an IBC is unhindered by Belize law: no withholding taxes apply to dividends, interest, or royalty payments, provided the IBC maintains its statutory records and corporate registry filings in Belize. The only practical hurdle is bank-level compliance, which requires the beneficial ownership disclosure and source-of-funds documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For ordinary resident Belize exporters, the Exchange Control Act requires export proceeds to be remitted to Belize within 90 days of shipment and ultimately surrendered to the banking system. Under the Central Bank's Foreign Exchange Administration Practice, 50% of repatriated export earnings must be sold to an authorised dealer (commercial bank) for Belize dollars at the official pegged rate, while the remaining 50% can be held in a foreign-currency account. An IBC registered under the International Business Companies Act is exempt from all surrender and conversion requirements; it may retain 100% of foreign-currency receipts in offshore or domestic foreign-currency accounts. Therefore, for the AI/LLM API platform structured as a Belize IBC, there is no statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applicable.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wires for capital injection should be sent via SWIFT MT103, using the purpose code 'GDDS' (equity capital) or 'R004' (investment contribution) depending on the correspondent bank's acceptance. The beneficiary line must state the IBC's full name and account number at the Belize bank; the remittance reference must read 'SHARE CAPITAL INJECTION — REPORT FILED WITH BELIZE IBC REGISTRY'. The IBC should issue a board resolution authorising the issuance of shares, a subscription agreement, share certificates, and a funds-receipt ledger. For USDC-based capital injection, USDC is first liquidated into USD at a licensed OTC desk or exchange (e.g., Coinbase) under a documented treasury policy, then wired to the Belize bank; banks will require proof of the OTC trade ticket and wallet-address ownership. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Business Company (IBC) under the Belize International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270. The headline rate for ordinary Belize-resident companies is 25% under the Income and Business Tax Act, but an IBC that does no business in Belize and holds no Belize-situs assets is wholly outside the tax net.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Belize imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions made by an IBC to non-resident shareholders. Dividends, distributions of capital, and liquidation proceeds are entirely exempt under the International Business Companies Act. No administrative waiver or advance ruling is required; the exemption is statutory and automatic.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The filing window opens on the incorporation anniversary and closes 3 months after that anniversary; the annual government license fee must accompany the return. A 10% late surcharge applies thereafter, and continued default can lead to automatic strike-off after approximately 12 months.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The ESR return must be filed no later than 6 months after financial year-end. Every IBC carrying on a 'relevant activity' must file. An IBC that claims tax residence in another jurisdiction must file the non-Belize resident form and submit a valid foreign tax residence certificate; otherwise the onerous IP substance test applies.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable. Belize has no codified transfer-pricing documentation threshold (Local File / Master File) for IBCs because the IBC has a zero domestic taxable base and is excluded from the Income and Business Tax Act. In practice, under the 2019 Economic Substance Regulations, an AI/LLM API platform generating IP-related revenue above approximately USD 1,000,000 (BZD 2,000,000) per year is treated as a high-risk IP company and must evidence economically substantive onshore functions; the IFSC compares local payroll, premises, and expenditure against this revenue to test substance.

Economic substance scope: The International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations, 2019 apply to every IBC carrying on a relevant activity. A Generative-AI and LLM inference API-token platform that licenses model weights, inference endpoints, and metered API usage is characterized as an 'Intellectual Property Business' (high-risk) if income derives from IP exploitation, or as a 'Distribution and Service Centre Business' if it resells third-party API capacity. Either classification requires filing the Economic Substance Return within 6 months of financial year-end, performing core income-generating activities in Belize, maintaining adequate physical premises and qualified full-time employees, and keeping all core decision-making onshore. The pure equity holding exemption does not cover IP or distribution businesses. An IBC may be excluded only where it is tax-resident in a qualifying foreign jurisdiction and can prove that residence with a current foreign tax residence certificate.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Sham Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under the IBC Act, an IBC that carries on business with Belize residents or owns Belize real property is liable to fines and strike-off; knowingly making false declarations to the IFSC exposes the company to fines of up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment of officers up to 2 years. In the shareholder's home jurisdiction, the structure is recharacterized as a sham and the full AI API income is assessed to tax with penalties and interest; international exchange-of-information requests will be honoured by Belize under applicable TIEAs and the Multilateral Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance.

Director personal liability: Directors, including nominee directors, face piercing of the corporate veil: personal tax assessments on the repatriated income, civil restitution orders, asset freezes, travel bans, disqualification, and imprisonment where fraud or evasion is proved. Foreign prosecutors may invoke mutual legal assistance and extradition channels against directors who signed false declarations or concealed beneficial ownership.
Economic Substance (ESR) Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the ESR Regulations include monetary sanctions, typically USD 10,000 to USD 25,000 per year of continued default, mandatory remedial directions from the Registrar, and ultimately compulsory strike-off and dissolution of the IBC. The IBC and its beneficial owner become reportable information under Belize's exchange-of-information framework and are automatically transmitted to the tax administration of the ultimate beneficial owner, destroying the confidentiality layer.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false substance declarations are personally liable under the false-statement provisions of the IBC Act to fines of up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years; the registered agent may resign and the directors are exposed to civil actions by counterparties and to personal sanction in the parent group's jurisdiction for knowingly maintaining an artificial structure.
AML and Shell Company Abuse in Crypto Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Money laundering under the Belize Money Laundering Act, Cap. 104 as amended is punishable on indictment by fines up to BZD 500,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years; summary conviction carries significant fines and imprisonment. Crypto assets and bank accounts linked to the IBC can be frozen and forfeited, and the Registrar may strike off the company for failing to preserve accounting records for the statutory minimum retention period.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are individually indictable for AML offences; travel bans, asset forfeiture, and extradition under bilateral and multilateral mutual assistance treaties are available; nominee directors who permit unknown principals to control the IBC face criminal liability for failing to identify and verify beneficial owners and for permitting the shell to operate without an effective compliance officer.
Annual Fee Default and Statutory Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: Late payment triggers a 10% surcharge on the unpaid license fee; continued non-payment for approximately 12 months results in administrative strike-off and dissolution. Upon dissolution, all assets of the IBC, including GenAI model IP, API contracts, and platform receivables, vest in the Government of Belize or an appointed official, and contracts transacted thereafter by directors are void. Restoration requires payment of all arrears, surcharges, restoration fees, and, in contested cases, a court order.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue trading after strike-off become personally liable for all debts of the dissolved IBC; counterparties may petition the court to restore merely to sue the directors; the registered agent's resignation places directors under immediate personal responsibility for a non-compliant structure, and restoration proceedings may impose personal costs and penalties against the directors.
