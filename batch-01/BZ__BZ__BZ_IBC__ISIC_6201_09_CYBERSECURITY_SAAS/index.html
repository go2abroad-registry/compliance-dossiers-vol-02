Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Income Tax Department, Ministry of Finance and Economic Development of Belize; national electronic filing portal: Belize Tax Services Portal (TAMIS - Tax Administration and Management Information System) at https://tamis.belizetaxservices.gov.bz; IBC-specific registry: Belize International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) and Companies Registry, Belmopan.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270, obtaining the Certificate of Incorporation from the Belize Companies Registry. Step 2: Apply to the Belize Tax Services (Income Tax Department) via TAMIS for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) using the IBC's certificate and registered office particulars; there is no separate corporate tax registration where the IBC is entirely tax-exempt, but a TIN is issued for administrative and economic substance filings. Step 3: If the IBC makes taxable supplies in Belize exceeding BZ$75,000 per annum (or elects voluntary registration), register for General Sales Tax (GST) under the General Sales Tax Act, Cap. 86, Form GST-1, using the same TIN; a local tax representative may be required where the foreign entity is not physically present. Step 4: The IBC must file an annual Economic Substance Return with the IFSC Registrar to preserve its tax-exempt status and avoid penalties.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize does not maintain a US-style 'check-the-box' classification regime. An IBC may statutorily elect to be treated as a fully taxable domestic Belize company by converting under the IBC Act, thereby relinquishing exempt status. For indirect tax purposes, a person may elect voluntary GST registration under section 17 of the GST Act. Under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Regulations 2023, an IBC may elect its relevant activity category and, for a pure equity holding IBC, claim reduced economic substance requirements. Belize has not enacted OECD hybrid mismatch election rules; the IBC's tax-exempt status instead follows the entity's non-resident territorial scope.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZ$75,000 (Belize dollars) of taxable supplies in any twelve-month period; approximately USD 37,500 at the fixed exchange rate of 2.00 BZD/USD.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for: (i) export of tangible goods physically delivered outside Belize; (ii) supply of services to a non-resident customer who is outside Belize at the time of supply and where the services are performed entirely outside Belize or the benefit is enjoyed outside Belize; (iii) cybersecurity SaaS and cloud penetration testing services consumed offshore qualify if the contract, intellectual property, and server/delivery location are outside Belize. The IBC must obtain written confirmation of the customer's non-resident status, maintain the executed SaaS agreement, and demonstrate that no Belizean taxable supply is made. Where the customer is a Belize resident, standard-rate GST applies.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Form CED/SAD) filed with the Belize Customs Department, required only for physical goods or on-premise cyber-security hardware appliances; not applicable to pure cloud SaaS
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export, where goods are shipped
  • Executed SaaS subscription / penetration testing service agreement identifying the non-resident customer, scope of work, and offshore delivery terms
  • Commercial GST zero-rated tax invoice with complete supplier/customer particulars and zero-rate declaration
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign currency bank statement from the IBC's external or Belize bank account evidencing receipt of export proceeds in convertible foreign currency
  • Technical service delivery documentation (e.g., scan reports, IP usage logs, server location evidence) proving that the service is supplied and consumed outside Belize

Belize has not enacted a mandatory real-time e-invoicing fiscal clearance regime with government-side validation or digital stamping as of the current tax period. GST-registered suppliers must issue prescribed tax invoices under the GST Act and retain them for audit. Electronic filing and payment of GST returns through TAMIS is mandatory for all registered businesses. A phased national e-invoicing modernization is under official consideration by Belize Tax Services but is not yet legally binding. Foreign zero-rated invoice issuance by the IBC is permitted outside any local clearance system.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims are treated as tax evasion under the General Sales Tax Act, Cap. 86 and the criminal law of Belize. Penalties include: a late-filing penalty of the greater of BZ$100 per month or 10% of the tax due; interest at 10% per annum on unpaid tax; fines of up to BZ$10,000 and imprisonment for willful evasion; seizure and forfeiture of goods where a customs export declaration is falsified; and retrospective reassessment without time limit where fraud or gross negligence is established. For the IBC itself, the 2023 economic substance regime imposes penalties of BZ$50,000 for a first default and BZ$100,000 for repeated default, plus involuntary striking-off of the company, and authorizes the tax authority to claw back the IBC's entire tax-exempt status and assess all avoided Belize taxes plus compound interest retrospectively if the IBC fails to meet substance or truthful export documentation requirements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize Dollar (BZD) is statutorily pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, producing de facto full convertibility against the US Dollar at the fixed official rate for all settlements routed through authorized dealers. Belize operates a dual-tier convertibility regime: (i) residents and resident-owned companies are subject to exchange control administered by the CBB, requiring authorized-dealer routing, documentary justification for capital transfers, and compliance with CBB notices on repatriation; (ii) every International Business Company is statutorily deemed a non-resident under Section 96(1) of the International Business Companies Act, Chapter 270, and is expressly exempt from the Exchange Control Act and all subsidiary exchange-control legislation. The cybersecurity SaaS IBC therefore enjoys unrestricted current- and capital-account convertibility: it may hold, receive, remit, and convert USD, EUR, GBP, and any other currency without CBB licence, approval, or reporting. Profit-remittance hurdles are absent at the exchange-control layer; the only operative frictions are bank-level enhanced due diligence, filing compliance under the Economic Substance (Companies and Partnerships) Act, 2019, and maintenance of good standing through the annual IBC licence fee.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident Belize exporters are required under CBB exchange-control notices to route export proceeds through an authorized dealer and to surrender or convert qualifying receipts into BZD within the statutory window, generally 90 to 180 days from shipment or service delivery. This surrender and conversion obligation does NOT apply to a Belize IBC: Section 96(1) of the International Business Companies Act disapplies the Exchange Control Act, and the IBC's non-resident classification allows the SaaS IBC to retain 100 percent of USD subscription, licensing, and penetration-testing proceeds in foreign-currency accounts with no mandatory conversion into BZD and no CBB surrender reporting. Licensed banks nevertheless apply internal source-of-funds verification to large inbound credits, requiring the underlying invoice or contract and the payment-processor settlement record; this is AML monitoring only, not a statutory surrender or conversion requirement. If the group were restructured to operate through a resident Belize trading company, surrender duties, statutory timelines, and official-rate conversion would revive.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), constituted under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262 of the Laws of Belize, acting as the exchange-control authority under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 48, and as prudential supervisor under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, Chapter 263; the Anti-Money-Laundering and Counter-Financing-of-Terrorism framework is administered by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) of Belize under the Financial Intelligence Unit Act and the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM as the primary treasury concentration point; MEDIUM-HIGH only as a supplementary collection and disbursement channel. Belize has no licensed electronic-money-institution category, so no domestic EMI exists and the CBB charters no offshore EMIs. International digital banks and EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, Mercury, Revolut Business) commonly reject or restrict Caribbean IBCs under global de-risking policies, CFATF mutual-evaluation scrutiny, and US FinCEN beneficial-ownership expectations. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound mechanics: shareholder funds are remitted by SWIFT MT103 in USD from the investor's bank to the IBC's USD account at the Belize licensed bank, cleared through the bank's US correspondent via Fedwire or CHIPS, with the payment narrative citing the share capital contribution, the IBC corporate name, and the subscription agreement reference. No CBB exchange-control approval or filing is required because of the Section 96(1) IBC Act non-resident exemption, and no surrender obligation applies. The receiving bank requires a pre-submitted corporate file comprising certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, certificate of incumbency, substance evidence, the board resolution authorizing the capital call, the notarized subscription agreement, and source-of-funds and source-of-wealth declarations for the contributing shareholder. Transactions crossing the statutory cash and wire-reporting thresholds under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act generate routine FIU reports requiring no additional action where documentation is clean; pre-notification and file submission before transmission remain best practice.The SaaS IBC's outbound clearing architecture is services-based rather than goods-based: (i) SWIFT MT103 wires for international vendors, subcontractors, legal counsel, and tax advisors, each carrying a clear beneficiary reference and a supporting vendor invoice for bank file retention; (ii) ACH or Fedwire for US-based disbursements if a US operating account is maintained; (iii) Stripe payout rails collecting subscription and licensing fees and settling in USD to the designated Belize bank account on the daily or scheduled payout cycle; (iv) commercial letters of credit under UCP 600 are legally permissible but commercially immaterial, reserved solely for procurement of physical network appliances such as firewalls and testing hardware for on-premise engagements; and (v) periodic transfers to the Belize registered agent for the annual IBC licence fee and economic-substance filing costs. Non-USD payables are converted by the Belize bank's treasury desk at the daily interbank rate plus a spread; USD-to-BZD conversion occurs at the fixed peg of 2:1, eliminating intra-Belize FX risk. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM as the primary treasury concentration point; MEDIUM-HIGH only as a supplementary collection and disbursement channel. Belize has no licensed electronic-money-institution category, so no domestic EMI exists and the CBB charters no offshore EMIs. International digital banks and EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, Mercury, Revolut Business) commonly reject or restrict Caribbean IBCs under global de-risking policies, CFATF mutual-evaluation scrutiny, and US FinCEN beneficial-ownership expectations. Onboarding succeeds only when the IBC presents a Belize licensed-bank reference, a live website and verifiable SaaS product, executed enterprise contracts, and a registered-agent cover letter; even then EMIs often impose volume caps and funds-hold reviews. Foreign EMIs are unsupervised by the CBB and outside any Belize deposit-protection net. Optimal architecture: Stripe as the front-end subscription collector settling to a Belize bank USD account; the Belize bank account as the treasury spine; and a selected EMI used solely for low-value non-USD disbursements.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable (Belize IBC zero-tax regime); however, for any related-party transaction forming part of a relevant activity, contemporaneous documentation is required under the Economic Substance Rules regardless of monetary threshold.

Economic substance scope: Applicable under the International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Rules 2019 and the Integrated Cabotage and International Business Companies legislation. A Belize IBC deriving IP-related SaaS revenue or providing services to related parties is a 'relevant activity' and must maintain physical presence, employees, expenditures, and strategic management in Belize proportionate to the relevant income; an IBC selling SaaS to unrelated third-party customers generally files a nil declaration because no relevant activity is carried on.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Abusive Use of IBC Exemption

Statutory sanction: Reassessment under the Income and Business Tax Act at the standard corporate rate of 25% plus monthly interest charges and penalties of up to 150% of the tax evaded; criminal prosecution may result in additional fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve false tax classifications are jointly and severally liable for restitution; criminal tax evasion can trigger travel bans, disqualification as directors, and custodial sentences generally up to five years.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Defect

Statutory sanction: Enforcement ladder under the Economic Substance Rules: advisory correction notice, financial penalty up to BZD 10,000, daily default penalties, administrative strike-off, and dissolution; reinstatement is conditional on full compliance and payment of arrears.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for penalties arising from false declarations; the court may order restitution, compel disclosure, prohibit departure from Belize, and impose imprisonment for continued non-compliance.
Transfer Pricing / Related-Party Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Belize's zero-tax IBC exemption is not a shield against general anti-avoidance; if the exemption is pierced or the entity is reclassified as carrying on a domestic taxable activity, the Commissioner may recharacterise intercompany prices and assess tax on unreported profit, with penalties and interest.

Director personal liability: In group insolvency or fraud, courts may pierce the corporate veil, impose civil restitution for diverted profits, and freeze assets of directors who approved artificial pricing structures.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, corporate penalties can reach BZD 1,000,000 with confiscation of proceeds; individuals may face imprisonment up to 14 years and additional financial penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who consent to, facilitate, or wilfully ignore such breaches are personally subject to criminal liability, travel bans, disqualification, and civil confiscation orders; the corporate veil will be pierced for restitution.
