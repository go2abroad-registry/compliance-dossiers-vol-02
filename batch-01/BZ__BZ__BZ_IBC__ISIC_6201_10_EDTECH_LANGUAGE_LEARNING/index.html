Competent corporate registry

Companies Registry, Ministry of Financial Services and Investment, Belize City, Belize

International Business Companies Act, Chapter 270, Laws of Belize (Revised Edition 2020), as amended by the IBC (Amendment) Acts to 2024; notably ss. 3 (offshore scope), 7 (share capital), 9 (Articles of Incorporation), 10-11 (registration and Certificate of Incorporation), 76 (directors), 124 (registered office), 125 (registered agent).

Formation / constitutional filings

Articles of Incorporation (prescribed Form IBC-1) lodged under s. 9 of the International Business Companies Act, together with the Registered Agent Consent and the corporate Compliance Declaration

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The IBC Act prescribes no minimum authorized or paid-up capital. An IBC may incorporate with a single share of no par value; the subscriber sets the consideration in the Articles. No escrow account, bank capital deposit, or paid-up capital remittance is required for offshore EdTech operations.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable to a registered-office-only IBC with no employees in Belize. If a physical Belize workplace is established in the future, the following clearances would then be mandatory: (1) Fire Safety Certificate from the Belize National Fire Service under the Fire Service Act, Chap. 267, confirming smoke detection, extinguishers, and egress; (2) structural and electrical load inspection certificate under the Belize Building Code of Practice issued by the Belize City Council building authority; and (3) workplace registration and occupational health clearance under the Labour (Occupational Health and Safety) Act, No. 25 of 2023.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable. An IBC is statutorily restricted from carrying on trade with Belize residents and from maintaining a physical local operating establishment under ss. 3(2)-3(4) of the IBC Act. Category: Not required. The offshore EdTech subscription business (ISIC 6201) triggers no local operating or manufacturing permit. If the operations were ever localized through a domestic subsidiary, the applicable instrument would be a Trade License under the Trade Licensing Act, Chap. 293, subject to conversion of the IBC to a domestic company under s. 99 of the IBC Act.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (BTSD), Ministry of Finance; official e-services portal: https://www.tax.gov.bz. The Belize Companies Registry issues International Business Company registration identifiers and is the corporate registry counterpart to tax registration.

E-government filing portal

An International Business Company incorporated under the Belize International Business Companies Act receives a company registration number from the Belize Companies Registry; because the IBC is statutorily tax-exempt, BTSD does not automatically issue a tax TIN or GST/VAT registration number. If a GST/TIN identifier is required, the registered agent must file a GST registration application through tax.gov.bz, attaching the certificate of incorporation, constitution, registered agent certificate, beneficial ownership particulars, director/shareholder identification, and a declaration of taxable business activity in, into, or affecting Belize. BTSD will issue a nine-digit taxpayer identification number upon acceptance. A non-resident foreign entity without Belize nexus has no tax registration obligation merely because it sells online services from outside Belize; registration becomes mandatory only when it makes taxable supplies in Belize above the statutory threshold or voluntarily elects GST registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: The Belize IBC is treated as an offshore exempt company and is not subject to Belize income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, or GST on foreign-source and offshore activities for 50 years from incorporation. The entity is opaque for Belize tax purposes, but local exemption does not flow through to shareholders. Domestic Belize-source taxable activity would generally require loss of the exempt classification or separate registration.

Available election forms

Belize has no U.S.-style check-the-box election and no domestic hybrid-mismatch election. Exempt IBC status is mandatory unless the company formally surrenders or converts its status, obtains a special licence to undertake expressly permitted local activities, or registers for GST because it has crossed the domestic supply threshold. Any such registration or conversion is a material status event reportable to BTSD and may prospectively remove the IBC exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 annual taxable supplies in Belize (exclusive of GST). Registration is mandatory within 21 days after the threshold is crossed; there is no separate digital-services registration threshold in Belize. A non-resident supplier of digital or EdTech services has nexus for GST registration only to the extent its sales are made to Belize consumers and the BZD 75,000 threshold is exceeded in Belize. A pure offshore IBC selling to non-resident subscribers generally has no such nexus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Belize GST legislation zero-rates the export of goods and the supply of services to a person who belongs in a place outside Belize and who is outside Belize at the time of supply, provided the supplier maintains sufficient contemporaneous documentary evidence. An EdTech IBC supplying online language-learning subscriptions to non-resident end-users therefore qualifies for 0% GST. Where Apple IAP or Google Play Billing is used as merchant-of-record, the IBC's taxable supply is normally to the non-Belize platform operator; the zero-rating analysis then rests on the contract between the IBC and the platform, the platform's non-resident status, and the exclusively offshore delivery of the software-as-a-service. The same supply will be recharacterized as taxable at 12.5% if the recipient or beneficial user is in Belize or if the evidence of foreign residence is inadequate.

  • Customs Export Declaration, if physical goods or tangible media are exported; for pure online EdTech services this declaration is not required
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, for physical export shipments only
  • Signed commercial contract, subscription agreement, or platform merchant agreement identifying the non-Belize customer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/payment-provider receipt evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds, where available from Belize banking channels
  • Apple App Store / Google Play settlement reports or payout statements showing merchant identity, transaction amounts, and foreign user locations when the platform is merchant-of-record
  • Evidence of the end-user's non-Belize residence and jurisdiction of benefit, including billing address, IP address, device geolocation, and account country
  • Digital service consumption logs or course access records showing the service was delivered and enjoyed outside Belize
  • GST invoices issued at 0% and an internal reconciliation mapping revenue recognized to payment rail settlements

Belize does not operate a real-time electronic invoicing clearance system. There is no national e-invoice API, continuous transaction control regime, digital fiscal stamping, or pre-clearance approval from BTSD. GST-registered persons must, however, issue tax invoices containing the statutory particulars, retain complete accounting records for at least 7 years, and file periodic electronic GST returns through the BTSD web portal. Electronically generated invoices, platform settlement invoices, and Apple/Google accounting statements are acceptable where complete and auditable.

BTSD can audit export substantiation files and cross-check app-store/payment reports against declared revenue. If the zero-rating evidence is incomplete or false, BTSD may recharacterize the supply as taxable in Belize, assess 12.5% GST on the gross proceeds, disallow input-tax credits or refunds, and recover unpaid tax with interest and late-payment penalties. Assessment periods extend to at least 6 years, and there is no time limit in cases of fraud, false statements, or willful evasion. Knowingly obtaining a GST benefit by false documentation is a criminal offense exposing directors, officers, and registered agents to fines and imprisonment under the GST Act and Belize criminal legislation. An IBC engaged in unauthorized business with Belize residents or materially violating the IBC exemption may also face loss of exempt status, striking off, or reinstatement penalties, and registered agents are expected to report serious non-compliance through AML/CFT channels.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize Dollar (BZD) is pegged to the USD at 2:1. Current account transactions, including software subscription revenues, royalties, and cross-border service payments invoiced in foreign currencies, are freely convertible through licensed authorized dealers. Capital account transactions generally require exchange control approval, but Belize International Business Companies (IBCs) are statutorily classified as non-resident and therefore fall outside the domestic exchange control perimeter; provided no transactions are conducted with Belize residents or in BZD, profit remittance and capital repatriation face no onshore convertibility hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act, onshore resident exporters must repatriate and surrender export proceeds to an authorized dealer within 90 days of shipment. However, IBCs are exempt from all exchange control surrender obligations by virtue of their non-resident status under the International Business Companies Act; EdTech subscription receipts derived from foreign users are payable to accounts outside Belize and do not enter the Belizean exchange control jurisdiction.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) acting as the Foreign Exchange Administration under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 55 of the Laws of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for regulated fintech EMI onboarding. Belize does not maintain a separate electronic money institution licensing framework; no Belize-domiciled EMI can lawfully open a local digital wallet in the IBC's name. Cross-border fintech EMIs domiciled in the UK, EU, or Hong Kong may accept the IBC on a case-by-case basis, but they will require substantive UBO disclosure, evidence of Apple/Google payout flows, and a clean sanctions profile. Given the target payment rails (Apple_IAP, Google_Play_Billing), direct EMI accounts are not operationally necessary; platform aggregators settle in bulk via international wire transfers.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections should be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's corporate bank to the IBC's foreign currency settlement account at Belize Bank Limited or an equivalent offshore clearing account. Use purpose code 'C.24 – Other Investment: Loans and Credits' for shareholder loans and 'S.12 – Investment Fund Shares' or 'A.6 – Direct Investment Equity Capital' for paid-up capital, as mapped to the IMF Balance of Payments classification. Wire instructions must include the IBC's full legal name, account number, and reference 'Capital Contribution — BZ_IBC' or 'Shareholder Loan — BZ_IBC'. For high-value or contractually structured procurements, a Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700) may be used with onshore correspondent banks in USD or EUR. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for an International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270, s. 6(1)); the domestic general corporate rate is 25% under the Income Tax Act (Cap. 55) and applies only if IBC tax-exempt status is lost or not maintained.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Dividends, distributions, and deemed distributions paid by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders are statutorily exempt from all Belize withholding taxes under IBC Act (Cap. 270) s. 6(1)(c).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filed within 60 days after each anniversary date of incorporation; the annual government license fee is payable by the same anniversary date, failing which the late fee regime is triggered.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within six (6) months after the end of the IBC's financial year (e.g., by 30 June for a 31 December financial year-end).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No codified Local File or Master File monetary threshold is published under Belize's Income Tax Act; the Commissioner may demand arm's-length documentation, benchmarking studies, and financial statements for any related-party transaction where adjustment is considered. Conservative compliance practice: prepare and maintain contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation where annual cross-border related-party transactions exceed BZD 2,000,000 (approx. USD 1,000,000), and full OECD-aligned Local File/Master File where annual group revenue exceeds EUR 750 million under BEPS Action 13 reporting standards.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act, 2019 applies in full. The EdTech IBC deriving income from automated language-learning software, in-app purchases, and subscription licensing is presumed to carry on 'Intellectual Property Business' and 'Distribution and Service Centre Business' and must demonstrate the following in Belize: (i) management and direction exercised in Belize; (ii) sufficient qualified full-time employees; (iii) physical operating premises; (iv) adequate annual operating expenditure; and (v) core income-generating activities performed in Belize. An IBC may avoid the substance test only by proving tax residence in a foreign jurisdiction through a valid certificate of tax residence; otherwise, the presumption of insufficient substance is applied, exposing the entity to penalties and reclassification as a Belize tax resident.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Sham Transactions

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act (Cap. 55), the Commissioner may assess unpaid tax at 25% on the concealed income, impose a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded, and charge interest at 10% per annum; criminal prosecution may lead to a fine of up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years. The Economic Substance Act simultaneously imposes a BZD 50,000 civil penalty plus BZD 5,000 per day, and the IBC may be struck off.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders who knowingly participate in or authorize the evasion are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax, penalties, and interest; Belize courts will pierce the corporate veil where the IBC is used as a mere instrument of fraud, and travel bans as well as asset-freezing orders may be issued in criminal tax proceedings.
Economic Substance Failure and Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Act, 2019: failure to satisfy the substance test attracts a civil penalty of up to BZD 25,000 per financial year; failure to file the return attracts BZD 50,000 plus a daily penalty of BZD 5,000; the IFSC may strike the IBC off the register, automatically forfeiting the tax exemption and restoring full 25% income tax exposure on worldwide income.

Director personal liability: Officers who sign false or misleading substance declarations may be fined up to BZD 250,000 and imprisoned for up to 2 years; directors may be disqualified from acting as directors or managers of any Belize-licensed entity for up to 5 years and may be personally sued for resulting revenue losses.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Transparency Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the IBC Act (Cap. 270) and the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act, 2008 (as amended), non-compliance attracts fines up to BZD 10,000 for the IBC and up to BZD 1,000,000 for the IBC or its officers; imprisonment up to 10 years applies to individuals convicted of willful AML breaches; the IFSC may suspend or cancel the IBC's registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and the licensed registered agent are personally liable to the IFSC for non-disclosure and may be subject to civil restitution orders, court-ordered travel restrictions, and international asset-freezing orders; director bank accounts in Belize can be garnished, and criminal prosecution may lead to extradition.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Profit Repatriation Red Flags

Statutory sanction: The Income Tax Act (Cap. 55) arm's-length adjustment provision allows the Commissioner to recharacterize and reallocate revenue, impose the 25% corporate rate on the adjusted amount, and add a penalty of up to 100% of the understated income; the transaction may also be voided under general anti-avoidance rules; the ESR Authority can additionally impose the substance penalties above.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute or authorize artificial pricing arrangements are jointly and severally liable for the shortfall in tax and penalties; foreign courts may pierce the corporate veil and treat the IBC and its director as the alter ego of the ultimate parent; travel bans, extradition, and imprisonment for deliberate cross-border tax fraud are available in the jurisdiction where the taxable economic activity truly occurs, in addition to Belize judicial remedies.
