Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize, and the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry; official e-services portal: Belize Tax Service e-Tax portal (taxs.gov.bz) and Registry portal (registry.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity forming an International Business Company (IBC) engages a licensed registered agent in Belize, files Articles of Incorporation with the Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry under the International Business Companies Act, and receives a Certificate of Incorporation and Registrar Number. The Registrar Number is the IBC's statutory tax identity and TIN-equivalent for banking, licensing, and withholding purposes. The IBC is not required to register with the Belize Tax Service for income tax or General Sales Tax because it is statutorily tax-exempt and is prohibited from conducting domestic business with Belize residents; however, if a 50-year exemption certificate is not automatically issued, the registered agent applies to the Tax Service Department for the exemption certificate. No separate TIN acquisition is required unless the IBC later undertakes domestic taxable activity, in which case a GST registration number would be required from the Tax Service Department.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid mismatch election, or elective pass-through mechanism exists under Belize tax law. An IBC's exempt status is automatic and mandatory under the International Business Companies Act. The company may elect to be struck off, re-domiciled, or voluntarily converted to a domestic company under the Act, in which case it becomes subject to ordinary Belize tax treatment. Foreign jurisdiction classification elections, such as a U.S. check-the-box, do not affect Belize tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 50,000 (approx. USD 25,000) in taxable turnover within any consecutive twelve-month period; IBCs are generally outside the threshold because they may not make domestic taxable supplies and only supply nonresident customers

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the General Sales Tax Act, supplies are zero-rated where goods are exported from Belize within the statutory period, or where services are supplied to a nonresident recipient and are not used in Belize. For the IBC's financial portfolio tracking cloud software, the service is supplied to nonresident customers over the internet; the revenue is generally outside the territorial scope of Belize GST, and if considered a taxable supply it qualifies for zero-rating upon production of proof of nonresident status, evidence that the service is consumed outside Belize, the export service invoice, and foreign exchange receipts.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic export entry showing exit from Belize)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for goods exports from Belize)
  • Commercial Invoice or Service Invoice clearly marked zero-rated
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of foreign consideration
  • Contract or order documentation identifying the nonresident customer and place of use

Belize does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based electronic invoicing for GST. Registered taxable persons must submit GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Service e-Tax portal and must issue prescribed tax invoices containing the supplier name, GST registration number, supply description, consideration, and tax amount. Records must be retained for at least six years for audit purposes.

The Belize Tax Service may audit export zero-rating claims and require full documentation proving the supply left Belize or was enjoyed outside Belize. Failure to substantiate a zero-rated claim within 180 days results in assessment of GST at 12.5% on the invoice value, plus penalties up to 100% of the tax due, statutory interest, and possible criminal prosecution for fraudulent export claims under the General Sales Tax Act and the Money Laundering (Prevention) Act. The Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry may also strike off or revoke the IBC status for filing false declarations or for conducting unlicensed local business.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is legally pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, with the CBB maintaining full convertibility for all domestically issued banknotes and reserves. Belize applies a split regime: current-account transactions (service fees, royalties, software subscriptions) are freely convertible through authorised dealers on presentation of underlying invoices, while capital-account movements (equity injections, shareholder loans, liquidation proceeds) require case-by-case Exchange Control approval unless the account holder is a non-resident or an exempt International Business Company. A Belize IBC is expressly exempted from the Exchange Control Act by section 6 of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), so profit remittances, dividends, and loan repayments to non-resident shareholders are not subject to CBB convertibility certificates, surrender requirements, or repatriation barriers. The only residual friction is at the correspondent-bank level (OFAC and US KYC screening), not at the CBB level.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Regulations require resident exporters and service providers to offer foreign-currency proceeds to an authorised dealer within three months of receipt and to comply with any CBB direction to surrender all or part of such proceeds at the pegged rate. For the exempt IBC, the IBC Act disapplies the Exchange Control Act: no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline apply to ISIC 6201/NAICS 511210 software-subscription revenue received by the IBC. The residual obligations are only AML/CFT reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit for large or suspicious transactions and the maintenance of transfer-pricing documentation evidencing the foreign-source character of the revenue.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) - the sole monetary, foreign-exchange and banking-supervision authority, established under the Central Bank of Belize Act (Cap. 262, Laws of Belize). The CBB, acting jointly with the Ministry of Finance, administers the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 36) and the Exchange Control (Authorised Dealers) Regulations, and issues banking licences under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act (as amended).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant route: the board first passes a resolution fixing initial paid-in capital at USD 50,000 (no statutory minimum, but an adequate equity layer improves bankability and EMI acceptance). The non-resident shareholder then instructs a SWIFT MT103 wire from his or her own bank account using purpose narration 'CAPITAL SUB', ISO 20022 code 'SECU', and the IBC registration number in the beneficiary reference field. The Belize receiving bank screens the ordering customer against OFAC/UN/EU sanctions, matches the wire against the board resolution, and verifies the certificate of incumbency and UBO register extract before crediting. The funds are held either in the Capital Escrow Account (Belize Bank Limited) or the operational USD account (Atlantic Bank Limited). No CBB capital-import certificate, surrender obligation, or exchange-control approval is required for the exempt IBC. Stripe and Apple IAP settlements are not capital: they must arrive as merchant payouts into the Operational Account with the payment-aggregator settlement reference attached.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for all vendor and SaaS disbursements above USD 10,000 (AWS, OpenAI, marketing platforms), quoting the invoice number in field 70 and using charge code BEN; the Belize clearing bank routes each message through its New York correspondent for Fedwire settlement. Recurring sub-threshold payouts are batched via MT101 or the bank's host-to-host API with pre-vetted beneficiary aliases to reduce OFAC screening delays. Dividends and shareholder loan repayments are executed by MT103 with narration 'DIV' or 'LOAN REPAYMENT'; no IBC withholding tax applies, but the remitter bank requires an up-to-date certificate of incumbency and a UBO-consent letter. For physical procurement such as data-centre hardware, the accepted trade instrument is a sight or usance Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Belize clearing bank and confirmed by the seller's bank; for this cloud-native software business, LC usage is expected to be negligible and MT103 settles over 95% of obligations.Outright USD/BZD forward contracts with a CBB-authorised dealer - legally permissible, though functionally redundant because the fixed peg at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 eliminates bilateral BZD/USD exchange risk.USD/EUR and USD/GBP outright commercial forwards with the Belize clearing bank or a licensed international counterparty, in tenors up to 12 months, to hedge non-USD SaaS receivables from EU and UK users.Cross-currency swaps (CCIRS) for tenor-matched hedging of any future non-USD borrowing or intercompany liability against USD revenue streams.FX swaps (spot against forward) for short-dated liquidity conversion between USD, EUR, and GBP settlement balances.BZD NDFs are not market-available and are not a permitted instrument under the CBB's tightly controlled foreign-exchange framework; the permissible substitute for any perceived offshore BZD exposure is the onshore authorised-dealer outright forward.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium, with severe but manageable constraints. Belize has no domestic EMI licence class, so the IBC cannot itself act as an EMI and may only hold client funds through third-party licensed institutions. EU/UK-based EMIs (Wise Business, Payoneer, Airwallex, Dukascopy) generally accept Belize IBCs only under enhanced due diligence, requiring a licensed Belize registered-agent introduction, a natural-person UBO chain, and B2B SaaS invoicing as the stated account purpose. US-based EMIs (Mercury, Relay, Brex) typically decline Belize IBCs absent a US EIN and demonstrable US business substance, due to the offshore IBC classification and correspondent de-risking. The resilient architecture is a CBB-licensed bank account as the treasury anchor, one EU EMI for EUR/GBP receivable diversification, and direct USD settlement from Stripe and Apple IAP into the anchor account.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Belize International Business Company (IBC). A Belize onshore company subject to the Income Tax Act is chargeable at 25% on Belize-source profits, but a compliant IBC is outside that charge for its offshore income, profits, capital gains, and distributions.
