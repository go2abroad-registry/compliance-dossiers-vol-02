Competent corporate registry

Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry, Belize City (Ministry of Financial Services); registered agents are licensed and supervised by the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC).

Governing statute

The International Business Companies Act (Chapter 270, Revised Edition 2020) of the Laws of Belize - incorporation under Part II (ss. 5-15); registered office and agent under s. 12; notice of first directors/officers; 50-year tax exemption under s. 130.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form IBC-1 Application to Incorporate, accompanied by the Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association; the Certificate of Incorporation is the statutory instrument evidencing formation.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), a department of the Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; official taxpayer portal at https://taxservice.gov.bz.

E-government filing portal

An offshore International Business Company (IBC) is incorporated by a licensed registered agent under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270, and files its annual license fee with the Belize Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR). The IBC is a non-resident tax-exempt vehicle, so it is not required to obtain a Belize Income Tax TIN for offshore income. However, if the IBC makes taxable in-country supplies or voluntarily registers for General Sales Tax (GST), it must apply to the Belize Tax Service using the Taxpayer Registration Form accompanied by the certificate of incorporation, registered agent details, and supporting identity documents. The BTS issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN) in the format 'BZ-########' for GST, employer withholding, and other domestic tax obligations. For pure offshore activities, the registered agent may also obtain a 'non-resident exempt' notation in the BTS register.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: An IBC incorporated under Cap. 270 is exempt from Belize income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and exchange control on all non-Belize-sourced income, and is treated as non-resident for Belize tax purposes provided it does not carry on business in Belize with Belize residents or own Belize land.

Available election forms

Belize law provides no general check-the-box election. Under section 2 of the Income and Business Tax Act, an IBC may irrevocably elect to 'opt in' to the domestic tax system by giving written notice to the Belize Tax Service, thereby surrendering its statutory exemption and becoming taxable at the standard income/business tax rate. For hybrid mismatch purposes, Belize has not enacted OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch legislation; an IBC remains eligible for US federal pass-through classification only through a Form 8832 entity classification election made by its beneficial owner for US tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 annual taxable turnover is the mandatory GST registration threshold, raised from BZD 40,000 effective 1 July 2021; voluntary registration is permitted below the threshold for businesses making taxable supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated under section 6 of the GST Act where the supplier proves export within 90 days of supply. Services (including logistics fleet-route optimization and telematics SaaS) are zero-rated as exported services where the supply is made to a non-resident person who is outside Belize at the time of supply, the services relate to goods delivered outside Belize, or the benefit is enjoyed wholly outside Belize. An IBC supplying such digital SaaS to non-resident logistics customers qualifies for zero-rating if the recipient has no Belize establishment and the services are consumed outside Belize; evidence of non-resident status and foreign consumption must be retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Entry Form) lodged with the Belize Customs Department
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export from a Belize port
  • Commercial Invoice bearing the supplier's GST registration number and a zero-rating annotation
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (when required by the importing jurisdiction)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or wire-transfer bank receipt evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Foreign customs entry evidence or signed certificate of receipt from the non-resident customer

Belize does not mandate continuous transaction controls, real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoicing API clearance, or digital stamping. Registered GST traders must issue simplified tax invoices for taxable supplies and file monthly GST returns electronically via the Belize Tax Service online portal (Form GST-01) on or before the 15th day of the following month, with electronic payment due on the same date. Electronic fiscal devices and digital invoice clearance are not yet compulsory.

GST returns may be audited and assessments reopened by the Belize Tax Service within four years of the filing date; where zero-rating is claimed fraudulently or without the required export substantiation, the BTS may assess the uncollected GST at 12.5%, impose penalties of up to 100% of the tax under section 44 of the GST Act, charge interest at the statutory rate, suspend or cancel GST registration, and order retrospective clawback of refunds. Willful evasion is a criminal offense punishable by fines up to BZD 10,000 and imprisonment for up to two years, with directors, officers, and agents jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is hard-pegged to the US dollar at BZD 2.00 per USD 1.00. Belize maintains separate exchange-control treatment for residents versus non-residents. Current account transactions for Belize residents are generally free of exchange-control impediments, but resident capital account movements require Central Bank approval. Non-resident International Business Companies (IBCs) are statutorily exempt from exchange-control restrictions; their capital accounts, operational revenues, and profit distributions may be freely converted and remitted through authorized dealers. Practical remittance hurdles are therefore documentary rather than regulatory: banks demand source-of-funds, beneficial ownership, and substance evidence before processing dividends or intercompany payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Belize residents engaging in export activities must surrender foreign currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer under the Exchange Control Regulations. The normal statutory timeline is prompt surrender upon receipt, and the mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 100% of export proceeds received, unless the Central Bank grants an exemption or the exporter holds an external account designation. For a non-resident Belize IBC, the surrender rules do not apply; all export proceeds, subscription fees, or telematics service income may be retained in USD or any other permitted foreign currency without mandatory conversion to BZD.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (as sole monetary and exchange control authority under the Central Bank of Belize Act and the Exchange Control Act)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Belize does not maintain a separate e-money institution licensing category. An offshore digital EMI cannot independently bank a Belize IBC without a local banking license or an authorized-dealer arrangement. Foreign fintech platforms such as Mercury, Wise, or Airwallex may be unable to onboard a Belize IBC because of enhanced correspondent-bank de-risking, lack of local purpose codes, and AML/KYC extraterritorial concerns. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Belize imposes no dividend withholding tax on distributions made by an IBC to non-resident shareholders; IBC distributions are outside Belizean tax jurisdiction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable — a tax-exempt Belize IBC has no Belize corporate income tax base, therefore local transfer-pricing documentation thresholds and Local File/Master File obligations do not arise. If the IBC is ever converted or redomesticated as a Belize resident company, arm’s-length pricing of related-party logistics SaaS transactions would need to be supported by functional analysis and contemporary documentation consistent with OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines; Belize does not publish a separate local-currency threshold for IBCs.

Economic substance scope: As of the current legislative framework, Belize has not imposed Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) specifically on IBCs comparable to those of BVI/Cayman. The IBC’s tax exemption does not depend on demonstrating economic substance, but foreign tax authorities may apply their own CFC, place-of-effective-management, or economic-substance doctrines against a logistics-fleet SaaS IBC. To reduce recharacterization risk, the IBC should maintain substance commensurate with its business: board minutes evidencing strategic decisions, real technical/contractual personnel, third-party service agreements, telematics data-processing contracts, bank accounts, and accounting records outside Belize.
