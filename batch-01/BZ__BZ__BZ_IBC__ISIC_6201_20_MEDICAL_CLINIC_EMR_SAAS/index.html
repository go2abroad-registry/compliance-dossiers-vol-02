Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Belize Tax Service (BTS), an executive agency of the Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment, is the sole competent national tax administration for income tax, General Sales Tax (GST), and stamp duty, operating the official e-filing and taxpayer portal at https://taxservices.gov.bz. The Belize Companies Registry, also under the Ministry of Finance, administers International Business Company (IBC) incorporation, continuation, annual returns, and certificates of good standing. Belize operates a unitary national fiscal system with no sub-national VAT, sales tax, or income tax distinctions, and no separate municipal tax registration for this entity type.

E-government filing portal

A qualifying Belize IBC is statutorily exempt from all Belizean taxes under section 5(1) of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270, Revised Edition 2020), including income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, business tax, and stamp duty; therefore, no Belize income-tax TIN or GST registration number is required for a purely offshore IBC. The standard operating procedure for a foreign-owned EMR SaaS IBC is: (i) incorporate through a licensed registered agent and maintain a Belize City registered office; (ii) obtain a Certificate of Good Standing and a Tax Exemption Letter from the Belize Companies Registry evidencing the fifty-year statutory tax exemption from the date of incorporation (or continuation); (iii) apply to the Belize Tax Service for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) only where a Belizean bank account, a domestic counterparty, or a conversion to a domestic company requires one, submitting the Certificate of Incorporation, register of directors and shareholders, certified passport copy of the ultimate beneficial owner, and registered-office certificate; and (iv) file the annual IBC return through the registered agent, including a nil tax declaration and, where the entity is a Belize tax resident carrying on a relevant activity, the economic substance reporting return. If the IBC carries on business with Belizean residents or holds Belizean real property, it forfeits IBC status and must promptly register for income tax and GST as a domestic taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch election regime. An IBC is a corporation subject to entity-level taxation at a zero per cent effective rate by statutory exemption rather than by election. Available statutory mechanisms include: (i) conversion of an IBC into a domestic ordinary company under the Companies Act (Cap. 250) by special resolution filed with the Companies Registry, thereby surrendering the IBC tax exemption and becoming a resident Belizean taxpayer subject to normal income tax and GST; (ii) continuation of a foreign company into Belize as an IBC under Part XIII of the IBC Act, which preserves the exemption date and benefits; (iii) voluntary GST registration where a Belize-resident subsidiary is interposed to contract with domestic parties while the IBC retains offshore licensing revenue; and (iv) for U.S. federal tax purposes, classification of an eligible foreign-owned IBC under U.S. Treas. Reg. section 301.7701-3 (Form 8832) as a disregarded entity or partnership, though such election has no effect on Belizean tax status. Belize has not domesticated OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch rules; the IBC Economic Substance Regulations nevertheless require a Belize-tax-resident IBC carrying on a relevant activity (including software and SaaS) to maintain adequate physical presence, local expenditure, and locally managed boards, and any claim of non-residence must be evidenced by documentation of management and control outside Belize.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Compulsory GST registration applies under the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 65) where the value of taxable supplies made in the course of a business exceeds BZ$75,000 (Belize dollars) in any consecutive twelve-month period, equivalent to approximately US$37,500 at the fixed pegged rate of BZ$2 = US$1. Registration must be applied for within 28 days of the end of the month in which the threshold is crossed. A purely offshore IBC is outside the scope of Belize GST because it makes no taxable supplies in Belize, and therefore no registration threshold is triggered; voluntary registration is available to a business making zero-rated exports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Belize zero-rates the export of goods and qualifying exported services under the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 65) and its Schedules. For an EMR clinical workflow SaaS provider, zero-rating eligibility requires: (i) the recipient is a non-resident of Belize, being a foreign-licensed clinic, hospital group, or health system, and the services are supplied for use or consumption wholly outside Belize; (ii) the supply consists of qualifying exported services such as software-as-a-service, cloud hosting, data processing, remote EMR workflow, maintenance, and support delivered from servers located outside Belize with no service performance in Belize; (iii) the IBC is not a Belize tax resident and its contracts, invoicing, payment routing, and management are effected offshore in foreign currency; and (iv) for any tangible goods, physical exportation occurs under a valid Belize customs export declaration. A GST-registered domestic exporter may reclaim input GST attributable to zero-rated exported supplies by filing a monthly GST return showing the zero-rated exports and claiming the corresponding input credit, with refunds payable within the statutory examination period.

  • Customs Export Declaration: the Belize Customs automated export entry certified by the Belize Customs and Excise Department at the port of departure, evidencing date, value, HS code, and foreign destination; for services, the executed Master SaaS Agreement and Statement of Work supplemented by server-region certificates, IP-delivery logs, and cloud-hosting records demonstrating performance wholly outside Belize
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill: for physical exports, the original negotiable bill of lading or master air waybill naming the IBC as consignor and the foreign medical institution as consignee; for EMR SaaS exports, the equivalent documentary evidence comprises the executed subscription contract, the offshore cloud infrastructure certificate, and electronic delivery records proving that no service was rendered or consumed in Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate: the SWIFT MT103 or MT202 credit advice, or the IBC's offshore bank statement, confirming receipt of the foreign-currency export proceeds in the IBC's designated bank account, cross-referenced to the invoice number, contract reference, and subscription date

Belize does not mandate a real-time electronic invoicing clearance system, continuous-control fiscal API, or digital tax stamping regime comparable to Latin American electronic invoicing mandates. GST-registered businesses must issue serially numbered fiscal invoices and receipts in the form prescribed by the Belize Tax Service and must file monthly GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Information Portal, retaining records for the statutory period. A pure offshore IBC has no domestic electronic fiscal invoicing obligation; its standard commercial SaaS invoices and receipts are sufficient, provided they are serially numbered and reference the contract, subscription term, and payment currency. If the IBC or a Belizean subsidiary registers for GST, the prescribed domestic invoice format, e-filing cycle, and audit-trail requirements become mandatory.

The Belize Tax Service may conduct an audit within the statutory assessment periods, and cases involving fraud, wilful default, or failure to file are not time-barred. Where a registered exporter claims zero-rating or input GST credits to which it is not entitled, the BTS will disallow the claim, claw back the full input credit, and impose statutory interest together with civil penalties, which may reach a multiple of the tax evaded. Fabricating export documentation, mischaracterizing domestic EMR subscriptions as foreign exports, or falsely invoicing Belizean health providers as offshore constitutes an offence under the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 65) and customs legislation, and renders directors and officers personally liable to fines and imprisonment. For a pure IBC, the effective penalty framework operates through status loss: failure to substantiate the offshore character of revenue, or non-compliance with the economic substance regulations, may cause the BTS to treat the IBC as a Belizean resident domestic taxpayer, reopening historical tax-exempt years and exposing the entity to retrospective assessment at the 12.5 per cent GST headline rate and the domestic corporate income tax rate, together with interest, penalties, and potential revocation of IBC status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize maintains a fixed exchange rate of BZD 2:1 to USD. The capital account is selectively convertible and generally requires Central Bank approval for non-exempt resident transactions, while the current account is freely convertible for bona fide cross-border trade and service payments supported by invoices and contracts. A Belize International Business Company is statutorily treated as a non-resident entity; it may maintain foreign-currency accounts, receive subscription revenue abroad, and remit profits or dividends without local tax, and no Belize-sourced income arises from offshore SaaS operations. Practical convertibility is unfettered for USD-denominated flows, but banks will require documentary evidence of the beneficial owner, the source of funds, and the commercial substance of the EMR SaaS activity before allowing outward transfer of capital or profit distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Regulations, exporters of goods are required to collect the full invoice value within a prescribed realization period and surrender foreign-currency proceeds to an authorized dealer in accordance with Central Bank directions unless a specific exemption is granted. For cross-border services supplied by the Belize IBC, including medical EMR SaaS subscriptions, the foreign-currency invoices are considered invisible exports; the proceeds must be repatriated into the IBC's approved foreign-currency account through the named correspondent bank, and because the IBC is a non-resident offshore entity the mandatory conversion to Belize dollars does not apply. The IBC must nevertheless document each receipt against the underlying invoice, contract, and payment gateway settlement report for exchange-control and AML/CFT audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262 of the Laws of Belize, with exchange control administration under the Exchange Control Act and the Exchange Control Regulations)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Belize has no licensing or passporting regime for foreign electronic-money institutions, and offshore digital EMIs are not recognized as authorized deposit-taking institutions for the purpose of holding the IBC's corporate treasury funds or capital escrow. A Belize-regulated bank remains the only acceptable primary banking layer; an unlicensed EMI may only be used as an ancillary payment gateway for customer collection if it is independently licensed in a recognized jurisdiction, but it must not hold the IBC's ultimate treasury balance and must not be represented as a Belize bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The shareholder capital injection must be routed through a licensed correspondent bank to the IBC's Belize-based USD ledger using SWIFT MT103 for wire complexity, with the ordering customer name, the IBC beneficiary name, and the field 70 remittance information stating 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION TO NON-RESIDENT IBC — NOT COMMERCIAL REVENUE'. The required exchange-control purpose code for the receiving Belize bank is a non-resident capital transfer; the bank will require the shareholders' notarized identification, a board resolution approving the funding, and the completed source-of-funds questionnaire to evidence that the injection is equity and not undisclosed taxable income or crypto-tainted proceeds. The funds must land in the capital escrow sub-account rather than the operational account, and any subsequent movement to the operating ledger should be recorded as an inter-account transfer supported by the same resolution.Trade settlements for the EMR SaaS operations will use SWIFT MT103 for vendor payments, subcontractor license fees, cloud-infrastructure costs, and regulatory-assistance invoices, with clear invoice references and the IBC's non-resident status stated in the remittance narrative. For high-value procurement of hardware, servers, or medical-integration equipment, a commercial Letter of Credit (MT700/MT710) may be used with an advising bank in the vendor's jurisdiction to guarantee payment against presentation of bills of lading or delivery certificates; the Belize issuing bank will require a collateralized balance in the IBC's USD account or a standby credit undertaken by a parent entity. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% (IBC statutory exemption; domestic non-exempt resident corporations are otherwise subject to Belize corporate income tax at 25%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — distributions, dividends, deemed dividends and liquidation proceeds paid by a Belize IBC are exempt from all Belize withholding taxes.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: None (BZD 0) — a tax-exempt Belize IBC has no Belize corporate income tax assessment and therefore no mandatory Local File or Master File threshold. The arm's length principle applies only if the IBC creates a taxable Belize permanent establishment or is reclassified as a resident taxable company.

Economic substance scope: Belize Economic Substance Regulations apply to IBCs carrying on relevant activities: banking, insurance, fund management, financing and leasing, headquarters, distribution and service centres, shipping, intellectual property and pure equity holding. A Medical Clinic EMR SaaS IBC becomes subject to ESR if it holds EMR software IP for licensing or acts as a group service centre; the test requires local Belize management, employees, premises and expenditure proportionate to core income generating activities, with strict sanctions for bare IP shells.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of all hidden economic profits at the 25% Belize corporate rate, interest at 10% per annum, penalty additions up to 100% of the undeclared tax, and criminal tax fraud charges under the Belize Criminal Code and Income Tax Act, including fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who participate in or knowingly permit the fraudulent use of the IBC may be made jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax; courts may lift the corporate veil, order civil restitution, impose travel bans and issue custodial sentences for tax evasion.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act and IBC Act, the Registrar may impose administrative penalties, levy monthly default penalties for continuing non-compliance, strike the company off the register and dissolve the entity, causing assets to vest in the Belize Government at common law.

Director personal liability: Directors who allow the shell company to trade after strike-off become personally liable for the debts of the company; they may be disqualified, ordered to pay civil restitution and prosecuted for continuing to carry on business while insolvent, with imprisonment available on conviction.
AML / Beneficial Ownership / Nominee Misuse

Statutory sanction: Belize AML/CFT and IFSC compliance enforcement imposes monetary penalties for failure to submit beneficial ownership information, financial penalties on registered agents, possible asset forfeiture and criminal money-laundering charges where the EMR SaaS funds are disguised.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face personal criminal liability for false declarations, custodial sentences, travel bans and confiscation orders; the real beneficial owner can be personally exposed once veil-piercing is ordered to recover laundered or diverted funds.
Transfer Pricing / Treaty and Repatriation Abuse

Statutory sanction: Foreign tax authorities may apply CFC rules, treaty anti-abuse rules, withholding tax and transfer pricing adjustments, including Section 482 allocation in the United States and equivalent adjustments in other health-tech markets; Belize itself cannot grant relief because it has imposed no tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false certificates, inaccurate W-8BEN-E forms or misleading transfer pricing documentation are exposed to civil fraud penalties, personal restitution for diverted tax, travel restrictions and imprisonment in the source jurisdiction after lifting the corporate veil.
