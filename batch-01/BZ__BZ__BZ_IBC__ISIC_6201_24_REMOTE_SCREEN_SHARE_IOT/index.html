Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office acceptance is allowed for the registered office address only to the extent that the address is a physical street address in Belize maintained by the licensed registered agent, serving as the statutory registered office. A virtual office arrangement that includes mail forwarding and call answering is permissible, but the company must appoint a resident registered agent, not merely a virtual address provider. For a remote desktop access and unattended machine telemetry software company with no physical operations in Belize, the substantive business cannot be 'located' at the virtual office; however, the IBC regime does not require economic substance at the entity level for pure-holding/exempted activity, but the Ministry of International Financial Services is entitled to request details of the commercial substance of non-resident owned IBCs under the Economic Substance Guidance Notes (2021) if the entity is licensed or engaged in relevant activity; 'pure holding' status is available if no active business. Since the target activity is a digital software company relying on Stripe and Paddle as PSPs, the registered agent must confirm that the company does not engage in regulated financial services activities and that remote screen-share/IoT software is not a regulated activity under the International Business Companies Act; therefore, a residency-free status was maintained, but the registered office must accept service of process during business hours.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned IBC, no tax ID is obtained at incorporation; the Companies Registry-issued registration number serves as the legal identifier. If taxable Belize GST activity is contemplated, the IBC must apply to the Belize Tax Service for GST registration on Form GST-1, attaching the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles, directors'/shareholders' identification, registered office details, and expected annual turnover. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

None. Belize has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election for IBCs; the IBC is automatically and mandatorily exempt from Belize income, capital, withholding, stamp, and business taxes under the International Business Companies Act, unless it carries on trade in Belize, in which case it forfeits exempt status and becomes subject to ordinary Belize taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 150,000 of annual taxable supplies in Belize under the General Sales Tax Act, Cap. 64; registration is optional below threshold but compulsory once exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods physically removed from Belize, and services supplied to a person who belongs outside Belize and consumed wholly outside Belize, are zero-rated under the General Sales Tax (Zero-Rated Supplies) Schedule. An IBC providing remote screen-sharing/IoT telemetry software to foreign customers qualifies if the customer and usage sit outside Belize and contractual/technical evidence supports foreign consumption.

  • Customs export declaration / shipping bill
  • Bill of lading or air waybill
  • Commercial invoice with zero-rating statement
  • Foreign customer contract or purchase order
  • Evidence of foreign-currency receipt / inward remittance certificate

No national e-invoicing, real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or tax-authority e-reporting/API mandate exists for GST in Belize. Taxpayers must issue prescribed GST invoices retaining hard-copy/electronic records for audit.

False zero-rating or fictitious export claims are treated as tax evasion under the General Sales Tax Act. On audit, the Belize Tax Service may assess the unpaid GST at the standard rate, add interest at statutory rates, impose penalties of up to 100% of the tax due, and refer directors/shareholders for criminal prosecution with fines and imprisonment. Refund claims arising from zero-rated exports are subject to substantiation review within four years of the tax period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize operates a fixed exchange rate regime pegged at BZ$2.00 = US$1.00. An International Business Company is statutorily classified as a non-resident entity for exchange control purposes under the International Business Companies Act, and is expressly exempt from the Exchange Control Act. Consequently, both current account flows (software license fees, subscription revenue, vendor payments) and capital account flows (shareholder capital injections, dividend distributions, share redemption proceeds) may be conducted freely in foreign currency without Central Bank approval. No Belize withholding tax applies to dividends paid by an IBC to non-resident shareholders, and no exchange control permit is required for profit remittance. Practical hurdles are documentary rather than regulatory: the IBC must maintain valid incorporation and good-standing evidence, a filed beneficial ownership declaration, and demonstrable economic substance in offshore software and telemetry services, and it must not transact with Belize residents or carry on business locally.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Belize-resident operating exporters, the Exchange Control Regulations require foreign currency export receipts to be sold to an authorized dealer in Belize within 30 days of receipt, with a mandatory conversion ratio of 100% of foreign currency proceeds. However, an International Business Company is excluded from the definition of a resident for exchange control purposes and is therefore not subject to export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion. All export receipts generated by the IBC from remote screen-share and IoT telemetry software subscriptions may be retained offshore in fully convertible US dollars, or in any permitted foreign currency account, without conversion into Belize dollars. The IBC should maintain written notice of its non-resident status with its banking partner and ensure that all customer contracts reflect offshore export-of-service treatment.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High for this low-risk SaaS and software service scope, subject to enhanced corporate KYC and beneficial ownership disclosure. Belize IBCs are not prohibited from using offshore digital EMIs or global payment processors, and a non-resident IBC has no domestic licensing constraint in opening an EMI account. Legal constraints are limited to the IBC Act restriction against transacting with Belize residents and the requirement that accounts remain offshore-facing. In practice, EMIs may classify Belize as a higher-risk offshore jurisdiction, so additional substantive evidence, such as software agreements, merchant processing statements, and a real office or remote staffing model, is necessary. Primary recommended rail is a US-dollar EMI account paired with the existing Stripe/Paddle merchant settlement flow, while a Belize licensed commercial bank account serves as the underlying treasury anchor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must be routed through the SWIFT network using an MT103 customer credit transfer for transparent beneficiary naming. The IBC should issue a capital contribution resolution and a written owner's certificate identifying the non-resident shareholder, the amount, and the account reference. The remitting bank must include a purpose description such as IBC Paid-in Capital or Additional Capital Contribution, and the Belize receiving bank or offshore EMI should internally record the credit as an intercompany capital ledger line. Supporting documents include the signed board resolution, updated share allocation statement, source-of-funds evidence from the remitter, and confirmation that the IBC remains non-resident and not conducting business with Belize residents. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a qualifying Belize International Business Company (IBC); the domestic Belize corporate income tax headline rate of 25% is not triggered because the IBC is exempt from taxation on all non-Belize-source profits and activities.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%; Belize IBC distributions to shareholders are wholly exempt from Belize withholding tax and from income tax in the hands of non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

On or before the anniversary of incorporation; the IBC is required to pay its annual government fee and lodge the statement of compliance before the anniversary date. There is no fiscal-year income tax filing deadline because no tax return is due.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold is codified for tax-exempt Belize IBCs. Under Belize ESR, contemporaneous records are mandatory for a relevant activity without monetary threshold. Prudent global-mobile software entities adopt OECD-aligned documentation when annual related-party software licensing/intercompany service flows exceed BZD 5,000,000, and a Master File when the consolidated group exceeds BZD 50,000,000 in turnover.

Economic substance scope: The Belize Economic Substance (Amendment) Act applies ESR to IBCs carrying on relevant activities: distribution and service centre, financing and leasing, fund management, banking, insurance, shipping, headquarters, holding company base, or intellectual property business. A remote screen-share and IoT telemetry operation earning subscription or licensing income must demonstrate, in Belize: local management and control, adequate employees with appropriate qualifications, physical premises, and operating expenditure proportionate to income. A pure equity holding IBC only needs records, registered office, and compliance with corporate filings. Penalties for failure include fines, daily default penalties, exchange-of-information disclosure to foreign tax authorities, and strike-off.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and cross-border tax fraud

Statutory sanction: False declarations are sanctionable under the Belize IBC Act and the Income and Business Tax Act, with fines up to BZD 50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; the challenged IBC can lose its exempt status retroactively, resulting in tax assessment of the entire historical offshore income plus penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute the fraud face civil restitution of diverted profits, personal assessments for the company's unpaid tax through veil-piercing, travel bans while criminal proceedings are pending, and imprisonment up to 5 years.
Shell company and absence of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act, the Registrar may impose a fixed penalty of BZD 30,000 per relevant activity, a daily default penalty of BZD 3,000, and ultimately strike the company off the register; names of defaulting entities are transmitted to foreign competent authorities under tax information exchange agreements.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false substance declarations are personally liable for the penalties, are disqualified from serving as directors, can be barred from leaving Belize, and may face confiscation of personal assets used to conceal substance failure.
Money laundering and non-compliant beneficial ownership register

Statutory sanction: Money laundering is punishable by a fine of BZD 250,000 and up to 14 years' imprisonment; failure to maintain an accurate UBO register carries a civil penalty of BZD 25,000 and BZD 1,000 per day of continued violation.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners can be prosecuted as principal offenders; the court may order forfeiture of the IBC's accounts, proceeds of the subscription sales, proportional restitution to victims, and restriction orders limiting the defendant's overseas travel.
Trading while administratively dissolved

Statutory sanction: A struck-off IBC loses juridical personality; its contracts are generally unenforceable, the Government of Belize may claim its assets, and restoration is subject to court order and payment of all arrears plus restoration fees up to BZD 1,200.

Director personal liability: Directors who cause a dissolved entity to contract are personally liable for all resulting liabilities and can be prosecuted for making false filings with fines up to BZD 10,000 and imprisonment up to 2 years.
