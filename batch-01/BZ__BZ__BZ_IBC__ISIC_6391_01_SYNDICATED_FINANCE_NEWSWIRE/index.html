Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the company must state its authorized share capital in the Articles of Incorporation. Capital may be issued at par, and no escrow or bank deposit requirement applies under Belize IBC law.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable for a data-only business. If the company opens a physical office in Belize, obtain a fire safety inspection certificate from the Belize National Fire Service, verify the structural load rating of the premises, and comply with the Health and Safety at Work Act for workstations and ergonomic requirements. For a remote-only newswire API operation with no employees in Belize, no civil defense clearance is required.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the company must state its authorized share capital in the Articles of Incorporation. Capital may be issued at par, and no escrow or bank deposit requirement applies under Belize IBC law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (Belize Tax Service, 'BTS'), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; e-filing and taxpayer portal: https://www.bts.gov.bz (BTS Online e-Services). Corporate registration for IBCs is administered separately by the Registrar of International Business Companies within the Companies Registry of Belize.

E-government filing portal

An IBC is incorporated under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270 and is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and, for qualifying offshore activities, the General Sales Tax. No mandatory income tax or GST registration applies; the IBC's corporate identity comprises its Certificate of Incorporation and unique company registration number issued by the Registrar of IBCs. Where a Belize Tax Identification Number is required by foreign banking counterparties or payment rails (Swift_Wire, Stripe) for FATCA/CRS due diligence, the IBC files a written declaration with the BTS attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, register of directors and shareholders, and a statutory declaration of non-resident ownership and absence of Belize-source activity; the BTS then issues a taxpayer reference number or tax clearance certificate without imposing assessment. Annual compliance is limited to the statutory IBC renewal filing with the Companies Registry; no tax return is due for a qualifying exempt IBC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone (IBC statutory exemption): all profits, income, dividends, rents, royalties, compensation, capital gains, and other revenue derived by the IBC are exempt from Belize national taxes; the entity is treated as non-resident for exchange control and lacks nexus to Belize-source income; the exemption does not extend to land in Belize or to business conducted through a permanent establishment with Belize residents.

Available election forms

None applicable. The IBC Act confers exemption by operation of law; there is no check-the-box election, fiscal unity, or consolidated GST filing mechanism under the Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55) or the General Sales Tax Act (Cap. 305). Belize has not enacted OECD/G20 hybrid mismatch rules, and an IBC cannot elect to be taxed as a resident company or to opt into the GST regime for offshore services.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 in annual taxable supplies in any twelve-month period triggers compulsory GST registration; however, a qualifying non-resident-owned IBC making only offshore exports of services is outside the scope of Belize GST and is not a registrant.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply is zero-rated under the General Sales Tax Act when (i) goods are physically exported from Belize within the prescribed period with customs evidence; (ii) services are supplied to a non-resident for use entirely outside Belize, provided the benefit is not received in Belize; or (iii) the supply qualifies as international transport or an ancillary supply. A Belize IBC supplying syndicated finance newswire and financial market news aggregation feeds (ISIC 6391 / NAICS 519110) as exported services to non-resident subscribers via API, Swift_Wire, or Stripe may claim zero-rating on the basis of the non-resident counterparty, offshore use, and offshore payment evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Department export entry/CUSDEC)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or other proof of physical export (for goods) or delivery/usage certificate for exported services
  • Commercial invoice and executed supply contract identifying the non-resident recipient
  • Swift/Wire inward remittance advice, Stripe payout record, or bank credit confirmation evidencing receipt of offshore payment
  • Certificate of Origin or non-resident status declaration (where required)

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API integration, or digital stamping regime exists in Belize. GST-registered persons must issue sequentially numbered GST tax invoices, preserve underlying transaction records, and file monthly GST returns electronically through the BTS online portal; zero-rated export claims must be substantiated by the same documentation bundle retained for audit.

The BTS audited zero-rating is subject to retrospective reassessment within six years from the end of the accounting period (unlimited in cases of fraud or wilful default). Wrongfully claimed zero-rating is clawed back with interest and penalties, which may reach 50% of the tax evaded for civil default; fraudulent evasion additionally attracts criminal fines up to BZD 10,000 and imprisonment up to three years. The BTS may disregard artificial or sham arrangements designed to exploit the IBC exemption or zero-rating, and directors are personally liable for penalties where the IBC was used to defeat GST.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged to the US dollar at 2.0000 BZD per USD. Belize maintains a bifurcated convertibility framework: current account transactions are freely permitted for residents and non-residents upon documentary proof of an underlying transaction, whereas capital account transactions (direct investment, portfolio investment, loans) require prior approval from the Central Bank of Belize. Profit remittances by a Belize International Business Company (IBC) are not subject to domestic exchange-control limitations provided the IBC qualifies as a non-resident entity under the International Business Companies Act, maintains all offshore currency accounts in foreign currency, and derives no income from a Belize-source trading activity; distributions to non-resident shareholders are free of withholding taxes and are settled in US dollars or any freely convertible currency. No mandatory conversion of offshore-currency account balances exists for IBCs; the CBB's approval is only triggered when the IBC seeks direct investment in Belize-onshore assets or borrows locally.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For Belize-resident exporting companies, all export proceeds must be repatriated to a BZD or foreign-currency account with an authorised dealer within 90 days from the date of export and offered for sale to an authorised dealer at the official pegged rate, unless an exemption permit has been obtained from the Central Bank. Mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of net export receipts except that, with CBB approval, up to 20% may be retained in foreign currency for licit trade expenses. Belize IBCs operating as financial newswire syndication services are not 'exporters' in the exchange-control sense, so this surrender requirement does not apply to non-resident IBC revenue streams; however, any transaction involving the sale of goods from Belize territory would trigger the standard regime.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), operating under the Central Bank of Belize Act and the Exchange Control Act; the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) administers AML reporting, while the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) regulates offshore entities

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low feasibility. Belize IBCs are generally treated as higher-risk legal persons by regulated electronic money institutions (EMIs) due to the non-transparent bearer-share history and lighter statutory filing regime. No Belize-licensed EMI exists under the International Financial Services Commission; therefore a Belize IBC cannot obtain a domestic EMI licence. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Intergiro, Advcash) commonly reject Belize IBCs for merchant clearing and virtual IBAN rails because of FATF-identified reputation risk and limited financial audit trail availability. A non-resident IBC may, however, open an account with a Belize international bank (offshore bank) such as Caye International Bank Ltd. for multi-currency settlement, but this is not a fintech EMI product.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The bank reference field (field 72) must contain the IBC registration number and the purpose phrase 'INCAP — Equity Contribution to Belize IBC'. Supporting verification includes the board resolution approving the equity call, the issued-share certificate, an updated register of members, and a tax-receipt/foreign-exchange reference from the remitting jurisdiction. No cash deposits or third-party wire transfers are permitted.
