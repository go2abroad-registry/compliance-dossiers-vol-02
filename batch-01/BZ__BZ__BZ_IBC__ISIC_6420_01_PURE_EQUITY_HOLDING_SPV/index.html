Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Administration (BTA), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; official web portal: https://www.taxadministration.gov.bz/

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Belize IBC obtains a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by submitting an application through its licensed registered agent to the Belize Tax Administration, accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, a declaration of beneficial ownership, and the registered agent's due diligence file. For a pure equity holding SPV, the TIN is issued without requiring local tax filings during the statutory tax-exemption period, and is used for banking, customs, and cross-border invoicing purposes. The IBC is not automatically registered for income tax; registration with the BTA is limited to obtaining the TIN and, if applicable, VAT registration for taxable local supplies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Belize law for an International Business Company. The IBC is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and business tax on all foreign-source income and transactions, with the exemption effective for a minimum of 20 years from incorporation (renewable by election for subsequent periods). The sole statutory election available is the optional election to be treated as a resident ordinary Belize company, which would subject the entity to Belize income tax at the standard corporate rate; this election is irrevocable except with ministerial consent and is not recommended for a passive equity holding SPV.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

An enterprise is required to register for VAT if its annual taxable turnover for goods or services exceeds BZD $75,000. A pure equity holding SPV receiving only dividends, capital gains, and proceeds from the disposal of shares is not engaged in a taxable supply of goods or services and therefore generally remains below the registration threshold; voluntary registration may be made where input VAT refunds are sought.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A taxable person may zero-rate exports of goods where the goods are physically exported from Belize within a prescribed period and the supplier retains evidence of export; exports of services are zero-rated only if the services are supplied to a non-resident and are not used or consumed in Belize. A pure equity holding SPV selling shares or receiving offshore dividends is not a supplier of taxable services; such transactions are outside the scope of Belize VAT, and no zero-rating claim is necessary. If the SPV were to invoice management or treasury services to a foreign affiliate, the invoice must explicitly state zero-rated supply, and the supplier must hold the export contract, proof of foreign residence of the recipient, and evidence of foreign currency settlement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Belize Customs Form C-24) stamped by the Comptroller of Customs
  • Commercial Invoice stating the true value and zero-rated nature of the export
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice showing receipt of foreign currency from abroad
  • Packing List and Certificate of Origin (where required by the importing jurisdiction)

Belize has not implemented a mandatory real-time electronic invoicing clearance or digital stamping regime. Taxpayers must issue prescribed VAT invoices, retain them for a minimum of six years, and transmit summary data to the BTA only upon request during audit or as part of periodic VAT returns. Online VAT filing is permitted through the Belize Tax Administration portal, but no API-based fiscal clearance is mandated for standard commercial invoices.

The BTA and Customs Department maintain a joint audit program for export zero-rating claims. A false or unsupported zero-rating claim triggers reversal of the output VAT, plus a penalty equal to 100% of the tax sought to be evaded, interest at the statutory rate of 20% per annum, and monetary fines up to BZD $10,000 for a first offence; deliberate fraud may also result in criminal prosecution and imprisonment for up to three years. Retrospective clawback is permitted for a period not exceeding six years from the date of the offending return, and the entity's export registration may be suspended pending full repayment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize maintains a fixed rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 under the CBB's statutory currency peg. The International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) exempts IBCs from the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 343), rendering both current account and capital account transactions fully convertible for this non-resident pure equity holding SPV. Profit remittance and dividend repatriation incur no statutory FX approval or surrender obligation because the IBC is treated as non-resident and tax-exempt; the only administrative hurdles are standard commercial bank due diligence requirements, including board resolutions, source-of-funds evidence, and beneficial ownership disclosure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory baseline: under the Exchange Control Regulations (Cap. 343), resident exporters must offer all foreign currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer within one month of receipt and sell 100% of eligible receipts at the fixed parity. This IBC is a passive equity holder with no export trade and is expressly designated non-resident under the IBC Act; therefore, the surrender regime is not triggered. Should any residual receivable arise, the Belize custodial bank would classify the IBC as non-resident, permitting full retention in a USD-denominated account without conversion or surrender.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low for a Belize-incorporated IBC. The Banks and Financial Institutions Act (Cap. 316) requires a deposit-taking license, and the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) does not maintain an EMI licensing passport comparable to UK or EU regimes. A foreign fintech EMI may process remittances to the IBC but cannot open a Belize clearing account or hold IBC client funds domestically without a banking license; local authorized dealers will typically refuse wiring to unregulated EMI pools lacking a correspondent banking chain. Recommended posture: use regulated EMIs only as marginal payment channels for expenses below USD 25,000, never as the primary escrow or operational treasury rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's or ultimate beneficial owner's home bank to the IBC's Belize account at an authorized dealer. The wire must carry the bank-specified purpose code (e.g., 'Capital Injection / Paid-in Capital') and reference the IBC's legal name and account number. The supporting file must contain board and shareholder resolutions authorizing the capital call, subscription statements, the current register of members, a beneficial ownership declaration, and a source-of-funds narrative backed by the remitter's bank reference. Because the IBC is exchange-control exempt, no Central Bank of Belize approval is required; however, the receiving bank applies standard AML threshold screening above USD 10,000, including customer identification and suspicious transaction reporting. Funds typically settle in USD within T+1 or T+2 via the correspondent network, and the IBC should maintain a fully USD-denominated ledger to avoid any BZD conversion.Outbound flows, such as dividends to the parent entity, intercompany loans, and professional fees, clear through SWIFT MT103 instructions originated by the Belize bank. USD payments route via the bank's US correspondent Nostro account, while BZD payments clear through the Central Bank of Belize-operated domestic ACH and RTGS framework. For any trade-backed or subsidiary-financing disbursement, a Commercial Letter of Credit under UCP 600 may be issued with the IBC as instructing party and confirmed by the beneficiary's international bank. Continued default results in loss of good standing, restriction of corporate legal rights, and administrative strike-off by the Registrar.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Not applicable for an IBC because the entity has no Belize taxable income. There is no Belize statutory threshold triggering Local File or Master File preparation for a tax-exempt IBC. Group-level transfer pricing documentation obligations, including CbC reporting thresholds, are assessed by the ultimate parent jurisdiction, not by Belize. If the IBC were to lose its exempt status and become taxable, Belize would follow OECD-aligned transfer pricing principles and contemporaneous documentation standards.

Economic substance scope: The Belize International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019 applies to IBCs carrying on relevant activities. A pure equity holding SPV is expressly treated as a pure equity holding company and qualifies for reduced economic substance requirements. The SPV must comply with the IBC Act, maintaina registered office and registered agent in Belize, maintain adequate financial and shareholder records, and file or provide information as required by the competent authority. It is not required to demonstrate management and control in Belize unless it carries on a separate relevant activity such as financing, distribution, or intellectual property holding.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through Misuse of IBC Exemption

Statutory sanction: The IBC loses the protection of section 59 of the International Business Companies Act and becomes liable under the Belize Income and Business Tax Act at the prevailing corporate rate of 25%, with interest, late payment penalties, and potential criminal fines. The Belize courts and Revenue Department may impose heightened penalties for deliberate false statements and fraudulent exemption claims.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly approve false declarations or direct transactions while the exemption is invalid can be held personally liable for the unpaid taxes, subjected to civil restitution, fined, disqualified from corporate management, and in egregious cases imprisoned.
Annual Renewal Default and Administrative Strike-Off

Statutory sanction: The company loses good standing, incurs late penalty surcharges, and may be administratively struck off the Belize register. Post-strike-off, the company cannot enforce contracts, defend legal proceedings, or legally hold assets, and its assets may be subject to vesting in the Government of Belize pending restoration.

Director personal liability: Directors who trade or cause the corporation to incur obligations after dissolution or strike-off are personally liable for the resulting debts. The limited liability shield is pierced where continued trading is done with knowledge of the default; directors also face civil restitution and travel restrictions in enforcement proceedings.
Money Laundering, Beneficial Ownership Concealment, and Shell Company Abuse

Statutory sanction: Violations attract criminal liability under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act and related Belize anti-money laundering legislation. Sanctions include financial penalties calibrated to the offence, confiscation of laundered property, cancellation of corporate registration, and imprisonment of responsible natural persons. The IBC may also lose all statutory immunities.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and controlling shareholders face personal criminal conviction, asset freezing orders, travel bans, and confiscation of proceeds. Belize courts will pierce the corporate veil where the IBC is a facade for fraud, sham, or money laundering; restitution may be ordered against the individuals who controlled the vehicle.
Economic Substance Act Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The company becomes subject to penalties under the International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019, including administrative fines for each failure and daily penalties for continuing default. The Belize competent authority may also exchange information with foreign tax administrations, resulting in loss of treaty or foreign tax relief benefits elsewhere.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly file false economic substance declarations can be fined, disqualified, and held personally liable for penalties. Repeated non-compliance can trigger criminal sanctions and removal from the Belize register; foreign enforcement actions may extend to personal assets and travel restrictions.
