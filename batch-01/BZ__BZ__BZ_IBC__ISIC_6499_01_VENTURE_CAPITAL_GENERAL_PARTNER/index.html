Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (Ministry of Finance), 9/10 Treasury Building, Belmopan; corporate registry: Belize Companies Registry under the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC); e-services portal: https://www.taxservice.gov.bz/; IFSC registry portal: https://www.ifsc.gov.bz/

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned IBC is incorporated at the Belize Companies Registry through a licensed registered agent; the statutory tax identity is the IBC registration number stated on the Certificate of Incorporation. If the IBC engages in non-exempt domestic economic activity, it must apply to the Tax Service Department (TSD) using Form TIN-01 for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN). For General Sales Tax (GST) purposes, the IBC files Form GST-01 at TSD once the statutory turnover threshold is met or voluntarily to support export zero-rating. A tax-exempt offshore IBC performing pure cross-border venture-capital GP services is not ordinarily required to obtain a local income-tax TPIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270), an IBC may file an election to convert into a domestic company registered under the Companies Act, thereby revoking its IBC tax-exempt classification and subjecting it to ordinary Belize income and business taxation. No United States-style check-the-box election and no OECD hybrid-mismatch structural election exist under Belize domestic law; the domestic-conversion election is the sole material classification election. An IBC may also elect to maintain exempt status while filing the mandatory annual Economic Substance Report with the IFSC.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period; voluntary registration is available to zero-rated exporters, including offshore service suppliers

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated upon physical export from Belize evidenced by customs departure records. Services supplied by a Belize IBC venture-capital GP to a non-resident fund — including management fees, investment-advisory fees, carried-interest administration, and GP oversight services — are zero-rated as exported services where the recipient is outside Belize, the services are wholly consumed offshore, and the IBC holds the requisite IFSC license. Carried interest and capital-call allocations are capital in nature and remain outside the GST charge.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Bill of Entry with export permit stamp)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export from Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing non-resident settlement

Belize does not impose a mandatory real-time electronic-invoicing, fiscal-clearance, or digital-stamping regime. TSD accepts paper and standard electronic tax invoices. Registered persons must issue serialized GST tax invoices, retain supporting export documentation, and file GST returns through the TSD e-services portal in batch mode; no statutory API or real-time government clearance gateway is required.

TSD may conduct post-export verification audits within a six-year assessment window. Failure to substantiate the zero-rated supply with the complete customs and remittance documentation bundle triggers retrospective clawback of the 12.5% GST, statutory interest from the date of supply, and a civil penalty up to double the evaded tax. Fraudulent zero-rating claims additionally attract criminal prosecution with fines up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment up to three years, plus disqualification of the registered agent or IFSC licensee.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is legally pegged to the US dollar at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 with no intervening fluctuation band, effectively removing USD convertibility risk for current account transactions. Current account convertibility is generally permitted for trade in goods, services, and ordinary business remittances, subject to underlying documentation and authorized dealer review. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment, portfolio flows, and loan principal repayments, require prior approval of the CBB for residents; however, Belize International Business Companies (IBCs) are statutorily deemed non-resident for exchange control purposes, allowing them to maintain foreign currency accounts freely and remit funds abroad without CBB approval, provided no transaction involves a Belize resident or Belize-sourced activity. Profit remittance from a Belize operating subsidiary or local venture investment would trigger the capital account approval process and require evidence of tax clearance and audited financials, whereas an IBC GP vehicle repatriating capital or carried interest from offshore sources faces no practical remittance barrier.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control (Export of Goods) Regulations, a Belize-based exporter must ensure full foreign currency proceeds are received through an authorized dealer within six months from the date of shipment or within such extended period approved by the CBB. For goods shipped to CARICOM or designated countries, the surrender requirement obliges conversion of not less than 100% of foreign currency proceeds into Belize dollars at the official pegged rate within the mandated timeframe, unless the exporter obtains blanket or specific retention approval from the CBB. For the VC GP entity structured as a BZ IBC, export proceeds surrender rules do not apply because the IBC is outside Belize's exchange control net; however, if the GP operates through a domestic Belize subsidiary or manages onshore investments, the full surrender obligation must be observed and any exemption sought in writing.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), operating under the Central Bank of Belize Act and the Exchange Control Regulations (CAP. 147)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low. Belize does not have a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime; offshore digital EMIs are neither prohibited nor recognized by the Central Bank of Belize and are generally not authorized to provide banking services to IBCs without a banking or money services license under the International Financial Services Commission. Use of a foreign EMI (e.g., an EU/UK licensed EMI) as the sole or primary treasury rail is legally permissible for an IBC GP as a non-resident entity, but presents material operational risk: pose accounts have no deposit protection, FSCS or central bank oversight; Belizean correspondent banks may refuse inward wires from EMI frontal accounts due to correspondent de-risking; and the VC fund's limited partners may reject EMI rails on reputational and custody grounds. Blueprint: use a licensed Belize bank as the primary clearing house, and employ an EMI only for low-value, non-capital operational expenses such as SaaS subscriptions or travel if the bank maintains an acceptable relationship with the EMI.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% - International Business Company (IBC) tax-exempt status under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) of Belize; the ordinary Belize corporate income tax headline rate is 25% for Belize-resident taxable companies under the Income Tax Act (Cap. 55).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Belize imposes no withholding tax on dividends, profits distributions, or capital redemptions paid by an IBC to non-resident shareholders; dividend distributions paid by an ordinary taxable Belize company out of taxed profits are likewise not subject to domestic dividend withholding.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due annually on each anniversary of incorporation; the annual return and the annual licence fee are payable on the anniversary date, with a one-month statutory grace period before late penalties accrue.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 6 months following the end of the financial reporting period; for a 31 December financial year-end, the ESR return is due by 30 June of the following year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Nil (BZD 0) - Belize imposes no statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold on tax-exempt IBCs. No local-file or master-file trigger is codified for an exempt IBC under current Income Tax Act administration; if the IBC derogates into the taxable regime by transacting with Belize residents, the arm's-length principle applies from the first Belize dollar of related-party consideration.

Economic substance scope: The International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Regulations, 2019 (as amended) apply to the IBC. Fund Management is a specified relevant activity; therefore the venture capital general partner must prove directed and managed control in Belize, maintain adequate local premises and employees, incur proportionate operating expenditure in Belize, and file the annual ESR return. Satisfying the economic substance test preserves the 0% tax-exempt status, and the evidentiary record is decisive both for Belizean compliance and for defending the entity's tax residence abroad.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Stewardship Filing

Statutory sanction: False-declaration offence under the Economic Substance Regulations: fine up to BZD 100,000 and/or imprisonment for up to two years; the Registrar may strike the company off after three months of arrears; the IBC tax exemption may be revoked retroactively, exposing the GP to Belize income tax at 25%, business tax, stamp duty, and statutory interest for the whole open tax period, together with penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded under the Income Tax Act.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations are prosecuted personally; upon conviction they are liable to imprisonment, disqualification from Belize directorships, and court orders requiring them to discharge the company's tax arrears and penalties from personal assets, thereby piercing the corporate veil. Non-resident directors remain amenable to Belize criminal summons, and travel bans may be imposed pending trial.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Evasion

Statutory sanction: Economic substance non-compliance penalty of BZD 25,000 escalating to BZD 100,000 with daily continuing penalties; the Economic Substance Unit may strike the company off, refer the matter to the Registrar, and exchange the IBC data with the tax authorities of the ultimate beneficial owner's country under the OECD standard, exposing the entire structure to foreign disclosure, double taxation, and investor litigation.

Director personal liability: The registered agent and the directors are jointly liable for the penalty if they failed to correct a knowingly deficient return; courts may lift the corporate veil and render directors personally liable for investor losses caused by the substance failure. Non-resident controllers face foreign criminal investigation once the economic substance data is automatically exchanged.
Unlicensed Fund Management (Violation of the FISCO Licensing Regime)

Statutory sanction: Unlicensed regulated activity constitutes a criminal offence under the FISCO enabling legislation, attracting fines per offence, custodial penalties, a regulatory winding-up order, restitution of investor monies, revocation of the IBC annual licence, disqualification orders against the operator, and freezing of the GP's bank accounts by the Commission.

Director personal liability: Senior officers and directors are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution to investors; criminal conviction carries imprisonment, travel bans, asset freezes, and permanent disqualification from financial-services licensing. Non-resident directors are subject to extradition proceedings and international warrant issuance where investor funds are traced through the unlicensed GP.
AML / Counter-Financing of Terrorism Compliance Failures

Statutory sanction: Failure to register or report under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act exposes the GP to administrative fines, restriction of banking relationships, and criminal liability; money laundering convictions carry imprisonment of up to seven years together with substantial fines, and the FIU may issue freezing and confiscation orders over the funds under management.

Director personal liability: AML obligations attach personally to the beneficial owner, the directors, and the designated compliance officer. Non-resident directors who approve unscreened capital flows may be prosecuted in absentia, extradited, placed on international wanted lists, or subjected to travel bans; the corporate veil is pierced where the GP is used as a conduit for investor fraud or proceeds of crime.
