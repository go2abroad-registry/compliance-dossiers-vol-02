Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance, Economic Development and Investment; official portal: https://taxservice.gov.bz. Customs: Belize Customs and Excise Department via the Customs Border Management System. Corporate registry: Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR), Ministry of Investment.

E-government filing portal

Foreign owners of a Belize IBC obtain the statutory identifier through: (1) incorporation under the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270, at CCAR, issuing Certificate of Incorporation; (2) automatic issuance of the Business Tax Identification Number (BTIN) by BTS upon registration on the taxservice.gov.bz portal, using the CCAR company number as reference; (3) filing Form GST-01 with BTS only if the IBC makes taxable supplies in Belize (e.g., services to Belize residents) or voluntarily elects GST registration; (4) annual filing of the Economic Substance Notification and IBC Declaration on the BTS portal. No income tax registration or return obligation arises because the IBC is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax on non-Belize-source income and has no domestic trading nexus.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone: the IBC is a Belize-incorporated, non-resident limited corporation whose foreign-source income, capital, assets and transactions enjoy a 50-year exemption from income tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax and stamp duty (IBC Act s.140); the entity cannot elect pass-through status, as Belize law treats it as a per se corporation.

Available election forms

No check-the-box regime exists. Available elections: (a) stamp-duty exemption certificate for all IBC documents/instruments; (b) voluntary GST registration under GST Act s.15, with right to remain outside the domestic VAT chain if turnover is below BZ$100,000; (c) exchange-control exemption under the IBC Act, removing the 180-day repatriation benchmark for offshore receipts; (d) for hybrid-mismatch purposes, Belize has adopted no OECD hybrid legislation, but an IBC that is tax-resident elsewhere may present its foreign tax certificate to BTS to confirm non-double taxation; no dual-inclusion or reverse-hybrid election is locally recognized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZ$100,000 (≈US$50,000) aggregate annual value of taxable supplies made in Belize in any 12-month period; an equity crowdfunding IBC serving exclusively non-resident issuers/investors is outside the scope of Belize GST and is not required to register.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Schedule 0 of the GST Act zero-rates: (a) goods physically exported from Belize within 30 days of supply (or such longer period as BTS approves); (b) services supplied to non-residents, performed wholly or partly outside Belize and not consumed in Belize — including crowdfunding platform fees, escrow facilitation and regulatory-compliance services where the issuer, the investors and the payment rails are non-resident and the benefit is enjoyed offshore; and (c) services directly related to exported goods. Zero-rating is conditioned on complete documentary substantiation, non-resident KYC, and a written BTS ruling if any portion of the service could be viewed as consumed in Belize.

  • Customs Automated Export Declaration (AED) / Form CUS-EXP filed electronically via the Customs Border Management System
  • Original or electronic Bill of Lading, Airway Bill or equivalent carrier receipt evidencing physical export
  • Commercial invoice and signed platform services/escrow agreement for the crowdfunding engagement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT/ACH escrow credit advice proving foreign-currency settlement
  • Non-resident attestation and KYC file of the issuer, investors and target payment rails (ACH escrow and bank wire)

No real-time fiscal clearance, API transaction validation or digital stamping mandate applies. Registered persons must issue sequential tax invoices (manual or electronic) showing the BTIN, date, description, consideration and GST charged, and retain them for 5 years; GST returns and customs declarations are filed electronically via the BTS portal and Customs Border Management System, but continuous transaction controls are not implemented.

BTS audit desks verify the export nexus, the 30-day dispatch rule for goods and the 180-day exchange-control benchmark for foreign-currency settlement. False zero-rating claims, forged export/escrow documents, or diversion of benefits to Belize residents constitute GST evasion under GST Act s.79, triggering reassessment of 12.5% GST, clawback of all input VAT credits, statutory interest at 1.5% per month, a civil penalty not exceeding the amount of tax undercharged, and, on summary conviction, a fine up to BZ$5,000 and/or imprisonment up to two years; directors and officers may be personally liable where the portal is used to defraud the revenue. The IBC's statutory tax exemption does not shield import transactions or taxable supplies consumed in Belize.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged at BZD 2.00 per USD and is effectively fully convertible for current and capital account transactions involving International Business Companies. The IBC Act expressly removes an IBC from the domestic exchange control regime, so equity injection, distributions, dividends, and liquidation proceeds may be freely transferred to non-residents. No profit remittance approval is required provided the IBC maintains its non-resident status, pays annual licence fees, and does not transact with Belize residents or own locally-situated real property.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion rules apply to IBC export proceeds or trade receipts because IBCs are deemed non-residents for exchange control purposes. For domestic Belize exporters, the Exchange Control Regulations require repatriation of proceeds through an authorised dealer within 90 days of shipment and conversion to Belize dollars unless the Central Bank grants foreign currency retention for approved imports or debt service.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMI solutions have low-to-medium viability for the crowdfunding portal. Central Bank policy and correspondent banking risk controls generally require domestic funding settlement through a Belize-licensed commercial bank. An FCA-regulated EMI may be used only for ancillary outbound expense payments, provided it is not used to hold investor escrow funds; local banks require proof of the EMI's regulatory licence and its ability to supply full payment-trail audit data.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity investor funds should be routed through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers to the Belize IBC's USD account at a Tier 1 local bank, referencing the escrow subscription id. The remitter must use ISO 20022 purpose code 'GVER' (Equity Capital) or 'INCR' (Inflow of Capital) when required by correspondent banks.
