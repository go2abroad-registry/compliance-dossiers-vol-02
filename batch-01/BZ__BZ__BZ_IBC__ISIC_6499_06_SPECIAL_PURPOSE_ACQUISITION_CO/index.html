Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize — official online portal: https://taxservice.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

A Belize International Business Company (IBC) is statutorily tax-exempt and is not required to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) for income tax purposes; the certificate of incorporation serves as the primary identifier. If the IBC nonetheless engages in any domestic taxable activity or applies for GST registration, it must file a Taxpayer Registration Application with the Belize Tax Service Department via the e-Tax portal, submitting the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office certificate, director/shareholder particulars, and proof of physical address, whereupon a TRN is issued. Foreign entities without a Belize presence may obtain a TRN only through a licensed local representative or registered agent and must appoint a resident point of contact for tax administration purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270, as amended by the IBC (Amendment) Act 2017), an IBC may irrevocably or conditionally elect to be treated as an ordinary domestic company (resident IBC) subject to the Income Tax Act, thereby opting out of the automatic tax exemption and becoming taxable on worldwide income; conversely, a domestic company may apply to be redomiciled as an IBC to obtain exemption. The regime includes hybrid mismatch election safeguards under the Tax Transparency Compliance Act and Economic Substance (Amendment) Act, whereby an IBC can elect to comply with economic substance reporting and exchange-of-information obligations to avoid adverse fiscal treatment; no check-the-box U.S.-style classification election exists, but an IBC may elect to convert to a Belize Limited Liability Partnership or Limited Company to alter its transparent/fiscally opaque status.

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Belize GST-registered person may zero-rate exports of goods where the goods are removed from Belize within one month of supply (or such longer period as the Comptroller allows) to a recipient who is outside Belize; zero-rating also applies to international transport services, services physically performed wholly outside Belize, and supplies to approved enterprises under export processing zone/qualified enterprise legislation. A SPAC IBC must demonstrate that the supply is made to a non-resident person and that title and risk pass outside Belize; domestic consumption or any supply to a Belize resident disqualifies the zero rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Entry / Form C-100) endorsed by the Belize Customs Department
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Belize
  • Original commercial invoice describing goods, value, and foreign purchaser
  • Proof of payment / Inward Remittance Certificate (foreign exchange conversion record through a licensed Belize bank)
  • Purchase order or contract with the non-resident buyer
  • Packing list and port authority/terminal receipts confirming export

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based e-invoicing, or digital stamping mandate currently exists in Belize; however, registered persons must file GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Service Department online filing system, retain sequentially numbered GST invoices, and comply with electronic record-keeping obligations. The Government has signaled phased introduction of e-invoicing interoperability, but as of the current regime there is no mandatory pre-clearance of invoices with the tax authority.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to documentary audit by the Belize Tax Service Department and Customs Department under the GST Act and the Customs Act. False claims or unsupported zero-rated exports trigger assessment of the unpaid GST at 12.5%, plus interest at the statutory rate, an additional penalty of 25% of the tax (up to 100% in cases of fraud or deliberate evasion), fines up to BZD 10,000, and imprisonment for up to two years. The Comptroller may reopen returns and claw back input tax credits and zero-rated refunds retrospectively for up to four years (unlimited in cases of fraud or misrepresentation), and the IBC's tax exemption certificate may be revoked with retrospective taxation under the International Business Companies Act and the Economic Substance (Amendment) Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize Dollar (BZD) is officially and statutorily pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00, with exchange control administered through authorized dealers only. Current-account transactions are freely convertible for non-residents, and a Belize IBC is statutorily classified as a non-resident for exchange-control purposes under section 8(3) of the IBC Act. Consequently, the SPAC vehicle may maintain a segregated USD non-resident external account, receive IPO subscription proceeds, and repatriate capital, dividends, and trust-escrow releases without mandatory conversion into BZD or Central Bank pre-approval. Practical profit-remittance hurdles are limited to documentary verification: the account-holding bank requires (i) a current IBC non-resident designation letter from the IBC Registrar, (ii) the entity's certificate of incorporation demonstrating tax-exempt status, and (iii) an annual director's declaration that no transactions with Belize residents occurred. Capital-account operations of resident Belize companies remain subject to exchange-control approval, but these do not apply to the IBC SPAC provided its counterparties, investors, and acquisition targets are non-resident. No SPAC-specific FX licensing is required; however, any future conversion of trust proceeds into BZD for a domestic target acquisition must be executed through the Central Bank's authorized-dealer window at the pegged rate, with a 2-business-day settlement calendar.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Exchange Control Regulations 92 and 100, resident exporters must surrender all export proceeds to an authorized dealer in full within 180 days of the date of shipment, unless exempted by the Central Bank. Non-resident IBCs are wholly exempt from surrender and conversion obligations, provided the entity is not engaging in trade with Belize residents. For the SPAC IBC, the board must file with its Belize commercial bank: (a) the IBC Registrar's non-resident status certificate; (b) a sworn statutory declaration of non-resident economic activity; (c) the trust agreement demonstrating that escrowed funds are held exclusively for external mergers or acquisitions; and (d) an annual compliance confirmation that no export invoices, trade receivables, or domestic service revenue have been booked in the Belize operating account. Any inadvertent credit from a Belize-resident source must be quarantined and re-exported within 30 days to avoid reclassification as resident export proceeds triggering surrender.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262 of the Laws of Belize, Revised Edition 2011), which administers exchange control pursuant to the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 36, and the Exchange Control Regulations, 1968, as amended; the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) exercises supplementary supervisory authority over International Business Companies under the International Business Companies Act, Chapter 270.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Belize maintains no domestic electronic-money-institution licensing regime under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, and global EMIs (Mercury, Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) routinely decline Belize IBCs because of enhanced-AML tiering, legacy bearer-share reputation, and the absence of a recognizable EMI-licensing home for Belize-domiciled entities. Legal constraints: (i) the AML/CFT Act Chapter 104 prohibits Belize payment intermediaries from settling any transaction for a Belize IBC without a physical or video-verified KYC file; (ii) offshore EMIs may clear USD through EU or UK partner banks but will require the SPAC's UBOs to appear in the EMI's own KYB process, which often exceeds Belize bank standards; (iii) a U.S.-bound SPAC cannot rely on a fintech EMI as the designated trust-custodian because the sponsor's investment-management agreement requires a regulated depositary bank; (iv) where an EMI is nevertheless used for operational petty cash (<USD 10,000 per wire), the EMI must hold a valid license in its home jurisdiction and the IBC's Belize bank must be identified as the originating-correspondent beneficiary to preserve audit trail. Recommended posture: use Belize Bank Limited as the sole treasury node; adopt Currenxie or Airwallex only for non-trust travel-and-legal expenses, and never for escrowed IPO proceeds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (i) each SPAC investor wires via SWIFT MT103 to the Belize bank's U.S. correspondent (Belize Bank Limited uses Bank of America, New York; Atlantic Bank uses Citi, New York), with purpose code 'NEC 111 - Capital contribution to non-resident investment vehicle' and beneficiary account suffix 'TRUST-<DEALNAME>'; (ii) the Belize bank credits a segregated USD non-resident external account at the Belize City branch, capitalizing the trust through a non-resident external-draft entry; (iii) the trust-custody agreement contractually blocks withdrawal until a release condition (redemption deadline or acquisition closing) is met; (iv) if any subscription is funded by a non-bank platform or an SPV aggregator, the aggregator's regulated settlement bank must appear on the MT103 intermediary-beneficiary line to avoid violation of the bank's third-party funds rule; (v) all inbound credits are subject to 24-hour verification: sanctions screening, gold-AML pricing, and Board-in-committee approval whenever single-day inbound volume exceeds USD 5,000,000; (vi) EUR, GBP, or CAD wires are converted to USD upon provisional credit using the prevailing market rate, not the BZD peg, to avoid adverse FX; the IBC does not hold BZD; (vii) the SPAC's external auditor receives read-only access to the trust ledger and produces a monthly source-of-funds verification report addressed to the bank's compliance officer.Primary clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 for escrow release to the acquisition target at closing, with purpose code 'NEC 984 - Acquisition of equity in non-resident enterprise' and a 140-character remittance-info block quoting the SPA execution date and deal reference; (ii) Commercial letters of credit (UCP 600) issued by Belize Bank Limited in favor of the target's pre-closing suppliers, each confirmed by a top-25 global bank (HSBC or Citi) to eliminate Belize jurisdiction risk; (iii) Documentary collections (URC 522) for payments against shipping or share-transfer documents, used only where the counterparty is well-established and LC confirmation charges are disproportionate; (iv) Internal DDA sweep from trust escrow to the operational account for management and administrative fees, capped at 0.5% of trust AUM per quarter with trustee countersignature. All BZD-denominated disbursements (rare and discouraged) convert at the Central Bank's pegged settlement window, but the SPAC's treasury policy mandates USD-only disbursements to eliminate conversion and intraday-liquidity constraints. Any outbound USD wire exceeding USD 100,000 requires prior-day notification to the bank's AML officer and a board-certified Release Request referencing Schedule 1 of the trust agreement. Same-day clearing is not guaranteed: Belize RTGS last settlement window closes at 14:00 (CST); wires initiated before 10:00 (CST) are delivered to the U.S. correspondent the same day via Fedwire, otherwise T+1.USD/BZD outright forwards (up to 12-month tenor) offered by Belize Bank Limited and Atlantic Bank Limited at the Central Bank's pegged rate of BZD 2.00 per USD with a 10-20 pip margin; no initial margin required when collateralized by escrowed U.S. Treasuries.Non-deliverable USD/BZD forwards (NDF) executed offshore through Hong Kong or New York dealing desks for 3-12 month tenors, used solely to hedge a BZD-denominated contingent obligation of the future acquisition target; limited offshore liquidity of approximately USD 5 million per day and settled on a net basis without conversion of principal.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (USD vs EUR, USD vs GBP) entered under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a top-tier global dealer, with the SPAC's independent trustee providing credit support; permitted only when the trust portfolio holds non-USD securities or the target's cash flows are materially non-USD.USD interest-rate swaps converting fixed-coupon U.S. Treasury yield to SOFR-based floating exposure in anticipation of post-merger acquisition leverage; eligibility requires the Belize bank's derivatives-confirmation letter and the target's acquisition agreement as a condition precedent.Plain-vanilla USD/BZD FX options (both USD call/BZD put and BZD call/USD put) to protect projected BZD-denominated terminal costs if the SPAC merges with a Belize-resident operating company; writing options is prohibited by treasury policy, and purchased premiums are capped at 2% of Trust AUM per annum.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Belize maintains no domestic electronic-money-institution licensing regime under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act, and global EMIs (Mercury, Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) routinely decline Belize IBCs because of enhanced-AML tiering, legacy bearer-share reputation, and the absence of a recognizable EMI-licensing home for Belize-domiciled entities. Legal constraints: (i) the AML/CFT Act Chapter 104 prohibits Belize payment intermediaries from settling any transaction for a Belize IBC without a physical or video-verified KYC file; (ii) offshore EMIs may clear USD through EU or UK partner banks but will require the SPAC's UBOs to appear in the EMI's own KYB process, which often exceeds Belize bank standards; (iii) a U.S.-bound SPAC cannot rely on a fintech EMI as the designated trust-custodian because the sponsor's investment-management agreement requires a regulated depositary bank; (iv) where an EMI is nevertheless used for operational petty cash (<USD 10,000 per wire), the EMI must hold a valid license in its home jurisdiction and the IBC's Belize bank must be identified as the originating-correspondent beneficiary to preserve audit trail. Recommended posture: use Belize Bank Limited as the sole treasury node; adopt Currenxie or Airwallex only for non-trust travel-and-legal expenses, and never for escrowed IPO proceeds.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a tax-exempt Belize International Business Company (IBC); the ordinary Belize domestic corporate income tax rate for Belize-resident companies is 25% on chargeable income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders; Belize's ordinary domestic dividend withholding tax, where a domestic Belize company is used, is generally 25% for non-residents, subject to DTA relief, but the IBC exemption overrides it.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No CIT due date applies while the IBC exemption is uncontaminated. The annual license fee / government fee must be paid on or before the anniversary of incorporation, subject to the Registrar's grace period of up to 30 days.

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before each anniversary date of incorporation, or as notified in the Registrar's billing cycle for existing companies.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 12 months after the end of the SPAC's financial year; a nil return is due even for zero-revenue entities unless categorically outside ESR scope.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Belize does not prescribe a separate Master File or Local File filing threshold under the IBC exempt regime. For any residual taxable Belize activity, Belize transfer pricing principles apply where controlled transactions exceed BZD 1,000,000 annually; otherwise assume OECD-aligned documentation with a USD 500,000, approximately BZD 1,000,000, practical benchmark.

Economic substance scope: Belize Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to IBCs conducting relevant activities. A SPAC's holding of funds and prospective acquisition may be treated as holding company or financing and leasing activity. The entity must demonstrate, in Belize or a qualifying jurisdiction, adequate management and control, employees, premises, and expenditure; alternatively, if central management and control is outside Belize, the SPAC should file a nil return and maintain documents proving non-residence. Belize authorities reject shell arrangements without CIGA substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion by abuse of IBC exemption

Statutory sanction: Assessment of unpaid tax at 25% CIT; interest at the statutory rate; surcharge of up to 100% of tax; criminal fine for each false declaration; denial of exemption and retroactive loss of IBC status.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors, and sponsors may be assessed for the corporate tax arrear under look-through provisions; travel bans and seizure of assets are possible; imprisonment up to 3 years for fraudulent tax evasion convictions.
Failure to file Economic Substance Return or false nil ESR

Statutory sanction: ESR fine of BZD 5,000 plus daily ongoing penalties; possible anti-abuse ruling that the IBC is not tax-exempt; exchange of information with foreign tax authorities under the OECD Global Forum or Inclusive Framework.

Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified from managing companies in Belize; the Registrar can strike off the SPAC; directors remain personally liable for restoration fees and any third-party trust claim for misapplied funds.
Shell company abuse and lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty residence status; imposition of 25% Belize corporate tax on the SPAC's worldwide income; referral to the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit; possible deregistration under the IBC Act for false particulars.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who sign false declarations can be held criminally liable; the ultimate SPAC sponsor may be treated as the beneficial owner and assessed personally; courts may pierce the corporate veil to impose fiduciary liability for breach of trust.
Money laundering and AML breach

Statutory sanction: Under Belize AML/CFT legislation and the Financial Intelligence Unit Act, fines up to BZD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering; freezing of assets; cancellation of the IBC registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who conceal beneficial ownership face personal criminal liability, confiscation orders, industry debarment, and restrictions on re-entering Belize or any jurisdiction where extradition applies.
Late annual fee and struck-off SPAC

Statutory sanction: Strike-off from the Belize Register; assets held by the struck-off company may be held to the order of the Registrar and applied to restoration fees; contracts become unenforceable; restoration requires court order plus fees and possible tax default settlement.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue transacting on behalf of a struck-off IBC are personally liable on those contracts; they may also face contingent liability for any taxes or penalties retroactively incurred if the IBC loses exemption.
