Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service, Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize (official portal: https://www.belizetaxservice.gov.bz); customs administration is handled by the Belize Customs and Excise Department through the ASYCUDA World electronic platform; the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) regulates BZ_IBC incorporation, licensing, and annual compliance (https://www.ifsc.gov.bz).

E-government filing portal

A BZ_IBC (International Business Company) is generally not required to hold a Belize Tax Identification Number (TIN) purely for offshore business because its income is statutorily excluded from Belize chargeable income. A TIN becomes necessary for local payroll (PAYE), GST registration on local supplies, customs clearance, banking KYC, or tax-treaty/administrative assistance procedures. The foreign entity, acting through its licensed registered agent in Belize City, must file Form TIN-01 with the Belize Tax Service together with: certificate of incorporation and certificate of good standing, registered office lease or letter from the registered agent in Belize, certified list of directors and shareholders, notarized identification and proof of tax residence of beneficial owners, and a sworn declaration of no Belize-sourced income. The Belize Tax Service issues a 9-digit TIN within 5 business days for a complete application; a foreign IBC without an office in Belize must maintain the registered agent as its process agent for all tax correspondence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize does not provide a check-the-box election or a hybrid-mismatch election comparable to US or EU regimes. The only material statutory election for a BZ_IBC is to surrender automatically granted offshore tax-exempt status by special resolution of shareholders filed with the Registrar of Companies and the Belize Tax Service, thereby opting into the ordinary corporate income tax regime under the Income Tax Act (which then subjects the company to 25% income tax on its worldwide chargeable income). The election is irreversible absent ministerial consent, and no partial election is permitted on a business-line basis. A domestic company incorporated under the Companies Act 2020 has no statutory route to elect IBC-style exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 in gross taxable turnover (including zero-rated and standard-rated supplies) in any consecutive 12-month period. A BZ_IBC engaged exclusively in cross-border consultancy services without supplies connected to land or goods situated in Belize has no compulsory GST registration nexus; however, voluntary registration is permitted where the IBC wishes to recover input GST on local overhead purchases and can demonstrate that all taxable supplies are zero-rated exports. If local standard-rated supplies exceed BZD 75,000 per annum, compulsory registration applies without regard to the entity's offshore tax-exempt status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) the physical export of goods removed from Belize within 30 days of supply under a contract of sale to a non-resident, evidenced by customs documentation; and (ii) international consultancy services — including tax structuring and transfer-pricing advisory — supplied to a non-resident person who receives the benefit entirely outside Belize, provided the service is not performed in Belize, does not relate to Belizean land or goods situated in Belize, and the consideration is paid in foreign currency to the IBC's overseas or Belize account from a foreign bank. The IBC must hold written evidence of the service recipient's non-residence (passport copy, certificate of residence, or equivalent) and must not carry out the advisory work on Belizean soil if the benefit is deemed enjoyed locally.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World export entry certified by the Belize Customs and Excise Department)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or parallel consignment note evidencing physical export and destination outside Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, foreign bank credit advice, or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of the export consideration in foreign currency
  • Executed consulting agreement, invoice bearing the 'Zero-Rated Export' legend, date-stamped deliverables transmitted outside Belize, and certificate of tax residence for the non-resident client (for service exports)

Belize has no real-time fiscal clearance, no government digital stamping, and no API-based pre-clearance mandate for GST. Invoices may be issued in paper or electronic format and must contain a unique sequential number, date, seller legal name and TIN, buyer legal name and address, description and quantity, consideration expressed in Belize dollars, the GST amount or a clear 'Zero-Rated Export' legend, and the exchange rate for foreign currency transactions. Customs export entries must be filed electronically through ASYCUDA World using a licensed customs broker. There is no continuous fiscal authorization; instead the Customs Department applies a selectivity engine for documentary risk-scoring. GST and income tax records must be retained for 6 years after the end of the relevant tax period.

The Belize Tax Service may audit any zero-rated export claim from the date the relevant GST return is filed. Where the statutory export documentation is missing or inadequate, the zero-rating is reversed: additional GST is assessed at 12.5% of the invoiced consideration, plus a penalty of 25% of the tax and interest of 2% per month or part-month. Knowingly submitting false export declarations or fraudulently claiming zero-rating to obtain a refund constitutes an offence under the General Sales Tax Act and the Tax Administration Act, carrying a civil penalty of 200% of the tax sought, loss of input-credit entitlement on all related purchases, retrospective cancellation of GST registration, and reassessment for a 6-year look-back period. Criminal prosecution is available on indictment with fines up to BZD 250,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years; the statutory burden of establishing that the goods or services were actually exported and consumed or received outside Belize rests entirely on the BZ_IBC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize maintains a fixed-exchange-rate regime pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Current-account transactions for trade, services, dividends, and royalties are generally permitted without prior Central Bank consent when executed through an authorized dealer with underlying documentation. Capital-account transactions, including inbound share-capital injections, external debt, and outbound direct investment, require advance Exchange Control approval or reliance on a statutory exemption. An International Business Company (IBC) incorporated under the IBC Act, Cap. 270 may obtain an Exchange Control exemption certificate, allowing retention of foreign currency in external accounts without mandatory conversion. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is lawful where profits derive from non-resident business and no Belize tax liability arises; the paying bank will require the IBC tax-exempt/exemption certificate and an FX declaration form. The practical hurdles are documentary: beneficial-ownership certification, source-of-funds evidence, and confirmation that the IBC has not transacted with Belize residents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Exchange Control Regulations (as amended) require resident exporters to receive payment for goods or services within six months of the date of export or performance and to surrender foreign currency received to an authorized dealer within one month of receipt, unless the Central Bank grants a dispensation. A Belize IBC that holds a valid Exchange Control exemption under the IBC Act is excluded from mandatory surrender for receipts generated from non-resident clients in the course of cross-border tax-structuring and advisory services. Fee invoices, engagement letters, the IBC registration certificate, and the Exchange Control exemption certificate must be retained to substantiate the non-surrender treatment in any bank or regulatory review.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize, exercising exchange-control supervision under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 37 of the Laws of Belize, acting through authorized dealers (licensed commercial banks) and its Exchange Control Department.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low. Belize has no statutory passporting regime for foreign electronic-money institutions (EMIs). The National Payment System Act, 2022, confers on the Central Bank of Belize a licensing framework for payment service providers, but the regime is nascent and no broad cross-border digital-EMI licences have been operationally recognized. Belize commercial banks generally reject wire transfers sourced from or destined to unlicensed offshore digital EMIs because of correspondent-banking and AML/CFT concerns. For the target payment rails (Bank_Wire and Credit_Card), credit-card acquiring must be routed through a licensed commercial bank or a Central Bank-approved payment processor in Belize. Offshore EMIs may be used only as a supplementary collection vehicle where the settlement account sits at a regulated Belize bank and the EMI is itself licensed in a FATF-compliant jurisdiction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory TP documentation threshold applies to tax-exempt IBCs; for any controlled transaction, the entity should maintain arm's length evidence for economic substance compliance.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance (Amendment) Act, 2019 requires IBCs carrying on 'relevant activities' to maintain substantive operations in Belize. Advisory and consultancy services may be treated as a 'service center' activity when performed for non-residents; the entity must demonstrate adequate local premises, employees, and expenditure, and must file an annual economic substance return.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Belize's Criminal Code and the Income Tax Act, penalties include fines up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years; foreign jurisdictions may apply separate criminal sanctions and civil penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling officers may be personally liable for restitution of evaded taxes, with travel bans and extradition; courts may pierce the corporate veil where the IBC is a façade.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance penalties: fines from BZD 10,000 to BZD 50,000; automatic exchange of information with foreign revenue authorities; potential strike-off of the company.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for unpaid fines and penalties; may be disqualified from acting as a director for up to 5 years and prohibited from incorporating a new IBC.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act and the Belize Crimes Act, fines reach BZD 500,000 and imprisonment up to 14 years; proceeds of crime are subject to confiscation and asset forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution, asset seizure, travel bans, and extradition to the jurisdiction of offence; conviction may also trigger permanent prohibition from financial services.
Non-Filing of Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Strike-off under Section 149 of the IBC Act and accrual of late fees and restoration charges up to BZD 250 plus default penalties; the company loses its separate legal personality and tax-exempt status.

Director personal liability: Directors who continue to trade after strike-off become personally liable for all company debts and may be fined up to BZD 1,000; each defaulting director is subject to an additional daily fine.
