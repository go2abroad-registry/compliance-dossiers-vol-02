Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The IBC Act permits registration with a single share of no par value or a nominal par value; no escrow account, capital deposit, or proof of paid-up funds is required for incorporation, and share capital may be declared issued and fully paid without bank remittance evidence.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the National Fire Service of Belize is required for any leased office premises accommodating more than 10 persons, accompanied by a structural load inspection where archival audit files and server racks exceed floor-loading allowances; workplace clearance per the Labour Department under the Occupational Safety and Health Ordinance covering first-aid kits, emergency lighting, exit signage, and electrical wiring certification; additionally, AEO client engagements require background screening and security-cleared audit staff aligned with ISO/IEC 27001 information security controls.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (BTSD), unified revenue authority under the Ministry of Finance and Economic Development; portal: taxservice.gov.bz. Customs and border controls are operated by the Customs and Excise Department, now an operational division within BTSD, running the ASYCUDA World single-window platform. IBC incorporation and statutory registry matters are administered by the Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity incorporating a Belize International Business Company (IBC) must engage a licensed registered agent, which files the Certificate of Incorporation and Memorandum/Articles of Association with CCAR. The IBC receives a Registration Number; due to the 50-year statutory tax exemption in Section 11 of the International Business Companies Act, no TIN is automatically issued and no income tax registration or return filing applies. If a TIN is nonetheless required (e.g., for CRS/FATCA reporting, banking KYC, or voluntary GST registration), the registered agent files a TIN application with BTSD attaching the Certificate of Incorporation, registered agent letter, and beneficial-owner identification; BTSD processes the application in approximately 30 days. An IBC that voluntarily registers for GST under the General Sales Tax Act obtains its taxpayer identity through that GST registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Belize. An IBC may elect to surrender its exempt status and convert to a domestic company under the Companies Act, subjecting itself to income tax and GST, an election that is effectively irreversible and rarely used. An IBC may voluntarily register for GST. Under the International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Rules 2019, the IBC's annual return includes a self-declaration electing whether it carries on a relevant activity and which exemption category applies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZ$100,000 of taxable supplies per rolling 12-month period. Registration is mandatory for any person carrying on a taxable activity in Belize with GST-exclusive annual turnover exceeding BZ$100,000. An IBC supplying AEO certification and supply-chain security audit services exclusively to non-resident clients realises zero domestic taxable turnover and is outside the registration nexus; voluntary registration is available but generally yields no input-credit benefit because all inputs to an IBC are themselves zero-rated or excluded supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Section 26 of the General Sales Tax Act zero-rates (i) goods physically exported from Belize within 90 days of supply, and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Belize at the time of supply, provided the benefit of the service is enjoyed wholly outside Belize. For AEO certification and security auditing services, the IBC must maintain evidence that: (a) the contractual counterparty is a non-resident; (b) fieldwork, analysis, and deliverables are performed or consumed outside Belize; (c) the audited supply chain does not involve a Belize domestic taxable supply; and (d) payment is received in foreign currency from abroad. Because the IBC is statutorily barred from transacting with Belize residents, substantially all of its services qualify as zero-rated or outside the scope of GST.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD) submitted via ASYCUDA World when goods move through Belize in the audited supply chain; for pure offshore service exports this declaration is replaced by the service contract and deliverables log
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill issued by an international common carrier evidencing movement of goods out of Belize to a foreign destination
  • Commercial Invoice and executed Statement of Work describing the AEO certification or supply-chain security audit deliverables
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the Belize-licensed receiving bank evidencing foreign-currency conversion and credit to the IBC's account
  • Executed service agreement together with the non-resident client's certificate of incorporation, tax residency certificate, or valid passport/ID evidencing foreign residence

Belize imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no government e-invoicing API, and no digital stamping obligation. The regime is self-assessment based: registered persons must issue invoices containing prescribed particulars (seller name, address, TIN, date, supply description, and tax amount) and retain them for six years. E-invoicing is permitted in PDF or EDIFACT formats but is filing-based, not clearance-based. Customs declarations are processed exclusively through the ASYCUDA World system; no invoice pre-clearance or e-stamping is required.

BTSD may reopen returns and make retrospective assessments within six years, or ten years where fraud or gross negligence is established. A disallowed zero-rating claim triggers full GST assessment at 12.5% of consideration, penal interest of approximately 1.5% per month compounded, and a civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated. Criminal conviction for fraudulent zero-rating or forged export documentation carries fines up to BZ$50,000 and imprisonment up to three years. Additionally, the Belize Financial Services Commission may revoke or suspend the IBC licence, strike the entity from the registry, or impose monetary penalties for non-compliance with the IBC Act, and the Central Bank of Belize may levy separate fines for failure to repatriate export earnings within the 180-day deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged to the U.S. dollar at a fixed rate of 2.00 BZD = 1.00 USD. Current account transactions for resident commercial enterprises are generally free from exchange control impediments, while capital account movements are subject to the Exchange Control Act and regulations administered through authorized dealers. A Belize International Business Company (IBC) is treated as non-resident for exchange control purposes, making it eligible to hold and operate foreign-currency accounts outside the domestic exchange control restrictions. Profit remittances and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders from an IBC's exempt international business are permissible without domestic exchange control approval. Practical hurdles are primarily bank-side: correspondent banking due diligence, reputational risk screening, and adherence to international sanctions frameworks rather than statutory convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Belize Exchange Control Act and subsidiary regulations, resident exporters are generally required to repatriate and, where directed by an authorized dealer, convert export proceeds into BZD within prescribed timelines. However, IBCs are classified as non-resident and are exempt from mandatory export-proceeds surrender and conversion obligations. An IBC's export receipts may be retained in foreign currency in its designated external or offshore account. If an IBC were to be recharacterized as resident or engaged in domestic trade, the full surrender and conversion rules would apply. For this AEO supply-chain audit scope, all service export receivables should be routed through the IBC's foreign-currency operating account with clear contract and invoice documentation to preserve non-resident treatment.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise and Payoneer are not licensed as banks or money transfer operators under Belize law. A Belize IBC may be accepted by certain EMIs for multi-currency account opening, but onboarding is generally subject to enhanced due diligence, jurisdiction-risk scoring, and the EMI's independent willingness to support non-resident Belize IBCs. Legal constraints include absence of local regulatory recourse, reliance on a foreign e-money license, and potential correspondent-bank restrictions on payments originating from or destined to Belize. EMIs should be used only for low-value operational disbursements, not as the primary clearing rail for capital injections or AEO audit fee settlements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections must be routed by wire transfer from the shareholder's regulated bank account to the IBC's designated foreign-currency account at an authorized dealer in Belize. The remittance should include the SWIFT MT103 cover and proper SWIFT purpose code, using a generic 'capital contribution' or 'equity injection' code such as 'C11 - Capital Contribution' or the receiving bank's internal equivalent. The following documentary package must be retained: executed share-capital resolution, register of members, bank's internal KYC form, source-of-funds evidence for the transferring account, and a written confirmation that no BZD conversion is being requested. If the injection is financed from a related offshore treasury entity, a subordinated loan agreement or equity subscription document must be executed to avoid recharacterization as assessable domestic income.Outbound settlements for AEO supply-chain audit vendors, customs-security equipment purchases, and subcontracted forensic services should be conducted through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers, or through commercial letters of credit opened by an authorized dealer in Belize for larger procurement transactions. For routine service provider payments, the MT103 message must include the beneficiary's full legal name, IBAN or account number, SWIFT BIC, and concise payment reference matching the underlying invoice. Clearing rails should be restricted to (i) USD-cleared via correspondent banks in the United States (e.g. CITI, JPMorgan, Bank of America), (ii) EUR-cleared via a European correspondent, and (iii) GBP-cleared via a London clearer. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% for a Belize International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act; standard Belize corporate income tax rate is 25% for taxable resident companies under the Income and Business Tax Act, Cap. 55

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid by an IBC to any shareholder, resident or non-resident. For a non-IBC Belize taxable company, the statutory domestic withholding tax rate on dividends paid to non-residents is 15%.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No CIT deadline applies because the IBC is tax-exempt. The IBC Annual Return must be filed within the period fixed by the Belize Companies Registry, generally considered a calendar-year or anniversary-based renewal cycle; no audited financial statements or tax computation are due.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No specific statutory IBC TP documentation threshold in Belize. As a general good-practice threshold, taxable Belize companies should maintain a Master File and Local File when aggregate controlled transactions exceed BZ$1,000,000; for exempt IBCs, contemporaneous records are still recommended to prove beneficial ownership and non-residence.

Economic substance scope: Belize Economic Substance Act 2019 applies to companies tax-resident in Belize carrying relevant activities. An IBC that is managed and controlled in Belize must prove core income-generating activities, staff, premises, and expenditure in Belize. A pure equity holding IBC is entitled to reduced substance compliance; an IBC that is managed and controlled outside Belize is generally outside the ESR net and must document non-residence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Sham Transactions

Statutory sanction: Assessment of all concealed tax at 25%, plus interest and penalties up to 200% of the tax evaded; criminal fines and imprisonment for directors who sign false declarations

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be held personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties and may face imprisonment for willful evasion; professional disqualification orders may also apply.
Shell Company Abuse and Substance Avoidance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Act 2019, administrative fines for non-compliance, enforcement penalties, and ultimate strike-off of the company; financial penalties may be imposed on the registered agent for continuing non-compliance

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for false economic substance declarations, restitution of missed fees, and are subject to travel bans and director disqualification if the company is used as a hidden front.
AML/CFT and Beneficial Ownership Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Belize AML/CFT legislation include monetary fines, freezing and forfeiture of assets, suspension or revocation of the corporate license, and criminal prosecution of the responsible individuals

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly allow the IBC to launder proceeds of crime face civil asset recovery, personal fines, restriction from leaving Belize, and custodial sentences.
Customs and AEO Certification Fraud

Statutory sanction: Under Belize Customs powers, goods are subject to detention, seizure, and forfeiture; civil penalties may reach three times the value of the goods, and the AEO certification is immediately revoked with cross-border facilitation rights withdrawn

Director personal liability: Directors signing false customs declarations can be prosecuted criminally, ordered to pay restitution to the customs authority, banned from acting as directors, and subjected to travel restrictions during investigation and prosecution.
