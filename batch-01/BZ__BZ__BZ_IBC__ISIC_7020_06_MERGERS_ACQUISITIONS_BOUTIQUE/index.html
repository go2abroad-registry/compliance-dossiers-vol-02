Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; taxpayer portal: https://taxservice.gov.bz

E-government filing portal

IBCs are exempt from Belize income tax and generally do not require a Belize income tax TIN. Where a Belize VAT registration or employer withholding registration is needed (e.g., local office, local staff, or local taxable supplies), the foreign/Belize IBC must apply to the Belize Tax Service for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) using Form VAT-01 or the relevant registration form, supported by the Certificate of Incorporation, registered office details, directors' and shareholders' records, and proof of a local place of business if any. Registration is completed online or in person at the BTS; a TIN and VAT certificate are issued upon approval. In the absence of Belize-source activities, the IBC remains outside the Belize tax net and no TIN procurement is mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize IBCs are statutorily exempt from Belize income tax, capital gains tax, stamp duty, and business tax under the International Business Companies Act; no check-the-box election is required. An IBC may, however, elect to be treated as a domestic Belize company by filing the prescribed notice with the Companies Registry (thereby losing the IBC tax exemptions); such election is revocable under statutory conditions. For hybrid mismatch and foreign tax purposes, the IBC can obtain a Certificate of Residency or tax residence confirmation from the Belize Tax Service only if it meets management-and-control presence requirements, but no substantive Belize tax classification election regime exists beyond the domestic/offshore status election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 (Belize dollars) in taxable supply value in any consecutive 12-month period, unless the supply is zero-rated or exempt; nonresident suppliers of electronic, telecommunications, or digitally supplied services to Belize consumers may have a separate registration obligation as prescribed by the Belize Tax Service.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of goods where the goods are physically removed from Belize by the supplier or a third party on behalf of the supplier, as evidenced by customs documentation. International services (including M&A advisory, financial and professional services supplied to nonresidents for use outside Belize) may qualify for zero-rating where the recipient belongs in a foreign country and the supply falls within the categories of services designated as exported services under the Belize VAT Act. The supplier must retain sufficient evidence of exportation and of the foreign-place-of-supply test; services supplied to a Belize-resident person or for direct benefit in Belize are taxable at 12.5%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Belize Customs and Excise Department single administrative document or electronic export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill with exporter and consignee details
  • Commercial invoice evidencing the export sale
  • Certificate of shipment or customs endorsed export certificate where applicable
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/foreign exchange confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign currency export proceeds

No real-time fiscal clearance or compulsory digital stamping regime is currently in force for VAT invoices in Belize. Taxpayers must issue prescribed VAT invoices containing the VAT registration number, supplier and customer details, description and value of supply, VAT amount, and invoice date; invoices must be retained for audit purposes and filed through the BTS online portal on monthly or quarterly return cycles as assigned.

Belize VAT law authorizes assessment of unpaid VAT, interest, and penalties where a claimed zero-rating is unsupported or where goods are exported under a false declaration. Misdeclaration may attract civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded or attempted to be evaded, plus interest, and criminal liability including fines and imprisonment for fraudulent export documentation. The Belize Tax Service may claw back input VAT credited on zero-rated supplies and impose late-filing and non-compliance penalties where the exporter fails to produce the required customs and foreign-currency repatriation evidence within the prescribed period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Belize operates a managed currency regime with the Belize dollar pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Current account transactions such as cross-border advisory fees, trade settlements, and operational payments are generally freely convertible through authorized dealers, subject to underlying documentation. Capital account transactions, including non-resident direct investments, subscription of IBC shares, and high-value M&A consideration, require Central Bank approval or are processed with enhanced scrutiny. An IBC classified as non-resident is permitted to hold foreign currency accounts and transact offshore without accessing the domestic current-account conversion window; however, any conversion involving Belize-resident counterparties or the local financial system will attract capital-control declaration requirements. Profit remittances from Belize-resident operating subsidiaries are contractible through authorized dealers after documentary proof of tax clearance, audited financial statements, and Central Bank Form compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Belize Exchange Control Act and related Central Bank Notices, resident exporters must repatriate and surrender export proceeds to an authorized dealer in Belize within the stipulated statutory timeline, generally not later than 90 days from the date of export shipment. The mandatory conversion ratio is 100% of the FOB value of the exported goods into Belize dollars at the official pegged exchange rate, subject only to a Central Bank-approved foreign currency retention quota for allowable operating expenses. For IBCs transacting on a strictly offshore basis, export proceeds derived from services or goods provided wholly outside Belize may be retained in an approved foreign currency account without mandatory surrender, provided the IBC maintains clear separate records and does not create Belize-source income.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB) is the sole monetary and foreign exchange control authority in Belize, responsible for licensing authorized dealers, administering exchange control regulations, and maintaining the fixed BZD/USD exchange rate.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Belize does not recognize offshore digital EMIs or virtual payment institutions as lawful bank-licensed settlement providers for IBC treasury operations. A non-resident EMI cannot itself open a domestic BZD account or act as an authorized dealer under the Exchange Control Act. Cross-border payment platforms may participate only as unregulated payment intermediaries, which creates unacceptable legal risk for an M&A advisory boutique under the Medium_Risk_Enhanced AML tier. A viable path is to use a fully licensed Belize commercial bank as the primary settlement hub and, if necessary, a foreign-regulated EMI only for fringe expense payments in jurisdictions where the employer has a subsidiary or branch. Any such foreign EMI must hold a valid fit-and-proper license from a recognized FATF-compliant regulator and must not handle Belize-source deal consideration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections from shareholders, holding companies, or investors must be routed as SWIFT MT103 wires through the IBC's licensed Belize commercial bank. The payment instruction must specify the bank's SWIFT BIC, the IBC's account number, and a precise remittance information field reading 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / SHARE SUBSCRIPTION – BZ_IBC' together with a reference to the board resolution authorizing the issuance. The bank, acting as authorized dealer, will require a certified board resolution, an updated share register, a subscription agreement, source-of-funds documentation for each remitting shareholder, and a completed foreign exchange declaration. Inbound BZD wires must be converted at the official pegged rate; USD wires may be cleared through the bank's U.S. correspondent account. Digital asset transfers, third-party debit-authority arrangements, or unlicensed payment intermediaries may not be used as primary settlement rails for capital injection without creating an AML/CFT compliance breach.For standard trade settlements and M&A success fees, the IBC should use SWIFT MT103 from its operational account at a Belize authorized dealer to the beneficiary institution. The payment purpose code must be unambiguous, such as 'BUSINESS ADVISORY SUCCESS FEE' or 'ACQUISITION CONSIDERATION', and must attach the supporting closing invoice or sale-and-purchase agreement reference. For high-value M&A transactions where the counterparty requires payment security, a commercial letter of credit under SWIFT MT700 issued by a top-tier bank may be used, but the reimbursement and presentation documents must align with the escrow release conditions. Domestic BZD disbursements are cleared through the Belize local clearing house; all cross-border USD disbursements flow through the bank's U.S. correspondent using Fedwire or CHIPS. To ensure smooth reconciliation, every outbound wire should be backed by a board resolution, a copy of the invoice, and the executed advisory agreement, with all records archived for a minimum of five years.Plain vanilla FX forward contracts with a licensed Belize authorized dealer for USD/BZD, used solely to hedge known M&A consideration or fee receivables.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for BZD exposure executed through a foreign licensed bank, as the BZD is not freely deliverable offshore.Cross-currency swaps (USD/BZD) entered into with an international bank that is licensed under Belize's International Banking Act, subject to Central Bank regulatory oversight and documented commercial purpose.FX option contracts, specifically European-style plain vanilla options, used only to hedge transaction success-fee exposure and not for speculative treasury trading.

0% – dividends paid by a Belize IBC to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Belize withholding tax and income tax; no dividend tax, no remittance tax, and no undistributed profits tax applies; a domestic Belize company paying dividends to residents/subject entities would incur 15% WHT, but the IBC statutory exemption fully supersedes this

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local File/Master File threshold has been enacted in Belize (no BZD-denominated threshold exists); the general anti-avoidance provisions of the Income and Business Tax Act (ss.28–30) apply, and the Belize Tax Service expects contemporaneous arm's-length documentation for any related-party transaction; the Economic Substance regime is the operative gatekeeper: all IBCs with a relevant activity must file ES-01 regardless of revenue, meaning the effective documentation threshold is BZD 0

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Act 2019 (as amended) and the International Business Companies (Amendment) Act 2021 apply; an M&A advisory boutique is classified as a 'distribution and service centre business' because it supplies cross-border services to non-resident clients; the IBC must demonstrate in Belize: (i) physical premises, (ii) full-time qualified employees proportionate to income levels, (iii) operating expenditure in Belize commensurate with revenue, (iv) CIGA performed in Belize including deal sourcing, due diligence, valuation analysis, negotiation, and execution, and (v) board meetings and strategic decisions occurring in Belize; the Tax Service applies the proportionality test by comparing substance to gross income, and failure results in a BZ$50,000 penalty, strike-off, or forced conversion to domestic taxable status at 25%

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Sham Managed-and-Controlled Structure

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of the entire income at 25% corporate tax under the Income and Business Tax Act; interest at 10% per annum; penalties of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed; the taxpayer bears the burden of proving economic substance; potential strike-off plus revocation of the IBC certificate

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid assessments where assets have been dissipated; civil restitution orders, asset freezes, and travel bans are available under the Tax Administration procedures; courts will pierce the corporate veil upon finding the IBC is a sham, exposing personal bank accounts and foreign assets
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: BZ$50,000 civil penalty under the Economic Substance Act; daily default fine of BZ$250 for non-filing; mandatory strike-off and a 10-year prohibition on re-registration of the same or substantially similar entity; the Tax Service may exchange information with the registered agent and foreign tax authorities under the OECD transparency framework

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false ES-01 face personal penalties of BZ$10,000 each and imprisonment up to 2 years upon conviction; director disqualification orders for 5 years; directors are personally liable for penalty amounts if the company is insolvent; nominee directors who 'rubber stamp' filings without verification bear equal criminal exposure
AML/CFT Violation (Money Laundering)

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Act Cap.129: corporate fine up to BZ$100,000 per offense and/or imprisonment of officers up to 10 years; civil forfeiture of all proceeds derived from the advisory engagement; the IBC may also be blocked from using Belize bank clearing systems and reported by the Financial Intelligence Unit to foreign counterparts

Director personal liability: Directors and senior officers are personally liable as principals where they knew or should have known of the source of funds; imprisonment up to 10 years, extradition risk under bilateral treaties, mandatory travel bans, and a 7-year disqualification from serving as company director; professional indemnity insurance is void for willful AML violations
Unlicensed Investment & Securities Business

Statutory sanction: Fines up to BZ$250,000 and/or imprisonment up to 7 years for carrying on regulated securities business without a license; court-ordered restitution of all fees collected; the IFSC may publish a cease-and-desist order and freeze the IBC's Belize bank accounts

Director personal liability: Directors are deemed to be carrying on business individually if they signed engagement letters or supervised execution; personal criminal conviction leads to automatic disqualification, repatriation of client funds, joint liability for restitution, and voiding of the IBC's professional indemnity coverage
Prohibited Local Business (IBC Act Breach)

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the IBC certificate by the Registrar; statutory fine of BZ$5,000 per prohibited transaction; the offending agreements are unenforceable, preventing recovery of success fees; the Registrar may also report the breach to the Tax Service, triggering domestic tax assessment at 25%

Director personal liability: Directors who directed the prohibited transaction are personally liable to refund all fees received, with interest from the date of receipt, and for any losses sustained by third parties who relied on the IBC's lawful status; criminal conviction attracts imprisonment up to 3 years and a permanent director disqualification order in Belize
