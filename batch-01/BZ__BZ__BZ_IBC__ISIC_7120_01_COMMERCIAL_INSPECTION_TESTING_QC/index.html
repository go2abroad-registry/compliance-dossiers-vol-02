Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department (TSD), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; unified national tax administration for income tax, General Sales Tax (GST), and other fiscally material levies. Primary online portal: Tax Administration Management Information System (TAMIS) at https://tamis.gov.bz; TSD headquarters located at Treasury Building, Belize City, Belize.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Business Company (IBC) with the Belize Companies Registry (a division of the Ministry of the Attorney General) and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; a licensed registered agent and a registered office in Belize are mandatory. Step 2: No automatic national tax registration is triggered, because the IBC Act (Chapter 270) grants a 50-year exemption from all Belize taxes, including income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, withholding tax, stamp duty, and GST. Step 3: If an optional Belize Tax Identification Number (TIN) is required for banking, foreign-contract, or KYC purposes, submit Form TSD-REG-001 (Registration of Taxpayer) to the Tax Service Department together with the IBC Certificate of Incorporation, the registered agent engagement letter, a certified passport copy of the ultimate beneficial owner, and a declaration of exclusively offshore business purpose. Step 4: The TSD issues the TIN certificate on approval, typically within 7 to 14 business days, without imposing any tax-filing obligation on the exempt IBC. Step 5: Voluntary GST registration is possible but not required; it is only advisable where the IBC seeks input GST refunds on locally sourced overhead, and it requires a Belize address for service and a domestic licensed representative. Step 6: Annual compliance is limited to payment of the IBC annual license fee and filing of the annual return with the Companies Registry; no income tax or GST returns are submitted to the TSD during the statutory tax holiday unless the entity elects to convert to a domestic taxable company.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone — the IBC is a non-resident offshore legal entity enjoying an automatic 50-year exemption from Belize income tax, business tax, capital gains tax, withholding taxes, stamp duty, and GST, conditioned upon refraining from trading with Belize residents and holding no real property in Belize (other than under an IFSC license). Its foreign-source income and services physically rendered abroad are treated as entirely outside the Belize territorial tax net.

Available election forms

Belize law provides no U.S.-style check-the-box election and no specific hybrid-mismatch election. The available mechanisms are: (1) conversion election — an IBC may by special resolution elect to convert into a domestic company under the Companies Act (Chapter 250), thereby permanently forfeiting the IBC tax holiday and becoming subject to the normal domestic income tax, business tax, and GST regime; (2) voluntary GST election — an exempt IBC may register as a GST licensee solely to recover input GST on Belize-origin expenses, while maintaining its statutory exemption on offshore supplies; and (3) economic substance election — under the Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019, an IBC performing a relevant activity must elect a tax-resident jurisdiction in which the economic substance test is met, with default deemed residence in Belize for substance purposes only; this election does not disturb the tax exemption.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.125%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 150,000 (approximately USD 75,000) aggregate taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months or reasonably expected in the next 12 months. An IBC is deemed to be outside this threshold because supplies made by an IBC in the ordinary course of its international business are expressly exempt from GST under the IBC Act (Chapter 270) and section 5 of the General Sales Tax Act; GST registration is therefore generally neither required nor available unless the IBC voluntarily registers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under the General Sales Tax Act for: (a) exported goods — actual removal of goods from Belize; and (b) exported services — services supplied to a non-resident of Belize who is outside Belize at the time the services are performed, provided the services are not connected with land situated in Belize or with goods situated in Belize at the time of performance. For the ISIC 7120 on-site factory pre-shipment quality control and audit agency, eligibility is satisfied where: (i) the inspection, testing, or QC audit is physically performed at a factory outside Belize, or, if performed remotely, the service delivery occurs outside Belize; (ii) the client is a non-resident who is not present in Belize at the time of delivery; (iii) the services do not involve land or goods located in Belize (testing of Belize-origin goods prior to export is excluded); and (iv) the IBC maintains contemporaneous evidence of overseas performance, including travel records, inspection site documentation, and foreign-currency receipts. The IBC, being statutorily exempt, may lawfully make the supply without charging GST; alternatively, if voluntarily registered, it may apply 0% GST to such offshore supplies and claim a refund of input GST incurred on Belize-origin overhead in accordance with standard TSD refund procedures.

  • Customs Export Declaration — required only where physical samples or test goods are exported from Belize; not applicable to a pure services supply
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill — required only for physical sample exports; for pure services, replaced by a dated on-site inspection certificate and travel records
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Purchase Record — issued by an authorized Belize bank or licensed financial institution evidencing receipt of the export consideration in a convertible foreign currency
  • Offshore Service Agreement — stating the inspection scope, the overseas factory location, delivery terms, payment currency, and the non-resident status of the client
  • Signed Inspection Report with GPS-tagged photographs, flight itineraries, boarding passes, and hotel invoices proving that the service was rendered outside Belize

Belize has not implemented real-time electronic invoicing, mandatory fiscal clearance, API-based transmitter certification, or digital stamping. Invoices may be issued on paper, PDF, or other electronic media, but must contain a sequential serial number, the supplier's GST registration number (where registered), the tax point date, a description of the supply, the total consideration, and a separate statement of the GST charged or the zero-rating applied. Registered persons must file GST returns electronically via TAMIS by the 15th day of the month following the end of the tax period, with payment due on the same date. A compliance modernization roadmap covering future e-invoicing has been publicly announced by the TSD, but no binding electronic clearance obligation is in force as of the effective date of this dossier; non-resident IBCs with no Belizean domestic turnover are unaffected.

The Comptroller may reopen export zero-rating and refund claims within 7 years where fraud, misrepresentation, or gross negligence is established. Any refund improperly claimed is clawed back retrospectively with statutory interest at 20% per annum, a late-payment surcharge of 10%, and an additional penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated for fraudulent or dishonest claims. Criminal prosecution may result in a fine not exceeding BZD 10,000 and/or imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years per offense. For an IBC, false economic-substance declarations or forged offshore-performance records additionally trigger the Companies (Economic Substance) Act 2019, which permits administrative penalties up to BZD 50,000, strike-off of the company from the register, and disqualification of directors. In practice, the TSD cross-checks the inward remittance certificate, inspection-site records, and travel logs, and applies a rebuttable presumption of domestic taxable supply where the IBC fails to produce the required export documentation bundle within 30 days of a written demand.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged to the United States dollar at a fixed rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Current-account transactions are generally convertible for residents and licensed entities, but capital-account movements and outward remittances are subject to Exchange Control approval thresholds and documentary review. Belize International Business Companies (IBCs) that are non-resident and conduct all business outside Belize are treated as outside the exchange control net for foreign-currency purposes, allowing unrestricted USD receipt and disbursement via external or domestic USD accounts. Profit remittances by a non-resident IBC are not subject to Belize withholding tax or repatriation tax because the entity is tax-exempt under the International Business Companies Act, but clearing banks may require evidence of the commercial transaction, source-of-funds documentation, and the absence of any local resident business activity to trigger Exchange Control approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Belize Exchange Control does not impose mandatory surrender or statutory conversion ratios on export proceeds earned by a non-resident IBC. The IBC is expressly prohibited from carrying on business with Belize residents and is therefore not an export trader under Belize domestic exchange-control definitions. All proceeds from factory pre-shipment inspection, quality-control audit, and testing services are invoiced in USD and may be retained in an approved foreign-currency account or cleared offshore. If, exceptionally, funds are received in BZD, the account-holding bank may require application to the Exchange Control Authority for retention or conversion, with documentary evidence of the underlying corporate service contract.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262, Revised Edition 2011), acting in concert with the Belize Ministry of Finance and the Exchange Control Authority under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 37 of the Laws of Belize.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Wise and similar non-bank payment institutions are viable for incidental low-value receivables and operational disbursements, but they are not substitutes for a regulated Belize commercial bank account for capital-introduction and LC settlement. Belize is not a SEPA participant, and most global EMIs apply restrictive country coverage, enhanced due diligence on Belize IBCs, and may reject payment routing if the IBC uses nominees or cannot present a demonstrable underlying commercial contract. A compliant EMI onboarding is possible only when the IBC holds a valid registered agent certificate, a certificate of good standing, and a substantive overseas client-supply agreement; the EMI must also be satisfied that the IBC does not transact with Belize residents. The treasury architecture should therefore use a Tier-1 Belize bank as the primary clearing anchor and the EMI as a liquidity sub-channel only.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Bearer share structure or undisclosed registered owner of the IBC

Root cause: Bearer share structure or undisclosed registered owner of the IBC

Defense evidence: Statutory declaration from the registered agent confirming that the company has no bearer shares; certified extract of the register of directors and shareholders; IBC certificate of incorporation and certificate of good standing; notarized passport copies and proof of residential address of all ultimate beneficial owners; resignation/termination letters for any historical nominee officers.
Commingled funding between unrelated foreign clients and unexplained large inward payments

Root cause: Commingled funding between unrelated foreign clients and unexplained large inward payments

Defense evidence: Executed factory inspection and quality-control service agreements naming the client; commercial invoices, purchase orders, and inspection reports; bank statements showing a clear business pattern; source-of-funds letters from the client's bank; profit and loss and treasury reports of the IBC.
Use of nominee director with no demonstrable management authority

Root cause: Use of nominee director with no demonstrable management authority

Defense evidence: Registered agent certificate identifying the real director and shareholder; signed trustee/declaration of nominee; comprehensive UBO register with ownership percentages; prior written management mandate approving bank account signature rights and payment instructions.
Absence of physical office or economic substance in Belize

Root cause: Absence of physical office or economic substance in Belize

Defense evidence: Registered office agreement with the licensed registered agent; board resolutions confirming centralized treasury decisions; management services contract with the local agent for corporate filings; evidence that all operational activities occur in foreign factory locations through mobile inspection teams.
Potential adverse media or sanctions exposure of Chinese factory counterparties or trade routes

Root cause: Potential adverse media or sanctions exposure of Chinese factory counterparties or trade routes

Defense evidence: Enhanced due-diligence reports on all counterparties using third-party screening; copy of the commercial contract proving the inspection services are non-military and non-sanctioned; certification of nature of goods inspected; detailed end-user and factory-site identification; confirmation that no payments are made to sanctioned jurisdictions.
Large cash or third-party settlement arrangements contrary to wire-transfer standard

Root cause: Large cash or third-party settlement arrangements contrary to wire-transfer standard

Defense evidence: Bank letter confirming all payments are collected exclusively by SWIFT MT103 wire transfer; client-side beneficial ownership attestation; audit trail of invoice-to-wire reconciliation; rejection of any check, cash proxy, or unrelated third-party payer arrangement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from the parent or shareholder shall be executed exclusively by SWIFT MT103 wire transfers in USD from the shareholder's regulated corporate bank account. The remitter must use an appropriate beneficiary reference and the Belize receiving bank must be provided a pre-advice copy of the board resolution approving the capital injection and the IBC certificate of good standing. For equity contributions, the payment field must clearly identify the amount as paid-in capital to the IBC; for shareholder loans, a signed interest-bearing loan agreement with a repayment schedule is required. All USD wires will be credited to the IBC's USD current account without mandatory conversion to BZD. The receiving bank should be instructed that the purpose of payment is 'intercompany capital contribution for working capital' and that the underlying business is cross-border inspection and quality-control services. The transaction value threshold triggering additional Exchange Control or AML review in Belize is generally USD 10,000 and above; therefore, the client must maintain a complete source-of-funds and purpose-of-payment file for every inward transfer.Trade disbursements for the factory-inspection commercial model include subcontractor payments, inspector payroll, travel and logistics expenses, corporate service fees, and refundable client deposits. The primary outbound clearing rail shall be SWIFT MT103 USD wires via the Belize bank's correspondent banking network, with all wire instructions transmitted under the IBC's approved signature authority. For larger factory pre-shipment contracts where the buyer requires title or quality-condition assurance, a commercial letter of credit (confirmed or unconfirmed) may be issued by the buyer's bank and advised through the Belize bank or a third-country confirming bank; however, inspection fees are normally settled by direct invoice wire rather than documentary LC. The IBC may also use a fintech EMI for sub-threshold disbursements to field inspectors in multiple jurisdictions, but every outbound payment must be matched to a contractual obligation, an invoice, or an expense receipt. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: BZD 2,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions in a financial period (Income and Business Tax (Transfer Pricing) Rules, 2022); Master File and Local File contemporaneous documentation are required above this threshold; below the threshold, a benchmarking study is commercially prudent and is mandated if the Commissioner issues a notice of assessment adjustment.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations 2019 (as amended) apply to all IBCs. For ISIC 7120 (commercial inspection/testing/QC services): if the IBC renders services to connected entities, the activity is classified as 'distribution and service centre business' and the CIGA test must be satisfied: directed and managed in Belize, adequate physical premises, full-time employees, and minimum annual operating expenditure proportionate to income; the registered office alone is insufficient. If services are rendered solely to unrelated clients, the IBC must still file the annual notification declaring 'no relevant activity' and must not hold itself out as exercising management in Belize without substance. Pure equity holding IBCs enjoy a reduced substance test.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Tax-Exempt Claims

Statutory sanction: Civil assessment of 100% to 200% of the evaded tax under the Income and Business Tax Act; daily default penalty of BZD 500 under the ESR; false declaration fine up to BZD 20,000; imprisonment up to 2 years for willful false filing under the Criminal Code and the Income and Business Tax Act s.121.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the false return are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties; the court may pierce the corporate veil, order restitution, impose travel bans pending criminal trial, and issue disqualification orders prohibiting service as a director of any Belize company.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Strike-off and automatic dissolution by the Registrar under the IBC Act (re-instatement penalty of BZD 300 plus arrears); exchange of information under OECD/CRS leading to foreign revenue reassessment; the Belize registered agent may be ordered to terminate the agency relationship.

Director personal liability: Directors of a struck-off company acting in its name are personally liable for debts incurred after dissolution; in fraud scenarios the Belize courts lift the veil and impose restitution orders; beneficial owners can be subjected to civil asset freeze orders and travel bans under the Supreme Court (Summary Jurisdiction) Act.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalty of BZD 5,000 plus BZD 100 per day under the Companies (Amendment) (Beneficial Ownership) Regulations; money laundering charge punishable under the Money Laundering and Terrorism (Prevention) Act with a fine up to BZD 250,000 and/or imprisonment up to 14 years; confiscation of the full value of tainted assets under the Proceeds of Crime Act.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and beneficial owners are personally subject to criminal liability; the court may issue a restraint order freezing personal and corporate assets, cancel passports, and impose a travel ban; conviction leads to disqualification from holding a corporate office in Belize for 10 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Artificial Margins

Statutory sanction: Primary adjustment to taxable income and a secondary adjustment; penalty of 20% of the transfer pricing adjustment plus monthly interest of 2%; the Commissioner may apply the general anti-avoidance rules to re-characterise the arrangement; the notice of assessment carries a 30-day appeal deadline after which it becomes final.

Director personal liability: If the mispricing is designed to evade foreign tax, Belize authorities will cooperate through exchange of information; foreign revenue agencies may pierce the IBC, impose joint liability on the parent and its directors, and refer the matter for criminal tax prosecution with extradition exposure for directors resident abroad.
Fraudulent Inspection Certification and Trade Licensing Violation

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the commercial inspection licence by the responsible trade authority; fine up to BZD 10,000 for unlicensed operation; criminal liability for forgery under the Criminal Code (imprisonment up to 5 years and a fine); international consequences include the loss of preferential tariff treatment and listing by the WTO TBT Committee.

Director personal liability: Directors personally signing fraudulent certificates are criminally liable; civil claims by purchasers for reliance losses pierce the corporate veil; the Belize courts may grant a Mareva injunction freezing personal and corporate assets abroad and impose a travel ban pending the fraud trial.
