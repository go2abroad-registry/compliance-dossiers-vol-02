Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (Successor to the Income Tax Department), Ministry of Finance, Government of Belize; primary portal: Belize Tax Service Dashboard / Belize Integrated Tax System (BiTS) at https://taxservices.gov.bz and the International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) for IBC incorporation and annual licensing.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the International Business Company (IBC) under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) through the Registrar of IBCs, Belize International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) and receive the Certificate of Incorporation. Step 2: Obtain a Belize Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) by registering with the Belize Tax Service in the BiTS taxpayer registration module; this TIN is normally issued for administrative and banking facilitation, though the IBC enjoys statutory tax exemption. Step 3: If any domestic taxable supply is made or acquired in Belize (e.g., lease of a local office), file GST Registration Form GST-01 with the Belize Tax Service; otherwise no GST registration is required. Step 4: Comply with the Economic Substance Act notification requirements by filing the annual Economic Substance Return if the IBC is tax-resident in Belize and carries on a relevant activity. Step 5: Pay the annual IBC license fee and file the annual IBC return with the IFSC to preserve the exemption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize law does not provide a domestic check-the-box election. The IBC is statutorily exempt from all Belize income, business, capital gains, estate, stamp and similar taxes for 50 years under IBC Act ss.6-7. Available mechanisms: (i) election to register for GST on a voluntary basis under the GST Act to recover input tax on local acquisitions; (ii) under the Economic Substance Act, an IBC may elect or evidence status as non-resident where management and control are exercised outside Belize, thereby excluding it from relevant activity reporting; (iii) re-domiciliation in or out of Belize under the IBC Act; (iv) foreign check-the-box elections, such as the U.S. Form 8832, may reclassify the IBC for foreign tax purposes but do not alter Belize statutory tax-exempt treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 (approximately USD 37,500) of annual taxable supplies. For a bona fide offshore IBC conducting no domestic activity in Belize, no threshold is triggered and GST registration is not required; the IBC may voluntarily register to claim input tax credits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the GST Act, First Schedule, zero rating applies to: (i) export of tangible goods from Belize substantiated by a customs export entry; (ii) supply of services to a person who is not resident in Belize, or services physically performed wholly outside Belize; and (iii) supplies involving international transport. For the Bermuda-affiliate media-buying entity, all advertising, lead-generation and media-arbitrage services are supplied to non-resident clients and performed offshore, therefore qualifying as zero-rated or fully outside the domestic scope of Belize GST. Input tax attributable to such zero-rated export activity is refundable through the BiTS return process. Additionally, the Belize IBC's offshore income is exempt from all Belize business tax and income tax under the IBC Act.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic CEX entry filed with the Belize Customs Department)
  • Commercial Invoice with zero-rated marking and full description of the media-buying services or goods
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or equivalent transport documentation (for physical goods only)
  • Signed Service Agreement / Affiliate Media Buying Contract evidencing offshore performance and non-resident counterparty
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 / bank statement demonstrating payment from outside Belize
  • Certificate of non-residence or certified corporate registry extract of the overseas client

No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API integration, or digital stamping regime exists in Belize. GST invoices must follow the format prescribed in the GST Regulations, showing the supplier's GST registration number, date, description, invoice number, and tax charge. Monthly/quarterly GST returns are filed electronically through the Belize Tax Service BiTS portal. Invoices for export zero-rated supplies must be clearly marked 'zero-rated export' and cross-referenced to the supporting customs and payment evidence.

Belize imposes no statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline; the 180-day benchmark is applied as a prudent internal compliance and transfer-pricing control. Incorrect or fraudulent zero-rating claims are subject to GST Act s.50 misdeclaration penalties of 25% of the undercharged tax plus interest at 12% per annum; criminal conviction for fraudulent evasion carries a fine of up to BZD 25,000 and/or imprisonment up to 18 months. Any use of the IBC to carry on domestic Belizean business, trade with Belize residents, or hold Belize real property without ministerial approval breaches IBC Act s.4 and voids the 50-year tax exemption retrospectively, exposing the company to full business tax/income tax reassessment at rates up to 30% equivalent, plus penalties and interest. Export refund claims are audited by the Belize Tax Service and require submission of the complete documentation bundle; ineligible refunds are clawed back with a 100% surcharge in cases of abuse.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is pegged to the US dollar at a fixed rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Current account payments, including cross-border service fees and operating disbursements, are generally freely convertible through authorized dealers. Capital account transactions may require exchange control consent for resident entities, but a Belize International Business Company is exempt from the Belize Exchange Control Act provided it does not carry on business with Belize residents and does not hold local assets or incur local liabilities. Profit remittance for non-resident shareholders is not subject to Belize withholding tax, capital gains tax, or dividend tax, which eliminates most remittance hurdles for a typical media-buying IBC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Belize exchange control regulations generally require exporters of physical goods to repatriate export proceeds within the prescribed period and sell the foreign currency to an authorized dealer, usually within six months, unless an exemption is granted. A non-resident-owned IBC engaged in overseas affiliate media buying is not a resident exporter for exchange-control purposes; its foreign-currency receivables can be retained in USD accounts and are not subject to mandatory conversion into Belize dollars. If the IBC voluntarily holds BZD, conversion occurs at the official pegged rate and requires an authorized dealer.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No published Belize statutory transfer-pricing documentation threshold applies to an IBC; Belize has not enacted Local File or Master File requirements for IBCs. The operative threshold is the Economic Substance requirement: any IBC with a relevant activity, including advertising and media-buying services, must maintain contemporaneous records demonstrating economic substance in Belize. In practice, any intercompany media buying, affiliate payout, or IP royalty above USD 250,000 equivalent should be supported by an OECD-compliant transfer-pricing study, even though the Belize domestic threshold is not codified in the IBC context.

Economic substance scope: The Belize Economic Substance Regulations apply to IBCs carrying on relevant activities, including 'distribution and service business', 'financing and leasing business', and 'intellectual property business'. A digital advertising and affiliate media buying operation falls within the service-business category and must satisfy all of the following in Belize: core income-generating activities must be performed in Belize, the IBC must be managed and controlled in Belize, it must have adequate physical premises and an adequate number of full-time employees in Belize, and it must incur operating expenditure in Belize proportionate to the advertising income generated. IP-heavy ad-tech structures face the higher 'substantial economic substance' standard and must show that all crucial IP development, exploitation, and risk decisions occur in Belize.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Belize Economic Substance Rules impose administrative penalties for false or misleading ESG filings, including fines, striking-off of the IBC, and exchange of the filed data under the competent authority framework. A false declaration can be prosecuted as an offence, leading to imprisonment, and the IBC loses its tax-exempt status retroactively if the exemption was procured by fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may have the corporate veil pierced on a finding of fraud or sham. Directors are jointly and severally liable for all taxes, penalties, and restitution, and may be subject to travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment in Belize or in the foreign jurisdiction conducting a corresponding tax investigation.
Shell Company and Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with Belize's beneficial ownership and record-keeping obligations can trigger monetary penalties, disqualification of the registered agent, cancellation of the IBC's license, and freeze of the IBC's bank accounts. Where the concealment is part of an international tax avoidance arrangement, the home country may treat the IBC's income as taxable in the hands of the undisclosed owner.

Director personal liability: Directors, nominees, and beneficial owners are personally liable for fines and for any taxes evaded in the residence jurisdiction. Travel bans and extradition can apply where the shell company is part of a serious tax-fraud or money-laundering scheme.
AML and Money Laundering by Payment Intermediation

Statutory sanction: Belize's Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing legislation imposes criminal liability for money laundering and unlicensed financial activity, with penalties including substantial fines and imprisonment for up to 15 years. The Belize Financial Intelligence Unit may also impose civil penalties and issue freezing orders against the IBC's bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors cannot rely on the corporate form to shield themselves from money-laundering offences. Individual officers face criminal prosecution, confiscation orders, personal fines, travel restrictions, and extradition, and in severe cases the Supreme Court of Belize may issue a worldwide asset freezing order.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Although Belize does not maintain a robust domestic transfer-pricing penalty regime for IBCs, the counterpart high-tax jurisdiction will apply transfer-pricing adjustments, recharacterize payments, impose secondary adjustments, and levy understatement penalties of up to 100% of the tax underpayment. In treaty or tax information exchange contexts, Belize may be required to disclose the IBC's ownership and financial information.

Director personal liability: Directors of the foreign operating affiliate can be held personally liable for knowingly signing inaccurate transfer-pricing documentation or tax returns. Where the arrangement is found to be a sham, the corporate veil is pierced and the director's personal assets are reachable for the entire adjusted tax, penalties, and interest.
Misrepresentation to Foreign Revenue Authorities

Statutory sanction: Offshore jurisdictions treat failure to report an IBC as a controlled foreign corporation as a serious civil or criminal tax violation. In the United States, the penalty for failure to file an international information return begins at USD 10,000 per form and escalates to 40% of the understatement or criminal fines and imprisonment under tax evasion statutes. In the UK or EU, equivalent civil penalties and criminal fraud sanctions apply.

Director personal liability: The controlling shareholder and any director who approved the misrepresentation face personal liability, including fraud penalties, recovery of all evaded taxes, travel bans, and imprisonment in the residence jurisdiction. Belize nominee directors who signed misleading declarations may also be prosecuted in Belize and disqualified from acting as directors.
