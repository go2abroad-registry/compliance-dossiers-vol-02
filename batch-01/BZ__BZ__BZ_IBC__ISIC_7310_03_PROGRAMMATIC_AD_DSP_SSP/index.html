Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service Department, Ministry of Finance (portal: https://www.taxservice.gov.bz); customs matters administered by the Customs and Excise Department (https://www.customs.gov.bz)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Belize International Business Company (IBC) is incorporated under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) and is automatically registered for tax ID purposes by the Registrar of Companies. To obtain the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN), the IBC must submit to the Tax Service Department: (i) taxpayer registration application form; (ii) certificate of incorporation; (iii) registered office/registered agent details; (iv) certified passport copies of directors and ultimate beneficial owners; and (v) statement of exempt status under the IBC Act. The TIN is issued within 7-10 business days. The IBC must also register for General Sales Tax (GST) if annual taxable turnover reaches BZD 150,000.

Tax ID type & application path: Tax_Exempt_Zone — A foreign-owned Belize International Business Company (IBC) is incorporated under the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) and is automatically registered for tax ID purposes by the Registrar of Companies. To obtain the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN), the IBC must submit to the Tax Service Department: (i) taxpayer registration application form; (ii) certificate of incorporation; (iii) registered office/registered agent details; (iv) certified passport copies of directors and ultimate beneficial owners; and (v) statement of exempt status under the IBC Act. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

Belize has no check-the-box or OECD hybrid-mismatch election statute. The IBC Act provides elective instruments: (i) election to receive a Ministerial Certificate of Tax Exemption (irrevocable 50-year zero-tax guarantee); (ii) election to convert an IBC into a domestic company under the Companies Act, thereby entering the ordinary Belize income tax and GST regime; (iii) election of registered-share capital mode for transparency; and (iv) election for annual return-based taxpayer verification. A domestic Belize company may also elect to redomicile as an IBC, which resets taxable status to exempt.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 150,000 annual taxable turnover (approx. USD 75,000) within a 12-month period; non-resident digital-service suppliers with substantive Belize nexus (local ad inventory/SSP buyers) are registrable if they exceed the threshold, although Belize does not yet operate a separate digital-services tax

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

GST zero-rating is claimable for (i) goods physically exported from Belize under customs supervision; and (ii) services supplied by an IBC to a non-resident of Belize where the benefit is consumed wholly outside Belize. For programmatic ad DSP/SSP services, zero-rating applies when the contracting customer is a non-resident ad network/advertiser/publisher, the campaign delivery geographies are outside Belize, and the IBC maintains documentary evidence of the offshore place-of-supply, non-resident status of the counterparty, and no Belize-based recipient of the service.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Customs Form C-10 / electronic manifest filing)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Belize
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice describing service/goods with HTS/ISIC coding
  • Packing list (for goods) or service delivery report (for ad-tech services) evidencing offshore consumption
  • GST zero-rating certificate and counterparty non-resident declaration

No real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, digital stamping, or API-based e-invoicing mandate exists in Belize. GST-registered persons must issue sequential pre-numbered tax invoices, retain electronic copies for 6 years, and file monthly/quarterly GST returns through the Tax Service Department online portal (e-filing with JSON/PDF attachment, scheduled payment via Swift Wire/ACH). Approved B2B e-invoicing is permitted, but there is no government clearance database for export invoices; physical evidence packet must be kept for audit.

Belize has no mandatory FX repatriation statute; however, for audit substantiation the tax authority may inspect the export packet within 4 years (6 years for fraud). False zero-rating/refund claims trigger: GST refund clawback with 200% penalty plus interest at 8% per annum; customs fraud penalties up to 5 times the duty and forfeiture of goods; criminal liability for wilful evasion; and administrative deregistration of the IBC's tax-exempt certificate under the IBC Act for carrying on prohibited domestic business. The Tax Service cross-checks against the IBC annual return and bank records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar is legally pegged to the US dollar at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00 under a fixed-rate regime effective since 1976. Current-account transactions (trade in services including programmatic advertising) are freely convertible against documentary proof of the underlying transaction. Capital-account transactions (equity injections, inter-company loans, offshore investments) require exchange-control filing or approval for resident entities; however, an International Business Company (IBC) registered under the IBC Act, Chapter 270, Laws of Belize, is statutorily exempt from the Exchange Control Act where it transacts in foreign currency or maintains an external account. Profit remittance and dividend distribution by an IBC are unrestricted because the IBC enjoys zero corporate income tax, zero withholding tax, and no capital gains tax in Belize; only bank-level documentary review (audited or management accounts, platform revenue invoices, registered agent certificate) is triggered. The practical hurdle is not convertibility but source-of-funds substantiation: an undercapitalized IBC with high-velocity programmatic ad receipts requires a detailed commercial narrative to pass bank-level economic sanity checks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 263) and its General Orders, a resident exporter of goods or services must receive payment within 180 days of the shipment or service date and must surrender all foreign-currency export proceeds to an authorized dealer in exchange for Belize dollars (statutory surrender ratio is 100% of export receipts). However, an IBC is deemed non-resident for exchange-control purposes where it operates in a foreign currency or through an external account; Section 6 of the IBC Act expressly disapplies the Exchange Control Act to such an IBC. Consequently, the 180-day surrender timeline and the mandatory 100% conversion requirement are not applicable to the IBC, provided that: (i) all treasury flows are denominated in USD or another freely convertible foreign currency; (ii) the Belize bank account is classified and maintained as an 'external account'; and (iii) the IBC does not conduct domestic Belize-dollar business.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (established under the Central Bank of Belize Act, Chapter 262, Laws of Belize), acting as the sole monetary authority, bank licensing supervisor, and statutory exchange-control administrator; exchange-control administration is delegated to authorized dealers (licensed commercial banks) under the Exchange Control Act, Chapter 263, Laws of Belize.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Belize has no standalone Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing framework; non-bank payment service providers must obtain a money remittance or currency-exchange license under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act (Cap. 266) or register as money services businesses under the AML/CFT Act. International digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) cannot be used as the primary inbound clearing rail for Belize because Belize is outside SEPA and no international EMI passporting regime exists. EMIs are structurally viable only as downstream outbound payment rails for sub-disbursements (e.g., LATAM contractor payouts or media supplier settlements) once funds have been cleared into a Belize-licensed external USD account. Legal constraint: any EMI arrangement must not be structured to circumvent Belize exchange-control or AML/CFT obligations; the licensed Belize bank's settlement account must remain the treasury anchor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections are executed via standard SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's home-country bank to the Belize IBC's external USD account. The Remittance Information field (field 70) must read: 'Capital contribution for operating expenses — IBC No. [registration number] — ISIC 7310 programmatic advertising platform'. The applicable SWIFT purpose code is 'TRAN' (transfer between related accounts) or 'CASH' (cash flow management); codes implying investment into Belize domestic securities or 'TOCC' grants must not be used. Because the IBC is exchange-control exempt and the account is classified as an external account, prior Central Bank approval is not required; nevertheless, the Belize receiving bank must log the credit against the external-account ledger and may request a Source-of-Funds Declaration, the UBO's bank statement showing account opening history, and a board resolution authorizing the capital injection. For injections exceeding USD 1,000,000, route the credit via the bank's US correspondent (e.g., Wells Fargo for Atlantic Bank, Bank of America for Belize Bank) and pre-clear the transaction with the Belize compliance desk using the same KYC dossier to avoid SWIFT holds. The use of an Escrow Account is mandatory until initial UBO verification and platform counterparty contracts have been validated.The primary clearing rail for vendor and supplier settlements is the SWIFT MT103 (single customer credit transfer) with the invoice number, invoice date, and service descriptor embedded in field 70; the Belize bank applies de facto automatic current-account remittance approval where the payee and amount reconcile to the filed commercial narrative and no sanctions flag is raised. Intercompany treasury movements between the IBC and any sister holding entities are executed via SWIFT MT202/MT103 tagged with ISO 20022 code 'INTRA' and supported by a board resolution and latest management accounts. For high-value media-inventory purchases exceeding USD 250,000 per settlement cycle, a Commercial Letter of Credit is constructively permissible but practically limited: Belize banks will only issue fully cash-covered documentary credits or MT760 standby letters of credit, because digital goods/media services do not fit traditional trade-documentary examination; the standby LC is therefore recommended solely as a payment guarantee with the underlying settlement still executed by MT103. For LATAM contractor payouts, the IBC may combine a Belize MT103 to the contractor's local bank, or use an integrated fintech sub-disbursement rail (e.g., a licensed money remitter in Mexico, Guatemala, or Colombia) funded from the external USD account; all sub-disbursements must be traceable to the master settlement ledger to satisfy the bank's periodic review.Deliverable FX forward contracts with a Belize-licensed authorized dealer (hedging EUR/USD and GBP/USD exposure from European ad buyers; tenor up to 12 months; underlying commercial invoices required)Vanilla FX options (European-style EUR/USD and GBP/USD protective puts and calls) purchased through an international investment-grade counterparty outside Belize jurisdictionCross-currency swaps (payment-versus-payment collateralized) under an ISDA master agreement between the IBC and an offshore FATF-compliant bank, converting USD revenue streams into EUR/GBP for matched media purchase and sales cyclesNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) quoted against the USD/BZD pegged rate solely as a treasury liquidity overlay, tenor limited to 6 months and executed via an offshore dealer desk, not a Belize domestic bankFX overlay on a cash-collateral basis through the Belize bank's treasury desk for net settlement balancing of multi-currency programmatic receivables, provided documentation of the underlying current-account transactions is maintained

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within 30 days after the anniversary of the date of incorporation and in every subsequent year; the annual license fee is payable in full at the same time, without audited financial statements being required.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Component A - Entity Particulars Registered IBC name and unique registration number. Transcribe the exact name and Reg. No. from the Certificate of Incorporation (e.g., Reg. No. 000012345-IBC); a prior name change must be evidenced by Registrar approval. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
Component B - Registered Office Belize registered office street address maintained by the licensed registered agent. The address must be the agent's registered Belize address; a non-resident IBC maintains no other required physical presence in Belize. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
Component C - Capitalization Authorized share capital, issued shares, and class rights, used to compute the government fee. Declare capital precisely, e.g., US$50,000 divided into 50,000 common shares; confirm no bearer shares remain outstanding. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
Component D - License Fee Computation Annual government fee payable by fee band. US$100 minimum up to US$1,000 maximum depending on authorized capital; remit via the registered agent or directly through the IFSC portal. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
Component E - Registered Agent Attestation Counter-signature and IFSC license number of the registered agent. Non-resident owners must instruct the agent in writing; the agent bears separate IFSC disciplinary liability for filing defects. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
Component F - Tax Exemption Declaration Declaration that the company transacted no Belize-sourced business and holds no Belize domestic real property. Any domestic Belize activity voids the exemption for those transactions; if SSP or ad-servicing contracts have Belize counterparties, seek tax counsel before signing. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
Component G - Register Confirmation Confirmation that the Directors' Register, Members' Register, and Beneficial Ownership Register are held at the registered office. Director and UBO identities are not publicly filed; registers must be physically accessible to IFSC and the Financial Intelligence Unit under the Beneficial Ownership Act, 2019. Late filing incurs a penalty of US$200 per month or part month, plus 10% statutory interest on the unpaid annual license fee; three consecutive defaults trigger Registrar strike-off and dissolution; restoration costs US$400 plus all arrears; false declarations attract IFSC suspension and fines up to BZ$25,000.
