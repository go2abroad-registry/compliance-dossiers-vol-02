Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Belize Tax Service (BTS), Ministry of Finance – online portal: https://taxservice.bz

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must register for a TIN with the Belize Tax Service using the prescribed taxpayer registration form and supporting documentation (certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office details, and director/shareholder identification). International Business Companies (IBCs) incorporated under the IBC Act are exempt from tax and are not issued a BTS tax ID; instead, their statutory IBC registration number serves as the corporate identifier. If such an entity unexpectedly engages in domestic taxable activities, it must convert or register as a taxable person with BTS for GST/TIN purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available. An IBC is automatically exempt from Belize income tax and stamp duty under the International Business Companies Act. It may not elect to be treated as a taxable domestic corporation, and it is generally prohibited from carrying on business with Belize residents or holding Belize real property.

12.5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Belize IBC is exempt from GST and therefore cannot claim zero-rating or input tax refunds. If the CRO were instead a domestic GST registrant, zero-rating would apply to exported goods delivered outside Belize and to services supplied to a non-resident person outside Belize, upon satisfying BTS documentary conditions and showing the supply is for immediate export or directly linked to an overseas customer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ASYCUDA World generated)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export
  • Commercial invoice describing goods/services and consignee
  • Proof of foreign currency payment received, such as an Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT confirmation

Belize does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping. GST invoices must contain prescribed particulars and be retained in paper or electronic form; filing and payment are made electronically via the Belize Tax Service portal. Export declarations are processed through ASYCUDA World, but there is no integrated fiscal invoice clearance for services.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating claims attract reassessment with interest, monetary penalties up to 200% of the tax understated, and potential criminal liability. For IBCs, misuse of the exemption (e.g., domestic sales without GST registration) results in loss of IBC status, retrospective tax assessments, and fines under the International Business Companies Act and Belize Tax Administration Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is pegged at 2.0000 BZD : 1.0000 USD. The regime sharply separates residents from non-residents. A Belize IBC that does not carry on business in Belize is statutorily non-resident, and s. 7 of the International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) disapplies the Exchange Control Act to such an IBC. Consequently, capital-account items (equity subscriptions and shareholder loans) are freely convertible, subject only to the bank's due-diligence file: board resolution, share subscription ledger, source-of-funds declaration, and corporate registry extracts. Current-account inflows, including clinical milestone fees, are freely remittable in USD. Profit remittance is permitted upon filing the IBC's annual return with the Companies and Corporate Affairs Registry (CCAR), settling the IFSC annual fee, and—where the account-holding bank requires it—producing a tax clearance confirming the 0% rate on foreign-source income. The operational hurdle is not legal convertibility but documentary precision: every wire must identify the protocol and milestone, or the CBB's suspicious-transaction reporting framework will classify it as an unidentified capital inflow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must ensure payment for exported goods within six months of shipment and must surrender all foreign currency receipts to an authorized dealer immediately upon receipt; the mandatory conversion ratio for residents is 100% (Exchange Control Regulations 1963, regs. 6-9). This regime does not apply to a qualifying non-resident IBC. Section 7 of the IBC Act exempts an IBC from the Exchange Control Act, subject to Ministerial direction; the exemption holds where the IBC conducts no business with Belize residents and its management and control are outside Belize. Accordingly, the clinical-trial CRO IBC may retain 100% of its USD milestone receipts without surrender or conversion and may fund downstream investigator-site payments directly from its offshore operational account.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (CBB), acting through its Exchange Control Department under the Exchange Control Act (Cap. 37, Laws of Belize, Revised Edition 2020) and the Exchange Control Regulations 1963. The CBB is the sole monetary authority and the licensing/supervisory body for all commercial banks and authorized dealers under the Banks and Financial Institutions Act. The International Financial Services Commission (IFSC) separately supervises licensed international financial services providers and registered IBC service providers, but exercises no foreign exchange authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Low. Belize has no statutory Electronic Money Institution licensing regime; the CBB requires any money transmission or payment activity touching Belize to be conducted through a licensed banking or money-transfer licensee. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Mercury, Payoneer and similar) do not onboard Belize IBCs for corporate current accounts because Belize falls outside their supported-jurisdiction matrices or within their higher-risk de-risking categories. Payoneer may support USD collections for services in some cases, but it cannot lawfully replace a licensed Belize commercial bank for escrow, site-payment, or trade-finance operations. Routing milestone funds through an unlicensed EMI risks a suspicious-activity report, loss of the local banking relationship, and non-compliance with the CBB AML/CFT Guidelines.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: sponsor or parent treasury sends a SWIFT MT103 from its house bank to the IBC's USD account at the Belize authorized dealer. The wire narrative must state the invoice number or 'Capital Contribution' reference, the clinical trial protocol identifier, and the milestone description. The beneficiary bank verifies each credit against the invoice register and the CTMSA before posting; for equity injections, the bank additionally requires the board resolution, share certificate number, source-of-funds declaration, and registered-agent confirmation of the ledger entry. Use IMF BPM6 services code 358 (Research and development services) for milestone fees, or an explicit capital-transfer description for equity. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No BZD documentation threshold for an exempt IBC: transfer pricing obligations do not arise because the IBC is outside the Belize income tax net. If the activity is restructured into a Belize-resident trading entity, arm's-length documentation (Local File) should be maintained where cross-border intercompany clinical-trial revenue exceeds BZD 500,000 per annum

Economic substance scope: ESR (International Business Companies (Economic Substance) Act, 2019) applies to IBCs carrying relevant activities. A clinical-trial CRO serving independent multinational sponsors is not a relevant activity and files a nil declaration; a CRO rendering services to its own foreign parent/affiliates, or holding IP, is in scope and must evidence in Belize: local premises, qualified employees, board meetings/records, adequate operating expenditure, and performance of core income-generating activities in Belize.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / abuse of IBC exemption

Statutory sanction: Loss of exempt status retroactively, assessment of Belize corporate income tax at 30% on the undisclosed income, interest at 1% per month, plus a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded; criminal tax evasion is punishable by a fine of up to BZD 25,000 and imprisonment for up to 2 years per offence

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax and penalties; civil recovery proceedings may target personal bank accounts, and convicted directors may face a Belize travel ban and custodial sentences of up to 2 years
Shell company / economic substance failure

Statutory sanction: Immediate ESR penalty of BZD 10,000 plus BZD 2,000 per month of continued default; failure to file the ESR declaration for 3 consecutive years triggers automatic strike-off of the IBC, forfeiture of the annual license, and reporting to the Belize Financial Intelligence Unit and foreign tax authorities (potential EU blacklisting)

Director personal liability: Directors remain personally liable for winding-up costs and third-party creditor losses if they continued trading after strike-off; personal fines up to BZD 25,000 and disqualification from serving as directors in Belize apply for repeated ESR violations
Beneficial ownership / AML non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Failure to file beneficial-ownership particulars: fine of up to BZD 50,000 and imprisonment for up to 2 years; where money-laundering is established, assets are subject to forfeiture and the IBC may be wound up by the Belize Registrar; charges under the Belize AML legislation carry fines of up to BZD 200,000

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners and nominee directors face vicarious criminal liability, including asset-freezing orders, travel bans pending prosecution, and imprisonment of up to 2 years; evasion of the BO filing duty without honest mistake is treated as fraud against the Registrar
Transfer pricing / profit-stripping conduit

Statutory sanction: The Income Tax Department may re-characterize the conduit, disallow payer deductions at source, impose 30% withholding on the deemed Belize-source payment, and apply penalties at 100% of the tax shortfall; professional advisers enabling the scheme are separately indictable under the Belize anti-avoidance rules

Director personal liability: Directors who instruct the profit-stripping arrangement are personally assessed for the evaded tax where the IBC has no attachable assets; personal liability extends to the compensation of the Belize research provider and to any sanction of up to 2 years imprisonment for aiding tax evasion
