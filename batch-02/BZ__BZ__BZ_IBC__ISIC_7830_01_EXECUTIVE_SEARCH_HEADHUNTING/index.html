Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

A non-resident-owned International Business Company is statutorily exempt from Belize income tax, capital gains tax, business tax, stamp duty, and exchange control. Therefore no active income tax registration is required. If the IBC voluntarily wishes to obtain a Belize Tax Identification Number (TIN) or if it unexpectedly undertakes a taxable domestic supply, it must register with the Belize Tax Service through the eServices portal using Form 1 (Taxpayer Registration), submitting the certificate of incorporation, IBC public register extract, registered office address, and evidence of beneficial ownership. For GST specifically, an IBC will only be required to register if it makes taxable supplies in Belize exceeding the statutory threshold, which is generally incompatible with an IBC's offshore nature.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election, partnership pass-through election, or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Belize domestic law. The Belize International Business Companies Act (Cap. 270) confers statutory full exemption; an IBC may not elect to be treated as a separate taxable resident entity for domestic purposes. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

12.5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

BZD 75,000 of annual taxable supplies in Belize; a person whose taxable turnover exceeds BZD 75,000 in any twelve-month period is required to register for GST. An IBC exporting executive-search/headhunting services to wholly non-resident clients ordinarily has zero Belize-sourced turnover and thus remains outside the GST net.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under the Belize GST Act applies to the direct export of goods and to internationally supplied services, including executive search, recruitment and headhunting services, where the recipient is a non-resident person, the supply is wholly performed outside Belize, and the payment is received in foreign currency from outside Belize. The exporter must be GST-registered for the supply and must retain evidence that the benefit of the service occurs outside Belize. A tax-exempt IBC supplying only offshore services is not in the GST system and does not need to lodge zero-rating claims.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Belize does not currently impose mandatory real-time e-invoicing, fiscal clearance, or government digital stamping. GST-registered persons must issue fiscal invoices or simplified receipts containing prescribed particulars and file GST returns electronically through the Belize Tax Service eServices portal, but no API-based real-time clearance is required.

The Belize Tax Service may audit zero-rated export transactions for up to six years, with no limitation in cases of fraud. A fraudulent or substantively incomplete zero-rating claim results in a default GST assessment at 12.5%, a penalty equal to 100% of the tax avoided, interest at 20% per annum, and criminal fines of up to BZD 50,000 and/or imprisonment of up to three years. Where the invoice is zero-rated on the basis of export but the foreign-exchange proceeds are not received within the 180-day repatriation deadline, the zero-rating is clawed back with full statutory penalty.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Belize dollar (BZD) is held at a fixed exchange rate of BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00. Current-account operations, including cross-border service fee remittances, dividend declarations derived from audited financial statements, and ordinary operating disbursements, are freely convertible through Authorized Dealers without Central Bank pre-approval. Resident-company capital-account operations remain subject to Central Bank authorization, delegated Authorized-Dealer approval, or documentary compliance. A Belize IBC is outside the resident net and is statutorily exempt from exchange-control restrictions, so profit remittance, capital repatriation, and foreign-currency retention are not restricted. In practice, commercial banks will apply an AML/CFT review to the underlying corporate records, board resolutions, and beneficial-ownership filings before processing any outward payment of profits or redemption proceeds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Resident exporters must take all reasonable steps to ensure that the full face value of export proceeds is received in Belize and offered for sale to an Authorized Dealer within six months from the date of shipment; the mandatory conversion ratio is 100%, unless the Central Bank grants a Foreign Currency Account permit or a specific exemption allowing retention in foreign currency. Belizean IBCs are exempt from the exchange-control surrender obligation; therefore, USD-denominated retainer income from executive-search clients may be retained indefinitely in the IBC's offshore-capable USD bank account without forced conversion into BZD.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Belize (Central Bank of Belize Act, Cap. 262); exchange control is administered by the Foreign Exchange Department under the Exchange Control Act, Cap. 45, with commercial banks acting as Authorized Dealers for day-to-day current-account transactions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low feasibility for direct onboarding of a Belize IBC with Wise Business, Payoneer, Mercury, or other offshore digital EMIs in the near term. These platforms generally do not accept Belize-registered IBCs because Belize is not a supported establishment jurisdiction and the non-resident IBC does not satisfy the processors' preferred substance requirements. Medium feasibility exists where the IBC appoints a licensed Money Services Business in Belize or a regulated B2B payment agent in the United States as an authorized payment intermediary. Wise B2B may be used only as a sub-threshold disbursement rail for paying overseas researchers, job-board platforms, and independent contractors in supported recipient jurisdictions, on condition that the Belizean commercial bank remains the primary operating account and that all EMI activity is reconciled monthly against the bank ledger.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injection from the shareholder must be executed by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own personal or corporate account to the IBC's USD account at the Belizean commercial bank. The wire narrative must state 'Capital Subscription / Share Premium' followed by the IBC's registered name and corporate number. The receiving bank will require a certified board resolution allotting the shares, an updated register of members, a signed capital-contribution schedule, and confirmation that the injected funds are free of criminal taint. Because the IBC is exempt from Belize exchange control, no Central Bank approval is required for the inward remittance; the bank may apply a 24-to-72-hour clearing hold for transfers above USD 50,000 while verifying source-of-funds documentation. The standard correspondent route for USD is through JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., New York, or Bank of America, N.A.; the remitter should include the IBC's full account number, the Belizean bank's SWIFT code, and the beneficiary's registered-address reference.Outbound settlements for subcontractor fee-splits, overseas specialist-researcher payments, job-board subscriptions, and client refunds are cleared primarily by SWIFT MT103 messages from the Belizean bank's USD correspondent for amounts above USD 2,000, using the purpose narrative 'Professional Services Disbursement / Executive Search Fee Split' together with the underlying invoice reference. For sub-threshold operational payments, Wise B2B rails may be used where the recipient's jurisdiction is fully supported and where no BZD conversion is involved. Commercial letters of credit are not appropriate for ISIC 7830 executive-search services because the deliverable is intangible; LC clearing is reserved for any physical procurement or event media spend contractually supporting the search mandate. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

0% — Belize International Business Company (IBC) statutory exemption under s. 3(1)(a) of the International Business Companies Act, Cap. 270, exempting the company from all Belizean taxes for 50 years from incorporation, renewable for a further 50 years. The general Belize Business Tax headline rate of 1.75% of gross receipts (applicable to onshore taxable companies) is not chargeable to an exempt IBC.
