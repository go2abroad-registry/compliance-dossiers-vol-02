Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Generally permissible for a registered office, provided the address is a physical street address within Alberta and is not solely a post office box. A virtual office or registered agent service is acceptable for corporate registration for a cross-border e-commerce dietary supplement seller. However, if the entity obtains a Health Canada Natural Product Number (NPN) or a Natural Health Product Site Licence, or if it registers under the Alberta Pharmacy and Drug Act or Health Professions Act, the competent regulator may require an actual physical establishment address and may treat a virtual office as insufficient for the commercial substance requirements of the industry.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada operates a free current account convertibility regime under IMF Article VIII. Capital account transactions are largely liberalized, permitting unrestricted repatriation of corporate profits, dividends, and equity investments for Canadian-incorporated entities. Profit remittance requires only standard corporate documentation, tax filings, and beneficial ownership disclosure; no surrender obligations or quantitative restrictions apply to legitimate business remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canadian exporters are not subject to mandatory surrender of foreign exchange proceeds. Receipts must be reported to the Bank of Canada through the Electronic Data Interchange Reporting System (EDIR) within 30 days if exceeding CAD 50,000. Transactions occur at prevailing market rates with no statutory conversion ratios; compliance centers on accurate valuation and timely reporting.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Offshore digital EMIs face significant legal constraints under FINTRAC AML obligations and Alberta-specific health product regulations. High-risk merchant accounts for dietary supplements require enhanced due diligence, provincial licensing where applicable, and compliance with Health Canada Natural Health Product rules; many fintech providers impose geographic or product restrictions.
