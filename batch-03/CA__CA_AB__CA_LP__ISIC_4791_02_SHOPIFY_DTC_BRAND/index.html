Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Canada and offshore services supplied to non-residents qualify for zero-rated GST. Eligibility requires verifiable proof of export or non-resident recipient status.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

All GST/HST registrants are required to file returns and remit tax electronically via the CRA My Business Account portal. Real-time fiscal clearance and digital stamping are not mandatory for standard registrants but apply to large businesses under the Modernized GST/HST framework.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility under a floating exchange rate regime. The Canadian dollar (CAD) is a freely traded currency with no central bank restrictions on inward or outward transfers. Profit remittances from an Alberta Limited Partnership (CA_LP) to non-resident partners are permitted without Bank of Canada approval, but are subject to Part XIII Canadian withholding tax under the Income Tax Act (unless reduced by an applicable tax treaty), and partnership income allocation rules must be reported via T5013 slips. No surrender or repatriation approval is required for capital distributions, but the partnership must also comply with the Partnership Act (Alberta) and the Income Tax Act re: non-resident partner tax payment obligations via the T3/T5013 process. Dividends from a subsidiary corporation would be different, but in an LP pass-through flows, non-resident partners must receive their share via bank wire and the LP must remit withholding tax to the CRA using Form NR4 or equivalent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements in Canada. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency in a Canadian or offshore bank account, converted at market rates, or hedged, without any conversion mandate. Nevertheless, all cross-border wire transfers in or out of Canada equal to or exceeding CAD $10,000 must be reported by the financial institution to FINTRAC; the partnership must maintain underlying commercial evidence for such transactions and be ready to produce it under audit.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (BOC) with coordinated administrative oversight by the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) and the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC) under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA). Exchange control and currency policy are also subject to the Federal Department of Finance.

Frequent high-value FX forward mismatches or perceived FX speculation

Root cause: Frequent high-value FX forward mismatches or perceived FX speculation

Defense evidence: Cash-flow forecast of inbound Shopify sales and outbound trade supplier invoices; purchase orders and supplier contracts; a stated FX hedging policy restricted to natural hedging and averages; evidence that DTC proceeds are specifically used for operating expenses; no leverage or exotic option trades.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections into the Alberta LP escrow account should use the SWIFT MT103 channel with the bank's SSI, the LP's name exactly as per ALTIS registry, and a purpose description: 'Capital contribution for investment in Shopify DTC brand, pursuant to LP Agreement.'. A Canadian bank does not impose a statutory purpose code, but to ensure expedited onboarding, use CAD settlement and the following transaction detail: 'Payment for direct investment into CA_LP' (CBOPS/Statistics Canada classification 5.1.1 direct investment). The wire must be accompanied by a copy of the LP agreement, the authorized signatory certification, and a breakdown of funds from each limited partner. For amounts > CAD $10,000, the bank will report to FINTRAC; for incoming capital > CAD $100,000, a source-of-funds declaration form and recent bank statement of the remitting Algerian/North American LP may also be required. Ensure that the escrow account is tagged 'Capital Escrow Account – CA_LP' to avoid commingling with operating funds. If coming from an entity jurisdiction subject to sanctions, expect additional require a legal opinion on the absence of OFAC/UN/OSFI sanctions.Trade disbursements to offshore suppliers for product procurement must be effectuated through SWIFT MT103 with full beneficiary bank details and a narrative invoice reference, or via a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) issued by the Canadian bank (MT700/MT707) if the supplier requires confirmed payment security. For e-commerce inventory to Shopify Fulfillment Network or third-party logistics (3PL) centres, a series of SWIFT MT103 wires with purpose 'Trade settlement for goods' is acceptable. The LP must maintain the invoice, airway bill, purchase order, and customs entry for each MT103/NTRC. Payments to Shopify Inc. for platform fees and app subscriptions are via USD/CAD disbursements through the integrated PSB; those should be settled in CAD from the operating account. All outbound wire transfers must be batch-level screened against sanctions and flagged accordingly; a monthly reconciliation between the ERP clearing account, the PSP settlement report, and the bank statements is mandatory.Deliverable FX Forwards (CAD/USD, CAD/EUR, CAD/CNY) with a Canadian Schedule I bank; typically settled in CAD and requiring a bilateral ISDA/CSA or bank-internal ISDA Master Agreement.FX Swaps and Currency Swaps for maturity-matching of the GP's capital-injection forecast and forecasted supplier payables; permitted without issues due to full convertibility.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDF) are admissible but only for currencies with emerging-market central restrictions (e.g., CNY offshore NDF); since CAD is freely convertible, NDF on CAD/USD is not appropriate. All hedging instruments must be documented as risk-management in substance and marked-to-market monthly; no prohibited naked option writing.
