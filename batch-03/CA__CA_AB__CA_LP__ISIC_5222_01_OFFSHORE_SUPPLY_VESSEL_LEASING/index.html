Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Partial permissibility. The Alberta Partnership Act requires the limited partnership to maintain a registered office at a physical, serviceable street address in Alberta and to appoint an Alberta registered agent authorized to accept service of process. A post office box, mail-forwarding virtual address, or cloud-based office alone does not satisfy the statutory test. A commercial virtual office package that provides a physical Alberta street address and an agent for service is acceptable for this chartering administration structure, provided that books and records are reasonably accessible for FINTRAC examination and for inspection by the Alberta Registrar on lawful demand.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. Each limited partner must provide a capital contribution in cash or property as set out in the Limited Partnership Declaration; deferred or conditional contributions are permitted if expressly described. No escrow account is required under the Partnership Act, but capital contribution schedules must be maintained and signed by the general partner.

Funding & unlock path: Requires municipal fire safety certification for any quayside or shore-based offices, structural load inspection for vessel maintenance bays or warehouses, and occupational health and safety clearance under Alberta OHS Code or Canada Labour Code Part II for federally regulated marine operations.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Canada Revenue Agency / Agence du revenu du Canada (CRA-ARC); national portal: canada.ca/en/revenue-agency; digital services: Business Registration Online (BRO), My Business Account (My BA), GST/HST NETFILE.

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident Alberta Limited Partnership (LP): (1) determine Canadian nexus, i.e., Canadian-source business income and/or taxable supplies exceeding the GST/HST small-supplier threshold trigger registration; (2) obtain a CRA Business Number (BN) for the LP using BRO or Form RC1, including partnership deed, Alberta Companies Office LP registration, Canadian representative appointment (Form RC59 if non-resident), and NR301 declaration for each non-resident partner; (3) open the GST/HST program account (RT) because the LP is the registrant for indirect tax purposes even though it is fiscally transparent for income tax; (4) select the filing period (annual/quarterly/monthly) and file the T5013 Partnership Information Return; (5) for corporate partners, Alberta corporate tax accounts are created at the partner level, not the LP level.

Tax ID type & application path: Pass_Through_Eligible (Alberta LP is fiscally transparent under ITA s.96; the partnership is a reporting and GST/HST registration unit, while each partner is assessed on its share of Canadian-source business income; Alberta has no partnership-level provincial income tax). — For a non-resident Alberta Limited Partnership (LP): (1) determine Canadian nexus, i.e., Canadian-source business income and/or taxable supplies exceeding the GST/HST small-supplier threshold trigger registration; (2) obtain a CRA Business Number (BN) for the LP using BRO or Form RC1, including partnership deed, Alberta Companies Office LP registration, Canadian representative appointment (Form RC59 if non-resident), and NR301 declaration for each non-resident partner; (3) open the GST/HST program account (RT) because the LP is the registrant for indirect tax purposes even though it is fiscally transparent for income tax; (4) select the filing period (annual/quarterly/monthly) and file the T5013 Partnership Information Return; (5) for corporate partners, Alberta corporate tax accounts are created at the partner level, not the LP level.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (Alberta LP is fiscally transparent under ITA s.96; the partnership is a reporting and GST/HST registration unit, while each partner is assessed on its share of Canadian-source business income; Alberta has no partnership-level provincial income tax).

Available election forms

Canada has no general check-the-box election. Available elections: s.216 ITA election allowing a non-resident lessor of ships/aircraft to file on net Canadian shipping income instead of Part XIII gross withholding; s.273 ETA partnership election consolidating GST/HST registrations of the LP and its members; s.227 ETA election for zero-rating adjustments and streamlined accounting; anti-hybrid mismatch election and exclusion mechanisms under ITA ss.12.2 and 22.2 (OECD BEPS hybrid rules).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

C$30,000 of taxable supplies in a single calendar quarter or C$30,000 cumulative over four consecutive calendar quarters (small supplier exemption, ETA s.148); a legislated increase of the threshold to C$50,000 has been announced for supplies made after the 2025-2026 transitional period.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For offshore supply-vessel leasing, zero-rating applies under Schedule VI to the ETA when: (i) the supply is of a marine vessel, including a bareboat or time charter of an offshore platform support vessel, and the purchaser is a non-resident or the vessel is exported/departs Canada promptly (Part I, s.1); (ii) the service is supplied to a non-resident in respect of a marine vessel used predominantly in international commercial transportation of goods or passengers (Part V, s.10); or (iii) the service relates to the international carriage of goods by water (Part IV). The supplier must not be a small supplier for the relevant GST/HST net tax window and must hold contemporaneous evidence of the non-resident status, international voyage, and actual receipt of the charter consideration. Charters confined to Canadian domestic offshore waters remain taxable at 5% GST unless the vessel departs Canada.

  • Customs Export Declaration (CBSA B13A 'Export Declaration' or Canadian Export Reporting System (CERS) marine-mode record)
  • Bill of Lading (or Sea Waybill) evidencing the offshore voyage and the non-resident destination or international leg
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank SWIFT MT103 credit advice) evidencing actual receipt of charter hire from the non-resident charterer
  • Charter Party Agreement (bareboat, time, or voyage charter) identifying the non-resident charterer
  • Vessel International Registry Certificate and Canadian Certificate of Entry/Registry
  • GPS/AIS voyage log and bunker-delivery receipts proving departure from Canadian jurisdiction
  • CBSA A6 General Declaration / marine cargo manifest

No continuous-transaction-control (CTC), real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping mandate exists in Canada. Invoicing must contain the particulars prescribed by ETA s.221 and the Information Respecting GST/HST Regulations. Electronic filing via GST/HST NETFILE is mandatory for registrants with annual taxable supplies exceeding C$1.5 million and for claimants of certain rebates. The federal government is progressively requiring PEPPOL-compliant e-invoicing for its suppliers (phased 2025-2028), but this is a procurement rule, not a fiscal CTC mandate.

CRA audit scrutiny includes the Refund Integrity program and marine-industry verification of exported-vessel documentation. Penalties: ETA s.285.1(1) penalty equal to the greater of C$100 and 25% of any net-tax understatement or zero-rated refund overstatement; s.280.1 false-statement penalty for knowing or grossly negligent declarations; reassessment beyond the normal four-year period (up to seven years) where misrepresentation or negligence is established; clawback of zero-rated benefits with interest under ETA s.280; criminal prosecution for fraud against the government under Criminal Code s.380(1); and potential debarment from export zero-rating programmes.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian dollar (CAD) is fully convertible under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. There are no direct foreign exchange controls, capital account restrictions, or prior approval requirements for any current or capital account transactions in the Bank of Canada Act or the Income Tax Act (Canada). Profit remittance from an Alberta Limited Partnership is freely executable via SWIFT or Lynx, subject only to the withholding tax obligations embedded in Part XIII of the Income Tax Act for distributions to non-resident partners and standard FINTRAC reporting on international electronic funds transfers exceeding CAD 10,000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada imposes no mandatory export proceeds surrender ratio and no statutory repatriation timeline or forced conversion rule. Offshore marine charter hire invoiced by the LP is freely retainable in CAD, USD, EUR, or any other convertible currency. Reporting duties are limited to FINTRAC threshold reporting and CRA tax compliance for foreign accrual or transfer pricing risks, if any.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (BoC); prudential supervision by the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI); AML/ATF enforcement by the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC); monetary policy and systemic liquidity management by BoC.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium. Offshore fintech EMIs and digital banks are not recognized as banks under the Bank Act (Canada). To process CAD payments to or from Canadian counterparties, an EMI must be registered as a Money Service Business (MSB) with FINTRAC and maintain compliance under the PCMLTFA. Such institutions can provide ancillary liquidity routing, sub-accounts, and international payroll disbursement, but they are legally constrained from serving as the sole custodian for capital escrow or equity injection funds due to their lack of direct access to Lynx and limited deposit insurance. Use of an MSB requires an underlying Schedule I bank sponsor.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

UBO opacity in the Alberta LP ownership cascade or use of bearer partnership interests.

Root cause: UBO opacity in the Alberta LP ownership cascade or use of bearer partnership interests.

Defense evidence: A verified 25%+ UBO chart, a notarized Partnership Interest Register, certified copies of the Limited Partnership Agreement and all subsequent LP amendments, and a sworn declaration of source of wealth and source of funds for each direct or indirect partner exceeding an aggregate 10% interest.
Offshore maritime charter activity without clear Canadian commercial substance.

Root cause: Offshore maritime charter activity without clear Canadian commercial substance.

Defense evidence: Annual voyage forecast, vessel IMO registration certificates, tonnage certificates, certified charter hire agreements, a substantive Calgary or Edmonton office lease, Alberta employment records for operational staff, and a detailed corporate budget demonstrating ongoing Canadian expenditure.
Sanctions or adverse media flags on a vessel registry, bareboat charterer, or end-user of the offshore supply vessel.

Root cause: Sanctions or adverse media flags on a vessel registry, bareboat charterer, or end-user of the offshore supply vessel.

Defense evidence: A targeted OFAC/EU/UN sanctions screening certificate, rigorous background checks against the Consolidated Canadian Autonomous Sanctions List and the OSC's designation list, maritime P&I insurance certificates issued by an A-rating underwriter, and a completed counterparty risk questionnaire mapped to the entities in the charter chain.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign limited partners inject capital via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code G50 or G2X. The payment reference field must strictly contain the following syntax: Equity Injection - Capital Call - [LP Name] - [Date]. The clearing bank verification packet must include a capital call notice signed by the General Partner, a certified copy of the Limited Partnership Agreement, and the subscribing partner's full KYC/KYB file. Funds are cleared in CAD via Lynx (High Value Payment System) or in USD via SWIFT and then converted on the spot market through the Canadian bank's treasury desk.Trade settlements for offshore marine leasing are executed via SWIFT MT103 for operational disbursements and charter hire. For capital equipment purchases or vessel acquisition, use a Commercial Letter of Credit under UCP 600 or a Standby Letter of Credit for charter performance. Escrow_Maritime rails are recommended for disputed hire or demurrage settlements; these are structured as Canadian trust accounts or a commercial escrow agreement administered by the bank's corporate trust division, releasing funds against delivery certificates and endorsed invoices.FX Forwards (vanilla CAD/USD/EUR/GBP under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a Collateral Support Annex; tenor typically up to 24 months)Cross-Currency Swaps (utilized for long-term CAPEX and vessel debt servicing hedges matching CAD/USD cash flow profiles)FX Options (purchased European options for non-linear hedging of vessel charter fixture volatility and fuel price exposure)NDFs - Limited Viability: NDFs are not a standard instrument for CAD because CAD/USD is fully convertible, deeply liquid, and actively traded onshore; NDFs are quoted only on a by-exception basis for clients with restricted USD access or trapped cash concerns in high-risk jurisdictions.
