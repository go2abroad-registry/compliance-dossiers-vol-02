Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an Alberta limited partnership. Contributions are fixed contractually in the Limited Partnership Agreement. No escrow deposit rule is imposed by statute, though funds may be held in trust by the General Partner or an escrow agent pending issuance of the Certificate of Registration.

Funding & unlock path: Office occupancy clearance requires compliance with the Alberta Fire Code (AR 229/2007) and the Occupational Health and Safety Code (AR 87/2009), including fire safety certification, building occupancy permit, and workplace hazard assessment. Structural floor-load inspection is required where server racks or heavy equipment are installed.

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities register for a Business Number (BN) via CRA online portal or Form RC59. Non-residents obtain statutory TIN/VAT ID by submitting incorporation documents, Canadian business address, and financial statements. Alberta-specific provincial filings may apply separately for corporate tax.

0% GST rate applies to goods exported from Canada or services performed outside Canada and not consumed in Canada. Offshore services to non-residents qualify if properly documented.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains a floating exchange rate regime with full current account convertibility under the Foreign Exchange Act. Capital account transactions exceeding CAD 1 million per transaction or per annum for portfolio investments require prior Bank of Canada approval. Profit remittances to non-resident partners are permitted after Canadian tax withholding and filing of T1134 foreign property report with the Canada Revenue Agency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender timeline exists; exporters should repatriate foreign exchange proceeds and convert to CAD within 90 days of receipt. Authorized foreign exchange dealers must file large transaction reports with FINTRAC. Proceeds must be supported by commercial invoices, shipping documents, and partnership resolutions.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for offshore digital EMIs; Canadian entities are restricted to licensed domestic banks or authorized foreign exchange dealers for cross-border transfers under FINTRAC and OSFI oversight.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Alberta does not impose an independent audit on a limited partnership based only on revenue or asset thresholds. The audit becomes compulsory where: (a) the LP Agreement or an investor rights deed requires audited financial statements; (b) the LP is a reporting issuer or is investing in reportable securities; (c) the LP is registered as a dealer, exempt-market dealer, or investment fund manager with the Alberta Securities Commission; or (d) a lender, acquirer, or enterprise cloud-tools customer contract conditionally requires audited statements.
