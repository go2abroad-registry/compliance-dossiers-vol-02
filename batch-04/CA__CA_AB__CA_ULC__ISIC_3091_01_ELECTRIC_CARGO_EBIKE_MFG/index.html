E-government filing portal

Phase A — Incorporation: file Articles of Incorporation designating an unlimited liability corporation under the Alberta Business Corporations Act (RSA 2000, c B-9, s. 15) through the Alberta Corporate Registry online; receive the Certificate of Incorporation and Alberta corporate access number (no minimum capital requirement; non-resident ownership permitted; no Canadian-resident director requirement under the ABCA). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Pass_Through_Eligible (US federal default under Reg. § 301.7701-3: a foreign eligible entity with at least one unlimited-liability member is classified as a partnership/disregarded entity unless an election is made). For Canadian domestic tax purposes the ULC is subject to Entity_Level_Taxation as a resident Alberta corporation — federal Part I tax at 13.5% on qualified Manufacturing and Processing (M&P) profits (s. 125.1 Reduction) plus Alberta corporate income tax at 8%, for a combined headline rate of 21.5%; the CCPC small-business deduction is generally unavailable to a non-resident-controlled ULC. Territorial_Scope: residence-based taxation of worldwide income; export sales are zero-rated for GST/HST.

Available election forms

1) US check-the-box election (IRC § 7701; Form 8832): the ULC may elect to be taxed as an association (corporation) to align US and Canadian classifications and reduce hybrid mismatch. 2) Canadian ULC corporation-election under the federal hybrid-entity reforms (Income Tax Act, enacted following the federal 2021 Budget and related draft legislation): a Canadian-resident ULC may elect to be treated as a corporation for the purpose of computing its members' Canadian tax liabilities, addressing Canada-US Treaty Article IV(7) hybrid treatment. 3) Related-party GST/HST election under ETA s. 273(1): closely related corporations may elect to treat inter-corporate supplies as made for nil consideration. 4) No Alberta check-the-box or separate provincial elective classification exists; corporate status is determined solely by the ABCA.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Canadian dollar (CAD) is fully convertible under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. There is no distinction between current-account and capital-account controls, no prior approval regime, and no surrender/conversion obligation. Equity injections, intercompany loans, and profit remittances by a non-resident-owned Alberta ULC are unrestricted. Remittance of dividends is subject only to Part XIII withholding (statutory 25%, typically reduced to 5-15% under applicable tax treaties) and to the corporate attribution rules if the ULC has elected or is deemed not to be resident. No repatriation license or foreign-investment screening applies because the businss activity (urban electric cargo bicycle and scooter manufacturing) is not a reserved or restricted sector under the Investment Canada Act; only notification is required if the enterprise value threshold is exceeded.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Canada imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no designated account into which export receipts must be deposited. Export earnings may be freely held in CAD or any foreign currency in Canada or abroad. Compulsory reporting obligations are limited to (i) FINTRAC cross-border currency or monetary-instrument reports for movements exceeding CAD 10,000; (ii) FINTRAC large-value transaction reporting on cash or virtual-currency transactions above CAD 10,000 in a single transaction or within 24 hours; and (iii) Statistics Canada merchandise trade export declarations (via the Canadian Border Services Agency) for shipment values above the export reporting threshold. The ULC finance team may contractually request or invoice settlement in CAD, USD, EUR, or CNY without regulatory constraint.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada (Banque du Canada) as monetary and reserve-management authority; foreign exchange regulation is liberalized with no standing FX-control statute. Administrative oversight for AML/CFT wire and cross-border reporting rests with FINTRAC under the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act (PCMLTFA); tax-related cross-border compliance (Part XIII withholding, Form T1135 reporting) is administered by the Canada Revenue Agency; the Department of Finance Canada controls exchange rate and capital-account policy jointly with the Bank of Canada.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-High as a secondary liquidity rail; Low as a primary operating account. Non-resident digital EMI platforms (Wise, Airwallex, Ebury) operate in Canada as Money Service Businesses (MSBs) registered with FINTRAC rather than as Schedule I banks, and therefore cannot provide credit, commercial letters of credit, or regulatory capital treatment. Legal constraints under the PCMLTFA require full beneficial-ownership and source-of-funds verification, and foreign EMIs may not hold insured deposits under the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation (CDIC) framework. Recommended use: nominal, low-value FX conversion and last-mile cross-border supplier payments; all material trade settlements and capital injections must transit a Schedule I bank to preserve tax attribute stacking, escrow integrity, and audit integrity for the ULC.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shares and capital contributions from the non-resident parent are effected through the SWIFT MT103 CHIPS/lnterpay wire protocol into the ULC's BMO Capital Escrow Account. Field 70 must carry an explicit purpose descriptor such as 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — ISSUANCE OF UNLIMITED LIABILITY SHARES' to satisfy ISO 20022 remittance data expectations. The bank will require an escrow instruction letter, a certified copy of the Alberta incorporation certificate, and confirmation from the receiving officer that the transfer is not a loan but a permanent equity contribution. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium-High as a secondary liquidity rail; Low as a primary operating account.
