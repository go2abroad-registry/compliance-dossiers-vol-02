Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Canada maintains a fully convertible currency regime under the Bank of Canada Act. Both capital and current accounts are unrestricted; profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are permitted without prior approval, subject only to tax filing and beneficial ownership reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds is required. Exporters may retain foreign exchange receipts; voluntary conversion through authorized dealers is available. All transactions must be reported to the Canada Revenue Agency and comply with FINTRAC AML obligations.

Central bank supervision: Bank of Canada

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed in Canada. Platforms such as Wise provide cross-border rails but require Canadian entities to route through licensed banks. Legal constraints include FINTRAC registration and prohibition on direct EMI banking services.

Actually: remit via electronic funds transfer using pre-authorized debit or CRA Web payments; if no Canadian-payroll branch, set up a corporate tax account. Instalments are calculated on the prior-year's tax liability under s.156. Late filing penalty under ITA s.162(1): 5% of the unpaid balance plus 1% per complete month of delay (maximum 12 months); repeated failure: 10% on the first offence? Actually, the penalty is 5% plus 1% per month up to 12 months (max 17%). Late payment interest charged at the CRA prescribed daily compound rate (currently ~9% for 2024-25). Failure to remit Part XIII withholding tax triggers a 10% penalty on withheld amounts plus interest; gross negligence penalty under s.163(2) is 50% of any tax understated; criminal prosecution under s.239 can reach 200% of the tax evaded and imprisonment.
